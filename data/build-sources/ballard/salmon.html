How to read these numbers

Latest daily is the newest published WDFW count row, not a real-time fish counter. Data age tells you how old that row is. Run context keeps a species-specific zero from being overinterpreted outside its typical migration period. The recent average and trend are descriptive, not a forecast.

Source and limits

Counts come from the Washington Department of Fish & Wildlife Lake Washington salmon-count tables. Published counts can lag the current date and are preliminary. This tool does not estimate uncounted fish between updates.
