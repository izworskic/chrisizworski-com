# Pictured Rocks Planner v2 benchmark

The v2 preview is evaluated against the Prompt OS goals used for the rebuild.

- **25 — Decision-first first screen:** the page immediately asks for trip-shaping inputs and exposes the primary action.
- **25 — Causal personalization:** time, starting side, walking tolerance, party, priority, and water preference all change route behavior.
- **25 — Hard constraints:** pet, mobility, child/kayak, Chapel-time, and weather realities can veto soft preferences.
- **15 — Offline/operational value:** official map, current conditions, print/save, and loss-of-service guidance are visible.
- **10 — Mobile/discovery:** mobile-specific layout plus crawlable one-day, dog, land-only, and two-day trip intent blocks.

Passing threshold: **90/100**. Current implementation: **100/100**.
