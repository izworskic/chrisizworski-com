Superior Hiking Trail / Split Rock River

Can you actually hike the Split Rock River Loop?

Both sides are open again. The bridge at the top of the loop is not. Choose a route that doesn't assume the river can be crossed.

SHTA trail bulletin dated October 5, 2026 · Reviewed October 12, 2026
Critical distinction: the eastern and western renewed trail sections are open, but there is NO bridge over the Split Rock River at the former crossing. This is not a live trail-monitoring service.

SHTA describes the loop as approximately 5.0 miles including 1.2 miles of access spur. The alternate changes the itinerary; a verified alternate-loop mileage is not currently available in this tool.

    Parking and overnight restrictions

    Split Rock River Wayside is at Minnesota Highway 61 milepost 43.5. The SHTA says no overnight parking at this trailhead. If you are through-hiking or camping, check official designated options rather than assuming roadside parking is permitted.
