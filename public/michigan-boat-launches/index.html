How the launch data and weather are handled

Michigan DNR boating-access records create the statewide inventory. The tool keeps a stable DNR source ID, published coordinate and facility review state. Records marked Review Needed, reference-only entries and records without usable source coordinates are withheld. Carefully documented municipal launches can fill a public-data gap only when the operator and launch location are independently supported.

  • The map never creates a launch from fuzzy matching or a guessed coordinate.
  • Missing parking, ramp, fee or hours fields remain “Not listed.”
  • Driving distance is used when a destination has been searched and routing is available; otherwise distance is labeled as straight-line.
  • Local weather comes from the National Weather Service for the selected launch coordinate. It is planning context, not a statement that the ramp, lake or river is safe.
