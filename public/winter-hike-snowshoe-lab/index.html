How to read this evidence

Snow on the landscape

NOAA/NOHRSC provides modeled grid-cell snow depth, not a measurement on the trail. NASA MODIS historical images independently show regional satellite imagery, not parking-lot clearing. Conditions under trees, on boardwalks and in wind-exposed areas can be very different.

Forecast where you’ll walk

NWS supplies daytime temperatures, wind, freeze-thaw potential, precipitation and current warnings. It cannot tell you whether a specific bridge or footpath is passable.

Know before you drive

Satellite imagery and snow models do not establish which lot is plowed or which road is open. Show these separately as unknown unless an authoritative dated report exists. Groomed cross-country ski tracks must not be damaged by hikers or snowshoes.
