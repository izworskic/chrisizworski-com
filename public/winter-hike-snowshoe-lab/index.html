0.5 mi · marsh boardwalk · winter snowshoe or cautious walk

Sand Point Marsh Trail

The National Park Service describes Sand Point Road and its beach parking area as normally plowed in winter. The wetland boardwalk can be icy; snowshoes are often needed. No pets.

2026 construction caveat: NPS announced a Sand Point Road closure for October 1–14. A subsequent reopening has not been independently verified. Check current park alerts before driving.

2.65 mi · marked winter snowshoe loop

Munising Snowshoe Trail

NPS directs visitors to the ski trail parking area off East City Limits Road, then a short walk to the blue-blazed trail beyond the fence. The park describes its winter ski-area lots as normally plowed. The groomed cross-country ski loops are off limits to walking and snowshoes.

The exact current lot clearance and any storm or grooming restrictions have not been independently verified here. Use the NPS description, not a guessed trailhead map pin.

How to read this evidence

Snow on the landscape

NOAA/NOHRSC provides modeled grid-cell snow depth, not a measurement on the trail. NASA MODIS historical images independently show regional satellite imagery, not parking-lot clearing. Conditions under trees, on boardwalks and in wind-exposed areas can be very different.

Forecast where you’ll walk

NWS supplies daytime temperatures, wind, freeze-thaw potential, precipitation and current warnings. It cannot tell you whether a specific bridge or footpath is passable.

Know before you drive

Satellite imagery and snow models do not establish which lot is plowed or which road is open. Show these separately as unknown unless an authoritative dated report exists. Groomed cross-country ski tracks must not be damaged by hikers or snowshoes.
