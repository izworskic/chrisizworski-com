      Source freshness

      Official sources first, context sources second

      National Weather Service and National Park Service data outrank inferred planning signals. Attraction operators govern their own hours and ticket status. Secondary sources add context but do not override primary sources.

      Use this for a Gatlinburg winter day, not a generic list

      Static travel guides can tell you that Winter Magic, mountain attractions, the aquarium and Great Smoky Mountains National Park exist. This planner is for the next decision: which of those choices still fit your actual date, time window, weather, daylight, closures, budget and group.

      The published 2026–27 Winter Magic season runs from November 5, 2026 through February 15, 2027. Live weather is only shown when the selected date is inside the available National Weather Service forecast window. Farther-out dates remain useful for seasonal planning without pretending a forecast exists.
