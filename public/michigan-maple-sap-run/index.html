07 / How it works

What makes sap run?

+
☼THAWING DAYAbove 32°F
→
◈SAP OPPORTUNITYNot a flow measurement

Sap pressure responds to freezing and thawing, but species, tree health and bud stage matter. The graphic explains the physics; the chart above displays actual NWS inputs only when in season.

1. The tree freezes

Cold air helps reset internal stem pressures. Nights below 32°F, especially into the 20s, can precede useful sap-flow conditions.

2. The tree thaws

Above-freezing daytime temperatures, often in the 40s, can generate positive pressure that moves sap through the tap hole.

3. The season eventually ends

Watch buds and tree health. Consecutive warm days and bud expansion can end quality syrup collection even when another freeze occurs.

Science references: Cornell Cooperative Extension · MSU Extension · University of Wisconsin Extension.

08 / Your questions answered

Michigan maple syrup timing questions

When does maple sap run in Michigan?

Most Michigan tapping activity is in late winter and early spring. Southern Lower Michigan often starts before the north; the Upper Peninsula can continue later. Some years bring earlier warm spells, so there is no reliable single start date for every property.

What temperatures make maple sap run?

A night below freezing followed by a day above freezing is a familiar favorable pattern. Temperatures in the 20s overnight and around the 40s in daytime are often useful, though moisture, tree dormancy and site conditions matter.

Does the map show actual sap flow?

No. It summarizes hourly NWS forecast conditions. Neither a “favorable” marker nor an index score is confirmation that a specific tree or sugarhouse has flowing sap. The site currently has no direct sap sensors or producer reports.

Where can I watch maple syrup being made in Michigan?

Public maple programs have been offered at Maple Row Sugarhouse near Jones, Chippewa Nature Center in Midland, Blandford Nature Center in Grand Rapids and MSU Tollgate Farm in Novi. Shepherd has a multi-day festival. Follow the listed organizer for confirmed dates, tours and admission.

How late can I collect maple sap?

Collection should stop when tree buds begin expanding or sap develops off flavors. Conditions differ by species and by location, and some red maples bud earlier. A weather model cannot observe the bud stage of your trees.

Can I tap a maple tree in October?

The traditional Michigan sugaring season is late winter into early spring. October is outside the modeled tapping window; this site deliberately marks fall and summer as off season rather than showing misleading daily sap-flow ratings.
