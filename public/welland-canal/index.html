Before you drive · ships today

Welland Canal ship schedule today: three checks before you leave

There is no dependable public promise that a particular freighter will enter a particular lock at a specific minute. Use the official Seaway order of turn for named traffic, recent AIS reports for last observed positions, and a public viewing location that suits the ship watching you want.

1. Check the official ship queue

Start with the Seaway's order-of-turn and vessel-transit information. Identify the ship and its published movement or next reported point. Operations can change its progress; a listed estimate is not a confirmed Lock 3 arrival appointment.

Open the official vessel queue ↗

2. Check the last AIS report

Find the vessel on the Welland Canal live ship map. Read its last-reported time and direction: a recent dot tells you where a ship was observed, not when it will enter its next lock. If coverage is missing or stale, don't interpret an empty map as an empty canal.

Check recent ship positions ↑

3. Choose where to watch

For a first visit, choose Lock 3 in St. Catharines and its museum platform. For the Thorold escarpment, research Lock 7's viewing centre. For the Lake Erie entrance, consider Port Colborne's Gateway Park. Verify public access before traveling.

Which direction is the ship going? Upbound means south, from Lake Ontario toward Lake Erie (Lock 1 toward Lock 8); downbound means north, from Lake Erie toward Lake Ontario. These are shipping directions, not predictions about an individual vessel's next lock.

Pleasure craft · 2026 operational guide

Private boat? The schedule is different.

The Seaway's commercial shipping traffic is not the same as the pleasure-craft reservation schedule. For 2026, pleasure-craft Welland Canal transits are scheduled May 1–October 25, with regular upbound Monday/Wednesday/Friday/Sunday and downbound Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday services through September 27. October 3–25 uses weekend-only lockages: Saturday upbound and Sunday downbound. Reservations and same-day reporting requirements apply.

Upbound craft must arrive at the Port Weller small-boat dock and report by 9:00 a.m.; downbound craft report at Port Colborne by 7:00 a.m., subject to official revisions. This summary is for planning, not navigation or proof of a reservation.

Canal basics

Welland Canal questions

Where can I find the Welland Canal ship schedule today?

The official Seaway order of turn is the starting point for named commercial traffic. Compare it with the recently reported AIS map; an official estimate or last-reported position is not a guaranteed lock-entry time.

What time will a freighter arrive at Lock 3?

A trustworthy exact arrival time cannot be calculated from the ship's AIS location alone. Check the official vessel queue, then verify whether the ship still has recent movement reports. Plan extra time at the Lock 3 museum viewing platform rather than assuming a fixed appointment.

Are ships going through the Welland Canal today?

Check the official Seaway vessel-transit page for named commercial traffic and the latest lock queue. The map on this page shows only fresh public AIS positions within the canal corridor; no reports does not establish no traffic.

Where is the best place to watch freighters on the Welland Canal?

Lock 3 in St. Catharines has a public viewing platform and museum. Lock 7 in Thorold and Lock 8 in Port Colborne are other established watch areas.

How many locks does the canal have?

Eight. Locks 1–7 lift or lower vessels through most of the roughly 99.5-metre elevation change. Lock 8 serves as the southern control lock.

Can a 1,000-foot Great Lakes freighter pass through?

No. Seaway vessel-length limits are approximately 740 feet, shorter than the 1,000-foot lakers that can navigate the upper Great Lakes.

Is a vessel on the map guaranteed to enter the next lock soon?

No. AIS shows reported position and motion, not an operational lock appointment. Ships may wait, berth, turn, or experience operational delays. Official order-of-turn information takes precedence.

Can I watch a Welland Canal camera here?

Yes. The Port Colborne Bridge 21 area camera can be opened on this page by choosing Load camera video. Its stream and uptime are controlled by YouTube and the publisher; it is not a camera inside Lock 8. A second Port Colborne source and historical Thorold Lock 7 camera listing are linked, without claims that they are working now.
