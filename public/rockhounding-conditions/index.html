How to use these readings

The most useful signals do not require photographs. They do need careful interpretation.

1 · A real disturbance

Look for a sourced wave peak and good observation coverage—not an invented numerical "agate score."

2 · A sustained change

Compare recent wave heights and the preceding 24 hours. If waves rebuild, the offshore lull has not persisted.

3 · Independent hazards

Check NWS alerts and local shoreline conditions before a visit. Offshore easing doesn't authorize water entry or collecting.

Agate Beach faces open Lake Superior. Bayshore Park lies in the harbor; this buoy does not measure Bayshore's sheltered-water waves. The water-level gauge is at Marquette, not Grand Marais.
