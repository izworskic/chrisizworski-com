Stiggs Brewery & Kitchen

Mike Castiglione&apos;s brewery in the 1899 railroad office in Boyne City, 30-odd taps leaning German, house-smoked barbecue out of the kitchen, a dog-friendly patio and live music. The map pin is placed off Park Street at block level rather than a verified rooftop.

The basics

  • Where: 112 S Park Street, Boyne City, MI 49712
  • Hours: not published in a weekly format, call ahead
  • Pours: beer
  • Plan on about 55 minutes
  • Food: house-smoked barbecue
  • Website: www.stiggsbrewingcompany.com/
  • Not a Petoskey Wine Region trail member, listed here because it is a working tasting room in the same country

Hours checked September 18, 2026.

Worth pairing with

  • Red Mesa Grill, 0.6 miles straight line. Latin American on Water Street in Boyne City, and the easiest real dinner if your loop finishes on the south arm of Lake Charlevoix.
  • Avalanche Preserve, 1.7 miles straight line. The 462-stair climb to the overlook above Boyne City and the south arm of Lake Charlevoix. The best earned view in the region.
