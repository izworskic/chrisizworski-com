Maple Moon Sugarbush & Winery

America&apos;s first maple winery, and a working sugarbush. Fermented maple wine, maple cider and syrup, free production tours, and wineshoeing excursions once there is snow on the ground. About as Northern Michigan as a tasting stop gets.

The basics

  • Where: 4454 Atkins Road, Petoskey, MI 49770
  • Hours: not published in a weekly format, call ahead at (231) 487-9058
  • Pours: wine, cider
  • Plan on about 40 minutes
  • Phone: (231) 487-9058
  • Website: www.mmsyrup.com/
  • Current member of the Petoskey Wine Region trail, checked 2026-09-18

Worth pairing with

  • Bay View, 3.2 miles straight line. A National Historic Landmark Chautauqua community of Victorian cottages above the bay, right next door to Petoskey. Drive it slowly or walk it.
  • Petoskey State Park, 3.2 miles straight line. The classic Petoskey stone beach on Little Traverse Bay, with a dune trail behind it. Best hunting is right at the waterline after a blow.
  • Little Traverse Wheelway, 3.6 miles straight line. A paved shoreline trail running the bay between Charlevoix, Petoskey and Harbor Springs. The Petoskey to Bay View stretch is the one to walk if you only have an hour.
