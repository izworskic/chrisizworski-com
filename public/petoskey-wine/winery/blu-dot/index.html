Petoskey Wine Region planner / Charlevoix

Blu Dot Farm & Vineyard

A farm vineyard on Boyne City Road outside Charlevoix where the tasting room is a converted vintage Airstream. Small-batch wine and wine slushies, open in season from May through October, and reachable off the regional bike path if you would rather ride than drive.

The basics

  • Where: 11399 Boyne City Road, Charlevoix, MI 49720
  • Hours: Mon-Sun 12pm-7pm. Open in season, May through October
  • Pours: wine
  • Plan on about 40 minutes
  • Phone: (231) 622-3753
  • Website: www.bludotwine.com
  • Current member of the Petoskey Wine Region trail, checked 2026-09-18

Worth pairing with

  • Mount McSauba Recreation Area, 2.0 miles straight line. Dune and forest trails above the Lake Michigan beach on the north edge of Charlevoix, with a real overlook at the top.
  • Charlevoix South Pier Light, 2.9 miles straight line. The red pier light at the channel between Round Lake and Lake Michigan. Walk out and wait for a freighter or the drawbridge to open.
  • Downtown Charlevoix, 3.0 miles straight line. Bridge Street between the drawbridge and Round Lake, plus the Earl Young mushroom houses a few blocks off it.
