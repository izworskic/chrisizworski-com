Petoskey Brewing

In the 1898 brewery building on M-119 on the way to Harbor Springs, with a full kitchen. Open daily into the evening, which is useful when everything else has closed at five.

The basics

  • Where: 1844 M-119, Petoskey, MI 49770
  • Hours: Mon-Sun 11:30am-9pm
  • Pours: beer
  • Plan on about 50 minutes
  • Food: full kitchen
  • Website: petoskeybrewing.com/
  • Not a Petoskey Wine Region trail member, listed here because it is a working tasting room in the same country

Hours checked September 18, 2026.

Worth pairing with

  • Petoskey State Park, 0.3 miles straight line. The classic Petoskey stone beach on Little Traverse Bay, with a dune trail behind it. Best hunting is right at the waterline after a blow.
  • Bay View, 1.5 miles straight line. A National Historic Landmark Chautauqua community of Victorian cottages above the bay, right next door to Petoskey. Drive it slowly or walk it.
  • Little Traverse Wheelway, 2.1 miles straight line. A paved shoreline trail running the bay between Charlevoix, Petoskey and Harbor Springs. The Petoskey to Bay View stretch is the one to walk if you only have an hour.
