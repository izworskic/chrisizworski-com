High Five Spirits

The Kazanowski twins&apos; downtown cocktail lounge on Howard Street, a few doors from the rest of the Gaslight District stops. Award-winning gin, vodka, rum and whiskey, a seasonal cocktail list, and dogs allowed inside.

The basics

  • Where: 312 Howard Street, Petoskey, MI 49770
  • Hours: not published in a weekly format, call ahead
  • Pours: spirits
  • Plan on about 45 minutes
  • Food: cocktails
  • Website: highfivespirits.com/
  • Not a Petoskey Wine Region trail member, listed here because it is a working tasting room in the same country

Worth pairing with

  • Gaslight District, 0.1 miles straight line. Downtown Petoskey: brick storefronts, bookshops, a long block of restaurants, and Beards and Elder Piper both within a few minutes' walk.
  • City Park Grill, 0.1 miles straight line. The bar on East Lake Street where Hemingway reportedly worked up notes for the short stories. It is still a restaurant, and it is the most direct Hemingway stop in Petoskey itself.
  • Palette Bistro, 0.1 miles straight line. Two storeys on Bay Street with the bay in front of it, Mediterranean, and twenty years in. The reliable sit-down dinner at the end of a tasting day in town.
