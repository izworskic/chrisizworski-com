Gypsy Distillery

The Kazanowski brothers put this one in a 42,000 square foot repurposed equestrian facility on Charlevoix Avenue, indoor arena and all, with games and live music. Gypsy Vodka, Petoskey Stone Gin and Mackinac Island Rum.

The basics

  • Where: 5251 Charlevoix Avenue, Petoskey, MI 49770
  • Hours: not published in a weekly format, call ahead
  • Pours: spirits
  • Plan on about 50 minutes
  • Food: cocktails
  • Website: gypsyvodka.com/
  • Not a Petoskey Wine Region trail member, listed here because it is a working tasting room in the same country

Hours checked September 18, 2026.

Worth pairing with

  • Magnus Park Beach, 3.6 miles straight line. City park beach west of downtown, quieter than the state park and a good stone-hunting stretch in the shallows.
  • Gaslight District, 4.5 miles straight line. Downtown Petoskey: brick storefronts, bookshops, a long block of restaurants, and Beards and Elder Piper both within a few minutes' walk.
  • Palette Bistro, 4.5 miles straight line. Two storeys on Bay Street with the bay in front of it, Mediterranean, and twenty years in. The reliable sit-down dinner at the end of a tasting day in town.
