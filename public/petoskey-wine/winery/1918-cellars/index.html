Petoskey Wine Region planner / Charlevoix

1918 Cellars at Castle Farms

A tasting room in a historic wing of Castle Farms on M-66, so the stop comes with the grounds and the stonework attached. Wine and cider with grab-and-go food.

The basics

  • Where: 5052 M-66 North, Charlevoix, MI 49720
  • Hours: not published in a weekly format, call ahead at (231) 237-0884
  • Pours: wine, cider
  • Plan on about 45 minutes
  • Food: grab and go
  • Phone: (231) 237-0884
  • Website: castlefarms.com/
  • Not a Petoskey Wine Region trail member, listed here because it is a working tasting room in the same country

Hours checked September 18, 2026.

Nearest other tasting stops

  • Blu Dot Farm & Vineyard, 3.7 miles straight line (Charlevoix)
  • Spare Key Winery, 7.3 miles straight line (Bay Shore)
  • The Cellars at Royal Farms, 8.7 miles straight line (Ellsworth)
  • Rudbeckia Winery & Burnt Marshmallow Brewstillery, 10.3 miles straight line (Petoskey)

Worth pairing with

  • Downtown Charlevoix, 2.4 miles straight line. Bridge Street between the drawbridge and Round Lake, plus the Earl Young mushroom houses a few blocks off it.
  • Mount McSauba Recreation Area, 2.8 miles straight line. Dune and forest trails above the Lake Michigan beach on the north edge of Charlevoix, with a real overlook at the top.
  • Ironton Ferry, 2.8 miles straight line. A cable ferry that has been crossing the narrows of Lake Charlevoix since 1876 and carries four cars at a time. It is a shortcut and an attraction at once.
