Resort Pike Cidery & Winery

A restored historic barn on 40 acres south of Petoskey, run by the Stabile family, with 20 taps of sparkling drinks and resident goats. Local apple hard cider is the headline, with sparkling wine alongside.

The basics

  • Where: 3471 Resort Pike Road, Petoskey, MI 49770
  • Hours: not published in a weekly format, call ahead at (231) 753-2508
  • Pours: cider, wine
  • Plan on about 40 minutes
  • Phone: (231) 753-2508
  • Website: www.resortpike.com/
  • Current member of the Petoskey Wine Region trail, checked 2026-09-18

Worth pairing with

  • Magnus Park Beach, 3.4 miles straight line. City park beach west of downtown, quieter than the state park and a good stone-hunting stretch in the shallows.
  • Gaslight District, 3.8 miles straight line. Downtown Petoskey: brick storefronts, bookshops, a long block of restaurants, and Beards and Elder Piper both within a few minutes' walk.
  • City Park Grill, 3.9 miles straight line. The bar on East Lake Street where Hemingway reportedly worked up notes for the short stories. It is still a restaurant, and it is the most direct Hemingway stop in Petoskey itself.
