Beards Brewery

Ben Slocum and Peter Manthei have been at this since 2012, downtown and walking distance from the Gaslight District. More than 100 recipes through the taps, pizza, burritos and tacos, and a dog-friendly patio looking out over Little Traverse Bay.

The basics

  • Where: Petoskey, MI 49770
  • Hours: not published in a weekly format, call ahead
  • Pours: beer
  • Plan on about 45 minutes
  • Food: pizza, burritos and tacos
  • Website: beardsbrewery.com/
  • Not a Petoskey Wine Region trail member, listed here because it is a working tasting room in the same country

Worth pairing with

  • Palette Bistro, 0.1 miles straight line. Two storeys on Bay Street with the bay in front of it, Mediterranean, and twenty years in. The reliable sit-down dinner at the end of a tasting day in town.
  • Gaslight District, 0.1 miles straight line. Downtown Petoskey: brick storefronts, bookshops, a long block of restaurants, and Beards and Elder Piper both within a few minutes' walk.
  • City Park Grill, 0.2 miles straight line. The bar on East Lake Street where Hemingway reportedly worked up notes for the short stories. It is still a restaurant, and it is the most direct Hemingway stop in Petoskey itself.
