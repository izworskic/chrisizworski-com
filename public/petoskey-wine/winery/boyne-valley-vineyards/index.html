Boyne Valley Vineyards

Mary Ann Mahaney&apos;s place on US-131 between Walloon Lake and Boyne Falls. Cold-climate wine, hard cider and mead, tastings up in an elevated treehouse, heated igloos once it turns, and live music most weekends.

The basics

  • Where: 05325 US-131 South, Petoskey, MI 49770
  • Hours: Mon-Thu 11am-7pm, Fri 11am-7:30pm, Sat 11am-7pm, Sun 12pm-5pm
  • Pours: wine, cider
  • Plan on about 45 minutes
  • Food: specialty snacks
  • Phone: (231) 373-2090

Worth pairing with

  • Village of Walloon Lake, 1.3 miles straight line. The rebuilt lakefront village at the foot of Walloon Lake: a hotel, a couple of restaurants, the public dock, and the Hemingway connection that put the lake on the map.
  • Veterans Memorial Park, 5.9 miles straight line. Boyne City's waterfront park and swimming beach on Lake Charlevoix, with the farmers market nearby in season.
  • Red Mesa Grill, 5.9 miles straight line. Latin American on Water Street in Boyne City, and the easiest real dinner if your loop finishes on the south arm of Lake Charlevoix.
