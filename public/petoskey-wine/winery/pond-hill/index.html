Pond Hill Farm

A working farm on M-119 just up from Harbor Springs at the start of the Tunnel of Trees, with a winery, a brewery and a farm-to-table cafe. A trout pond, squash rockets and a Yappy Hour for dogs mean it holds a family longer than any other stop in the region.

The basics

  • Where: 5699 S Lake Shore Drive, Harbor Springs, MI 49740
  • Hours: Wed-Sat 12pm-8pm, Sun 12pm-6pm. Hours shift by season and during fall fest weekends
  • Pours: wine, beer, cider
  • Plan on about 75 minutes
  • Food: cafe
  • Phone: (231) 526-3276
  • Website: www.pondhill.com/
  • Not a Petoskey Wine Region trail member, listed here because it is a working tasting room in the same country

Hours checked September 18, 2026.

Worth pairing with

  • Thorne Swift Nature Preserve, 1.4 miles straight line. Cedar swamp boardwalk out to a Lake Michigan dune beach off M-119. Short, shaded and a good leg-stretch between Tunnel of Trees stops.
  • Downtown Harbor Springs, 4.2 miles straight line. Three blocks of shops and restaurants on the harbor, the start of the Tunnel of Trees, and the most photographed main street in the region.
  • Zorn Park Beach, 4.5 miles straight line. The town beach right at the foot of Harbor Springs, looking straight out at the deepest natural harbor on Lake Michigan.
