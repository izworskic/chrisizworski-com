Petoskey Farms Vineyard & Winery

A 22-acre estate on Atkins Road east of town with a 60-foot hilltop observation patio and heated covered seating, so it works when the air turns. Marquette and Frontenac Blanc off the estate, wood-fired pizza and a coffee bar alongside.

The basics

  • Where: 3720 Atkins Road, Petoskey, MI 49770
  • Hours: Sun 10am-6pm. The site currently posts Sunday 10am to 6pm and lists Monday through Saturday as closed. That is a narrow off-season schedule, so call before driving out on any other day.
  • Pours: wine, cider
  • Plan on about 45 minutes
  • Food: wood-fired pizza and a coffee bar
  • Phone: (231) 290-9463
  • Website: www.petoskeyfarms.com/
  • Current member of the Petoskey Wine Region trail, checked 2026-09-18

Worth pairing with

  • Bay View, 2.3 miles straight line. A National Historic Landmark Chautauqua community of Victorian cottages above the bay, right next door to Petoskey. Drive it slowly or walk it.
  • Little Traverse Wheelway, 2.6 miles straight line. A paved shoreline trail running the bay between Charlevoix, Petoskey and Harbor Springs. The Petoskey to Bay View stretch is the one to walk if you only have an hour.
  • Petoskey State Park, 2.6 miles straight line. The classic Petoskey stone beach on Little Traverse Bay, with a dune trail behind it. Best hunting is right at the waterline after a blow.
