Petoskey Wine Region planner / Vanderbilt

Vanderbilt Vines

The far eastern member, on Thumb Lake Road in Vanderbilt. It is a real drive from the Petoskey cluster, so treat it as its own trip or pair it with the Thumb Lake and Pigeon River country run.

The basics

  • Where: 302 W Thumb Lake Road, Vanderbilt, MI 49795
  • Hours: Mon 12pm-7pm, Wed-Sat 12pm-7pm, Sun 12pm-5pm
  • Pours: wine
  • Plan on about 40 minutes
  • Phone: (989) 448-6221
  • Website: vanderbiltvineswinery.com/
  • Current member of the Petoskey Wine Region trail, checked 2026-09-18

Hours checked September 18, 2026.

Nearest other tasting stops

  • Boyne Valley Vineyards, 9.5 miles straight line (Petoskey)
  • Maple Moon Sugarbush & Winery, 10.8 miles straight line (Petoskey)
  • Petoskey Farms Vineyard & Winery, 11.5 miles straight line (Petoskey)
  • Mackinaw Trail Winery & Brewery, 11.9 miles straight line (Petoskey)

Worth pairing with

  • Village of Walloon Lake, 8.6 miles straight line. The rebuilt lakefront village at the foot of Walloon Lake: a hotel, a couple of restaurants, the public dock, and the Hemingway connection that put the lake on the map.
  • Avalanche Preserve, 11.9 miles straight line. The 462-stair climb to the overlook above Boyne City and the south arm of Lake Charlevoix. The best earned view in the region.
  • Red Mesa Grill, 12.0 miles straight line. Latin American on Water Street in Boyne City, and the easiest real dinner if your loop finishes on the south arm of Lake Charlevoix.
