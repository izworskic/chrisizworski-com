Cellar 1914

The Shooks family farm, named for its founding year, and the southern outpost of the trail on Shooks Road in Central Lake. Wine and cider, sunflower picking, live music and glamping, close enough to the Chain of Lakes to pair with a paddle day.

The basics

  • Where: 5833 Shooks Road, Central Lake, MI 49622
  • Hours: not published in a weekly format, call ahead at (231) 676-8743
  • Pours: wine, cider
  • Plan on about 40 minutes
  • Food: light bites
  • Phone: (231) 676-8743
  • Website: www.cellar1914.com
  • Current member of the Petoskey Wine Region trail, checked 2026-09-18

Worth pairing with

  • Fisherman's Island State Park, 10.4 miles straight line. Miles of undeveloped Lake Michigan shoreline south of Charlevoix and the region's serious Petoskey stone beach. Wild, quiet, and worth the gravel road in.
  • Ironton Ferry, 11.2 miles straight line. A cable ferry that has been crossing the narrows of Lake Charlevoix since 1876 and carries four cars at a time. It is a shortcut and an attraction at once.
  • Downtown Charlevoix, 13.3 miles straight line. Bridge Street between the drawbridge and Round Lake, plus the Earl Young mushroom houses a few blocks off it.
