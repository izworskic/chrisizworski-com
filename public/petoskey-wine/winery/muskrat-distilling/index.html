Muskrat Distilling

A restored 1890s building on Water Street in Boyne City with a riverfront cocktail lounge and a seasonal patio. Small-batch house gin, bourbon and rum, mocktails for whoever is driving, and bar food.

The basics

  • Where: 121 Water Street, Boyne City, MI 49712
  • Hours: not published in a weekly format, call ahead
  • Pours: spirits
  • Plan on about 45 minutes
  • Food: Muskrat Munchies
  • Website: muskratdistilling.com/
  • Not a Petoskey Wine Region trail member, listed here because it is a working tasting room in the same country
