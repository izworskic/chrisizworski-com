Muskrat Distilling

A restored 1890s building on Water Street in Boyne City with a riverfront cocktail lounge and a seasonal patio. Small-batch house gin, bourbon and rum, mocktails for whoever is driving, and bar food.

The basics

  • Where: 121 Water Street, Boyne City, MI 49712
  • Hours: not published in a weekly format, call ahead
  • Pours: spirits
  • Plan on about 45 minutes
  • Food: Muskrat Munchies
  • Website: muskratdistilling.com/
  • Not a Petoskey Wine Region trail member, listed here because it is a working tasting room in the same country

Hours checked September 18, 2026.

Worth pairing with

  • Red Mesa Grill, 0.0 miles straight line. Latin American on Water Street in Boyne City, and the easiest real dinner if your loop finishes on the south arm of Lake Charlevoix.
  • Veterans Memorial Park, 0.3 miles straight line. Boyne City's waterfront park and swimming beach on Lake Charlevoix, with the farmers market nearby in season.
  • Avalanche Preserve, 1.1 miles straight line. The 462-stair climb to the overlook above Boyne City and the south arm of Lake Charlevoix. The best earned view in the region.
