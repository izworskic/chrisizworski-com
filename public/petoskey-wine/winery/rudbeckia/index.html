Rudbeckia Winery & Burnt Marshmallow Brewstillery

John and Vickie Wysokinski&apos;s 195-acre shared farm campus, and the one stop on the trail pouring all four: estate wine, craft beer, grappa and cider, plus custom s&apos;mores. Firepits, bocce, and pet-friendly igloos in winter. Reservations are a good idea for the igloos.

The basics

  • Where: 3379 Lake Grove Road, Petoskey, MI 49770
  • Hours: not published in a weekly format, call ahead at (231) 622-4173
  • Pours: wine, beer, cider, spirits
  • Plan on about 60 minutes
  • Food: custom s'mores and light bites
  • Phone: (231) 622-4173
  • Website: rudbeckiafarm.com/
  • Current member of the Petoskey Wine Region trail, checked 2026-09-18

Worth pairing with

  • Horton Bay, 3.9 miles straight line. The general store and the crossroads Hemingway wrote into the Nick Adams stories, on the back road between Walloon Lake and Charlevoix.
  • Magnus Park Beach, 4.0 miles straight line. City park beach west of downtown, quieter than the state park and a good stone-hunting stretch in the shallows.
  • Gaslight District, 4.7 miles straight line. Downtown Petoskey: brick storefronts, bookshops, a long block of restaurants, and Beards and Elder Piper both within a few minutes' walk.
