Petoskey Wine Region planner / Alanson

Seasons of the North Winery

Out on M-68 toward Indian River, at the eastern edge of the trail. Cold-hardy grape wine and craft beer under one roof, a dog-friendly wine garden and live music, and a Tuesday and Wednesday closure worth checking before you drive out.

The basics

  • Where: 9090 M-68, Alanson, MI 49706
  • Hours: Mon 12pm-6pm, Thu 12pm-6pm, Fri-Sat 11am-7pm, Sun 12pm-5pm
  • Pours: wine, beer, cider
  • Plan on about 40 minutes
  • Food: light bites
  • Phone: (231) 548-1280
  • Website: www.seasonsofthenorth.com/
  • Current member of the Petoskey Wine Region trail, checked 2026-09-18

Worth pairing with

  • Alanson Swing Bridge, 6.1 miles straight line. The hand-operated swing bridge on the Inland Water Route. Five minutes, but you will stop anyway if a boat is coming through.
  • Oden State Fish Hatchery, 8.7 miles straight line. A free visitor center and show pond on the Crooked River side of the region, and an easy add if you are already driving out to Crooked Vine or Seasons of the North.
  • Petoskey State Park, 11.4 miles straight line. The classic Petoskey stone beach on Little Traverse Bay, with a dune trail behind it. Best hunting is right at the waterline after a blow.
