Walloon Lake Winery

A third-generation family farm a mile off Walloon Lake, with seven acres of cold-hardy vines on 36 acres. The stacked-cordwood tasting building is built from local wood and looks out over the vineyard. They work the soil organically, and the list runs cold-hardy estate wine, a sparkling peach, and hard ciders.
