Spare Key Winery

On Upper Bay Shore Road between Petoskey and Charlevoix, the natural hinge stop if you are running the whole shoreline in one day. Everything is estate grown and bottled, there is a covered porch, and dogs are welcome inside as well as out. Closed Mondays.

The basics

  • Where: 06872 Upper Bay Shore Road, Charlevoix, MI 49720
  • Hours: Tue-Thu 1pm-6pm, Fri 12pm-6pm, Sat 11am-7pm, Sun 12pm-5pm
  • Pours: wine
  • Plan on about 40 minutes
  • Food: light bites
  • Phone: (231) 237-4785

Worth pairing with

  • Horton Bay, 4.9 miles straight line. The general store and the crossroads Hemingway wrote into the Nick Adams stories, on the back road between Walloon Lake and Charlevoix.
  • Mount McSauba Recreation Area, 6.5 miles straight line. Dune and forest trails above the Lake Michigan beach on the north edge of Charlevoix, with a real overlook at the top.
  • Magnus Park Beach, 7.1 miles straight line. City park beach west of downtown, quieter than the state park and a good stone-hunting stretch in the shallows.
