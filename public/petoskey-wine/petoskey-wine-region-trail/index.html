The 14 wineries of the Petoskey Wine Region

The Petoskey Wine Region is the trail formerly known as the Bay View Wine Trail, rebranded in 2019. It now lists 14 member wineries, all family owned, spread across Emmet, Charlevoix, Antrim and Otsego counties inside the Tip of the Mitt appellation.

The important thing to understand before you plan anything: this is not a compact trail. Vanderbilt Vines sits well east of Gaylord, Cellar 1914 is down in Central Lake, and Crooked Vine is north toward Alanson. End to end that is more than ninety minutes of driving before you have tasted anything. The members cluster into three usable groups, and a good day picks one group.

The Petoskey and Walloon Lake cluster carries seven of the fourteen and is where most first trips should go. The Charlevoix and south group carries four more along the shoreline and the Chain of Lakes. Crooked Vine and Seasons of the North sit together in the north, and Vanderbilt Vines is its own trip.

Membership below is checked against the official trail list rather than assumed. Pond Hill Farm and 1918 Cellars at Castle Farms both pour in the same country and both appear on the map, but they are listed separately because they are not currently on the trail roster.

The 14 trail members and their hours

  • Blu Dot Farm & Vineyard (Charlevoix): Mon-Sun 12pm-7pm. Open in season, May through October. Plan about 40 minutes.
  • Boyne Valley Vineyards (Petoskey): Mon-Thu 11am-7pm, Fri 11am-7:30pm, Sat 11am-7pm, Sun 12pm-5pm Plan about 45 minutes.
  • Cellar 1914 (Central Lake): call ahead for current hours at (231) 676-8743 Plan about 40 minutes.
  • Crooked Vine Vineyard & Winery (Alanson): Fri-Sun 12pm-5pm. Seasonal. Fall, September 1 to October 31, is Friday through Sunday 12pm to 5pm. Summer, June 1 to August 31, is Tuesday through Sunday. Winter and spring are not posted, so call.. Plan about 40 minutes.
  • Mackinaw Trail Winery & Brewery (Petoskey): Mon-Thu 11am-7pm, Fri-Sat 10am-7pm, Sun 12pm-6pm. These are the Petoskey tasting room hours. The winery also runs a separate Mackinaw City room on different hours. The site prints Monday through Thursday as 11am to 7am, which is a typo for 7pm.. Plan about 50 minutes.
  • Maple Moon Sugarbush & Winery (Petoskey): call ahead for current hours at (231) 487-9058 Plan about 40 minutes.
  • Petoskey Farms Vineyard & Winery (Petoskey): Sun 10am-6pm. The site currently posts Sunday 10am to 6pm and lists Monday through Saturday as closed. That is a narrow off-season schedule, so call before driving out on any other day.. Plan about 45 minutes.
  • Resort Pike Cidery & Winery (Petoskey): call ahead for current hours at (231) 753-2508 Plan about 40 minutes.
  • Rudbeckia Winery & Burnt Marshmallow Brewstillery (Petoskey): call ahead for current hours at (231) 622-4173 Plan about 60 minutes.
  • Seasons of the North Winery (Alanson): Mon 12pm-6pm, Thu 12pm-6pm, Fri-Sat 11am-7pm, Sun 12pm-5pm Plan about 40 minutes.
  • Spare Key Winery (Bay Shore): Tue-Thu 1pm-6pm, Fri 12pm-6pm, Sat 11am-7pm, Sun 12pm-5pm Plan about 40 minutes.
  • The Cellars at Royal Farms (Ellsworth): Mon-Thu 11am-6pm, Fri-Sat 11am-7pm, Sun 11am-6pm Plan about 45 minutes.
  • Vanderbilt Vines (Vanderbilt): Mon 12pm-7pm, Wed-Sat 12pm-7pm, Sun 12pm-5pm Plan about 40 minutes.
  • Walloon Lake Winery (Petoskey): call ahead for current hours at (231) 622-8645 Plan about 45 minutes.

Hours checked September 18, 2026. Call ahead where a stop does not publish a weekly schedule.
