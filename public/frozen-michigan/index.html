Sand Point ice curtains

The sandstone escarpment between Munising Falls and Sand Point grows dramatic hanging formations in cold weather.

The moveBegin with the NPS ice viewing map; the land-based formations are uphill from Sand Point Road.

Before you go: The NPS identifies Sand Point parking; roadside parking is prohibited. Use cleats and check current closure notices.

Across Munising Bay, binoculars can reveal ice formations on Grand Island without a dangerous crossing.

Field judgment

The photo inspires. The evidence decides.

Never interpret freezing-degree days, NOAA coverage, photos or a satellite ice map as permission to walk on a frozen lake or river.

Maps show a known destination area, not a specific permitted route. Typical seasonal suitability is not measured conditions. All ice-related activities require independent local safety decisions, never assumptions from this page.
