Use Detroit Outdoors to decide what is unusually worth your attention today. For a plan built around your starting point, interests, kids, dogs or accessibility needs, use Michigan Outdoors Now. Specialist tools remain the last check for water, ships, bird sightings, aurora and other fast-moving conditions.

Sources, freshness, safety rules, and how the ranking works

The first layer is deterministic: live place conditions from Michigan Outdoors Now, air quality, a conservative NWS alert veto, the statewide short-window opportunity comparison, and the Southeast Lower fall-color model when in season. JEV then makes a closed-set lead judgment. It cannot create a place, change a source fact or override a hazard stop. A separately gated writer turns the structured result into the short desk note and is cached so the prose changes only when the underlying state meaningfully changes.
