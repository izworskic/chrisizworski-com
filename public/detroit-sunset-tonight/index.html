How the window works

Sunset time first, sky and weather second

The Detroit engine calculates the local sunset time, then reads NWS sky-cover periods around that window. It combines that with nearby rain, gust and air-quality conditions before a sunset/photography opportunity can enter the safe candidate pool.

What it cannot know

Cloud cover is not sunset color

A percentage does not describe cloud height, texture or the exact western horizon. The page can identify a usable photography window; it cannot promise orange, pink or red skies. That distinction is deliberate.

Where this points

Detroit Riverfront

The Detroit Riverwalk provides a nearly five-mile public riverfront corridor for walking and river views. The engine uses nearby Belle Isle weather as Detroit context, not as an observation at every Riverwalk segment.

Best use

Decide whether the trip deserves your evening

If the live window clears, use the timing shown above to decide when to arrive. If it does not clear, the page says so rather than turning ordinary cloud cover into a photography recommendation.
