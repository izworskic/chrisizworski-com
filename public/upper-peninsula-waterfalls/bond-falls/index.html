What makes this waterfall different?

A wide fan of cascades breaks over fractured rock, visible from several boardwalk overlooks. The DNR’s official point is the scenic site; use its parking guidance, not the pin alone.

Spring runoff, green summer forest and October foliage. These are typical seasonal characteristics, not a prediction for a particular date. Spring volume, rain, low water and ice can change the appearance and the safest viewing points.

Before you leave the car

Vehicle access is typically mid-May through mid-October; trail access may remain possible outside those dates but is not groomed or monitored for ice. The accessible summer boardwalk should not be assumed snow-cleared in winter.

Remain on designated trails and platforms. Wet roots, drop-offs and powerful currents can create hazards well beyond the actual plunge pool.
