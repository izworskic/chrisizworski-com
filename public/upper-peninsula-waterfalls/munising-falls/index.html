Plan the actual arrival

Land manager: National Park Service · Pictured Rocks National Lakeshore.

Where to start: Parking near the visitor center at Washington Street / Sand Point Road, Munising does not reopen the trail.

Trail and viewing route: Normally an 800-foot paved route but trail CLOSED indefinitely after April 2025 washout.

Visitor decision: Do not travel expecting a waterfall view; choose Wagner or Miners until NPS reopens access.

What to verify today: Officially described visitor routes are not proof of current opening. Read NPS alerts and obey closures.
