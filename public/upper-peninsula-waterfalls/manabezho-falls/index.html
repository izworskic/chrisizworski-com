What makes this waterfall different?

A broad western U.P. cascade in an old-growth river valley with several neighboring drops. This is a river-scene pin, not a guarantee of approach/parking.

Strong spring river flow and early October leaf color. These are typical seasonal characteristics, not a prediction for a particular date. Spring volume, rain, low water and ice can change the appearance and the safest viewing points.

Before you leave the car

Porcupine Mountains park access applies; slick bedrock and strong currents occur along the falls chain. Manido and Nawadaha Falls belong to the same walking network, not separate easy roadside stops.

Remain on designated trails and platforms. Wet roots, drop-offs and powerful currents can create hazards well beyond the actual plunge pool.

Presque Isle River walking circuit

The Michigan DNR park visitor guide describes the Presque Isle River loop linking Manabezho, Manido and Nawadaha. Check the DNR visitor guide for the walking circuit as well as the current scenic site access notices before departure.

Plan the actual arrival

Recreation authority: Michigan DNR · Porcupine Mountains Wilderness State Park.

Where to park: Park at state-designated Presque Isle River Scenic Site, not at the falls geographic point.

Walking approach: DNR's approximately 2-mile loop links Manabezho, Manido and Nawadaha; uneven riverfront trail and stairs.

Trip decision: Plan a combined river-falls visit, watching slick bedrock and fast-moving water.

Opening and legal access: Official sources document a public viewing route, not today's trail or parking status. Check for current alerts before travelling.
