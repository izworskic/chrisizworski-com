What makes this waterfall different?

A high sandstone cascade and maple forest reward a deliberate hike into Chapel Basin. The map pin marks the fall, not Chapel trailhead or a vehicle approach.

Late spring and autumn hardwood foliage. These are typical seasonal characteristics, not a prediction for a particular date. Spring volume, rain, low water and ice can change the appearance and the safest viewing points.

Before you leave the car

Chapel Road is rough; parking often fills early. Pets prohibited on Chapel trails; no drinking water at trailhead. Do not drive a low-clearance vehicle on Chapel Road without checking conditions.

Remain on designated trails and platforms. Wet roots, drop-offs and powerful currents can create hazards well beyond the actual plunge pool.
