A county park, not a roadside scramble

The Keweenaw County Road Commission maintains Haven Park, and the county recreation plan names the Road Commission as the property's owner. The waterfall is inside the park, so visitors can enjoy it without pursuing unmarked paths along private driveways or crossing a state highway.

Park within the signed facility, walk a short distance toward the waterfall at the back of the park, and use the picnic grounds if available. A shallow or seasonal flow can be beautiful but should not be confused with a high-volume river fall. Accessibility of every surface, restroom opening and winter plowing has not been confirmed for today.

Choose this waterfall for the right experience

Choose Haven Falls for a simple family picnic and an easy short walk rather than an undeveloped gorge scramble.

Restrooms and winter vehicle access are seasonal; do not assume the park is plowed or facilities are open.

For a short, managed visitor experience, compare this stop to Haven Falls in its county park and Eagle River Falls at the MDOT bridge park. For a natural geology walk, Silver River Falls in the nature preserve requires more attention to road-side parking and walking surfaces.

What to check before you drive

Spring runoff and autumn color; low water can mean a delicate trickle. None of these descriptions measure the present water volume. A river's visual intensity changes with rain, spring melt, drought and freezing weather. The Michigan GIS waterfall pin and USGS hydrographic identifiers do not report real-time water levels.

Access conditions: Restrooms and winter vehicle access are seasonal; do not assume the park is plowed or facilities are open. Check the responsible authority and actual gates, barriers and signs for the day you travel. The Keweenaw can develop snow or ice early, and a published public walking route is not proof of current safe footing. Avoid river edges, unstable rock, dam structures and closed bridges.

Why this matters: A location can be known on a map long before its legal public visitor route is clear. This page cites parking, path, manager and waterfall coordinates separately so you can decide whether the visit makes sense before committing to the drive.
