Lac La Belle, Keweenaw County · Keweenaw

Haven Falls

A modest little waterfall spills across a copper-country rock outcrop at the back of a roadside picnic park near Lac La Belle. This is a county roadside park first and a waterfall stop second. Its strength is the easy arrival, picnic area and a chance to hear moving water up close.

Documented public visitor route; current conditions unverified. The land manager, official visitor approach and parking are independently sourced. Road, trail, bridge and snow conditions are not monitored live. Current opening and road conditions are unverified.

Public access
County-owned public roadside park with picnic tables and toilet facilities listed in the 2025–2029 recreation plan. Accessibility assessment is 2 on the county's scale; do not interpret that as a verified ADA route. Facilities may be seasonal.
Parking
Turn from US-41 onto Lac La Belle Road, continue about 4.5 miles to the fork, keep right and find Haven Falls Park about half a mile farther. Use the county park's signed parking area.

Parking source

What makes this the right stop?

Best for a relaxed picnic, a short stop with children, or people wanting water and forest without committing to a rocky hike. The creek slides across tilted conglomerate into a pool and then flows beneath a small footbridge toward Lac La Belle. Stay on park paths and avoid slick rocks.

Spring snowmelt brings lively cascades over conglomerate rock; in dry summer weather the creek may shrink. Autumn trees make the roadside setting particularly inviting. These are seasonal patterns, not a report of today's waterfall flow. A dry day can make a modest falls quieter, and ice can turn a short warm-weather approach into a much more demanding trip.

Follow the documented route

The falls sit at the back of Haven Falls Park and can be viewed from the parking and picnic area. An informal closer approach may have uneven rock; no surveyed accessible path length is published.

A geographic waterfall coordinate is not a safe navigation destination. Do not cut through adjacent private property, use an unmarked streambank scramble, or park along an active highway lane. Keep to established public visitor approaches and respect any temporary closure signs.

Is this a tall waterfall?

No. Haven Falls is a small creek drop whose character depends on recent rain and meltwater. It is better for a picnic and close observation than a dramatic vertical plunge.

Is it accessible?

The county gives Haven Falls Park an accessibility assessment of 2. That is not enough to guarantee step-free viewing, restrooms or winter maintenance. Confirm needs before travel.

What else fits this stop?

Lac La Belle is across the road from the park. This works as a quiet break between the Keweenaw interior and the shore, without assuming every picnic facility is available in winter.

How the river fits the map

USGS records this waterfall without a usable NHDPlusV2 stream ID. The Michigan GIS position and the USGS 2020 location differ by approximately 304 meters.
