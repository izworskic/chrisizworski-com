What makes this waterfall different?

Water slides down a broad rock slope inside an old-growth gorge. Use DNR state park and trail information for the actual arrival point.

Spring flow and autumn foliage. These are typical seasonal characteristics, not a prediction for a particular date. Spring volume, rain, low water and ice can change the appearance and the safest viewing points.

Before you leave the car

Moderate forest approach, steps and gorge terrain; no guaranteed winter maintenance. The view at the top and lower viewpoints differ significantly in effort.

Remain on designated trails and platforms. Wet roots, drop-offs and powerful currents can create hazards well beyond the actual plunge pool.

Plan the actual arrival

Recreation authority: Michigan DNR · Laughing Whitefish Falls State Park.

Where to park: From M-94 via Sundell Road then signed Laughing Whitefish Falls Road, seasonal 0.4-mile dirt segment.

Walking approach: 0.5-mile each way, three overlooks; 20 steps to middle and 137 steps to lower platform.

Trip decision: Upper overlook avoids the full stair descent. Road is unplowed and has no turnaround in winter.

Opening and legal access: Official sources document a public viewing route, not today's trail or parking status. Check for current alerts before travelling.
