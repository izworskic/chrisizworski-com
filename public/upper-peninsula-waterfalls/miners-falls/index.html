What makes this waterfall different?

One of Pictured Rocks' most powerful falls drops through a sandstone opening. Use the park's road/trail conditions before driving in winter.

Spring wildflowers and peak fall color. These are typical seasonal characteristics, not a prediction for a particular date. Spring volume, rain, low water and ice can change the appearance and the safest viewing points.

Before you leave the car

Access from Miners Castle Road (H-11). Vehicles over 32 feet should not attempt the small parking access road. Stay on official platforms; avoid steep unofficial routes to the base.

Remain on designated trails and platforms. Wet roots, drop-offs and powerful currents can create hazards well beyond the actual plunge pool.

Plan the actual arrival

Who maintains the public site: National Park Service · Pictured Rocks National Lakeshore.

Where to park: Small lot 0.5 mile off Miners Castle Road (H-11), four miles north of H-58; no vehicles over 32 feet.

Walking route: 0.6 mile each way on gravel/dirt trail; 64 stairs to lower viewing platform.

Why choose this stop: Powerful falls with upper and lower overlooks; RVs and stair-averse visitors need to plan.

Day-of check: Published visitor access is not a live opening notice. Read the manager's current alerts and obey signs, closures and road restrictions.
