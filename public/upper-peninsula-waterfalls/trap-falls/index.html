What kind of outing is this?

This is a moderate half-day walk for hikers who want the forest journey as much as the waterfall; not a quick add-on between roadside viewpoints.

Government Peak Trail is longer than the Falls approach. The 3.2-mile round trip is the DNR's published Trap Falls day-hike route, not the length of the entire Government Peak Trail.

Parking and the walking decision

Government Peak Trailhead on M-107 in Porcupine Mountains Wilderness State Park. Follow DNR park signs and road closure notices; the waterfall GIS point is not a parking area.

DNR's Porcupine Mountains visitor guide describes Trap Falls as a 3.2-mile round-trip hike from Government Peak Trailhead on M-107, typically taking 2–5 hours. Follow the Government Peak Trail to the river viewpoint.

Allow time to return the same way, rather than assuming the waterfall is a roadside stop. Follow designated park trails and posted closure signs. These trails are natural forest paths, with wet roots, slopes and the possibility of swift current near the falls.

Seasonal conditions and trip planning

Spring flow and autumn forest color, when the trail is passable.. Check current M-107 and park-road restrictions before departure; park road closures affect some segments from Dec. 1 to late spring. Avoid trail shortcuts, unbridged water crossings and uncertain ice conditions.

The DNR's published routes describe normal access, not a live trail opening or water-flow sensor. Snow, downed trees, mud, and seasonal closure signs can alter the route. Do not enter stream channels or attempt unsupported shortcuts around platforms and river crossings.
