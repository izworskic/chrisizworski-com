What makes this waterfall different?

A short mossy forest walk ends at a viewing platform over a layered creek cascade. The map pin is a waterfall reference; DNR provides the arrival route.

Summer greenery and fall color. These are typical seasonal characteristics, not a prediction for a particular date. Spring volume, rain, low water and ice can change the appearance and the safest viewing points.

Before you leave the car

Very small parking area, approximately 8–10 cars; no restrooms or drinking water. Short does not mean step-free; inspect the official DNR trail map.

Remain on designated trails and platforms. Wet roots, drop-offs and powerful currents can create hazards well beyond the actual plunge pool.

Plan the actual arrival

Who maintains the public site: Michigan DNR · Wagner Falls Scenic Site.

Where to park: Take M-28 east of Munising then M-94 west; signed lot for about 8–10 vehicles.

Walking route: 0.1-mile packed-gravel and boardwalk approach with some steps to a developed platform.

Why choose this stop: Good short waterfall visit near Munising; limited parking and no onsite water or restrooms.

Day-of check: Published visitor access is not a live opening notice. Read the manager's current alerts and obey signs, closures and road restrictions.
