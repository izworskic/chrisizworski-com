Is Superior Falls right for your day?

Choose the upper scenic overlook for a simpler visit. The lower tailwater path is steep and wet, and it is not required to appreciate the main gorge.

The Montreal River forms the state boundary. The designated public approach and parking are on the Michigan side after traveling north on Wisconsin Highway 122. The lower descent is considerably more demanding than the overlook.

Fences, facility signage and hydroelectric operations matter more than the map distance. Stay off diversion structures and steep, unmaintained riverbank routes.

Parking is a different location from the waterfall

Trail and what to expect on foot

Use the signed Superior Falls recreation parking area and follow the marked route to the upper scenic overlook. A separate, very steep descent reaches the lower tailwater recreation area; neither riverbank scrambling nor crossing fences is a recommended route.

Documented hydroelectric-project public recreation areas include an upper scenic overlook and a lower tailwater viewpoint. Check posted hydroelectric facility notices, closures and warnings before entry.

Do not use waterfall height or a straight-line map distance to estimate walking effort. Follow the posted corridor and turn back rather than attempting an improvised shortcut through dense vegetation, river beds or inaccessible structures.

Season, river character and hydrology
