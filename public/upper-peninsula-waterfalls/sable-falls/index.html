What makes this waterfall different?

Sandstone ledges lead to Lake Superior, with a stair-supported waterfall viewpoint. The location pin identifies the falls; consult NPS for parking and route.

Spring cascades and late September / October hardwood color. These are typical seasonal characteristics, not a prediction for a particular date. Spring volume, rain, low water and ice can change the appearance and the safest viewing points.

Before you leave the car

Steep stair count means this is not a barrier-free base-of-falls visit; seasonal facilities. Expect a strenuous climb back up 168 steps, especially on damp boards.

Remain on designated trails and platforms. Wet roots, drop-offs and powerful currents can create hazards well beyond the actual plunge pool.

Plan the actual arrival

Land manager: National Park Service · Pictured Rocks National Lakeshore.

Where to start: Sable Falls parking on H-58 approximately one mile west of Grand Marais; seasonal facilities.

Trail and viewing route: 168 steps descend to best waterfall view; approximately 0.4-mile round trip to Lake Superior and back.

Visitor decision: High stair count makes lower view unsuitable for visitors avoiding steep steps.

What to verify today: Officially described visitor routes are not proof of current opening. Read NPS alerts and obey closures.
