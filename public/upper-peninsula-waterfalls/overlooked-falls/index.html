Overlooked Falls

A short, often missed series of forest cascades on the Little Carp River, just upstream of the longer Greenstone Falls outing.

Documented public state-park hike. Current opening and road conditions are unverified; check the park before traveling. Park road closures begin Dec. 1 and usually continue into late spring. Do not promise winter vehicle access to Little Carp River Road. The streamside trail can be wet, uneven or icy. Check current DNR guidance.

What kind of outing is this?

Choose Overlooked when you want a genuinely short forest-waterfall walk; continue on the same river trail to Greenstone Falls only if time, trail and water conditions permit.

Overlooked and Greenstone are different falls on the same Little Carp River Trail. Do not claim this waterfall is at the Greenstone location.

Parking and the walking decision

Signed Little Carp River Road trailhead lot in Porcupine Mountains Wilderness State Park; access branches from South Boundary Road. Do not use the waterfall's stream coordinates as the parking entrance.

From the Little Carp River Road parking lot, take the signed Little Carp River Trail toward the first footbridge and continue just downstream to Overlooked Falls. Michigan DNR's visitor guide places the waterfall less than half a mile from the lot.

Allow time to return the same way, rather than assuming the waterfall is a roadside stop. Follow designated park trails and posted closure signs. These trails are natural forest paths, with wet roots, slopes and the possibility of swift current near the falls.

Seasonal conditions and trip planning

Spring runoff and fall color; small cascades can be less dramatic after dry weather.. Park road closures begin Dec. 1 and usually continue into late spring. Do not promise winter vehicle access to Little Carp River Road. The streamside trail can be wet, uneven or icy.

The DNR's published routes describe normal access, not a live trail opening or water-flow sensor. Snow, downed trees, mud, and seasonal closure signs can alter the route. Do not enter stream channels or attempt unsupported shortcuts around platforms and river crossings.
