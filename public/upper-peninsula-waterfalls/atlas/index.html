Read the evidence, not just the pin

Visitor guide means the site has a curated page, which may also report a closure or research limitation. It does not mean open today.

State GIS means Michigan maps a named geographic feature. Coordinates do not establish ownership or a legal approach.

Name-only means a cited directory mentions a waterfall, with a county and sometimes a river. We do not invent its position.

Tourism listing means a regional tourism name and road/town description, not a verified parking location.

What this atlas cannot prove

A waterfall name in a guide, a state GIS natural-feature point, or a third-party mapping observation cannot verify that a visitor can legally park, walk across every parcel or reach a safe viewpoint. The 80 curated records and 161 GIS records overlap; they must never be added into a claim of distinct places. The 291 independent name observations may also describe those same features. The sample of 12 UP Travel listings is not their complete dynamic directory or a complete 300-plus waterfall export.
