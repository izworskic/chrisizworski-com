Read the evidence, not just the pin

Visitor guide means the site has a curated page, which may also report a closure or research limitation. It does not mean open today.

State GIS means Michigan maps a named geographic feature. Coordinates do not establish ownership or a legal approach.

Name-only means a cited directory mentions a waterfall, with a county and sometimes a river. We do not invent its position.

UP Travel listing index means a discoverable source listing URL. Only 12 names also have individually observed directory road/town labels. Never a parking location.

What this atlas cannot prove

A waterfall name in a guide, a state GIS natural-feature point, or a third-party mapping observation cannot verify that a visitor can legally park, walk across every parcel or reach a safe viewpoint. The 80 curated records and 161 GIS records overlap; they must never be added into a claim of distinct places. The 291 independent name observations may also describe those same features. We indexed 108 waterfall-topic UP Travel listing URLs, of which 12 have independently observed directory-card road/town labels. This is not a verified export of their 104 dynamic directory cards or the region’s estimated 300-plus waterfalls.
