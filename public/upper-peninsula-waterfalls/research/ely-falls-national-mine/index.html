Ely Falls

A tiny ledge near industrial mining land, not a documented public footpath.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. Never use these natural-feature coordinates as a parking location or assume an old route is legal today.

What a knowledgeable local would tell you

Michigan GIS #170 and independently mapped Ely Creek feature agree within ~32m. Field account records two rocky drops, tallest roughly 3ft, near mine-affected tributary and cliffs. 2025 SEC filing documents the large active Tilden mining operation in the area but does not prove title at the waterfall.

The important access catch

Historic directions suggest pulling off County Road 476 and wandering through an overgrown swamp route; field visitor says no consistent trail. The right to park and cross this terrain, private mining surface boundaries and any restrictions have not been established.
