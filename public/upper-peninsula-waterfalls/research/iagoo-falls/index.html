Iagoo Falls: Which waterfall does the name actually identify?

Two published maps put Iagoo Falls in materially different places along the Presque Isle River. The name deserves careful geographic reconciliation before a visitor follows the river into unfamiliar backcountry.

Research only, no indexable visitor route. Do not drive to either waterfall coordinate or use a raw riverbank track as an access trail.

What the maps show

Michigan's named waterfall GIS #86 locates Iagoo at 46.691604,-89.970696 near the South Boundary Road reach. The Western U.P. visitor brochure instead locates Iagoo at 46.662334,-89.941527, only about 0.4 kilometre downstream of Lepisto, approximately 3.9 km from the Michigan GIS position. Both are geographic coordinates, not designated parking.

The NCTA's 2025 MI-008 guide verifies a maintained trail through the Porkies passing Lepisto Falls but does not establish a signed, mapped Iagoo turnout or specific dry bank viewpoint. No authoritative park map independently reconciles these two Iagoo points.

Why this is not a two-waterfall itinerary

The easy mistake is to append Iagoo to a Lepisto visit because one tourism map calls it downstream. Without a complete permitted spur and a consistent fall location, that could turn a marked trail visit into a poorly defined gorge walk. Crossing the Presque Isle River, scrambling down a steep bank, or navigating to the Michigan GIS coordinate is not a validated visitor plan.

What would establish a legitimate guide

We need the exact natural feature identity on an authoritative map, proof that the NCT or a clearly signed public trail actually reaches its safe viewing point, an approved arrival and any seasonal restrictions. Until then, the separately verified Lepisto Falls guide is the appropriate nearby visitor choice.
