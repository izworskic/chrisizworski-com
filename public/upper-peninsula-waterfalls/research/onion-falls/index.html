Houghton County · Onion Creek · Field research only

Onion Falls

A low sandstone slide above a gorge that does the dramatic work.

PUBLIC VISITOR ROUTE NOT VERIFIED. This source record is not a public parking destination, trail map or opening announcement.

What a knowledgeable local would tell you

Onion Falls is a modest ledge, roughly two feet high. The deep sandstone gorge downstream gives the place its character. Historic visitors came through meadows to a creek bank, not a maintained signed trail. A Forest Service trail nearby does not establish an approved descent to the water.

Before you even think about going

Research only: arrival not independently cleared. Research layer only. A natural waterfall coordinate is not a parking location, a public trail or an invitation to cross unverified land or gated roads.

Six things we have checked

Geographic identity and meaningful waterfall status
Michigan GIS #42 is about 194m from the independently field-mapped Onion Falls 46.56974,-88.98429. The original visitor account measures a roughly 2ft sandstone ledge slide across a ~7ft creek, and cautions that the downstream ravine—not the height of the fall—is the striking landscape. Do not inflate to a large waterfall. Read supporting evidence
Land ownership at the natural point (not visitor route)
The exact GIS point returned no USDA FS surface parcel and no Michigan DNR LOTS state parcel or easement intersect; this is NOT proof of private title. Three published Forest Service trail segments NFST-1137, -1129 and -835 lie within 1km, but none is verified as a trail to this drop, and nearby trail coverage does not certify creek-bank rights. Read supporting evidence
Historic or tourism approach versus current public permission
Historic waterfall-hunting directions lead past forest roads to an informal grassy opening, then across meadows and down to Onion Creek. The route is an old bushwhack through an unstable gorge, not a signed maintained trail. Current legal parking, motor-vehicle road status and continuous public right-of-way are unverified. Read supporting evidence
Legally signed road approach and waterfall parking
No present verified official signed waterfall visitor lot/turnout and continuous vehicle access to that arrival. Old gated routes, gravel-road shoulders, apartment parking and private entrances do not qualify. Read supporting evidence
Complete lawful walking trail and safe viewpoint
USFS trails in 1km: NFST-1137, NFST-1129, NFST-835. This is proximity, not a lawful final trail. No current complete legal public foot corridor to the natural point was established. Read supporting evidence
2026 season, stream, ice and closure conditions
No verified current site-specific public road/gate/footing/crossing condition or signed authorization for November 1, 2026. Older trip directions must not be treated as live opening information. Read supporting evidence

What would change this recommendation

Match Onion Creek natural drop with county parcel/road status and authoritative Forest Service MVUM/route line. Establish a signed and legal turnout and a complete sanctioned path/viewpoint with no unverified unstable gorge descent. Avoid presenting the historic meadow as a public parking lot.
