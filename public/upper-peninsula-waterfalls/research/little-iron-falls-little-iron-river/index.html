Little Iron Falls

A particularly revealing data conflict: an old state inventory identifies a Little Iron River fall in the Porcupines, while a current general directory describes an apparently easy waterfall near a different mapped falls.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. A named geographic waterfall coordinate is not a safe public trailhead, a lawful parking point or live access clearance.

What the evidence supports

Michigan GIS #106 locates Little Iron Falls at 46.749606,-89.730367 on the Little Iron River. A provisional 1956 Michigan Department of Conservation waterfall working inventory lists 'Little Iron (Little Iron River) Falls' under Porcupine Mountain State Park, but explicitly warns that names and exact locations are sometimes uncertain and its asterisk marks only probable State/Federal ownership. That provisional entry is historical naming evidence, not verification of today's park boundary or trail. A modern generic directory places Little Iron Falls at 46.8106,-89.5847, approximately 13.00 km northeast of the GIS record and only 0.47 km from state-mapped Pewabeck Falls #122. These are not interchangeable locations.

Why the public approach is not verified

The generic directory promises a ten-foot fall after an easy 0.1-mile path from an 'Iron River Access Track'. No corresponding manager-approved named access track, public parking, maintained footpath or current opening for this mapped GIS #106 feature was established. The Michigan DNR park overview confirms the park has public trails and many waterfalls but does not identify Little Iron Falls as a signed visit. Nearby Nonesuch Mine trails are separate routes and may not be applied to Little Iron Falls without verified continuous connections.
