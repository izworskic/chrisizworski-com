Baraga County / Silver River · Source review November 3, 2026

Upper Silver Falls: the Herman waterfall is not the picnic-area chute

There are two Upper Silver stories on this river. Michigan state GIS #68 points to a separate Herman-area series of falls, well south of the more accessible Lower Silver picnic area. Two independent field positions corroborate the Herman feature, but not a visitor route.

NOT CLEARED FOR PUBLIC WATERFALL NAVIGATION

Do not navigate to the Herman GIS falls point or treat the separate Lower Silver picnic-area upper chute as the same location. No permission, parking or approved walk has been verified.

What the evidence actually establishes

The geography is clearer. Two independent published waypoints land within about 150 metres of Michigan's Upper Silver Falls GIS #68. That is strong geographic corroboration of the Herman-area location, not evidence of a public parking area or a managed trail.

The Lower Silver Falls picnic site is approximately 12.4 km to the north. Historical accounts also describe a different "upper" chute roughly a quarter mile above that picnic spot. Those are different geographic references. Old logging-track descriptions are not landowner consent.

Six access checkpoints

Each checkpoint needs its own authority. Correct coordinates alone do not pass the visitor-route gate.

  1. 1. Geographic feature identity

    Two independently published field waypoint sources lie roughly 151 m and 121 m from GIS #68 near Herman. This is a geographic identity match, not an access grant.

    Original evidence for this checkpoint

  2. 2. Name collision with lower Silver picnic cascades

    GIS #68 is 12.4 km from lower picnic #110. The informal rough quarter-mile-upstream chute described at Lower Silver is not this Herman feature; do not merge records or recommend a single connected walk.

    Original evidence for this checkpoint

  3. 3. Land manager and lawful permission

    A third-party site labels nearby tribal lands but an authoritative parcel-level landholder/public-access determination has not been established. Do not assume tribal, state or private land grants access.

    Original evidence for this checkpoint

  4. 4. Public road and parking

    Historic field descriptions mention a gravel pit, rough two-track and Lahti Road. No current agency motor-vehicle-use designation, landowner consent or safe waterfall-visitor parking verified.

    Original evidence for this checkpoint

  5. 5. Continuous lawful walking route and viewpoint

    No official continuous public lot-to-waterfall path, trail easement, bridges, crossings or safe viewpoint published for this exact GIS feature.

    Original evidence for this checkpoint

  6. 6. Season and present conditions

    No present opening, road maintenance, permission, water level, logging operations or safe-current-conditions statement confirmed. Historic photos do not establish visitability.

    Original evidence for this checkpoint
