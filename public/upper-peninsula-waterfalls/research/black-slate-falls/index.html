Black Slate Falls

Black Slate Falls is a mapped feature of the Slate River in Baraga County. The mapped natural feature is not a parking location or a promise of public access. This research review separates what the sources actually establish from what remains unknown.

Research only. No approved parking, visitor trail or public guide.

What the evidence establishes

Michigan GIS #105 at 46.784430,-88.241702 is about 385 m from the field-mapped Black Slate Falls reference (46.78754,-88.23947); the independent account describes a dispersed half-mile run of small upstream drops, so the point mismatch may represent a different section, not necessarily an error. It is distinct from Quartzite #107.

Baraga County's original visitor brochure and its later hosted waterfall map explicitly describe a short forest trail off Arvon Road and this upper series above Quartzite, but neither identifies the actual trail/bridge parcel owner or grants 2026 public parking at an unmarked logging-road bridge. An independent account reports the longer upstream bank has no established trail.

Land title / land manager
Michigan DNR LOTS statewide ownership and conservation-easement point queries returned no intersecting features. This is NOT proof of private ownership or proof of public access. County property and road right-of-way records remain necessary. Source
Road and historical approach
Baraga County's original visitor brochure and its later hosted waterfall map explicitly describe a short forest trail off Arvon Road and this upper series above Quartzite, but neither identifies the actual trail/bridge parcel owner or grants 2026 public parking at an unmarked logging-road bridge. An independent account reports the longer upstream bank has no established trail. Source
Authorized visitor parking
No current land-manager or county road authority evidence establishes a signed/legal visitor parking area at the mapped approach. Historic logging-road or two-track stopping places are not certified parking. Source
Full legal and safe walking corridor
A complete public approach from lawful arrival to safe viewing has not been verified across every parcel/streambank. No river crossing, steep scramble or cross-private-land route is authorized. Source
Current restrictions and seasonal conditions
No dated November 2026 public notice confirms motorized road maintenance, permitted trail passage, bridge integrity, posted restrictions or safe flow. Do not imply live opening. Source

What would justify promotion

Match county parcel/land manager for both falls and the logging bridge, verify Arvon/All Wood road easement and legal turnout, and limit any future guide to a clearly documented safe short view rather than inventing a continuous upstream trail.
