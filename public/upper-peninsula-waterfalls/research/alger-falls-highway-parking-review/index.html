Alger Falls: visible from the highway is not safe parking

The cascade at Alger Creek is real, and many older travel pages call it a quick roadside stop. But the city's adopted recreation plan and the Alger County recreation inventory explicitly identify a parking-safety problem. A photograph or visible waterfall is not permission to stop in moving traffic.

NOT CLEARED FOR PUBLIC WATERFALL NAVIGATION

Do not recommend stopping on M-28, crossing a guardrail or using a road shoulder as a visitor parking lot. The local planning record says no safe parking option; current MDOT site approval remains unverified.

What the evidence actually establishes

Two independent local recreation plans describe the problem. The adopted Munising 2024–2028 plan and the Alger County 2024–2029 plan both describe a roadside-visible waterfall but no safe parking options at the time of planning.

Not an official closure notice. The records do not prove that every highway shoulder is prohibited or that no later improvement occurred. They do mean that an old tourism claim of a turnout, or general MDOT shoulder-parking rules, cannot be marketed as a safe waterfall visitor lot. There is no recommended M-28 stopping point here.

Private guest access is not a public roadside stop

A nearby lodging operator's public website says that Alger Falls is on its property and that its guests may enjoy the waterfall. This is the operator's own statement about guest access; it is not independently verified parcel-title evidence, an invitation for non-guests, or permission to use an M-28 shoulder, drive, or cross private land. Anyone interested in a stay should check directly with that property about current guest policies and walking conditions. The public visitor-route verdict remains not cleared.

What must change before publication?

Require current MDOT site-specific safety determination and certified legal visitor arrival/overlook, resolving the city/county no-safe-parking warning. Do not substitute general shoulder rules or tourist turnout advice.
