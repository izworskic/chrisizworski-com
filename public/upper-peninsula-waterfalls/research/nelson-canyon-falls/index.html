Nelson Canyon Falls

A short plunge over an overhanging ledge in a wooded canyon near Lake Gogebic; regional tourism describes the waterfall, but the walking path is not verified as a maintained Forest Service trail.

Research only. No verified public visitor parking or complete authorized walking route.

What we know

Michigan GIS #25 at 46.391819,-89.579675 agrees within roughly 3m with independent Nelson Canyon Falls field point 46.39180,-89.57971; USGS GNIS #1620996 is also nearby. The named waterfall itself is strongly corroborated.

Why it is not yet a visitor stop

Regional tourism provides historic C Camp Road/two-track walking suggestions; independent 2015 and 2026 field comments describe severely overgrown tracks, a vanishing trail and a final rocky descent. Another derivative website asserts a developed Forest Service NFST 7418-D segment, but no independent official Forest Service route listing, signed trailhead and lawful parking for this exact falls was verified. Do not repeat its 'easy developed trail' claim.
