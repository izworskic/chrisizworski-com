Powder Horn Falls

A genuine two-tier drop tucked into the narrow Powder Mill Creek ravine beside the Big Powderhorn neighborhood.

Research only: no verified legal public visitor parking or complete walking route.

What the geographic evidence shows

Michigan GIS #39 at 46.505974,-90.081969 is approximately 3m from the field documented Powderhorn Falls at 46.50595,-90.08195. UP Travel names and describes it. Other mass-generated lists place their 'Powder Horn' parking point about 1.4km away, so the location is a falls location only, never a navigation lot.

Why this is not a recommended visitor stop

Older field directions suggest parking at a former lodge and following a barely marked path past private driveways, then a rope-assisted lower gorge descent. A current October 2026 MLS #50224628 describes privately owned Powdermill Creek frontage adjoining a neighboring waterfall property; it does NOT independently establish ownership of GIS #39, but reinforces the need for parcel-specific and parking consent. UP Travel's generic road address is not a signed trailhead.
