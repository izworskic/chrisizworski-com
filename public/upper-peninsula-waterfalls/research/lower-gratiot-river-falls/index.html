Lower Gratiot River Falls

The lower basalt ledges are accurately located, but visitor access requires reconciling a 2019 conservation ownership warning with today's property rights.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. Geographic coordinates are not legal parking locations, signed trailheads or current access permission.

What the source evidence supports

Michigan GIS #137 Lower Falls is 47.335138,-88.444003; firsthand Gratiot River Falls at 47.335340,-88.444360 is just 35 metres away, with a GNIS-derived Lower Falls record about 146 metres from GIS. It is a distinct lower drop around 1.03 km from GIS Upper Gratiot #136. In a June 23, 2019 KNA-attributed comment, the group identified these pictured ledges as Lower Gratiot and said it was INTERIM owner, seeking funds to protect public access, otherwise possibly selling the parcel by the end of 2020. There is no verified 2026 resolution of that historical ownership statement.

Why the public approach is unresolved

Current KNA organizational information lists Gratiot River North and Conglomerate Falls natural areas open to the public; that does not prove the Lower Falls parcel is presently owned by KNA, nor that the muddy access two-track off Bumbletown Road or shoulder stopping is authorized. Historical informal directions and 2019 INTERIM ownership cannot be converted into a present-day visitor parking/route claim.
