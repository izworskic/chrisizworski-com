Alder Falls

A real, multi-drop gorge—but an unmarked sand road is not a verified public trailhead.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. Never use these natural-feature coordinates as a parking location or assume an old route is legal today.

What a knowledgeable local would tell you

State GIS point 46.780948,-87.708440 and USGS-derived hydro feature are about 114m apart. An independent field record maps the lower main cascade near 46.78188,-87.70739 and identifies three smaller upstream drops. A regional tourism listing describes a 30ft cascade, but the larger total is not one vertical 30ft plunge.

The important access catch

Older field guides use the rough Alder Creek Truck Trail off CR550 and describe a No Motorized Vehicles sign at the steep final path. No current authorized forest-road vehicle status, public signed parking lot, property corridor, or bridge status established. Private home driveways occur near the truck trail.
