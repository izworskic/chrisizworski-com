Narrow Falls

A shallow riffle on the Black River, locally also called Narrows Falls, rather than a significant plunge or viewing-platform attraction.

Research only: no verified legal public visitor parking or complete walking route.

What the geographic evidence shows

State GIS #48 'Narrow Falls' at 46.562534,-90.081582 is approximately 63m from independent 'Narrows Falls' point 46.56304,-90.08122. Field survey calls it a submerged-rock ripple of around 3 inches, while generalized tourism lists repeatedly ascribe a 10-foot waterfall/steep scramble to a point around 46.6264,-90.0483, about 7.5km farther north. These cannot be treated as identical visitor destinations.

Why this is not a recommended visitor stop

Historical account mentions an informal roadside campsite, unsurveyed small clearing and muddy fishing path, but gives no agency permission to park or a designated public route. Official Ottawa visitor materials describe five maintained Black River Scenic Byway waterfalls, not this tiny rapids feature.
