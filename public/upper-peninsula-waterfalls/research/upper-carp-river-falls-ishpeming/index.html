Carp River Falls (upper)

The Ishpeming-area upper drops are not Marquette's celebrated Carp River Falls.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. Never use these natural-feature coordinates as a parking location or assume an old route is legal today.

What a knowledgeable local would tell you

Michigan GIS #163 locates a feature near 46.521504,-87.572418, consistent with independent Upper Carp River Falls at 46.52167,-87.57631, two small ledges outside Ishpeming. This is far west of the major Marquette gorge falls found in state GIS #169 near 46.503807,-87.44655.

The important access catch

An old field report instructs parking at a SEMCO Energy facility; a 2019 first-hand visitor comment reports its trail posted No Trespassing. No later permission, approved parking, easement or complete legal route was verified. Roadside/utility grounds are not public lots.
