Schweitzer Falls

A legitimate twenty-foot cascade reached by old informal forest travel, not an approved trail.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. Never use these natural-feature coordinates as a parking location or assume an old route is legal today.

What a knowledgeable local would tell you

GIS #173 places Schweitzer Falls at 46.407401,-87.596355, close to independently visited coordinates 46.40778,-87.5975 and 2022 field guide coordinates 46.40739,-87.59637. Waterfall described as two-tiered ~20ft, not an invented easy public park.

The important access catch

2012 field description uses an unnamed two-track beyond Schweitzer Creek bridge and a faint bushwhack; the author specifically could not find an obvious safe approach to upper tier. No present land ownership, public trail designation, signed road parking, permissions or safe pool-crossing established.
