Erick's Falls: West Branch Huron River access review

Michigan GIS #114 46.806264,-88.087136 agrees within about 16m of specialist West Branch Erick's Falls 46.8064,-88.08705. Older field accounts describe an informal Black Creek Road bridge turnout and a short walk downstream, but lack a signed public parking site, county-road status and verified landowner permission along the banks. This is a distinct river branch and bridge several miles upstream from officially public Big Eric's Bridge State Forest Campground (GIS #126); campground permission does not transfer to this location.

Research only; no verified complete public visitor route or parking. Natural waterfall points are not road destinations.

What would permit a public guide

Verify public Black Creek Road seasonal access, approved bridge-area parking, current parcel title/easement for both banks, signed foot corridor and visitor safety. Do not transfer Big Eric's DNR campground route.

Do not substitute nearby public forest, a dated tourism suggestion or informal roadside parking for documented legal access. Use the existing curated waterfall explorer to find an established alternative.
