Upper Gratiot River Falls

The upper Gratiot waterfall has a historical map identity, but its present appearance is uncertain after a reported beaver-pond change.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. Geographic coordinates are not legal parking locations, signed trailheads or current access permission.

What the source evidence supports

Michigan GIS #136 Upper Falls at 47.329239,-88.433472 is 370 metres from a GNIS-derived Upper Falls coordinate 47.331389,-88.437222. GIS Lower Gratiot #137 is 1.03 km downstream. In a June 23, 2019 firsthand-source comment attributed to Keweenaw Natural Areas, the group wrote that 'The Upper Falls are now part of a beaver pond.' That is a dated 2019 observation, not a November 2026 proof of permanent inundation or a guaranteed flowing waterfall.

Why the public approach is unresolved

Keweenaw Natural Areas currently describes some Gratiot-area preserves as public, but it does not establish legal public foot access to the exact Upper Falls bank or a signed visitor trail. Its 2019 comment discusses INTERIM ownership of LOWER Gratiot lands, not a visitor-use permit or permanent title assurance for Upper. Do not transfer road or parking information from the separate Conglomerate Falls preserve or lower Gratiot point.
