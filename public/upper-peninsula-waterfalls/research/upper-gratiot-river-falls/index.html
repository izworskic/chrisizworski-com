This older research record is retained for source history. The owner-published Conglomerate Falls entrance and parking evidence now supports a separate public visitor guide.

Why the record changed

Michigan GIS #136 describes “Upper Falls” on the Gratiot River at 47.329239, -88.433472, about 77 metres from a specialist's Conglomerate Falls / Upper Gratiot coordinates. KNA now directly provides the relevant falls-preserve entry, daylight visitor policy, parking and private-road easement. These are site-specific ownership and access sources, rather than unrelated state land inferred from a map.

A 2019 beaver-pond observation remains historical. It does not certify today's river depth, visible flow, or safety. The separately mapped Lower Gratiot River Falls (GIS #137), farther downstream, is still under its own title and arrival audit; do not transfer the Conglomerate parking route to Lower Gratiot.
