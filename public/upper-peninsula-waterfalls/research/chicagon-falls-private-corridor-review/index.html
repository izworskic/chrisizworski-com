Chicagon Falls: where the map ends and access begins

Chicagon and Chicaugon are two names used for the same general waterfall area near Crystal Falls. Its geography is credible. The public way to reach it is not yet established. The official land records and visitor advice describe different parts of the problem.

Do not route visitors here yet

The waterfall point is on Michigan DNR-managed land, but ownership of one parcel does not establish a legal approach through adjoining parcels, approved parking or a continuous public walk. There is no confirmed open-now trip or proposed vehicle waypoint.

What would change this decision?

Iron County Road Commission would need to establish the legal road classification and permitted stopping location. The Michigan DNR Crystal Falls Management Unit and county property records would need to establish title/easements across any intervening private land and a complete lawful footpath. Current washouts and posted restrictions would still need checking.
