Carp River Falls (Marquette gorge)

Travel Marquette's real gorge waterfall, but the final steep river descent is not a signed safe viewpoint.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. Never use these natural-feature coordinates as a parking location or assume an old route is legal today.

What a knowledgeable local would tell you

State GIS #169 names Carp River Falls II but incorrectly lists MORGAN CREEK as waterway. The natural point 46.503807,-87.446550 is about 18m from Travel Marquette's Carp River Falls 46.50367,-87.44668. Morgan Falls at 46.50737,-87.44516 is a different waterfall on Morgan Creek, with a separate published card.

The important access catch

Travel Marquette specifies parking at NTN South Trails, walking/biking a motor-vehicle-closed Mt. Marquette Road, reaching Morgan Falls, then continuing about half a mile on an increasingly rough Carp River trail. Independent visits say the high rim offers poor views and getting near the base involves precarious climb. Do not generate a safe trail line, permit vehicle traffic on closed road or imply official parking at the natural falls point.
