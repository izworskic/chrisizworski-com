Reany Falls

A genuine bridge-view waterfall; the nearby powerhouse lot is not automatically approved parking.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. Never use these natural-feature coordinates as a parking location or assume an old route is legal today.

What a knowledgeable local would tell you

GIS #162 on Reany Creek is near USGS-derived 46.573626,-87.478228 (~73m apart), and independent 2022 field trip pin 46.57355,-87.47692 and GNIS references agree this is the Forestville Road waterfall, not a similarly named feature along Reany Street in downtown Marquette. The drops appear from the road bridge.

The important access catch

Michigan tourism and UPtravel recommend parking by an old power station then walking back to the bridge; the current site-specific utility/land manager's legal visitor parking, any gate rules, bridge pedestrian facilities and safe road shoulder have not been established. Do not substitute Dead River Falls private route rights.
