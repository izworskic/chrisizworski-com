Silver Bell Falls: public route still under investigation

Michigan GIS maps Silver Bell Falls on Nelson Creek close to an independent 2016 field location. Historic approach descriptions go through rough Hiawatha National Forest roads FR2276, possibly FR2293, and an intermittently gated logging-road segment. One account describes a short footpath; an older hiker reports wet beaver-affected terrain. Neither establishes the current USFS Motor Vehicle Use Map road status, public parking, safe crossings or current forest permissions. Field-to-source coordinate separation approximately 35 metres. Raw natural waterfall coordinates are NOT visitor parking or a legal footpath.

Research only. No lawful complete visitor parking and footpath are established. Do not drive to the raw waterfall coordinate.
