Silver Bell Falls

Michigan GIS #195 matches an independent Nelson Creek falls report near Chatham. The 2016 route used rough Forest Road 2276 and a gate that may close. No current authorized parking and continuous path confirmed.

Research only. Full public route and parking are not proven.

What we know

State GIS Silver Bell Falls #195 on Nelson Creek at 46.450945,-86.970673 matches a 2016 field report at 46.45117,-86.97035, about 35m away. Field account places it within Hiawatha National Forest, with badly deteriorated FR2276, potentially closed gate and an additional mile to a short approximately quarter-mile fall footpath. Historic field driving access is not current USFS MVUM motorized permission, visitor parking, an authorized walk or guaranteed open gate. Do not conflate with Upper/Lower Silver Falls on Baraga's Silver River.
