Little Quinnesec Falls (hydropower)

Little Quinnesec is a real place, but its strongest current identity is an operating hydroelectric station rather than a signed waterfall-hiking destination.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. An active hydropower facility and project-wide amenities do not make a public waterfall trail. Raw GIS feature points are not parking lots.

What this location actually is

Michigan GIS #4 locates Little Quinnesec Falls on the Menominee River at 45.773089,-87.989459. A secondary federal plant-location listing places the Little Quinnesec hydro facility at 45.7736,-87.9894, about 57 metres from that geographic point; this supports geographic vicinity, not surveyed identity of the natural ledge. Eagle Creek Renewable Energy identifies Little Quinnesec as active FERC Project P-2536, licensed through April 2037, with an approximately 9.1-megawatt generating station in Niagara, Wisconsin.

What the official recreation evidence does and does not say

Eagle Creek's operator-level recreation table lists parking, picnic area, canoe portage, fishing access, boat launch and camping for the Little Quinnesec project, but lists no hiking trail and identifies no waterfall overlook, specific legal entry road, certified parking waypoint or permitted visitor bank-to-falls route. Project-level recreational opportunities are not a permit to enter power facilities or a verified riverbank walking corridor.

Flow and current conditions

The operator publishes Menominee River streamflow context for the Little Quinnesec facility, but upstream or downstream gauge flow is not a direct measurement of water over an original waterfall ledge. The public operator pages do not supply a live visitor-access condition for a named falls overlook. Research reviewed November 2, 2026; no field visit or live gate/trail inspection.
