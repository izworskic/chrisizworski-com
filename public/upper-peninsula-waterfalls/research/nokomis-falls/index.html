Nokomis Falls

A real waterfall on the Presque Isle River, but present property ownership and a public walking route remain unsettled.

Research only: no verified legal visitor parking or complete authorized walking route.

What the evidence shows

Nokomis river ledge documented by 2007 photo and GeoNames point 46.646613,-89.910168 about 80m from state GIS. No USFS managed surface polygon and no DNR LOTS state parcel/easement point intersects. 2007 photographer captions commercial forest land; online guides claiming state park or USFS ownership conflict, current title unresolved. No NFS published trail within 1km; no authorized walking route proven beyond developed downstream Presque Isle loop, nor signed parking for this feature.

What must be established before recommending a visit

Verify current parcel title, legal signed public visitor parking, road access and a continuous authorized footpath to a safe viewpoint. Do not adopt old logging-road, paddler put-in or unofficial riverbank shortcuts.
