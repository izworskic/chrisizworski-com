Caps Creek Falls

A named state GIS feature with little independent on-the-ground evidence. Public directories repeat a waterfall description but provide no documented authorized trailhead, trail or land manager.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. No permission, parking, signed trail or live opening can be inferred from a natural-feature point.

What the source evidence says

State GIS #187 maps Caps Creek Falls at 46.331182,-87.974281. A general waterfall-directory listing instead places it at 46.3214,-87.9403, several kilometres away; neither directory proves a distinct visited drop or a public footpath.

The access decision

A local-interest directory explicitly reports no firsthand account or confirmed directions. Another general directory lists an apparent short hike and County Road FX address without accessible official supporting trail authority; those details are not fit for navigation.
