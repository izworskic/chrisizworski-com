MARQUETTE County · GIS: Snake Creek; probable Salmon Trout River mainstem · Geographic evidence review

Lower Falls (Snake Creek GIS record)

This is likely a mislabeled waterway, not a second public waterfall on Snake Creek. The mapped Lower Falls lies near the private Salmon Trout River Lower Falls.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. A waterfall on a map is not permission to enter restricted land, drive a track or park at the geographic point.

The place and its source identity

Michigan GIS #152 labels Lower Falls on SNAKE CREEK at 46.815112,-87.804203. A USGS GNIS-derived Lower Falls record in the Ives Hill quad at 46.815278,-87.803611 is only about 49 metres away. The GNIS Snake Creek mouth at 46.811313,-87.812907 is about 786 metres from the state waterfall point. American Whitewater's Salmon Trout River description places Snake Creek and then Clear Creek as tributaries between Middle Falls and the downstream Lower Falls, not at the Lower Falls itself. Michigan DNR's November 2023 regulation describes Lower Falls as a Salmon-Trout River feature. These records strongly suggest the GIS #152 WATERWAY label conflicts with the actual mainstem Lower Falls. Do not merge the feature into a different waterfall record without official hydrography/alias confirmation.

What is actually possible for visitors

American Whitewater reports the Middle/Lower Salmon Trout River falls within the Huron Mountain Club's access-restricted private property, and the club confirms gated, patrolled road access. There is no public visiting corridor, approved parking, walking route, or legal backcountry approach to this exact waterfall. A broad old tourism list placing 'Lower Falls' on Snake Creek west of Big Bay is name evidence, not public admission or permission.
