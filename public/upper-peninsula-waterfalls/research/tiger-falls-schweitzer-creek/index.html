Tiger Falls

A low-relief creek rapid with conflicting coordinates and nearby private cabins.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. Never use these natural-feature coordinates as a parking location or assume an old route is legal today.

What a knowledgeable local would tell you

GIS #175 (46.402068,-87.564648) is ~779m from the independent USGS-v2 crosswalk point (46.403279,-87.574656). A first-hand field survey describes two loose 6-inch-to-1ft ledges and a field position 46.40336,-87.57497; a broad generic directory instead calls it 12ft with far-away Marquette directions.

The important access catch

Old unmarked route descriptions use a meadow, marginal tracks and cross near creekside cabins. Present private title, public parking and a continuous right-of-way have not been verified; a tiny rapid is not a reason to lead visitors near cabins.
