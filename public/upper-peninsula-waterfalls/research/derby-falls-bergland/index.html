What the evidence actually establishes

Michigan GIS #62 puts Derby Falls at 46.633606,-89.575682 on Derby Creek. The Lake Gogebic Chamber lists the name northeast of Bergland. World Waterfall Database calls its existence unknown and describes possible seasonal small drops in a different drainage. Hidden Falls GIS #61 is about 407 metres away. A mapped name is not an independent proof of a distinct visible waterfall.
