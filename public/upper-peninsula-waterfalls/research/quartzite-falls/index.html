Quartzite Falls

Quartzite Falls is a mapped feature of the Slate River in Baraga County. The mapped natural feature is not a parking location or a promise of public access. This research review separates what the sources actually establish from what remains unknown.

Research only. No approved parking, visitor trail or public guide.

What the evidence establishes

Michigan GIS #107 at 46.789247,-88.242098 lies about 50 m from independently mapped Quartzite (46.78882,-88.24191), strong geographic agreement. This is the small downstream cascade, not the separate Slate River Falls #119 far north at the Skanee Road bridge.

County visitor brochure, Upper Peninsula Travel and field observations agree a short walk descends from the Arvon Road/wooden logging-bridge vicinity. These historical descriptions do not establish who owns the bridge turnout, the intervening footpath, or a current signed parking right. Do not copy unverified riverbed scrambling/swimming instructions.

Authorized visitor parking
No current land-manager or county road authority evidence establishes a signed/legal visitor parking area at the mapped approach. Historic logging-road or two-track stopping places are not certified parking. Source
Full legal and safe walking corridor
A complete public approach from lawful arrival to safe viewing has not been verified across every parcel/streambank. No river crossing, steep scramble or cross-private-land route is authorized. Source
Current restrictions and seasonal conditions
No dated November 2026 public notice confirms motorized road maintenance, permitted trail passage, bridge integrity, posted restrictions or safe flow. Do not imply live opening. Source

What would justify promotion

Determine parcel owner at natural falls and the bridge turnout, confirm road/right-of-way and designated parking, documented legal short path and current restrictions. Only then promote the short-view visitor guide.
