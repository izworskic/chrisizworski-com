80-Foot Falls

A named place on the map, not a verified roadside waterfall.

UNCONFIRMED IN FIELD · PUBLIC ACCESS NOT ESTABLISHED. This geographic record is not an approved parking destination, public walking route or live opening report.

What a knowledgeable local would tell you

The mapped Eighty Foot Falls, also known as Ives Falls, lies on the River Styx in remote Huron Mountain country. World Waterfall Database explicitly marks the waterfall Unconfirmed, based on one-metre lidar suggesting an approximately 105-foot flume. Some lists call it a roadside view, while other sources describe private Huron Mountain Club land with no public trail. Neither the fall's height nor a legal roadside overlook has been established.

Before planning a trip

Do not drive to a supposed Ives Lake roadside overlook or cross club land to search for an unverified flume.
