Upper Falls (Sturgeon River)

The small, split slate fall beyond Canyon Falls is a real named feature, but the route past Canyon's maintained path is not verified as a signed visitor route.

What the source evidence supports

Michigan state GIS #51 locates Upper Falls at 46.621677,-88.489310 on the Sturgeon River; firsthand Upper Falls coordinates 46.621070,-88.482070 differ by 557 metres. This is a slate slide below the Canyon Falls gorge, about 1.17 km from the curated Canyon Falls point and 15.76 km from the unrelated, separately curated Sturgeon Falls in Sturgeon River Gorge Wilderness. No generic 'Upper Falls' point should inherit directions to either other waterfall.

Why the public approach is unresolved

The Northern Michigan University Baraga attractions guide and the specialist Canyon Falls article identify a maintained visitor path from the US-41 roadside park to Canyon Falls. The specialist explicitly describes an UNMAINTAINED continuation along the cliff/canyon edge toward Upper Falls.
