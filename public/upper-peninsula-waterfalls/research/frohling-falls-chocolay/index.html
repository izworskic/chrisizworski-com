Frohling Falls

Dramatic remote chutes, but documented posted private approaches make old shortcuts unsuitable.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. Never use these natural-feature coordinates as a parking location or assume an old route is legal today.

What a knowledgeable local would tell you

GIS #180 and USGS-derived hydro site agree within roughly 64m; independent field site 46.36069,-87.32913 places the true plunge/chutes on West Branch Chocolay. Tourism estimates 30ft while field report describes up to 50ft combined tiered falls: figures refer to differing drop definitions.

The important access catch

Regional tourism explicitly says no official maintained trail. Independent 2018 field comments identify Sporley Lake Road north approach as posted No Trespassing; other old routes start from M-94 shoulder and follow unmarked logging tracks. No proof of legal shoulder parking, safe road traffic, parcel ownership or public trail permission. Tourist use does not override posting.
