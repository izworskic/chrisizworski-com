KEWEENAW County · Montreal River upstream from Lake Superior · Source review, not a visitor route

Upper Montreal Falls

A separate higher-country chute upstream from the better-known river-mouth falls, with even less certainty about the last stretch of trail.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. Geographic coordinates are not legal parking locations, signed trailheads or current access permission.

What the source evidence supports

Michigan GIS #140 Upper Falls at 47.397970,-87.836302 is 267 metres from a firsthand Upper Montreal site at 47.395630,-87.835510 and about 290 metres from a GNIS-derived Upper Falls record. GIS Lower Montreal #139 is ~705 metres away. A field account also describes a smaller farther-upstream cascade, so the generic 'Upper Falls' name does not itself identify which drop is meant.

Why the public approach is unresolved

The older Smiths-Fisheries west-bank walking route reaches Lower Montreal, then continues upstream as an increasingly indistinct trail. The specialist says the path dies out before the smallest uppermost drop and describes road gates; Visit Keweenaw describes the existing Fish Cove unmaintained approach. KORC's NEW proposed parking and connector project remained unapproved by DNR in March 2026. No current signed visitor lot, legal continuity from vehicle to the exact Upper drop or verified safe approach exists in the collected evidence.
