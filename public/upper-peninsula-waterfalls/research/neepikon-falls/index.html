Neepikon Falls

A little-known named rapid upstream of Gabbro Falls rather than a developed waterfall viewpoint.

Research only: no verified legal public visitor parking or complete walking route.

What the geographic evidence shows

Michigan state GIS #37 (46.490065,-89.995318) is approximately 477m south of the independent field-mapped Neepikon rapids (46.49433,-89.99471). World Waterfall Database specifically disqualifies it as a significant waterfall and notes a historical topographic-name/location mismatch. Preserve both source identities without automatically reconciling to one tourist stop.

Why this is not a recommended visitor stop

Field account describes a half-foot riffle and a difficult, overgrown, unmarked approach off Blackjack Road, not an official tourist trail. USFS published-trails spatial query within 1km returns a named snowmobile trail (Bergland Snowmobile #11 N), not an identified walking route to Neepikon; it does not justify using its track or a gas utility road as a waterfall approach.
