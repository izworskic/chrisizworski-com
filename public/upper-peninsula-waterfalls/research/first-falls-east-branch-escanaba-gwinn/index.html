MARQUETTE County · East Branch Escanaba River · Geographic evidence review

First Falls (Gwinn)

This is a tiny river ripple, not a dramatic plunge. The meaningful experience is the public riverside day-use park and its rustic path.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. The public municipal park is documented, but this exact falls endpoint and parking-to-falls route are not certified.

The place and its source identity

Michigan GIS #176 locates First Falls on the East Branch Escanaba River at 46.285073,-87.434620, within about 10 metres of the field account at 46.285160,-87.434570. The firsthand source describes a roughly THREE-INCH drop into a dark pool. The community tourism page explicitly names First Falls, and Forsyth Township's adopted September 2024 recreation plan identifies Farquar/Metsa Tourist Park's public day-use area and an Eagle Scout-built rustic trail heading north along the river, with views of a small waterfall. This establishes a legitimate park-level place to walk, but the township does not certify exact waterfall naming/point geometry or current site conditions.

What is actually possible for visitors

Forsyth Township's adopted plan verifies a municipal day-use area, campground, picnic pavilion and rustic riverside trail, with accessibility grade 2 for the park. A separately accessible path links two portions of the day-use area; this MUST NOT be described as wheelchair access along the rustic waterfall trail. A 2009-era field description suggests parking near a loop/pavilion, but no current designated waterfall-specific lot, signed First Falls trail endpoint, live seasonal day-use hours or surveyed parking-to-fall polyline was found. Visitors can inquire at the park; do not advertise a safe swimming hole or promise waterfall-specific parking.
