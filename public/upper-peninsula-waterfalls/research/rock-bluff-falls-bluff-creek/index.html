Rock Bluff Falls

There is a real named Rock Bluff Falls on Bluff Creek, but old maps and field reports do not agree on which nearby drops deserve that name. No lawful, signed parking-to-falls walk has been verified.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. Natural-feature coordinates are not visitor parking, a public trail, or a verified safe approach.

What the source evidence establishes

Michigan GIS #22 maps Rock Bluff Falls at 46.370607,-89.183436. A Western U.P. Visitors Bureau brochure places its three-drop falls at 46.373742,-89.185312, about 377 metres from the GIS point. A distinct field entry called Upper Rock Bluff Falls is roughly 1.37 kilometres from the state point; a 2023 reader comment suggests further possible confusion with Mateson Creek. These sources cannot be silently combined into a single exact visitor waypoint.

What stops this from being a public waterfall trip

The regional tourism brochure suggests stopping near Old US-45 and walking alongside Bluff Creek; an independent field account describes steep forest, brush, an informal two-track and an ATV track. Neither confirms signed authorized parking, current vehicle access on Old US-45, ownership/right-of-way across the creek valley or a maintained footpath. A tourism direction is not a verified legal parking or public approach authorization.

Current road jurisdiction and lawful visitor parking
The USFS Motor Vehicle Use Map must be checked for any national-forest road. A road shown on a tourism brochure, on a base map or as a historical two-track does not establish legal parking here. No official lot or approved vehicle stopping point was independently verified. Source
Complete safe walking trail and viewpoint
No signed public visitor lot, official trailhead or maintained route; old tracks have uncertain current designation. Steep creek valley, brush and wet ground are described in firsthand field material, and travel in November can be hazardous. Source

What would change the verdict

Reconcile Rock Bluff, Upper Rock Bluff and the Mateson Creek report with independently surveyed or official geographic identity; verify parcels, road jurisdiction, signed public parking and a continuous authorized walking corridor to the correct drops.
