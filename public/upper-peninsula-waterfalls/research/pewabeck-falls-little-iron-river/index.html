Pewabeck Falls

This small Little Iron River ledge is widely named, but its whitewater description and tourist listings do not establish a safe signed hike or a lawful visitor parking point.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. A named geographic waterfall coordinate is not a safe public trailhead, a lawful parking point or live access clearance.

What the evidence supports

Michigan GIS #122 locates Pewabeck Falls at 46.806756,-89.587140; the GNIS-derived USGS location 46.807446,-89.587097 is approximately 77 metres away. The Lake Gogebic Chamber places Pewabeck on the Little Iron River north of White Pine. American Whitewater describes a four-foot ledge about six river miles downstream from Nonesuch Falls; the U.P. Travel Association describes a little-over-five-foot waterfall with a rugged 30–45 minute walk. These are source-dependent estimates, not a certified vertical measurement or official trail. A generic directory's Pewabeck point at 46.7242,-89.2056 is approximately 30.48 km away, and its 25-foot/highly accessible portrayal is unsuitable for a visitor guide.

Why the public approach is not verified

The U.P. Travel Association explicitly says there is little to no trail access, despite estimating a hiking time. American Whitewater describes experienced paddler put-in/take-out options along the Little Iron River; paddling access does not prove safe riverbank foot access. No current site-manager-signed trailhead, approved public parking, legal shoreline corridor or verified walking route to GIS #122 was found. The generic directory's 'Pewabeck Road' coordinates are too far away and cannot be used for directions.
