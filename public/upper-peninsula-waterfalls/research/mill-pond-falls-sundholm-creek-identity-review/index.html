Mill Pond Falls (Sundholm Creek)

A mapped name is not yet a place to send a hiker. Michigan GIS lists Mill Pond Falls on Sundholm Creek, while federal-name-derived lists show Upper and Lower Mill Pond Falls nearby at different points.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. The state point is not yet an established alias for Upper or Lower Mill Pond Falls. Raw GIS feature points are not parking lots.

What this location actually is

Michigan GIS #8 gives Mill Pond Falls on Sundholm Creek at 45.988890,-87.854767. USGS GNIS-derived Upper Mill Pond Falls in the Felch quadrangle is at 45.9863889,-87.8494444, roughly 496 metres from the state point; GNIS-derived Lower Mill Pond Falls is at 45.9844444,-87.8452778, about 884 metres from the state point. The GNIS upper and lower records are about 388 metres apart. These are source-coordinate comparisons, not evidence that all three names refer to one waterfall or that any location is exact.

What the official recreation evidence does and does not say

No authoritative land-manager map, designated public visitor parking, signed waterfall trailhead or continuous lawful walk to the state GIS #8 feature was established. Secondary geographic listings suggest backcountry setting but cannot certify that a two-track, woodland or private timber parcel is publicly traversable.
