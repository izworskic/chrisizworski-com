Ogemaw Falls

GIS Ogemaw #59 at 46.648930,-88.512220 differs about 537 metres from specialist Ogemaw Creek falls at 46.645410,-88.507430, while a second 2006 visitor lists 46.6473,-88.5110 nearer the GIS point. The same creek may contain multiple smaller drops; waterfall identity and exact point not reconciled. Historical visitors describe a small brown sign opposite a Baraga Plains Road pond and informal pull-off near culvert, with undergrowth, eroded creek-bank paths and scattered fallen trees; no county road commission designated parking or current manager approved public trail. Independent 2026 photo exists but cannot serve as county roadside approval. Exact state-specialist distance 536 metres. Reviewed Nov 1 2026. All traffic, property and seasonal rights subject to current land manager confirmation.

Research only: parking and complete public waterfall route are not verified.
