Ogemaw Falls: public route still under investigation

Michigan GIS and official GNIS place Ogemaw Falls at approximately the same point, but an independent waterfall field point lies about half a kilometre away. Old field reports identify a tiny signed creek trail from Baraga Plains Road and an informal pond-side road turnout; their road mileages differ. DNR's broad forest-compartment report lists portions of Ogemaw Creek but does not identify exact public access/owner or an approved visitor parking area. Field-to-source coordinate separation approximately 536 metres. Raw natural waterfall coordinates are NOT visitor parking or a legal footpath.

Research only. No lawful complete visitor parking and footpath are established. Do not drive to the raw waterfall coordinate.
