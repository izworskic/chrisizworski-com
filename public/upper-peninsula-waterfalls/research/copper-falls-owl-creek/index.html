Copper Falls

A beautiful historic creek, but private approaches are not visitor trailheads.

PRIVATE-ACCESS CONFLICT · NO AUTOMATED DIRECTIONS. This geographic record is not an approved parking destination, public walking route or live opening report.

What a knowledgeable local would tell you

Copper Falls is a series of different drops on Owl Creek near Eagle Harbor. The upper falls, main creek cascades and lower mine-adit flow are separate features. Historic mine workings also alter the stream. A specialist source's reader discussion identifies Copper Falls Loop as private, with an older purported owner comment permitting some foot visits while discouraging vehicles. That is not verified, current, property-specific visitor authorization.

Before planning a trip

Do not route drivers down Copper Falls Loop, into an old pit or to mine shafts. Treat the older private-land comment as context, not permission.
