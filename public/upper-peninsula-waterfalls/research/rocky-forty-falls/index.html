Rocky Forty Falls

A secluded Siemens Creek cascade in the western Gogebic uplands, with weak public-trail evidence despite confident online descriptions.

Research only: no verified legal public visitor parking or complete walking route.

What the geographic evidence shows

Michigan GIS #40 at 46.508451,-90.119916 is approximately 145m from a GNIS-derived reference 46.507222,-90.120556. A highly confident online 'easy' guide instead sends users to County Road 527 near Watersmeet at 46.3142,-89.2625, about 69.2km away. Its road, walk and 'Upper Rocky Forty' claim must NOT be attached to source #40.

Why this is not a recommended visitor stop

One recent research guide explicitly cannot confirm a public approach and suspects private surrounding land. A contradictory mass-generated source claims an easy 0.6-mile walk and roadside parking but locates the falls nearly 70km away. No reliable official trailhead, county access rights or maintained walking path is established.
