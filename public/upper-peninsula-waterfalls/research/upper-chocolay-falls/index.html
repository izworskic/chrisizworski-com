Upper Chocolay Falls

A modest wooded slide on the East Branch Chocolay River. The most direct historical approach involves private property; a lawful continuous public route has not been established.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. No permission, parking, signed trail or live opening can be inferred from a natural-feature point.

What the source evidence says

The Michigan GIS feature is near 46.343362,-87.250939. A firsthand falls catalog places a small domed drop at 46.34446,-87.25166; this is geographical corroboration, not a trail route or public lot.

The access decision

Independent descriptions acknowledge that the easiest approach crosses private property. Older two-track directions and a clearing are not evidence of legal parking or a public route in 2026.
