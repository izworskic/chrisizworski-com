MARQUETTE County · East Branch Escanaba River · Geographic evidence review

Second Falls (Gwinn)

The upstream sibling of First Falls is another tiny river ripple, not a separate major waterfall outing. Its mapped position needs correction before it becomes a precise visitor stop.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. The public municipal park is documented, but this exact falls endpoint and parking-to-falls route are not certified.

The place and its source identity

Michigan GIS #177 locates Second Falls at 46.285692,-87.434130. A firsthand waterfall catalog locates the named Second Falls at 46.288870,-87.433460, about 357 metres farther north. State GIS First #176 and Second #177 lie just 78 metres apart, whereas the two firsthand fall points are about 421 metres apart along the river vicinity. The firsthand account calls Second Falls an upstream THREE-INCH ripple approached along a park trail past First Falls. Forsyth Township's adopted 2024–2029 recreation plan independently documents a rustic trail heading north from the Farquar/Metsa Tourist Park day-use area, but only describes views of 'a small falls' and does not certify Second Falls or either coordinate.

What is actually possible for visitors

There is a genuine municipal public park and official riverside trail. That is a stronger access signal than a random unmarked woods fall, but its existence does not settle a 357-m name/coordinate disagreement or prove a signed endpoint to this particular Second Falls. A firsthand account mentions a rock slab and informal fishing branches. These are not an ADA route or current approved parking-to-viewpoint geometry; no route polyline or falls-point driving directions should be generated. River rocks, dark water and seasonal park restrictions require caution.
