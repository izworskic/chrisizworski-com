Lower Montreal Falls

The lower Montreal river-mouth cascade is real, but the legal and practical way to reach it changes with roads, gates and trail status.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. Geographic coordinates are not legal parking locations, signed trailheads or current access permission.

What the source evidence supports

Michigan GIS #139 Lower Falls at 47.392581,-87.841233 matches firsthand Montreal Falls at 47.392570,-87.841140 within 7 metres. A GNIS-derived Lower Falls is approximately 198 metres away, and Upper Montreal #140 is 705 metres upstream in the GIS layer. The Keweenaw Montreal River is not the unrelated Montreal River by Interstate Falls on the Wisconsin border.

Why the public approach is unresolved

Visit Keweenaw describes rustic Fish Cove parking and an UNMAINTAINED shoreline trail. A field guide reports gates on the historical Smiths-Fisheries road since 2021, up to three in 2024, changing hike length substantially. Keweenaw Outdoor Recreation Coalition reported its proposed NEW public parking area and trail connection still unapproved in 2026, corroborated by local reporting dated March 27, 2026. This does not prove all old approaches closed; nor can proposed infrastructure be presented as currently constructed or available. Current gate state, designated existing parking rights, walking-route continuity and shoreline ownership are not independently verified.
