West Branch Falls

A succession of slate-gorge cascades on the West Branch Huron River near Skanee, not a signed state-park waterfall stop.

Research only. No verified public visitor parking or complete authorized walking route.

What we know

Michigan GIS #121 at 46.838861,-88.090452 and independent West Branch Falls at 46.83868,-88.09081 agree within approximately 34m. The historic Baraga County brochure's unnamed West Branch bridge falls is a separate named/approach claim; do not assign that bridge parking or easy difficulty to #121.

Why it is not yet a visitor stop

The independent field route describes a remote two-track by a ford, multiple river fords and a private camp on one riverbank. This does not certify present motorized road use, visitor parking, a dry public corridor, or authority to cross private property.
