Ajibikoka Falls

An isolated multi-tiered drop at the Brush Lake outlet on Sucker Creek in Ottawa National Forest country; not a maintained waterfall viewing trail.

Research only. No verified public visitor parking or complete authorized walking route.

What we know

Michigan GIS #20 at 46.329847,-89.237160 lies roughly 41m from the 2015 field point 46.329862,-89.237695. GIS river attribute calls this BRUSH LAKE; field accounts identify the outlet as Sucker Creek. This is the same natural cascade, but waterway names should be displayed carefully.

Why it is not yet a visitor stop

Upper Peninsula Travel acknowledges Ajibikoka as an Ottawa National Forest waterfall requiring compass/GPS orienteering; independent field sources explicitly describe gated FR5120, a rough old road that fades into swamp, and no official trail for the final approach. Forest status is not a signed parking permit, a public motorized right through the gate, or a safe trail.
