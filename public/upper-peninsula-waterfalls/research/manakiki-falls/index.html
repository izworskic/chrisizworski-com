Manakiki Falls

Two waterfall maps. One creek. No confirmed way in.

PUBLIC VISITOR ROUTE NOT VERIFIED. This source record is not a public parking destination, trail map or opening announcement.

What a knowledgeable local would tell you

The regional map makes Manakiki sound like a quick side trip from Maple Creek Falls. The problem is that its own two waterfall pins are more than 1,100 feet apart, despite describing the drops as just 150 feet apart. Until that conflict is resolved on a real legal approach, a forest gate is not your trailhead.

Before you even think about going

Research only: arrival not independently cleared. Research layer only. A natural waterfall coordinate is not a parking location, a public trail or an invitation to cross unverified land or gated roads.

Six things we have checked

Geographic identity and meaningful waterfall status
Michigan GIS #75 matches the USGS/GNIS-derived Manakiki location 46.6408351,-90.1119938 within about 12m. The Western Upper Peninsula regional waterfall brochure instead lists Manakiki at 46.643023,-90.113405, 273m away. The same brochure places nearby Maple Creek Falls 356m north of its Manakiki point while describing Manakiki as only 150ft upstream. Preserve all three source points independently: none is certified parking. Read supporting evidence
Land ownership at the natural point (not visitor route)
No federal FS-managed polygon, Michigan DNR state-owned parcel or state conservation easement intersects the original Manakiki GIS waterfall point. Empty results are NOT proof of private ownership; legal parcel and corridor status remain unverified. Read supporting evidence
Historic or tourism approach versus current public permission
Regional Western U.P. tourism describes a gate on Maple Creek Road, a bridge and a creekside path. Its brochure and coordinates disagree, so it does not establish current signed public gate parking or a continuous lawful footpath. USFS published-trail layer finds no mapped trail segment within 1km of the original natural point. Read supporting evidence
Legally signed road approach and waterfall parking
No present verified official signed waterfall visitor lot/turnout and continuous vehicle access to that arrival. Old gated routes, gravel-road shoulders, apartment parking and private entrances do not qualify. Read supporting evidence
Complete lawful walking trail and safe viewpoint
No USDA NFS published trail was found within 1km of the mapped feature. No current complete legal public foot corridor to the natural point was established. Read supporting evidence
2026 season, stream, ice and closure conditions
No verified current site-specific public road/gate/footing/crossing condition or signed authorization for November 1, 2026. Older trip directions must not be treated as live opening information. Read supporting evidence

What would change this recommendation

Reconcile the two tourism waterfall points with GNIS and field evidence, identify Maple Creek Road gate and bridge as legal current public arrival points, verify actual parcels and walking rights to the safe upper waterfall viewpoint. Do not use the GNIS natural point or either brochure waterfall pin as a car destination.
