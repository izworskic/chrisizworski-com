White City Falls

A small notched chute between bedrock walls on the Middle Branch Escanaba River. A private cabin and posted shorelands make this unsuitable as a public walk-to waterfall recommendation.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. No permission, parking, signed trail or live opening can be inferred from a natural-feature point.

What the source evidence says

State GIS #183 places White City Falls at 46.390063,-87.719065; a firsthand field record places the same named chute at 46.39015,-87.71900. This is a feature location, never a parking coordinate.

The access decision

A firsthand waterfall account reports gated private property at the cabin. American Whitewater's route description and an August 2024 trip report describe posted private land on both riverbanks, and technical whitewater hazards at the falls. A paddling river route is not a certified public footpath.
