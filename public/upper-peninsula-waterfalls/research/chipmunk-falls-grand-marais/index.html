Chipmunk Falls — Grand Marais

Alger County Grand Marais GIS #205 matches USGS GNIS #623209; tourism listings for Chipmunk Falls near Amasa and Wetmore refer to different locations. No official Grand Marais trailhead/permission has been found.

Research only. Full public route and parking are not proven.

What we know

Alger County Michigan GIS Chipmunk Falls #205 at 46.669583,-85.955765 matches USGS GNIS #623209 at 46.6697032,-85.9557194 within about 14m. UPtravel calls a different IRON COUNTY site Chipmunk Falls - Amasa near Net River Road and warns no direct trail. Another statewide catalog falsely suggests Wetmore/FR2262 at about 46.2917,-86.5983, more than 40km from Grand Marais natural point. Grand Marais Chamber public waterfall guide does not document this Chipmunk visitor approach. Nothing establishes exact current parcel owner, legal parking or foot route. Do not inherit another Chipmunk's roads.
