Little Falls (Bond Falls Flowage)

Little Falls is a small, intermittently submerged ripple at the inflow to Bond Falls Flowage, not another roadside Bond Falls viewpoint.

NOT CLEARED FOR AUTOMATED VISITOR NAVIGATION. A waterfall on a map is not permission to enter restricted land, drive a track or park at the geographic point.

The place and its source identity

Michigan GIS #23 records Little Falls at 46.377188,-89.099486 with waterway attribute MID ONTONAGON RIVER. A firsthand source places the fall at 46.378580,-89.099840 on INTERIOR CREEK, about 157 metres away, and describes the main inlet to Bond Falls Flowage. Michigan DNR's Ontonagon River watershed falls table names Little Falls on the MIDDLE BRANCH Ontonagon River; its historical Bond Falls Flowage angler-study map shows Little Falls and Interior Creek in the same reservoir inlet vicinity. This is an authentic named natural feature but the precise inlet/mainstem label is unresolved. A generic tourism page instead claims 'Little Falls' on the MICHIGAMME River and maps it at 46.6903,-89.2247, about 36.1 km away; do not borrow that false label or location.

What is actually possible for visitors

A firsthand report describes unmarked logging two-tracks, forks, wet and potentially impassable soft sections, then an informal bank path near the impoundment. American Whitewater acknowledges Little Falls as a brief Class II riverbed drop on the reach to Bond Falls Flowage, possibly buried under reservoir water; its downstream paddling take-out is not certified public hiking parking at Little Falls. Michigan DNR confirms the wider Bond Falls Flowage but does not provide a present approved trailhead, safe driving end point, precise flowage-shore public access rights, or continuous legal trail to the GIS #23 point.
