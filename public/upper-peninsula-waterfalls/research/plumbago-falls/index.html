Plumbago Falls

State GIS #52 at 46.621751,-88.503255 versus specialist real Plumbago Falls at 46.64970,-88.43816 differ by about 5830 metres. Historical 2008-2009 trips describe >2mi route from Preacher Park / Lake Plumbago on a gated, branching, overgrown two-track, with dead-end footpaths, swamps and possible stream wading. No official parking/trail or current land-title and motorized access records established. Unverified online catalogue claiming easy US-41 roadside Plumbago Falls at 46.5814,-88.3411 conflicts even more with both field and GIS positions. Avoid giving impossible vehicle route or invented short scenic stop. Exact state-specialist distance 5862 metres. Reviewed Nov 1 2026. All traffic, property and seasonal rights subject to current land manager confirmation.

Research only: parking and complete public waterfall route are not verified.
