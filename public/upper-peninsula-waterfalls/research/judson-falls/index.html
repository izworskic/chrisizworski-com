Judson Falls

A modest drop on the swamp-fringed Slate River above Lake Gogebic, reached historically by old logging tracks rather than an established waterfall trail.

Research only. No verified public visitor parking or complete authorized walking route.

What we know

Michigan GIS #24 at 46.389566,-89.561062 is about 64m from the independent Judson Falls field point 46.38927,-89.56034. The local Lake Gogebic recreation map labels Judson Falls in the correct vicinity; that map is location context, not trail or parking evidence.

Why it is not yet a visitor stop

The independent description follows Stage Coach Road, a series of forestry-road forks and a gated branch before an almost-mile overgrown walk. A 2023 visitor note describes logging disturbance. No public parcel/right-of-way statement, current legal vehicle access or signed trailhead can be established from that account.
