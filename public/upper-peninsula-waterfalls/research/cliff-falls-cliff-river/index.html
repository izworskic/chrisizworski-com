Cliff Falls

The waterfall is real; several maps would send you to the wrong place.

REMOTE RIVER FALLS · ROAD / OWNERSHIP NOT CERTIFIED. This geographic record is not an approved parking destination, public walking route or live opening report.

What a knowledgeable local would tell you

Cliff Falls is also called Forty Foot Falls, a broad cascade on the Cliff River in northern Marquette County. Independent field descriptions place it at the state GIS point, but one commercial roundup assigns the same name a Keweenaw Cliff Drive destination far to the north. Other accounts describe an unsigned rough forest-road approach and an informal clearing, yet no official current parking or property right is established by those accounts.

Before planning a trip

Do not use the unrelated Keweenaw coordinates or assume that an unsigned campsite/pull-off is legal public parking.
