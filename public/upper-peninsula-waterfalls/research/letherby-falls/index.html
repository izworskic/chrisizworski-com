Letherby Falls

Letherby Falls is a mapped feature of the West Branch Huron River in Baraga County. The mapped natural feature is not a parking location or a promise of public access. This research review separates what the sources actually establish from what remains unknown.

Research only. No approved parking, visitor trail or public guide.

What the evidence establishes

Michigan GIS #103 at 46.781725,-88.102977 lies about 1,636 m from the independent main Leatherby falls (46.76748,-88.10836) and about 998 m from the independent LOWER Leatherby cascades (46.77376,-88.10902). A historical field account says a map label downstream of main falls represents weak rapids/swamp, not the photographed main waterfall. Neither alias nor visitor destination is confirmed.

Rough historic Black Creek/Leatherby-road narratives apply to a different field-mapped falls site; using those directions for GIS #103 risks taking visitors to the wrong natural feature or an unauthorized track.
