Chipmunk Falls

A named rapid, not a proven destination waterfall.

WATERFALL STATURE DISPUTED · NO VERIFIED VISITOR ROUTE. This geographic record is not an approved parking destination, public walking route or live opening report.

What a knowledgeable local would tell you

This is the Iron County Chipmunk Falls on the Net River near Amasa, not the differently named Alger County site. World Waterfall Database disqualifies this as a waterfall under its own size standard and describes a drop below three feet. Its reported place-name displacement and competing directions make a saved map pin unreliable.

Before planning a trip

Do not plan a dedicated waterfall visit or follow a generic logging-road or map-pin route.
