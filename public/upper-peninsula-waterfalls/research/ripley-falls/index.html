Ripley Falls

Spring water on the edge of a working ski slope.

PUBLIC VISITOR ROUTE NOT VERIFIED. This source record is not a public parking destination, trail map or opening announcement.

What a knowledgeable local would tell you

Ripley Creek tumbles down the side of Michigan Tech's Mont Ripley. Old field descriptions begin beside what is now private apartment parking and pass near residential yards. The ski area is publicly operated for winter sports, but that is not evidence of permitted year-round waterfall hiking.

Before you even think about going

Six things we have checked

Geographic identity and meaningful waterfall status
Michigan GIS #129 is about 117m from the independently located 47.13019,-88.55523 rippling cascade on Ripley Creek near Hancock. Field observers describe multiple seasonal short drops on the side of Mont Ripley ski hill; flow can disappear outside snowmelt. Read supporting evidence
Land ownership at the natural point (not visitor route)
Official Michigan Technological University records establish that Mont Ripley ski area is owned and operated by Michigan Tech for paid winter recreation, not that the exact waterfall location or the old Ripley School apartment lot is open to casual public hiking. Federal and state parcel point queries do not resolve the exact private/university boundary. Read supporting evidence
Historic or tourism approach versus current public permission
An old 2008 field report directs drivers to the old Ripley School, now private apartments, and then past neighboring yards to informal ski-hill access tracks, explicitly warning against trespass. The university promotes ski lifts and skiing/snowboarding tickets; no official Mont Ripley public waterfall hiking permit, parking lot or creekside public trail was found. Do not send waterfall visitors to the residential lot or along residential property. Read supporting evidence
Legally signed road approach and waterfall parking
No present verified official signed waterfall visitor lot/turnout and continuous vehicle access to that arrival. Old gated routes, gravel-road shoulders, apartment parking and private entrances do not qualify. Read supporting evidence
Complete lawful walking trail and safe viewpoint
No USDA NFS published trail was found within 1km of the mapped feature. No current complete legal public foot corridor to the natural point was established. Read supporting evidence
2026 season, stream, ice and closure conditions
No verified current site-specific public road/gate/footing/crossing condition or signed authorization for November 1, 2026. Older trip directions must not be treated as live opening information. Read supporting evidence

What would change this recommendation

Confirm the exact ski-hill/neighbor parcel boundaries, official MTU permission for out-of-season pedestrian access, authorized visitor parking and a signed foot route. Withhold all street/old-school parking and ski slope off-season walking directions until the owner publishes guidance.
