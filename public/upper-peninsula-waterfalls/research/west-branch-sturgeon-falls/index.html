Houghton County · West Branch Sturgeon River · Field research only

West Branch Sturgeon Falls

The falls are real. The route is a wilderness undertaking.

ADVANCED BACKCOUNTRY — NO VERIFIED ROUTE. This source record is not a public parking destination, trail map or opening announcement.

What a knowledgeable local would tell you

Visit Keweenaw documents a chain of secluded cascades and a route along an old forest road. Its last stage is not a marked hiking trail. The tourism waterfall coordinate is also more than a quarter-mile from the Michigan state GIS point. The river landscape is an authentic place, but it is not a destination for ordinary turn-by-turn navigation.

Before you even think about going

Advanced wilderness: use official tourism narrative only, no route pin. Research layer only. A natural waterfall coordinate is not a parking location, a public trail or an invitation to cross unverified land or gated roads.

Six things we have checked

Geographic identity and meaningful waterfall status
Michigan GIS #85 lies roughly 526m east of Visit Keweenaw's independently documented falls near 46.7217,-88.7990. An independent 2008 field guide also puts the cascades at 46.72165,-88.799. A GNIS-derived coordinate 46.7224356,-88.7884695 sits about 283m east of the Michigan GIS point. These are substantially different points in a chain of drops; treat the tourism/field waterfall coordinates as field descriptions, not a validated drive-to trailhead. Read supporting evidence
Land ownership at the natural point (not visitor route)
USDA FS PADUS polygon OBJECTID 120 (2026-10-30) intersects BOTH the Michigan GIS point and Visit Keweenaw's independent waterfall coordinate. The natural falls are within Ottawa National Forest; no land-title proof yet covers every road, gate, parking area or riverbank connection used to reach them. Read supporting evidence
Historic or tourism approach versus current public permission
Visit Keweenaw publishes an advanced backcountry route from Newberry Road via gated Forest Road 1755 (roughly 0.8mi along the old track) before an unmarked descent, and cautions that winter plowing may stop early and private driveways must not be blocked. The official published NFS trails layer finds NO trail within 1km of the Michigan GIS feature, consistent with an unsigned off-trail river approach. Visiting the forest is not equivalent to a safely navigable signed waterfall trail; parking, vehicle and final route status still need authoritative current confirmation. Read supporting evidence
Legally signed road approach and waterfall parking
No present verified official signed waterfall visitor lot/turnout and continuous vehicle access to that arrival. Old gated routes, gravel-road shoulders, apartment parking and private entrances do not qualify. Read supporting evidence
Complete lawful walking trail and safe viewpoint
No USDA NFS published trail was found within 1km of the mapped feature. No current complete legal public foot corridor to the natural point was established. Read supporting evidence
2026 season, stream, ice and closure conditions
No verified current site-specific public road/gate/footing/crossing condition or signed authorization for November 1, 2026. Older trip directions must not be treated as live opening information. Read supporting evidence

What would change this recommendation

Verify current Forest Service MVUM and authority for Newberry Road/FR1755, official public signed parking placement and whether a sanctioned 2026 foot corridor exists to a safe viewpoint. Reconcile the 526m site-offset so any visitor planning uses a river-feature location, never a navigable trailhead pin. Until then, link to the published Visit Keweenaw field narrative but withhold an auto-guided visitor route.
