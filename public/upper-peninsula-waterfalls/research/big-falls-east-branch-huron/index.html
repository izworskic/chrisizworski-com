Big Falls (East Branch Huron)

Big Falls is a distinct remote East Branch Huron River cascade, not the developed Big Eric's Falls and not a maintained visitor hike.

Why the public approach is unresolved

Firsthand records describe either a dangerous seasonal West Branch Huron River vehicle ford plus deep wet forest roads, or a several-mile rough creekbed and bank hike from historical logging routes. A second source specifically warns that private property lies around the East Branch falls. A secondary directory's ATV/ford advice is not a lawful authorization to drive a water crossing, park or traverse private land.
