What makes this visit worthwhile?

A lower-effort entry to the park's three-waterfall sequence before Manido and Manabezho. An upstream cascade with a recognized viewpoint among the old-growth Presque Isle River falls.

Trail and access reality

Nawadaha has a DNR-documented accessible observation platform on the West River Trail; other Presque Isle River paths are rough and stair-heavy.

One accessible overlook does not make the whole river loop accessible. Check M-519 and park road status. A known visitor route does not guarantee that a bridge, road or overlook is accessible on your visit date.

Seasonal waterfall conditions

River volume and the landscape can change after rainfall or snowmelt. Autumn offers hardwood colors; ice can make winter approaches hazardous. This page does not provide measured real-time waterfall flow or a current parking report.
