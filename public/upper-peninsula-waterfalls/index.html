Overlooked Falls

Plan your arrival: Pick Overlooked for the short stop from the Little Carp River parking area, then decide if you want to continue to Greenstone on the longer two-mile round-trip route.

Plan this stop

Access: Public Michigan DNR state-park walking route from the Little Carp River Road lot. No water-flow or current road-opening data is supplied here.

Make the trip worth it

Three different waterfall days

Low effort: Wagner Falls, Bond Falls or Tahquamenon overlooks—but first match your actual starting area. Hiking: Miners Falls and Chapel Falls have very different distances and parking constraints. Wilderness: Spray Falls needs a long hike or boat view.

Which season?

Snowmelt can produce high flow in spring, fall adds hardwood color, and winter creates ice formations. None of those seasonal patterns establish current access, ice safety or waterfall volume. Check local park conditions before setting out.
