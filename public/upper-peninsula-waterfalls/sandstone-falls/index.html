What makes this visit worthwhile?

Choose Sandstone for intimate rock-and-current detail photographs. Water threads across irregular sandstone ledges instead of one single plunge.

Trail and access reality

About ¼ mile from signed parking, including steps and uneven sections near river overlooks.

Wet rock near the river must not be treated as a public walkway. A known visitor route does not guarantee that a bridge, road or overlook is accessible on your visit date.

Construction and access checks

May 15, 2026 Forest Service notice lists Sandstone Falls CLOSED due to viewing-platform and stair replacement. Do not plan this stop until the agency posts reopening.

The project may continue through fall 2027. Before choosing this stop, read the Forest Service status notice and obey signs and barriers. Reopening is not assumed.

Seasonal waterfall conditions

River volume and the landscape can change after rainfall or snowmelt. Autumn offers hardwood colors; ice can make winter approaches hazardous. This page does not provide measured real-time waterfall flow or a current parking report.
