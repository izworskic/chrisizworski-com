What makes this visit worthwhile?

This is a hike-first waterfall day, not a scenic-byway roadside stop. A remote-feeling Baltimore River curtain viewed from a suspension bridge.

Trail and access reality

Approximately 1.1–1.3 miles one way following the NCT to a Baltimore River suspension bridge; roots, boardwalk and steeper grades near the river.

The eastern side of the Baltimore River is not a parking access point. Do not plan to scramble behind the waterfall. A known visitor route does not guarantee that a bridge, road or overlook is accessible on your visit date.

Seasonal waterfall conditions

River volume and the landscape can change after rainfall or snowmelt. Autumn offers hardwood colors; ice can make winter approaches hazardous. This page does not provide measured real-time waterfall flow or a current parking report.

Verified research evidence

Evidence reviewed October 26, 2026; source dates may be older. Geography, legal visitor access, parking, trail and manager are separate checks.
