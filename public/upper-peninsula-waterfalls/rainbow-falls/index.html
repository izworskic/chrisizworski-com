What makes this visit worthwhile?

The photo-driven finale to the five signed Black River byway falls. Spray and sunlight can turn the lower Black River into a rainbow-lit cascade.

Trail and access reality

About ½ mile by the signed west-bank approach with many stairs. The suspension bridge / east-bank walk from Black River Harbor is a separate, longer route.

Rainbows are not guaranteed; the return climb is significant and road access changes seasonally. A known visitor route does not guarantee that a bridge, road or overlook is accessible on your visit date.

Construction and access checks

May 15, 2026 Forest Service notice listed Rainbow Falls open, with later trail and platform closures planned. Do not presume it is still fully open.

The project may continue through fall 2027. Before choosing this stop, read the Forest Service status notice and obey signs and barriers. Reopening is not assumed.

Seasonal waterfall conditions

River volume and the landscape can change after rainfall or snowmelt. Autumn offers hardwood colors; ice can make winter approaches hazardous. This page does not provide measured real-time waterfall flow or a current parking report.
