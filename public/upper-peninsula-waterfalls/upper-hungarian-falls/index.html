What makes this waterfall worth your time?

Pick the upper falls when you want the shortest officially described walk and a look at the Keweenaw Fault landscape without committing to the steep lower gorge.

This is the shorter, easier access into the Hungarian Falls complex. The historic dam/pond is not a designated swimming facility; water and edges require caution.

This waterfall is one stop within a larger Michigan landscape, not a substitute for an up-to-date land-manager map. Decide on your route from the published visitor entry point, and always allow for weather, construction or trail conditions to change it.

The access decision: road, parking and trail

Parking: Shoulder of Golf Course Road near the Hungarian Falls Nature Area sign, about 0.5 mi uphill from 6th Street in Tamarack City. KLT's parking reference: 47.173159, -88.444743. Never drive the old gated woods road.

Trail: From the Golf Course Road shoulder near the Hungarian Falls sign, follow the old gated access road on foot approximately 400 yards toward the pond and upper falls. This is a rustic route, not a paved accessible path.

What the maps cannot certify: The upper waterfall is on the KLT preserve; much of the lower gorge is on Michigan state land, with private parcels nearby. Do not assume all visible paths are public.

Do not use the falls coordinate to navigate your vehicle. Keep to public trail corridors, respect private properties and obey posted barricades. If parking is full or a park is gated, make a different plan rather than using a road shoulder without an authorized stopping area.

Seasonal differences and safety

Spring snowmelt, after sustained rain, and hardwood color; no live water-flow measurement. These are patterns, not predictions for a particular day. Waterfall flow is highly sensitive to recent precipitation and thaw conditions; the USGS hydrography point is not a live streamgage or flow prediction.

Wet roots, loose rock, slippery ledges, ice and unprotected cliffs can make a short distance substantially more difficult. Winter driving access is a separate decision from legal trail access, and state park or roadside park vehicle gates may close seasonally.
