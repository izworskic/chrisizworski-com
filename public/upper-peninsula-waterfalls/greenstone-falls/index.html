Is Greenstone Falls right for your day?

Pick Greenstone for a short backcountry-feeling forest hike and a second possible waterfall stop at Overlooked Falls. This is not a drive-up waterfall.

Greenstone is a small, shaded forest cascade, not a tall vertical drop. The trailhead also serves nearby Overlooked Falls; do not confuse the first bridge cascade with the Greenstone destination farther downstream.

Check the park's South Boundary Road and Little Carp River Road conditions. The river crossing, mud and roots can change the difficulty after storms.

Parking is a different location from the waterfall

Park only at the marked Little Carp River Trailhead at the end of Little Carp River Road, reached from South Boundary Road in Porcupine Mountains Wilderness State Park. The falls coordinate is not the lot.

Trail and what to expect on foot

From the signed Little Carp River Road trailhead, cross the first bridge at Overlooked Falls and continue along the Little Carp River Trail toward Greenstone. Plan roughly a 2-mile out-and-back excursion; use the park trail map for current alignment and do not expect a paved path.

Public park trail on state-managed land, reached from Little Carp River Road off South Boundary Road. Road and trail access may change seasonally; no year-round vehicle access is implied.

Do not use waterfall height or a straight-line map distance to estimate walking effort. Follow the posted corridor and turn back rather than attempting an improvised shortcut through dense vegetation, river beds or inaccessible structures.

Season, river character and hydrology

Spring rain and snowmelt strengthen this relatively low cascade; autumn adds hardwood color.
