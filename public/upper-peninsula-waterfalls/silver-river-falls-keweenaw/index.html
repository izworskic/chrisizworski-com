Silver River Falls (Keweenaw)

Just below M-26, the Silver River steps across exposed conglomerate and basalt inside a protected public nature preserve. The protected riverbank shows the geology of the Keweenaw more clearly than a tall waterfall. Consider the visit an intimate geology stop, not a drive-up platform.

Documented public visitor route; current conditions unverified. The land manager, official visitor approach and parking are independently sourced. Road, trail, bridge and snow conditions are not monitored live. Current opening and road conditions are unverified. The M-26 crossing is a particular safety decision.

Public access
Michigan Nature Association confirms that Mariner's Preserve was donated to protect permanent public access to the waterfall. The preserve describes roadside parking and a short downstream walk. Neither private driveways nor unsurveyed streambank scrambles are authorized approaches.

What makes this the right stop?

Best for visitors who enjoy reading the bedrock and a short river walk, but only when the roadside crossing and footing are suitable. The river slips down exposed conglomerate and basalt, showing the tilt of the Keweenaw bedrock. This is not the Silver River in Baraga County.

Spring snowmelt, early summer after rain, and fall color show the rock ledges best. Water may be modest during dry spells. These are seasonal patterns, not a report of today's waterfall flow. A dry day can make a modest falls quieter, and ice can turn a short warm-weather approach into a much more demanding trip.

Follow the documented route

From the M-26 bridge area, a short informal path follows the river downstream into Mariner's Preserve. The roadside pull-off and footpath can be on opposite sides of M-26: crossing requires extreme care and may not be appropriate in busy, snowy or low-visibility conditions.

A geographic waterfall coordinate is not a safe navigation destination. Do not cut through adjacent private property, use an unmarked streambank scramble, or park along an active highway lane. Keep to established public visitor approaches and respect any temporary closure signs.

Is there an official parking lot at the waterfall?

The published approach names a small roadside pull-off by the M-26 bridge, not a large developed parking lot. Stay clear of traffic and don't mistake the waterfall coordinate for the pull-off.

Is the path accessible?

No continuous wheelchair-accessible or maintained step-free route is verified. The path is short but uneven, and the road crossing is a genuine safety decision.

Why is it called Mariner's Preserve?

The Michigan Nature Association says the site was donated as a memorial and to protect permanent public access. Its exposed basalt and conglomerate help tell the story of the Keweenaw's tilted geology.

How the river fits the map

For USGS NHDPlusV2, the mapped stream ID is 11936911 and its reach code is 04020103000478. The Michigan GIS position and the USGS 2020 location differ by approximately 12 meters.
