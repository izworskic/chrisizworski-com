Know the preserve and the M-26 crossing

The Michigan Nature Association expressly protected Mariner's Preserve for continued public access to Silver River Falls. It identifies roadside parking and trails. The preserved land is the visitor destination, not the highway bridge or a former culvert.

Some published directions put parking and the river trail on opposite sides of M-26. No protected pedestrian crossing has been established in our research. Park lawfully; only cross where lawful and safe, and skip the trail if that isn't possible. The old bridge was replaced in 2023; do not follow outdated instructions showing a demolished historic structure.

Choose this waterfall for the right experience

Choose this for Keweenaw geology; do not visit if a safe lawful route from a parking area to the trail is unavailable.

The historic M-26 bridge was replaced in 2023. Older online instructions depicting the old span or detour are outdated.

For a short, managed visitor experience, compare this stop to Haven Falls in its county park and Eagle River Falls at the MDOT bridge park. For a natural geology walk, Silver River Falls in the nature preserve requires more attention to road-side parking and walking surfaces.

What to check before you drive

Spring snowmelt or after rain to see water gliding across conglomerate and basalt ledges. None of these descriptions measure the present water volume. A river's visual intensity changes with rain, spring melt, drought and freezing weather. The Michigan GIS waterfall pin and USGS hydrographic identifiers do not report real-time water levels.

Access conditions: The historic M-26 bridge was replaced in 2023. Older online instructions depicting the old span or detour are outdated. Check the responsible authority and actual gates, barriers and signs for the day you travel. The Keweenaw can develop snow or ice early, and a published public walking route is not proof of current safe footing. Avoid river edges, unstable rock, dam structures and closed bridges.

Why this matters: A location can be known on a map long before its legal public visitor route is clear. This page cites parking, path, manager and waterfall coordinates separately so you can decide whether the visit makes sense before committing to the drive.
