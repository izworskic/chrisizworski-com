Mosquito Falls

Three low sandstone drops along a quiet Mosquito River forest trail; the hike is more substantial than the waterfall's height.

Published public visitor route; current opening and road conditions are unverified. Evidence reviewed October 26, 2026. Follow park closure notices first.

Before leaving the car

Land manager
National Park Service · Pictured Rocks National Lakeshore
Public visitor access
Official Pictured Rocks visitor trail. No pets on Chapel/Mosquito trails. No drinking water at trailhead; parking is limited and access road is rough.
Where to park
Chapel/Mosquito trailhead at the end of five-mile Chapel Road from H-58; heavily rutted road, limited signed parking; do not park outside permitted areas.
Walking trail and terrain
NPS lists a 2.0-mile round trip: walk 0.3 mile toward Mosquito Beach from Chapel/Mosquito trailhead, then take the signed 0.7-mile side trail to Mosquito Falls. Forest tread may be muddy and rough.
Visit allowance
75–150 min; planning estimate, not measured travel time
Season and river
Spring forest flowers and fall color, with varying seasonal streamflow.

Waterfall, not parking

Map marker locates the falls; it does not locate the trailhead, public road or surveyed parking. Location cross-checked against geographic source.

Official parking and trailhead source

Why choose this waterfall?

Three low sandstone drops along a quiet Mosquito River forest trail; the hike is more substantial than the waterfall's height. Mosquito Falls is not Mosquito Beach. Take the waterfall side trail instead of continuing directly to the lake.

For a visit focused on the scenery, consider the signed approach and actual walking requirements before adding another stop. The nearest waterfall point on a map is not necessarily the closest public visitor entrance.

How to plan a worthwhile visit

NPS lists a 2.0-mile round trip: walk 0.3 mile toward Mosquito Beach from Chapel/Mosquito trailhead, then take the signed 0.7-mile side trail to Mosquito Falls. Forest tread may be muddy and rough.

Download the NPS Chapel/Mosquito trail map before leaving cell coverage. Trail and road status are not live-confirmed.

Check posted signs, permit requirements and current park closures; this guide reports published routes, not a live trail-open feed. Never trespass or bypass closure barriers.

Seasonal decisions

Wet weather improves flow but can also worsen mud; autumn adds hardwood canopy color.

Spring forest flowers and fall color, with varying seasonal streamflow. are typical visual conditions rather than a forecast. Snowmelt and rain can change water volume and trail footing. No mapped hydrography feature confirms current parking conditions, ice safety or waterfall discharge.
