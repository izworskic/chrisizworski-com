Route-specific decisions

Beaver Island Boat Company publishes a date-by-date 2026 calendar with one to four departures depending on the day. There is no safe single 'daily ferry time'.

All vehicles and trailers require reservations; passenger reservations are generally not required.

Vehicle quote below covers only a standard vehicle under 7 ft 6 in high and no more than 16 ft long. Oversize cars, trailers, bikes and kayaks have separate published rates.

What is live and what is not?

These tools calculate against operator-published 2026 reference data and your inputs. The date selection does not retrieve ticket inventory or prove a ferry will sail. All current departures, booking availability and cancellations must be checked through the official operator links above. The planner deliberately withholds a total when rates are incomplete.
