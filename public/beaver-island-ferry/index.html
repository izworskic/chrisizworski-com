Questions worth asking before booking

Can I reserve seats for the family?

The operator says passenger travel is first-come, first-served rather than reserved. Vehicle transport does require separate arrangements.

How early should I check in?

The passenger FAQ asks for at least an hour. Vehicle reservations require at least 90 minutes, with additional time for some racks or freight situations. The timeline adjusts its allowance when a vehicle is selected.
