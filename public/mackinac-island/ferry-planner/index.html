Kids and strollers

2026 standard round-trip fare: Shepler's 5–12 $27, under 5 free with required ticket. Arnold Fast 5–12 $25; Arnold Classic fare pages conflict ($0 or $19 for ages 5–12). Arnold’s age-four wording also conflicts between pages; the calculator shows a price range rather than assuming free travel. Ages 13+ pay adult fare. Passenger strollers travel free, but adults must hold children during the crossing.

Military, groups and special discounts

Both operators publish military round-trip rates of $24 adults and $16 children 5–12, purchased in person with military ID. Shepler's extends its stated benefit to qualified spouses and household dependents. Shepler's lists group discounts for 20+; Arnold offers group quotes for schools, reunions and tours, including a wedding-party offer with complimentary bride-and-groom ferry tickets and dockside parking. Shepler's group rates ↗ · Arnold group offers ↗. Do not assume a general senior or AAA fare without a published rate.

Bikes, e-bikes and pets

Standard bikes cost $21 round trip on Shepler's and $19 on Arnold. Bike attachments and tandems use different fares. E-bikes require special Island authorization and cannot be booked as standard bikes. Shepler's carries leashed pets free; check Arnold's current pet-handling rules before departure.

Service and weather

Published 2026 Fast Ferry service: April 21–October 31. Arnold Classic ran May 23–September 6. The crossing is roughly 20 minutes on fast boats and 30–40 minutes on Classic. Wind, waves and fog can alter operations: a forecast is not a cancellation notice. NWS Straits marine forecast ↗.

At the dock

What to expect

Ferries run from both Mackinaw City and St. Ignace, and the crossing itself is short. Park, check in and give yourself a buffer: summer weekends and event days are busy.

Luggage and bikes can come with you; ask your ferry line how it’s handled. If you’re staying on the Island, your hotel may have porters meeting the boats.

Good questions

Before you go

Mackinaw City or St. Ignace, which is better?

Usually the one on your side of the bridge. Your starting city and the departure times can change that, so compare both.

Do I need to pick an exact return ferry for an overnight trip?

No. Unless you have a deadline, keep the return flexible; the planner shows the options for your return day.

Does the planner use live traffic?

No. Drive times are planning estimates with a buffer added, not live traffic.
