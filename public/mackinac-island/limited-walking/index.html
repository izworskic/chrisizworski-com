Mackinac Island with less walking: plan around the terrain before it plans the day for you

Mackinac is car-free, but that does not mean every useful trip requires long or steep walking. The official accessibility guidance describes the 8.2-mile perimeter and downtown as mostly flat while interior attractions sit on hills and bluffs. A better plan chooses where walking creates value and where horse-drawn transportation or a flatter route makes more sense.

Common questions

Before you lock the plan

Is Mackinac Island flat?

The 8.2-mile perimeter and downtown are mostly flat, but interior attractions sit on hills and bluffs. That distinction should shape the itinerary.

Can I visit Fort Mackinac without walking the steep south approach?

Official accessibility guidance identifies the north side as the more accessible entrance and notes that a horse-drawn taxi can take visitors there. Confirm current arrangements before the visit.

Are wheelchairs or mobility scooters possible on Mackinac Island?

Yes, the Tourism Bureau lists wheelchair and mobility-scooter options and accessible ferry parking, but rental inventory and individual property access should be checked directly.
