Can I day-trip Mackinac Island from Grand Rapids?

It is possible with an early start, but the live planner should confirm that the drive and check-in still leave enough usable Island hours and a sensible return.

Which ferry port should I use from Grand Rapids?

Mackinaw City is the natural Lower Peninsula baseline. The planner still evaluates St. Ignace against the actual schedule rather than assuming.

How do I avoid wasting time at the ferry dock?

Plan the mainland drive and ferry as one chain. The engine includes road and check-in buffers so it can prefer a departure that is reachable without excessive idle time.
