Beyond the Island: Mackinaw City, St. Ignace and the Straits

The ferry towns have their own reasons to linger: a reconstructed 18th-century fort and a lighthouse facing the bridge at Mackinaw City, Ojibwa history in St. Ignace, dark skies at the Headlands and the five-mile Mackinac Bridge in between. Add them on the way in or out, and keep the Island days for the Island.

Mackinaw City

The south side: forts, lighthouses and dark skies

On a history trip, Colonial Michilimackinac pairs naturally with the Mackinaw City ferries, with Old Mackinac Point Lighthouse close by, looking out at the bridge. On a clear night, the Headlands International Dark Sky Park just west of town is a remarkable place to watch the stars. Save these for the evening before or after your Island days.

St. Ignace

The north side: the Upper Peninsula way in

If you’re coming from the Upper Peninsula, St. Ignace is usually the easier ferry town. Its waterfront and the Museum of Ojibwa Culture make a good hour or two when your route already passes through.

Good questions

Before you go

Should I visit Mackinaw City and Mackinac Island on the same day?

It can work when a short stop fits before your ferry or after you return. Don’t give up your best Island hours for it; on an overnight trip, a mainland evening is often the better slot.

Is St. Ignace better than Mackinaw City?

Neither is better in general. Leave from the side of the bridge you’re arriving on, then compare ferry times: sometimes a better departure is worth the extra drive.

Can the planner build a broader Straits trip?

Yes. Tell My Trip you’d like to see the region, and a multi-day plan puts mainland stops on your arrival and departure days.
