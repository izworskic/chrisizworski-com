    Good to know before you go

    • Plan on five or six hours on the Island. That covers Fort Mackinac, Arch Rock or the bike loop, lunch and Main Street without rushing. If the drive is more than about four hours each way, a night on the Island makes the trip much better.
    • Use the dock on your side of the Mackinac Bridge. That’s Mackinaw City if you’re coming from the Lower Peninsula, St. Ignace from the Upper Peninsula. Shepler’s and Arnold run from both docks, and published 2026 tickets aren’t tied to a day or departure.
    • The shore loop is a flat 8.2 miles with no cars. M-185 takes most people about two hours with stops. You can rent bikes near the docks or bring your own on the ferry for a fee. The roads through the middle of the Island are much hillier.
    • Don’t plan on the last boat back. It’s a hard limit, so this planner puts you on an earlier boat and shows the last one as your backstop.
