Common questions

Before you lock the plan

Can I do Mackinac Island as a day trip from Traverse City?

Yes for many travelers. The key is leaving early enough to create a useful Island arrival and a comfortable return, which the live planner evaluates from your actual departure time.

Should I ferry from Mackinaw City or St. Ignace from Traverse City?

Mackinaw City is normally the cleaner Lower Peninsula approach. The planner still compares St. Ignace rather than hard-coding that assumption.

Is one night worth it from Traverse City?

Yes when you value dinner, sunset and the quieter Island after day-trippers leave. One night produces an arrival day and a departure day; two nights adds a true full Island day.
