Why Lake Michigan temperatures can change abruptly

A stretch of sunny weather may warm water near the surface, but persistent wind can redistribute it. Along an exposed coast, wind can move surface water away and allow colder water from depth to rise near shore, a process known as upwelling. A lakewide daily average often misses what swimmers actually experience at that moment.

What the graph measures

The 30-day line is a whole-lake satellite-derived surface average, not a location forecast. It helps show seasonal warming and cooling; always check a recent local observation if you need a particular beach reading.

When the lake is cold

Cold water matters even when the air is hot. Keep temperature interpretation separate from official beach warnings, dangerous currents, wave conditions and personal swimming ability. ShoreFeel never issues an all-clear.
