    The Seaway is usually open from late March to early January.

    Where to stand

    Two public riverbanks, one channel.

    Downbound ships come from Lake Ontario past Clayton first, then thread the American Narrows past Alexandria Bay. Upbound ships pass in the opposite order. Both parks below are public; check local parking rules and hours.

    Alexandria Bay

    Scenic View Park

    A small village park on the river with a view across to Heart Island and Boldt Castle. Ships pass through the American Narrows off the village.

    How the window works

    Tested before it was shown to you.

    We followed 454 ship passages through the Thousand Islands across 36 days of the 2025 season, using public-domain AIS records from NOAA. One third of the days set the typical travel time from each point on the channel, a second third set how wide each window needed to be, and the last third tested the result on passages the model had never seen. Only the combinations that kept at least three in four ships inside their window are published.

    River questions

    Thousand Islands ship-watching questions

    Is there a ship schedule for the Thousand Islands?

    No. Freighters move on cargo, weather and lock traffic, not a timetable. This page estimates when a ship that is already moving along the channel should reach Alexandria Bay or Clayton, from its latest public AIS report and the typical pace of 2025 passages. The official Seaway order of turn lists ships preparing to enter each lock.

    How accurate are the windows?

    On days held back from the 2025 model, 85% of ships passed Alexandria Bay inside a window like the one shown when it was up to two hours away. The windows are wider further out, and a ship can stop, anchor or slow for traffic. Treat a window as a good time to be on the riverbank, not a guarantee.

    Where can I watch ships pass Boldt Castle?

    Scenic View Park in Alexandria Bay is a public village park with a view across to Heart Island and Boldt Castle; the Alexandria Bay Chamber lists it among public facilities. Ships use the American Narrows channel past the village.

    Why is there no window for a ship I can see on a map?

    A window is shown only when the ship's latest report is recent, it is moving along the channel and the time to your viewpoint falls in a range we tested. Anchored ships, tour boats, very old reports and ships more than six hours away are left out on purpose.

    When are freighters on the St. Lawrence?

    The Seaway usually runs from late March to early January. The season box above shows this year's official dates when they are published. In winter, no freighters pass the Thousand Islands.
