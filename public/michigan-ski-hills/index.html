Before you commit to a ski day

Three checks before you buy or drive

1 · Is the hill operating?

The NWS does not report open lifts. Use each hill’s official mountain report, confirm the report date, then check the date you want to ski.

2 · What actually fell?

Dated NWS reports, modeled snowpack and resort-reported base depth are different observations. None guarantees groomed terrain.

3 · Is the trip practical?

Check current tickets and rentals directly with the operator and consult MDOT MiDrive. A snow forecast is not a lift-opening announcement.

Why the lake-effect angle matters

Wind direction, shoreline orientation, lake temperature and terrain can make nearby ski resorts experience radically different snowfall. The index distinguishes an NWS active warning, a nearby observer’s report, and climatologically favorable wind.
