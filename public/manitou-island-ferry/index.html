Questions worth asking before booking

Is the entire island closed in 2026?

NPS describes limited access and specific closures, including docks, villages and potable water; South Manitou has additional facility closures. The regular ferry does not operate. Consult the current NPS notice for exact boundaries.

Can I book the 2027 ferry now?

This planner has no verified 2027 ferry timetable or fares. NPS’s expected dock reopening is not an operator restart confirmation.

Is Manitoulin the same place?

No. Ontario’s Manitoulin Island is served by Chi-Cheemaun. Michigan’s North and South Manitou Islands are part of Sleeping Bear Dunes National Lakeshore.
