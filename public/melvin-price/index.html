Why the map and lock feed are separate

AIS is excellent for vessel identity and position, but can be delayed or incomplete. LPMS is the Corps' lock-operations record. Melvin Price Live uses each source for what it actually knows instead of forcing them into a false match.

What You're Watching

A Mississippi tow is not the same thing as a tugboat

On the inland rivers, the working vessel is usually called a towboat or pushboat even though it pushes most of its barges ahead. Large line-haul tows can be assembled several barges wide and several long. The lock's 1,200-foot main chamber is built around that scale of commerce.

Can we tell what a tow is carrying?

Sometimes the barge type is visually recognizable, but the public near-real-time LPMS feed intentionally does not publish privileged company or commodity detail for a specific movement. This tool therefore never labels an active tow as carrying grain, petroleum, chemicals or another cargo unless a separate authoritative source confirms it.

Why Melvin Price matters

Melvin Price replaced the old Locks and Dam 26 and sits at an important transition in the Upper Mississippi navigation system near the St. Louis confluence. USACE reported 45,857,531 tons and 4,797 lockages here in 2024.

Visitor logic

The best time to visit is not simply “when the museum is open.” A useful window is when active or pending lock traffic overlaps with decent outdoor conditions and, ideally, a scheduled tour. The live outlook at the top combines those pieces and lowers confidence when an upstream source is stale or unavailable.

Visiting Melvin Price & the National Great Rivers Museum

The National Great Rivers Museum is immediately adjacent to Melvin Price Locks and Dam at #2 Locks and Dam Way, East Alton, Illinois. USACE lists free admission, daily 9:00 AM–5:00 PM hours and regular public lock tours. The museum is ADA accessible.

Lock operations are industrial and security-sensitive. Use public visitor areas only and follow posted Corps instructions. The live tool is for trip planning and education, not navigation or operational decision-making.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a live Melvin Price lock schedule?

USACE publishes near-real-time lock status and traffic through LPMS rather than a simple tourist schedule. This page turns those operational counts into a visitor view and pairs them with live AIS.

Can I see barge traffic before I drive there?

Yes. Check the official pending-arrival and locking counts at the top, then use the AIS map to inspect AIS-equipped vessels around the approaches. Neither source alone sees everything.

How long is the main chamber?

The main lock chamber is 1,200 feet long and 110 feet wide. The auxiliary chamber is 600 feet long and 110 feet wide.

Are lock tours free?

Yes. USACE currently lists free public walk-in tours at 10:00 AM, 1:00 PM and 3:00 PM under the normal schedule. Same-day access can change.

Does this page identify what each tow is carrying?

No. Public LPMS near-real-time data omits privileged company and commodity detail. The tool does not guess cargo from vessel appearance or route.
