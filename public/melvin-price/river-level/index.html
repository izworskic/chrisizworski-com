River level questions

Is this the Mississippi River level at Melvin Price?

Yes. The live stage is the Corps' Melvin Price tailwater series, not a distant substitute gauge.

Why can stage and flow change differently?

River stage is water-surface elevation at the gauge. Flow is discharge. They are related but can respond differently to upstream water movement and control structures.
