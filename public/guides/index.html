Practical reference material on Great Lakes travel, AI operations, and Zone 6a Michigan gardening.

A guide here earns its place by being specific enough to be actually useful. General advice is easy to find. These try to go further, into the particular conditions of Zone 6a Michigan gardening, or the specific decision points that come up when deploying AI in an operational environment. The operations guides are written for people who are responsible for making decisions, not for an academic audience. The gardening guides are written from direct experience at Freighter View Farms on Saginaw Bay, not from general horticultural sources. The project guide connects that experience to the Michigan gardening cluster.

They are built from years of growing and seed saving at Freighter View Farms, not from general horticultural sources. The goal is information specific enough to actually change what you do in the garden.
