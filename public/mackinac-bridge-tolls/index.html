How much does it cost to cross the Mackinac Bridge?

The Mackinac Bridge toll is $4 one way, or $8 round trip, for a standard two-axle passenger car in 2026. Passenger vehicles are $2 per axle. Motorhomes and other vehicles outside the passenger classification are $5 per axle. The Mackinac Bridge Authority makes the final vehicle classification.

Passenger vs. “all other” vehicles

Passenger rate$2 per axle

Passenger cars, motorcycles, station wagons, SUVs, pickup trucks, school buses, and qualifying two-axle four-tire vans.

All other rate$5 per axle

Vehicles outside passenger characteristics, including tractor trailers, buses, motorhomes, and step or cube vans.

How towing is charged

The official rule classifies the entire combination by the lead vehicle. Anything towed is normally charged the lead vehicle's per-axle rate. That means a passenger vehicle towing a two-axle trailer is usually four total axles at $2 per axle, or $8.

The motorhome-towing-auto exception

A motorhome is charged $5 for each of its axles. If it tows a passenger auto, the auto is charged at the passenger-auto rate of $2 per axle. Select the exception in the calculator only for that specific combination.

Accepted payment methods

The Bridge Authority currently accepts:

  • Cash
  • Credit and debit cards
  • Apple Pay
  • Google Pay
  • MacPass prepaid accounts

Credit/debit fee: the Authority currently applies a 2.3% fee to credit and debit card transactions. Its fare page says cash, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and MacPass are quicker than credit/debit cards.

Common questions

Mackinac Bridge toll questions

Is the Mackinac Bridge toll charged both ways?

Yes, each crossing is charged separately. A standard two-axle passenger car is $4 each way, so a round trip is $8 at the current 2026 rate.

Does a trailer pay its own rate?

Usually no separate class is assigned. Towed axles use the lead vehicle's per-axle rate, except when a motorhome tows a passenger auto.

Does the toll calculator include driver assistance?

No. The official driver-assistance service costs an additional $10 plus the calculated vehicle toll. See the driver assistance guide.

Can the Authority charge a different amount?

Yes. The Authority reserves the right to classify and determine the charge for unusual or unconventional vehicle designs, weights, or construction.
