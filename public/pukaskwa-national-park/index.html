The road-trip reality

A wilderness stop is not a roadside pull-off.

Hattie Cove is reached by Highway 627, approximately 15 km off Highway 17 near Marathon. A return to the highway adds about 30 km of driving, before any hiking, picnicking or time at the visitor centre. Road conditions and actual drive time must be checked separately.

Know before entering the park

What the numbers cannot tell you

Bridge and long routes

The suspension bridge is not a short scenic stop. Its 18 km hike is difficult and normally takes 8–9 hours. The official closure overrides its popularity.

Conditions are dated

Our collection may refresh several times daily even while Parks Canada's last trail inspection is older. We show both dates instead of turning an old observation into a green “open now.”

Beyond Hattie Cove

Coastal backpacking and paddling demand distinct permits, route planning and marine-weather judgment. The short-hike chooser does not certify remote routes, campsites or shuttles.

Where the answers come from

Trail distances, durations and route descriptions come from Parks Canada. A separate scheduled monitor checks the agency's trail-condition table; its issue dates remain separate from each retrieval timestamp. Neither this page nor its automated monitor is an official Parks Canada service.
