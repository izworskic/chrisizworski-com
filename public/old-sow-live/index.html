WHAT YOU MAY SEE

Boils and eddies are the normal target.

A dramatic funnel is uncommon and cannot be promised from a tide prediction. Look for rotating eddies, standing water, boils, troughs and patches of sharply textured water.

SOURCES & METHOD

What the forecast actually knows.

1. Nearby current prediction

NOAA current-prediction station selection is provisional and visible by name.

2. Eastport tide cross-check

Eastport station 8410140 supplies tide predictions plus observed water level and wind when available. Observed Eastport water level is still not an Old Sow observation.

3. Daylight + weather

Daylight is calculated in code. NWS forecast language is treated as untrusted data and may be narrowly classified by the private decision harness; missing visibility wording stays “not stated.”

4. Unknown stays unknown

No visitor report means no observation. No camera means no live-view claim. Current strength percentiles describe a nearby prediction, not whirlpool intensity.

Source health and provenance
Why the forecast can miss

The method is a documented hypothesis until reviewed field observations span different tide strengths and seasons. Bathymetry, local countercurrents, wind, visibility and the shifting geometry of the vortex can all make a promising flood cycle look different from the prediction.
