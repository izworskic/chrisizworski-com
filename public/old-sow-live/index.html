COMING FROM EASTPORT

Eastport is not the best land viewpoint.

If seeing Old Sow is the goal, plan for Deer Island Point. This tool does not assume a direct Eastport–Deer Island ferry; route planning should use the operating ferry network.

HOW LONG

Give it about 90 minutes.

Arrive before the predicted energetic core and stay through it. Tidal turbulence develops and shifts; showing up for only the exact peak minute is the wrong way to use the forecast.

Coming through Campobello instead? Check the seasonal Deer Island–Campobello ferry ↗

WHAT WILL I ACTUALLY SEE?

Expect moving, chaotic water. Don’t expect a bathtub drain.

Most realistic

Rotating eddies, boils, seams, troughs and sharply textured water moving against itself.

Sometimes

Large coherent circular motion and smaller “piglet” whirlpools around the main turbulent area.

Not promised

A clean, deep funnel. NOAA describes Old Sow as variable in size and shape, not a permanent hole in the water.

NOAA says Old Sow can exceed 250 feet in diameter and warns that its turbulence can be dangerous to small craft. NOAA explanation ↗

Eastport tide

NOAA Eastport station 8410140 supplies tide predictions plus observed water level and wind. Those observations are not observations of Old Sow itself.

Weather + daylight

Weather changes whether you can comfortably see the water. Beyond the official forecast horizon, this tool says weather is unavailable instead of inventing it.

Truth boundary

There is no verified live Old Sow camera or direct vortex sensor in this product. Current strength is a nearby prediction used to time a visit.

Small-craft warning
