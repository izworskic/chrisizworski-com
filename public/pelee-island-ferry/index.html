Read the port on the booking

The 2026 calendar shifts the regular mainland gateway to Kingsville from July 29. Special sailings can still differ.

02

Reserve the return as well

Pelee requires reservations for foot passengers and vehicles. A trip to the island is not a guaranteed ride back.

03

Canada and Sandusky are different trips

Sandusky is an international crossing with different sailing dates, fares and document requirements. The fare calculator here covers the Canadian-mainland route.

Questions worth asking before booking

Why did the planner change my mainland port?

The regular 2026 schedule uses Leamington earlier in the season and Kingsville from July 29. Verify your exact sailing because exceptions exist.

Does this estimate cover Sandusky?

No. It covers the Canadian mainland–Pelee route only. Sandusky has distinct international fares, dates and border requirements.
