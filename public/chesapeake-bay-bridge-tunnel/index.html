What the crossing is like

Over the bay, then under the shipping channels

The CBBT runs 17.6 miles shore-to-shore on US 13. The drive alternates long low trestles with man-made portal islands and two approximately one-mile tunnels beneath major navigation channels.

Virginia BeachThimble ShoalChesapeake ChannelNorth ChannelEastern Shore

The crossing is unusual, but the live operational answer remains simple: use CBBT's current status and restrictions first.
