    Why the bay makes its own weather

    At Bay City State Park, the wide sand meets the shallow waters of Saginaw Bay, with the wetlands of Tobico Marsh just inland. A breeze moving across open water can bring the air temperature closer to the character of the lake, while a wind from the land may feel entirely different. And the bay itself is not one big thermometer: sheltered shallows can warm or cool differently from the open water. That is why we show the nearest defensible evidence rather than a single Lake Huron average.

    Is that a thermometer reading from the sand?

    No. Satellite values are NOAA analyses for offshore water pixels, normally updated daily, and may include older cloud-free passes or smoothing. Buoy 45163 is an instrument in the outer bay, not a shore sensor. If all local references are unavailable, ShoreFeel does not invent a Bay City State Park temperature.

    Is there a 48-hour Saginaw Bay water forecast?

    Not yet. The existing Lake Michigan pilot has experimental nearshore forecasts, but those results have not been validated for Saginaw Bay. No model estimate is marketed here as a local forecast until its error and uncertainty have been independently tested.

    Find Bay City State Park on Google Maps. This is a public park location search, not a route to an offshore temperature sensor.
