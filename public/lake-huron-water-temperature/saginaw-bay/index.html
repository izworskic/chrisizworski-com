Why Saginaw Bay deserves its own water-temperature model

A Lake Huron average describes an enormous area of open water. Saginaw Bay is different: its shallow reaches respond to heating and cooling, wind can shift surface water, and bayside wetlands and protected shallows are not equivalent to offshore sampling locations. A regional average is useful context, but a poor substitute for a particular beach.

Are these temperatures measured at the beach?

No. Satellite values are NOAA analyses for offshore water pixels, normally updated daily, and may include older cloud-free passes or smoothing. Buoy 45163 is an instrument in the outer bay, not a shore sensor. If all local references are unavailable, ShoreFeel does not invent a Bay City State Park temperature.

Is there a 48-hour Saginaw Bay water forecast?

Not yet. The existing Lake Michigan pilot has experimental nearshore forecasts, but those results have not been validated for Saginaw Bay. No model estimate is marketed here as a local forecast until its error and uncertainty have been independently tested.

Where can I go?

Bay City State Park has an established shoreline beach and accessible facilities. Nayanquing Point State Wildlife Area is a managed wetland and wildlife-viewing area, not a substitute swimming destination. Observe local rules and seasonal access notices before visiting.
