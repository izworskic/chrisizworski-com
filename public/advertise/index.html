Ad-first monetization roadmap

Audience first. Google ads next. Sponsors later.

ChrisIzworski.com is growing useful Michigan gardening, birding, aurora, maritime, travel, and live-condition resources before monetizing them. Search CTR and impressions come first; programmatic ads begin only after a measured economic gate; sponsor outreach waits until the site can show results.

No sponsor packages are currently being sold. Search figures below are verified benchmarks, not pageviews, earnings, or guarantees.

The measured sequence

Google does not publish a minimum-traffic requirement for AdSense eligibility. This site uses its own higher economic and reader-experience gates because technical eligibility alone does not make ads worthwhile.

Phase 1 · now

Raise CTR and qualified impressions

  • Repair five high-impression search results
  • Build connected gardening and birding clusters
  • Measure real pageviews and internal depth
  • Protect first answers and Core Web Vitals

Decision gate: 2.5% search CTR in a complete comparable 28-day window.

Phase 2 · after proof

Activate Google AdSense

  • 10,000 measured monthly pageviews
  • 28 days of stable Core Web Vitals
  • Owner-controlled, Google-approved account
  • Real publisher ID, privacy, consent, and valid ads.txt

Start with conservative placements and expand only if revenue does not damage search or reader experience.

Phase 3 · deferred

Open sponsor conversations

  • 25,000 measured monthly pageviews
  • Three consecutive months above the gate
  • Three months of real ad and category data
  • Privacy-conscious, evidence-based reporting

Inventory and pricing will come from measured demand. No speculative sponsor pricing is active now.

What the ad gate protects

Required before activation

  • A complete measured-pageview baseline
  • A Google-approved site and real publisher ID
  • A privacy policy matching the live configuration
  • Consent controls for applicable visitors
  • A small, measurable launch cohort

Automatic stop conditions

  • Ads obscure the first answer or tool controls
  • Priority-page clicks decline materially
  • Core Web Vitals move from passing to failing
  • A placement resembles navigation or editorial advice
  • Privacy or consent configuration is incomplete
Current status: no programmatic ad network, sponsor inventory, or affiliate offer is active in this roadmap. Before ads launch, the site will use the real owner-approved publisher configuration and publish any required consent and ads.txt records. Read the privacy policy and commercial disclosure.

Follow the evidence, not a package price

The benchmark fixes the order of operations: grow search demand, measure pageviews, prove a responsible display-ad baseline, then decide whether sponsorship inventory is valuable enough to sell.
