What a sponsor buys—and what it never buys

Included

  • Relevant, labeled placement near a useful tool
  • One agreed CTA and destination
  • Aggregate impressions, pageviews, and tracked clicks when available
  • A written hypothesis, launch date, and review date
  • Right to stop or revise a weak pilot

Never included

  • Control over rankings or editorial conclusions
  • Unlabeled native advertising
  • Guaranteed traffic, leads, or revenue
  • Access to personally identifying visitor data
  • Influence over safety guidance, data interpretation, or reviews
Editorial standard: sponsored placements are labeled next to the placement. Affiliate relationships are disclosed with the recommendation. Sponsors do not control the underlying tool, source selection, or conclusion. Read the full advertising and affiliate disclosure.
