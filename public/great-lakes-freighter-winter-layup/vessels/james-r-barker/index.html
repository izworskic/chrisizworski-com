THE GREAT LAKES / A SHIP’S WINTER STORY

James R. Barker

The first thousand-footer built entirely on the Great Lakes.

James R. Barker entered service in 1976 as a landmark of domestic lake shipbuilding. For winter 2025–26, this thousand-footer came into Sturgeon Bay for major inspections and painting in Fincantieri's graving dock.

Chapter 1

A turning point in Great Lakes shipbuilding

Interlake Steamship describes Barker as the first 1,000-foot-class freighter built entirely on the Lakes, marking a new era of longer vessels serving the largest locks and ore routes.

Read the original reporting ↗

Chapter 2

A winter with the hull exposed

The Wisconsin Marine Historical Society documented Barker's January 5, 2026 arrival in Sturgeon Bay, with tug assistance before entering Fincantieri Bay Shipbuilding's large graving dock for winter storage and a five-year inspection.
