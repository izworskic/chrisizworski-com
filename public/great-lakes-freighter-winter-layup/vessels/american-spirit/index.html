American Spirit

A thousand-footer racing a January freeze.

American Spirit illustrates how the final miles before winter layup can demand as much planning as a long lake voyage. In January 2026, Coast Guard icebreaking assistance helped the vessel reach Toledo.

Chapter 1

The Lake Erie ice escort

On January 21, 2026, the Coast Guard cutter Neah Bay helped American Spirit make headway through heavy Lake Erie ice. Contemporary reporting said the vessel reached Toledo bound for winter layup, but did not identify a berth. The atlas therefore treats the winter berth as reported, not confirmed.
