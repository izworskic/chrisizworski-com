GREAT LAKES / SHIP BIOGRAPHY

Stewart J. Cort

The Great Lakes' first thousand-foot freighter.

Before the now-familiar thousand-footers, Stewart J. Cort pioneered a new scale of cargo carrier in 1972. Its early construction story stretched from Mississippi to Erie, Pennsylvania, and reshaped what the Great Lakes could move.

Chapter 1

Building a ship in pieces

The forward and after sections originated at Ingalls in Pascagoula, Mississippi, and were later joined to a Great Lakes-built cargo midsection at Erie. The unusual construction process helped launch the first thousand-foot vessel on the Lakes.
