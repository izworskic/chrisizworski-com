Lee A. Tregurtha

A World War II tanker that became a Great Lakes working giant.

Few freighters have made a transformation like the Lee A. Tregurtha. Built as the tanker Chiwawa in 1942, it served during World War II before extensive reconstruction made it a Great Lakes ore carrier. Winter shipyard work keeps a remarkable hull's story moving.

Chapter 1

From wartime tanker to ore carrier

As Chiwawa, the vessel saw wartime service on both the Atlantic and Pacific oceans and was present in Tokyo Bay for Japan's formal surrender in September 1945. In 1959–60, conversion for Great Lakes use included substantial lengthening and hull alterations.
