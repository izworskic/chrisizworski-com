Questions worth asking before booking

Is this the Manitou Island ferry from Leland?

No. Chi-Cheemaun serves Manitoulin Island in Ontario. North and South Manitou are separate islands in Michigan’s Sleeping Bear Dunes National Lakeshore.

Is the crossing always 1 hour 45 minutes?

The operator describes about 1 hour 45 minutes in peak season and about 2 hours off-peak. The timeline uses that seasonal distinction.
