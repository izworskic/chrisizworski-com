Questions worth asking before booking

Can I take a car to Isle Royale?

No. NPS lists passenger services, not car ferries. Leave the vehicle on the mainland.

Does Voyageur II stop only at Windigo?

No. Its service includes additional island stops and Rock Harbor. This comparison’s simple timeline covers only Grand Portage–Windigo; use the operator for a complete multi-leg plan.
