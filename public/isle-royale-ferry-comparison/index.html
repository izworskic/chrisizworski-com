Route-specific decisions

Ranger III (Houghton) crosses to Rock Harbor in about six hours; some special dates go to Windigo.

Queen IV departs Copper Harbor for Rock Harbor in about 3.5 hours. Sea Hunter III is oriented to Windigo; Voyageur II serves multiple island landing points.

There are no car ferries. NPS requires matching inbound/outbound transportation and appropriate national park permits.

What is live and what is not?

These tools calculate against operator-published 2026 reference data and your inputs. The date selection does not retrieve ticket inventory or prove a ferry will sail.
