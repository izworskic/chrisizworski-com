How to use the Ambassador Bridge wait time

The Ambassador Bridge connects southwest Detroit with Windsor by way of Huron Church Road. It remains a major passenger and freight crossing, but a single number can be misleading unless its direction and traffic type match your trip. Select “To Canada” for the CBSA report or “To U.S.” for the CBP report; passenger and commercial records remain separate.

If you are deciding among Detroit crossings, open the live three-way comparison. It compares Ambassador with the Gordie Howe International Bridge and Detroit–Windsor Tunnel only after you choose the same direction, vehicle and lane program. It also preserves an exact tie instead of declaring an arbitrary winner.

Ambassador Bridge toll

The current standard passenger planning rate for your selected direction is $10 USD. The live tool will show the direction-specific note. The operator’s Premier account, payment method, currency, vehicle class and number of axles can change the price.

The published April 19, 2026 auto schedule lists a $10 USD standard U.S.-side rate for common U.S. payment methods and $14 CAD for Canadian cash. Confirm the official Ambassador Bridge toll rates rather than treating this snapshot as a quote.

Why the approach can feel longer than the reported wait

The customs agencies estimate processing delay at the border facility. Huron Church Road congestion, freeway incidents, toll activity and the drive from your starting point are separate. That is why this page places Ontario 511 events and cameras next to—but never inside—the official wait answer.

  • “No Delay” means the agency is currently reporting no border-processing delay for that stream.
  • A closed NEXUS, Ready or FAST lane stays closed; the tool does not copy the standard wait into it.
  • If CBP or CBSA fails to report, the page says “Not reported,” not zero.
  • Commercial wait values do not describe passenger vehicles, and vice versa.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Ambassador Bridge open 24 hours?

It normally operates around the clock. Temporary closures, inspections, maintenance and lane schedules can still affect a trip, so use the current port status and operator links.

Does the Ambassador Bridge take credit cards?

The operator publishes payment-specific rates and accepts several methods. Confirm the current payment options on the official toll page, especially when paying in Canadian currency or using a commercial account.

Is the Ambassador Bridge faster than the tunnel?

There is no permanent answer. Downtown destination, Huron Church Road conditions, current customs delay, tunnel vehicle rules and toll preference all matter. The live comparison supplies the current same-scope border numbers without claiming total travel time.

Are trucks reported separately?

Yes. Choose commercial to see the available standard or FAST report. A passenger wait must never be used as a truck estimate.
