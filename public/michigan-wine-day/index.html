What the engine is actually comparing

It is not a winery popularity ranking.

1 · Feasibility first

Round-trip travel, the usable local window and plausible operating coverage can veto a region before preferences are scored.

2 · Positive wine evidence

Verified Riesling, sparkling, red, white, food, view and experience signals can improve fit. Missing evidence never becomes “doesn't have it.”

3 · Regional driving reality

Old Mission is compact; Leelanau is broader; Petoskey spans several local areas; Southwest Michigan is evaluated by cluster instead of pretending it is one loop.

4 · Confidence stays separate

A region can be a strong fit with medium confidence. Hours freshness and adapter quality do not secretly turn into recommendation points.

Current production coverage

Four Michigan wine-country choices, with regional owners preserved.

Old Mission Peninsula and Leelanau Peninsula stay owned by the Traverse City wine system. Petoskey / Tip of the Mitt stays owned by the Petoskey planner. Southwest Michigan uses a smaller cluster adapter built from current official trail and winery sources.

Unknown is not closed. If a winery's weekly hours, reservation inventory, fee or wine style is not verified, this engine lowers evidence confidence or tells you to confirm. It does not invent a negative fact.

Source policy

Operational claims come from first-party or official sources.

Regional winery facts come from the regional planner datasets, official wine associations and winery-published information. Weather uses the National Weather Service when the selected date is inside its forecast range. Road travel is best-effort OSRM routing with a labeled fallback estimate if the routing service is unavailable. City/ZIP geocoding uses a curated Michigan origin list first and OpenStreetMap Nominatim only for a user-submitted location that is not already supported.

Multiple tasting stops require a sober transportation plan. Use a designated driver, shuttle or other sober transportation; this tool does not estimate blood-alcohol level or tell you how much you can drink before driving.
