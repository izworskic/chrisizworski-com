How to interpret these lines

When total outflow climbs, more water is passing the dam. The forebay is the upstream reservoir surface; the tailwater is below the dam. Head is their difference. It is not the same thing as today's navigation lock lift.

Fish passage is another story: official salmon and steelhead counts describe counted adults passing fish ladders, not a census of every fish in the Columbia.

Follow the river

Four chapters in a much larger story

01 / COMMERCE

A staircase for a working river

Towboats and barges move grain, fuel and other cargo along a navigation network whose locks climb from the lower Columbia to the interior. A reported nearby vessel has not necessarily joined a John Day lock queue; traffic reports and lockmaster operations decide what comes next.

02 / ENGINEERING

Two water levels, one enormous chamber

The John Day lock sits beside a hydroelectric powerhouse and spillway. The dam controls a broad upstream reservoir—Lake Umatilla—while the downstream Columbia remains much lower. The often-quoted 113-foot figure describes a maximum lock lift, not a constant daily difference.

03 / ECOLOGY

Salmon moving against the current

Fish ladders support returning adult salmon and steelhead moving upstream. Hydropower and navigation infrastructure also change river habitat and downstream juvenile migration. A useful data story must make room for ecology, fisheries science and the perspectives of Columbia River Tribes.

04 / HISTORY

A changed landscape

John Day was dedicated in 1968, with completion in 1971. Its engineering belongs within the longer Columbia River story, including Indigenous fisheries, dams, displaced landscapes and community impacts. Celilo Falls was submerged with The Dalles Dam's reservoir, not John Day's.

Sources, evidence and limitations

Operating rules are reproduced from specific dated Corps publications. The explanatory diagrams and historical water sample do not represent live operations. This preview does not have a verified live John Day vessel queue, current lock status, public lookout inventory, or lock webcam.
