When does fall color peak on Mackinac Island?

Mackinac Island and the Straits of Mackinac usually peak around October 5 to 13. The island bans cars, so the color comes from a bicycle or on foot along the bluffs, with Lakes Michigan and Huron on either side and the Mackinac Bridge to the west. Early to mid-October is the window, with fewer crowds than the summer season.

What you'll see

More than 80 percent of the island is state park, mostly hardwood forest laced with quiet roads and trails. In fall the maples turn over limestone bluffs and the two lakes meet in cold blue below. Without engines, the experience is unusually still: leaves, water, the occasional carriage. The Mackinac Bridge and the mainland color frame the western view.

The drive

There is no driving on the island, which is the point. The mainland approach is its own color route: I-75 over the Mackinac Bridge, with the Tip of the Mitt around Mackinaw City and the eastern U.P. shore on the far side. Take the ferry from Mackinaw City or St. Ignace and trade the car for a bike.

On foot

M-185, the only state highway in the country that bans cars, circles the island in about eight miles along the shore, level and easy by bike or foot. Inland, trails climb to Arch Rock and Fort Holmes, the high point, for color over the Straits. Walking the bluff roads past the historic cottages is a quieter alternative.

On the water

Sea-kayak outfitters at the Straits run paddles along the island and the Mackinaw shore on calm days, a low-water view of the bluff color. The Straits can be rough, so this depends on the weather.

How to time your visit

Aim for the first two weeks of October. The island turns on roughly the same schedule as the Tip of the Mitt, and the surrounding water moderates the extremes. Fall is quieter and cheaper than peak summer; check ferry schedules, which thin out later in the season.

Common questions

When do leaves peak on Mackinac Island?

Mackinac Island usually peaks around October 5 to 13, on a similar schedule to the Tip of the Mitt. The water around the island moderates the temperature, keeping the timing steady year to year.

How do you see fall color on Mackinac Island without a car?

The island bans cars. Most visitors bike the eight-mile shoreline loop on M-185 or hike inland trails to Arch Rock and Fort Holmes for color over the Straits. Ferries run from Mackinaw City and St. Ignace.

Is fall a good time to visit Mackinac Island?

Yes. Early to mid-October brings strong color, fewer crowds than summer, and lower rates, though some businesses and ferry runs begin to wind down later in the season.

Nearby color, same trip

The Tunnel of Trees on M-119 is a short drive from Mackinaw City and peaks the same week. The eastern U.P. across the bridge turns a few days earlier.
