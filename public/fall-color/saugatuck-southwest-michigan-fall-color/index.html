When does fall color peak in Saugatuck and southwest Michigan?

Saugatuck and the southwest Michigan lakeshore usually peak around October 18 to 26. Lake Michigan keeps the shore warm into fall, so color here runs a little later than inland, set against dune and big water. The back half of October is the window, holding toward the start of November in the most sheltered spots.

What you'll see

This is dune-and-shore color: hardwood turning gold and red along wooded dunes that drop to Lake Michigan, with the lake holding a deep late-season blue. The lakeshore microclimate stretches the season, so when the north is bare the Saugatuck dunes can still be lit. Vineyards and orchards inland add their own turning rows.

The drive

The Blue Star Highway runs the dune country between Saugatuck, Douglas, and South Haven, a slower parallel to the interstate through woods and lakeshore towns. Inland, the roads around the Kalamazoo and the fruit belt link orchard and river color. The Saugatuck Dunes State Park access roads reach trailheads above the lake.

On foot

P.J. Hoffmaster State Park north of Holland has dune trails climbing through hardwood to overlooks above Lake Michigan, with the Gillette dune center at the trailhead. Saugatuck Dunes State Park offers quieter wooded-dune walking to remote beach.

On the water

The lower Kalamazoo River winds through southwest Michigan toward Saugatuck and the lake, an easy paddle through bottomland color. The sheltered bayous near the river mouth stay calm when the big lake is up.

How to time your visit

Aim for the last full week of October into early November. The Lake Michigan shore is one of the later-peaking parts of the state, so this is a good fallback when northern color has dropped. Warm lake water can push the most sheltered color into the first days of November.

Common questions

When do leaves peak in Saugatuck and southwest Michigan?

The southwest Michigan lakeshore around Saugatuck usually peaks around October 18 to 26, later than inland and northern Michigan. Lake Michigan holds warmth into fall, stretching the season toward early November in sheltered spots.

Where is the best fall color along the Lake Michigan shore in the south?

P.J. Hoffmaster State Park near Holland has dune trails to overlooks above the lake, and the Blue Star Highway links the wooded dune country between Saugatuck, Douglas, and South Haven.

Is southwest Michigan a good late-season fall color trip?

Yes. Because the lakeshore peaks late, Saugatuck and the southwest shore are a good choice in the last week of October when northern Michigan has already gone bare.

Nearby color, same trip

Grand Rapids and the inland southwest turn a few days ahead of the immediate shore. Ann Arbor and the Irish Hills to the east peak on a similar late schedule.
