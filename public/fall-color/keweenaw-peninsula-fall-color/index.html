When does fall color peak on the Keweenaw Peninsula?

The Keweenaw Peninsula, Michigan's far northern tip reaching into Lake Superior, peaks around September 28 to October 6. It is one of the first places in the state to turn, and Brockway Mountain Drive between Eagle Harbor and Copper Harbor gives a ridge-top view over color and the big lake at once. The end of September into early October is the window.

What you'll see

The Keweenaw is a long finger of high ground surrounded by Lake Superior on three sides, so the color comes with water in nearly every direction. Old copper-country forest of maple and birch runs over the spine of the peninsula, and from Brockway the turning hills drop to the lake on both sides. It feels like the end of the road, because it nearly is.

The drive

Brockway Mountain Drive is the centerpiece, a ridge road climbing about 700 feet above Lake Superior with overlooks the whole way, often called the finest scenic drive in the Midwest. US-41 runs to its literal end past Copper Harbor, through tunnels of hardwood. The Eagle River and Eagle Harbor shore roads add lighthouses and Superior surf to the color.

On foot

Trails around Copper Harbor and the Estivant Pines Nature Sanctuary, with some of the last old-growth white pine in the region, give walks among big trees. Hunter's Point Park at Copper Harbor is a short, scenic shoreline loop.

On the water

The Keweenaw's harbors and the Montreal River country offer sheltered Lake Superior paddling on calm days, with color to the waterline. Conditions on the big lake change fast, so this is a fair-weather option.

How to time your visit

Plan the last week of September into the first week of October, the same early window as the Porkies. As the northernmost land in Michigan, the Keweenaw gets cold first. Brockway is exposed and the wind off Superior is real, so a calm clear morning is the prize.

Common questions

When does fall color peak in the Keweenaw Peninsula?

The Keweenaw usually peaks around September 28 to October 6, among the earliest in Michigan. As the state's northernmost land, surrounded by Lake Superior, it gets the first cold of the season.

Is Brockway Mountain Drive worth it for fall color?

Yes. The ridge road climbs about 700 feet above Lake Superior between Eagle Harbor and Copper Harbor, with overlooks over turning hardwood and the lake on both sides. Go on a calm, clear morning, since the ridge is exposed to wind off Superior.

How far north is the Keweenaw fall color?

The Keweenaw reaches farther into Lake Superior than any other part of Michigan, ending past Copper Harbor at the tip of US-41. That far-north position is why it turns first.

Nearby color, same trip

The Porcupine Mountains to the southwest peak the same week. The eastern U.P. around Tahquamenon follows about a week later.
