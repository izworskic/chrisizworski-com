When does the Tunnel of Trees peak?

The Tunnel of Trees, the stretch of M-119 between Harbor Springs and Cross Village, usually peaks around October 5 to 13. The road runs narrow and shoulderless along a bluff above Lake Michigan, and when the maples close overhead it becomes the canopy that gives the drive its name. Early October is the window most years, with the best light in the first hours after sunrise.

What you'll see

This is sugar maple and beech country, with the canopy arching close over a road barely wide enough for two cars. The color comes at you from both sides and overhead at once, broken by sudden openings where the bluff falls away to the lake. It is less a panorama than an immersion: you are inside the color rather than looking across at it.

The drive

Drive M-119 north from Harbor Springs toward Cross Village, roughly 20 miles of curves with no center line for much of the way. The pace is slow by design. Stop at Good Hart for the general store, and end at Legs Inn in Cross Village, a stone roadhouse worth the drive on its own. Going north to south puts the lake on your right and the morning sun behind you.

On foot

For a walk, the Lake Michigan shoreline at Wilderness State Park sits just past the north end of the drive, with hardwoods running down to the water. Thorne Swift Nature Preserve near Harbor Springs has short trails through the same maple-beech forest if you want to be among the trees on foot.

On the water

The Crooked River and the Inland Waterway near Conway and Alanson offer slow, sheltered paddling through color, a calmer counterpart to the roadside crowds on a peak weekend.

How to time your visit

Go on a weekday morning. The road is genuinely narrow, and on a peak-color Saturday it backs up with slow traffic and people stopping in the lane to photograph. First light is both quieter and better for the canopy, which glows when the sun is low and behind you. If the first week of October runs warm, the peak slides a few days later.

Common questions

When is the best time to drive the Tunnel of Trees for fall color?

Most years the Tunnel of Trees peaks around October 5 to 13. Aim for a weekday morning in the first half of October; the canopy glows in low early light and the narrow road is far less crowded than on a peak weekend.

Where does the Tunnel of Trees start and end?

It follows M-119 for about 20 miles between Harbor Springs and Cross Village in the northwest Lower Peninsula. Good Hart sits roughly in the middle, and Legs Inn anchors the north end at Cross Village.

Is the Tunnel of Trees worth visiting before or after peak?

Yes. The week before peak brings the first turning maples against green, and the week after still holds strong color lower in the canopy before the leaves drop, usually by about October 22.

Nearby color, same trip

The same week, color is usually strong across the whole Tip of the Mitt around Petoskey and Mackinaw City. The island at the Straits turns on a similar schedule.
