When does fall color peak in the Porcupine Mountains?

The Porcupine Mountains in the western Upper Peninsula are among the first places in Michigan to turn, usually peaking around September 28 to October 6. The Porkies hold one of the largest tracts of old-growth northern hardwood left in the Midwest, and from the escarpment the sugar maples run unbroken to the horizon. Late September into the first days of October is the window.

What you'll see

This is the big one for sheer scale: a wilderness of sugar maple with almost no development to interrupt it, seen from a ridge that drops away to Lake of the Clouds. On a peak day the color is solid orange and red from the overlook to the far hills. Because it turns early and turns hard, the Porkies are the first real fall-color destination of the Michigan season.

The drive

From Silver City, M-107 climbs to the Lake of the Clouds overlook, the most photographed view in the Upper Peninsula and an easy stop from the parking area. South County Road and the park's interior roads open more of the forest. Combine it with the Black River Harbor scenic byway near Bessemer for a string of waterfalls under color.

On foot

The Escarpment Trail runs the ridge above Lake of the Clouds and is the finest fall walk in the park, with the valley and lake below you the whole way. Even a short stretch out from the overlook is worth it. The Summit Peak observation tower gives a 360-degree view over the turning hills.

On the water

The Presque Isle River on the park's west end runs through gorge and hardwood down to Lake Superior, with a loop of boardwalks and falls; it is more a walk along water than a paddle, but a memorable one in color.

How to time your visit

Plan for the last week of September through the first week of October, earlier than almost anywhere else in the state. The western U.P. gets the first cold nights, which is what triggers the early turn. Weather off Lake Superior moves fast, so a clear morning at the overlook is worth chasing.

Common questions

When do the Porcupine Mountains peak for fall color?

The Porkies usually peak around September 28 to October 6, among the earliest in Michigan. The western Upper Peninsula gets the first hard cold nights, which turns the sugar maples ahead of the rest of the state.

What is the best view of fall color in the Porcupine Mountains?

The Lake of the Clouds overlook off M-107 is the iconic view, reached by a short walk from the parking area. For more, the Escarpment Trail follows the ridge above the lake and the Summit Peak tower gives a full panorama.

Are the Porcupine Mountains a good early-season fall color trip?

Yes, they are the best early option in Michigan. While the Lower Peninsula is still green in late September, the Porkies are often at or near peak.

Nearby color, same trip

The Keweenaw Peninsula to the northeast turns at the same early time, with Brockway Mountain Drive as its high point. The eastern U.P. around Tahquamenon follows about a week later.
