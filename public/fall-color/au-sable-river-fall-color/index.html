When does fall color peak on the Au Sable River?

The Au Sable River country around Grayling usually peaks around October 9 to 17. This is maple and aspen turning over dark stands of pine, mirrored on slow current, with the color sitting later here than to the north and west. The middle of October is the window, and the river itself is the best way to see it.

What you'll see

The Au Sable runs clear and cold through jack pine and hardwood, and in fall the gold of aspen and birch plays against the near-black of the pines along the banks. From a canoe the color comes doubled, real leaves above and their reflection on water so slow it barely seems to move. It is a quieter, more intimate color than the big U.P. overlooks.

The drive

The River Road Scenic Byway follows the Au Sable's south bank toward Oscoda, with the Lumberman's Monument overlook high above the valley and the Largo Springs stairway down to the river. Inland, the country roads around Grayling and Hartwick Pines string together pine and hardwood. The byway is the set-piece drive of northeast Michigan.

On foot

Hartwick Pines State Park near Grayling holds one of the last stands of old-growth white pine in Michigan, with easy trails among trees that predate the logging era. The interpretive loop is short and worth it. Trails at the Lumberman's Monument drop to the riverbank for a closer view.

On the water

The Holy Waters of the Au Sable, the mainstream from Burton's Landing down to Wakeley Bridge, is the classic float: easy, steady current, three to four hours through color. Liveries in Grayling, including Gates Au Sable Lodge, Carlisle, Penrod's, and Borchers, rent canoes and run shuttles into early fall. The Rifle River near Rose City is a gentler family alternative nearby.

How to time your visit

Mid-October is the reliable window, a week or so behind the northwest Lower Peninsula. Mornings on the river are still and often misted; the color is best with the sun up but low. Check livery hours, since canoe rental winds down as the season ends.

Common questions

When do leaves peak along the Au Sable River?

The Au Sable country around Grayling usually peaks around October 9 to 17, a week or so behind the northwest Lower Peninsula. Maple and aspen turn gold over the dark pine along the banks.

Can you canoe the Au Sable in the fall?

Yes. The Holy Waters from Burton's Landing to Wakeley Bridge is an easy three-to-four-hour float, and Grayling liveries rent canoes and run shuttles into early fall. Check current hours, since rental season winds down as the weather turns.

What is the best fall color drive near Grayling?

The River Road Scenic Byway along the Au Sable toward Oscoda, with the Lumberman's Monument overlook above the valley. Hartwick Pines State Park adds old-growth white pine and easy trails.

When does fall color peak in Gaylord and Alpena?

Both fall inside the northeast Lower Michigan window, roughly October 9 through October 17. Gaylord runs a little earlier on the high ground along I-75, and Alpena a little later on the Lake Huron shore, where the water keeps the nights milder.

Nearby color, same trip

Sleeping Bear and Traverse City to the west peak a few days earlier. The central Lower Peninsula and Saginaw Bay follow about a week later.
