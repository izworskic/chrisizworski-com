Along the water

What shapes this shore

The city skyline rises behind the sand, but the lake sets its own pace. A lake breeze can cool an exposed walk after a warm afternoon inland.

On Lake Michigan, dunes, piers and city blocks can shelter you from wind that becomes more noticeable at the waterline.

An open beach is not the same exposure as a wooded trail, parking lot or town street. The NWS point forecast represents a nearby land-area grid. Heat index or wind chill applies only when source values support it. Compare the issued time and wind before you leave, then reassess the conditions you actually encounter.

Source method and limits

The forecast is for an official weather reference near the beach, not an on-sand thermometer. Wind chill and heat index do not calculate the exact lake-breeze cooling or warming at the waterline. There is no calibrated beach-versus-inland temperature correction. If the source is missing or stale, the weather number remains unavailable.
