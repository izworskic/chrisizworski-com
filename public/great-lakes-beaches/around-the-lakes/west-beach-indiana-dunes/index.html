Along the water

Meet this shoreline

Climb toward the dunes and you can see how much shoreline the lake controls. The open beach is less sheltered than the woods and stairs behind it. On a visit through the dunes, notice where the stairs or wooded approaches give way to the full reach of lake wind. Check the park's current access information before you set out.

Lake Michigan often puts dunes, forest or city blocks a short walk from open sand. Watch how the breeze changes when those sheltered places give way to the lake.

At the water's edge, notice whether the breeze arrives from the lake, how the waves meet this shore, and which parts of the landscape offer shelter. The NWS point forecast represents a nearby land-area grid. Heat index or wind chill applies only when source values support it. Check current park and beach advisories, then judge the conditions when you arrive.

Source method and limits

The nearby forecast gives you an official picture of the air, not a thermometer planted in the beach. NWS wind chill or heat index is shown only when its inputs qualify. Neither tells us the exact difference between town and sand. Without a recent, suitable forecast, ShoreFeel leaves the number unavailable rather than inventing one.
