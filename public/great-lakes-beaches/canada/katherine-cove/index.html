Along the water

Meet this shoreline

The shoreline folds into a sheltered cove of clear water and rounded stone. Step beyond the trees and listen for the wind crossing the lake. This cove invites a careful look at how rock headlands shape a shore. Some wind directions may be sheltered, others not. The actual waves tell today's story.

Lake Superior is slow to warm compared with the land in spring and early summer. If wind arrives across cooler water, you may feel that contrast most clearly beyond the shelter of trees or rock.

At the water's edge, notice whether the breeze arrives from the lake, how the waves meet this shore, and which parts of the landscape offer shelter. An ECCC city forecast may be more than a few kilometres from the beach, and Canadian apparent temperature is wind chill only when conditions warrant. Check current park and beach advisories, then judge the conditions when you arrive.

Source method and limits
