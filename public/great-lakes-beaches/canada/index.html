A note from the shore

Four Canadian shorelines, four ways to meet a Great Lake

Lake Superior can bring a cool breeze through a sheltered cove. On Huron, long beaches and Georgian Bay's shape can alter the wind near the sand. Erie’s broad, shallow basin and Lake Ontario's exposed dune shore each bring their own character. A forecast for a nearby town is helpful, but it is not a beach thermometer or a scientifically validated lake-cooling adjustment.

Ontario Parks posts its sampled beaches separately from municipal beaches. Local public-health units monitor many municipal swimming areas. This page links to the correct authority for each stop without declaring a beach safe or open. During the off-season, a last summer sample is not a current swimming report.

How the Canadian forecast works

The Environment and Climate Change Canada city-page GeoMet API supplies hourly forecast air temperature and wind for nearby forecast communities. We identify the forecast community and its approximate distance from the selected beach, and withhold the number if no sufficiently close recent source is available. Calculated wind chill uses the Canadian formula only when applicable. No sampled water quality, on-sand air readings, measured wave hazard or swimming status is inferred.
