Along the water

What shapes this shore

The sandy peninsula stretches far into Lake Erie, with dunes, marsh and abundant birdlife. On an exposed shore, a breeze is part of the landscape.

Lake Erie can warm sooner than the deeper Great Lakes, but an exposed shoreline still deserves a close look at wind and posted swimming notices.

An open beach is not the same exposure as a wooded trail, parking lot or town street. An ECCC city forecast may be more than a few kilometres from the beach, and Canadian apparent temperature is wind chill only when conditions warrant. Compare the issued time and wind before you leave, then reassess the conditions you actually encounter.

Source method and limits

The forecast is for an official weather reference near the beach, not an on-sand thermometer. Wind chill and heat index do not calculate the exact lake-breeze cooling or warming at the waterline. There is no calibrated beach-versus-inland temperature correction. If the source is missing or stale, the weather number remains unavailable.
