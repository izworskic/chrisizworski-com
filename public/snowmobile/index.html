What the verdict does — and does not — mean

A ride-quality score requires a current local trail-surface report. Official Michigan DNR trail status and temporary closures establish the legal/operational boundary; a matched closure always overrides a favorable report. NWS forecasts and recent station observations can downgrade a trail for thaw, rain or icing risk, but favorable weather cannot manufacture a GOOD trail.

Official NWS Local Storm Reports show where snow has recently been reported. Those amounts are supporting evidence only: the reporting duration varies, and fresh natural snow does not prove an established base or recent grooming. NOAA/NOHRSC snow depth on the region maps is also context, never a trail-base estimate.

For a statewide view of reported snowfall amounts and source details, use Michigan Snow Totals.
