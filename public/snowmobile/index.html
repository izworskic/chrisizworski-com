DATA RULES

What we will not pretend

  • Natural snow depth is not trail base.
  • Forecast snow is not observed snowfall.
  • A groomer nearby does not mean the whole trail was groomed.
  • No closure record does not automatically mean “confirmed open.”
  • Fresh snow after a storm can mean wait for grooming, not “excellent.”

SEASON CONTEXT

Built before the snow arrives on purpose.

Michigan state-designated snowmobile trails run December 1 through March 31 and grooming is snow-dependent. During the off-season, this page stays useful by validating the official corridor, source freshness and preseason readiness without manufacturing a ride score.
