Local condition evidence

Club reports can describe surface condition and grooming. They do not override an official closure, and an old “last groomed” field is not treated as current just because the page itself is current.

Source contract

    Semantic rule: natural snow depth ≠ trail base; forecast snow ≠ accumulated snow; groomer nearby ≠ whole trail groomed; missing closure data ≠ confirmed open.

    How the score works

    The corridor is bottleneck-aware. A weak required segment can pull the route down, and an official closure breaks the route instead of being averaged away. Condition and confidence are separate: favorable weather with stale club evidence can produce a decent condition score and low confidence.

    JEV is used only behind the server-side shared harness for bounded interpretation of unstructured club text. It cannot create trail facts, determine legal status, alter DNR geometry, invent grooming, or convert snow depth into trail base. If the harness is unavailable, deterministic parsing and scoring continue.
