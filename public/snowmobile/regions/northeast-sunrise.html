    Local condition evidence

    A ride-quality score requires current local trail-surface evidence. Club/operator reports can describe surface condition and grooming, but they do not override an official closure. Old reports and old “last groomed” fields are visibly discounted instead of borrowing freshness from this page.

    Source contract

      Semantic rule: observed snowfall ≠ trail base; NOAA snow depth ≠ trail base; forecast snow ≠ accumulated snow; groomer nearby ≠ whole trail groomed; open ≠ good; missing closure data ≠ confirmed open.

      How the decision model works

      The model is bottleneck-aware. A weak required segment can pull a verified route down, and an official closure breaks the route instead of being averaged away. But a positive trail-quality score is issued only when there is current local trail-surface evidence. DNR legal status, snowfall, NOHRSC snow depth and favorable weather can provide context; they cannot manufacture a FAIR or GOOD trail when the surface itself is unverified. Aging positive reports are capped, stale reports become UNKNOWN, and rain/thaw observations or forecasts can downgrade otherwise favorable evidence.

      Snowmobile maps, routes and trail reports

      Mapped coverage: Cheboygan, Montmorency, Presque Isle, Alpena, Alcona, Oscoda, Iosco counties. Cheboygan is the road-trip hub; drive time to your unloading location is additional. Local reports cover the areas stated by each source, not every mapped trail.

      How do I plan a snowmobile route?

      Open a regional map, choose Plan a route, then select blue junction dots for your start, stops and finish. Choose Build route to see each leg, total miles, estimated ride time and trail condition evidence. Tap the starting dot again for a loop. Gray dots are disconnected from your chosen start. The planner excludes known closures; it is not turn-by-turn navigation.

      Does snow depth mean the trails are good?

      No. Snowfall and snow depth provide context. A positive riding verdict needs current local trail-surface evidence. Check the report date, grooming coverage and official closures before leaving. Off-season maps support future-trip planning.

      Where should I verify maps and conditions?

      Use Michigan DNR snowmobile maps and trail information for the official trail network and MISORVA local snowmobile trail reports for club report links. National Weather Service forecasts and NOAA NOHRSC snow analysis provide weather and snowpack context. The live panels show source age and missing evidence.
