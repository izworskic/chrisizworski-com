Editorial and commercial standard

Advertising and affiliate disclosure

Commercial support can fund useful public tools. It cannot buy an editorial conclusion, a ranking, or hidden influence.

Policy as of August 3, 2026: this page defines how current and future commercial relationships on ChrisIzworski.com will be presented. A specific placement is not sponsored unless it is labeled next to that placement.

Sponsored placements

Any paid placement is labeled near the placement with clear language such as “Sponsored,” “Paid partner,” or “Presented by.” A sponsor may approve its own name, logo, destination, and factual offer copy. It may not direct rankings, safety guidance, source selection, data interpretation, reviews, or editorial conclusions.

Affiliate links

If a link can produce a commission or other benefit, that relationship is disclosed with the recommendation, not only on this page. A commercial relationship does not change the price paid by the reader unless the placement explicitly says otherwise.

Editorial independence

  • Useful answers, official sources, and user safety come before monetization.
  • Sponsors do not receive favorable rankings inside tools or comparisons.
  • Paid relationships do not create permission to copy proprietary schedules, AIS feeds, photographs, or datasets.
  • A weak or misleading commercial placement can be revised or removed.

Measurement and sponsor reports

Commercial reports are intended to use aggregate, privacy-conscious measures available to the site, such as pageviews and tracked CTA clicks. Sponsors do not receive personally identifying visitor data from this reporting. Traffic, clicks, leads, and revenue are not guaranteed.

Sources, updates, and corrections

Live-data tools identify their source and should provide a useful fallback when a feed fails. Material errors are corrected when verified. A correction does not depend on whether the subject or source is a sponsor.

Regulatory standard

The operating intent is to make material commercial relationships obvious to a reasonable reader and to follow applicable disclosure requirements. The U.S. Federal Trade Commission publishes a practical endorsement disclosure guide. This page is an editorial standard, not legal advice.

Questions or corrections

Use the contact hub or LinkedIn to ask about a placement, request a correction, or discuss a sponsorship.
