Advertising and affiliate disclosure

Commercial support can fund useful public tools. It cannot buy an editorial conclusion, a ranking, or hidden influence.

Status as of August 3, 2026: no programmatic display-ad network, paid sponsor placement, or affiliate offer is active on ChrisIzworski.com. This page defines the standards that apply before and after any future activation.

Programmatic advertising

Google AdSense is the planned first monetization layer, but it will not be activated until the site meets its measured audience, search CTR, privacy, consent, and performance gates. When advertising is activated, this disclosure and the privacy policy will be updated to match the live configuration. Ads will not be placed where they obscure a first answer, primary tool control, safety guidance, or source attribution.

Sponsored placements

Sponsorship sales are deferred until the site can show at least 25,000 measured monthly pageviews for three consecutive months and three months of real ad/category performance. If sponsorships begin, every paid placement will be labeled near the placement with clear language such as “Sponsored,” “Paid partner,” or “Presented by.” A sponsor may approve its own name, logo, destination, and factual offer copy. It may not direct rankings, safety guidance, source selection, data interpretation, reviews, or editorial conclusions.

Affiliate links

No affiliate program is active as of the date above. If a link can later produce a commission or other benefit, that relationship will be disclosed with the recommendation, not only on this page. A commercial relationship will not be represented as an independent recommendation.

Editorial independence

  • Useful answers, official sources, and user safety come before monetization.
  • Sponsors do not receive favorable rankings inside tools or comparisons.
  • Paid relationships do not create permission to copy proprietary schedules, AIS feeds, photographs, or datasets.
  • A weak or misleading commercial placement can be revised or removed.

Measurement and sponsor reports

Future commercial reports will use aggregate, privacy-conscious measures available to the site, such as pageviews and tracked CTA clicks. Sponsors will not receive personally identifying visitor data from this reporting. Traffic, clicks, leads, and revenue will not be guaranteed.

Sources, updates, and corrections

Live-data tools identify their source and should provide a useful fallback when a feed fails. Material errors are corrected when verified. A correction does not depend on whether the subject or source is a sponsor.

Regulatory standard

The operating intent is to make material commercial relationships obvious to a reasonable reader and to follow applicable disclosure requirements. The U.S. Federal Trade Commission publishes a practical endorsement disclosure guide. This page is an editorial standard, not legal advice.

Questions or corrections

Use the contact hub to request a correction or ask how a future commercial relationship will be labeled. Sponsor packages are not currently available.
