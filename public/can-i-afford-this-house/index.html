Method

The point is the gap, not the mortgage payment.

The first run uses a fixed 30-year mortgage term and the latest available daily Optimal Blue 30-year conforming mortgage average, unless you replace it with your own rate. It then builds the estimated carrying cost of this property and explicitly subtracts the mortgage-only payment to reveal the Reality Gap. PMI is modeled below 20% down, and the tool calculates how much extra cash reaches 20% and how that changes the monthly estimate.

Mortgage P&I + PMI + estimated property tax + estimated homeowners insurance + maintenance reserve + any priced flood premium

The monthly result is shown as a range when taxes or insurance are uncertain. HOA dues, utilities and actual flood-insurance premiums are not invented when a dependable nationwide free source cannot provide them.

Planning tool, not underwriting. This is not a lender approval, appraisal or insurance quote. Property taxes, insurance, HOA obligations, utilities and flood-insurance premiums can change the real cost. Verify material costs before making a purchase decision.

What does “Can I afford this house?” mean here?

It means knowing the difference between the payment a mortgage calculator shows and the cost this property is likely to impose once taxes, insurance, PMI, maintenance and location-specific risk enter the picture.

This is not a market-value estimate or lender approval. Its job is narrower: turn a listing price into a property-specific carrying-cost reality check, expose the hidden monthly gap, and show the tripwires most likely to move the answer.
