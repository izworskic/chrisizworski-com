What is known, modeled, and still uncertain

Method

This tool solves backward from your monthly limit

A normal mortgage calculator starts with a home price and tells you the payment. This tool repeatedly tests possible purchase prices until the estimated monthly cost of this specific property reaches the limit you chose.

Mortgage P&I + PMI + property tax + homeowners insurance + HOA + maintenance reserve + optional flood quote + optional commute ≤ your monthly limit

When tax or insurance is not known, the engine solves both a lower-cost and higher-cost planning case and reports a range. It does not claim a precise future tax bill, insurance premium, appraisal value, lender approval, or recommended offer.

What does “Can I afford this house?” mean here?

It means: at what purchase price does this particular property's full monthly cost fit the boundary I set? The answer can change from one house to the next even at the same sale price because taxes, insurance, HOA costs, maintenance assumptions, flood-insurance needs and commute costs differ.

The Fit Ceiling is personal price capacity—not market value. A house can be fairly priced in the market and still sit above your Fit Ceiling, or fit comfortably below it. The tool intentionally keeps those two questions separate.
