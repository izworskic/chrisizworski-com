Method

You enter four things. The app assembles the rest.

The model uses a fixed 30-year mortgage term, your entered mortgage rate and down payment, then adds automatic estimates for property tax, homeowners insurance, PMI when applicable and a maintenance reserve. FEMA flood context is checked from the address.

Mortgage P&I + PMI + estimated property tax + estimated homeowners insurance + maintenance reserve + any priced flood premium

The monthly result is shown as a range when taxes or insurance are uncertain. HOA dues, utilities and actual flood-insurance premiums are not invented when a dependable nationwide free source cannot provide them.

Planning tool, not underwriting. The income benchmark is not a lender approval and does not know your other debts, credit profile, taxes, insurance quote, HOA obligations or cash reserves. Verify material costs before making a purchase decision.

What does “Can I afford this house?” mean here?

First, it means knowing what the house is likely to cost beyond the mortgage payment. This tool automates that first-pass property analysis from only four inputs and shows the uncertainty instead of hiding it.

It also gives a gross-income housing-cost benchmark without pretending to know your personal debts or lender underwriting. The goal is to turn a listing price into a usable real-cost picture in one pass.
