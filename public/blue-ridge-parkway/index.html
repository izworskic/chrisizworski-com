STARTING POINTS

Know the direction before you know every stop

These are useful orientation anchors even before the live planner runs.

AROUND MP 384Asheville

North climbs quickly toward Craggy Gardens and Mount Mitchell access. South runs toward Mount Pisgah, Graveyard Fields and the higher Balsam country.

 AROUND MP 291Boone / Blowing Rock

The High Country puts Moses Cone, Price Park, Rough Ridge, Linn Cove Viaduct and Linville Falls into a compact milepost band.

 AROUND MP 121Roanoke

North points toward Peaks of Otter. South becomes a longer historic drive toward Rocky Knob and Mabry Mill.

 AROUND MP 167Floyd / Meadows of Dan

Rocky Knob and Mabry Mill make a natural shorter pairing when you want landscape and Parkway history without a full-day drive.

 MP 0Afton / Waynesboro

Humpback Rocks and Ravens Roost give the northern entrance a strong short-drive shape before mileage starts to dominate the day.

 MP 469Cherokee

Waterrock Knob is the clean short target. More time lets the drive climb through Balsam Gap toward the Parkway's highest country.
