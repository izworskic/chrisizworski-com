WHAT THIS TOOL ANSWERS

The questions a static itinerary cannot settle for you

Which Blue Ridge Parkway section should I drive today?

The engine removes hard road conflicts first, then compares route fit, mountain weather, available hours and the stops that match your interests.

Is the Parkway open?

Official NPS road status is treated as a hard gate. Forecast weather and seasonal color are supporting signals, not substitutes for road facts.

What if I only have two, four or six hours?

The route and stop budget change with the clock. The planner does not simply shorten the same generic list of attractions.

Asheville: north or south?

Use the Asheville decision page to compare the Craggy/Mount Mitchell side with Mount Pisgah and the southern high country.

What is the best Parkway drive near Boone?

Use the Boone decision page for the compact High Country cluster around Rough Ridge, Linn Cove and Linville Falls.

Can I hand this off to navigation?

Yes. The selected plan keeps stop order fixed and can open the full sightseeing sequence in Google Maps after the route is built.
