From Asheville: north or south?

Craggy Gardens vs. Mount Pisgah

North of Asheville, the road climbs toward Craggy’s rhododendron-covered heights; south, it winds toward Pisgah and the Balsams. Cloud and road access decide which journey makes sense.

From Boone: how much High Country fits?

Rough Ridge · Linn Cove · Linville Falls

Near Boone, carriage paths, bare rock and the Linn Cove Viaduct sit within a short reach. Choose whether you want to walk through the landscape or watch it unfold from the overlooks.

From Roanoke: views or history?

Peaks of Otter vs. Mabry Mill

From Roanoke, Peaks of Otter offers mountains reflected beside a lake. Farther south, Mabry Mill tells a working-life story worth slowing down for.

Waterrock Knob · Richland Balsam

From Cherokee, the road climbs toward spruce and fir high country. A clear ridge can reveal layer after layer of mountains; fog may turn the same drive into a close-up of the forest.
