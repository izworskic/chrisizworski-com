Is the Blue Ridge Parkway open today?

HOW TO READ A CLOSURE

“The Parkway is open” can still be the wrong answer

Closures are segment-specific

A closure between two gates can break an otherwise logical route without affecting a different corridor dozens of miles away.

Detours change the time budget

Leaving the Parkway and re-entering later may be possible, but it can turn a four-hour sightseeing plan into mostly transit.

Weather is not road status

A cloudy ridge may still be open. A sunny valley does not prove a high-elevation section is open. The planner keeps those signals separate.

Winter is especially variable

The NPS map notes that motor-road status matters particularly from November through March, when closures can be widespread and change quickly.

Construction can create long interruptions

Bridge, paving and recovery projects can matter even in otherwise good weather. Always check the current official table before committing to a long corridor.
