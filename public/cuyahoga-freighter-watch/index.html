READ THE LANDSCAPE

A river that makes the ships slow down.

The Cuyahoga is not a straight harbor entrance. It twists through the Flats, where drawbridges and fixed viaducts cross a working ship channel. A vessel shown near a bridge may stop, turn, wait or disappear from AIS coverage. A photograph of an opening is not a timetable, and a ship's last fix is not a forecast.

For a relaxed visit, start with a publicly managed riverfront park and check the recent AIS timestamp. Scranton Flats offers a clear view of bends; Rivergate is suited to lingering beside the water; Wendy Park broadens the view to the mouth and Lake Erie. Stay out of industrial work areas and follow posted park rules.

Know what the map can and cannot tell you.

Observed position

A dated AIS broadcast shows where a vessel reported itself. Sources can miss a ship, lag or stop updating. NOAA historic AIS ↗
