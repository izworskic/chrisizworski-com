FIELD NOTES / THE LOWER RIVER

Why this is a remarkable place to watch a ship.

The Cuyahoga winds sharply through Cleveland's Flats. A freighter may appear between buildings, ease around a bend, and slip out of sight while the rest of its hull is still following the turn. From a public riverfront path, the geography makes the passage as interesting as the ship itself.

Start at Scranton Flats Pier for the bends near Hope Memorial Bridge. Rivergate Park offers a longer waterfront pause, while Wendy Park opens the view toward Lake Erie. Check park access before setting out, stay on public paths, and leave working docks to the crews.
