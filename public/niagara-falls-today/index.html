KNOW WHAT YOU’RE LOOKING AT

Your starting side changes which waterfalls you encounter first. The plan above is limited to one country so it doesn't assume a border crossing.

Horseshoe Falls

The broad curved waterfall. Table Rock and Terrapin Point stand near opposite ends of its brink.

American Falls

A wide curtain seen near Prospect Point in New York and across the gorge from Ontario.

Bridal Veil Falls

The narrow cascade divided from American Falls by Luna Island; the Cave of the Winds experience approaches it from below.

IMPORTANT / BEFORE YOU GO

What “feasible” means here

Plans use conservative dwell, transfer and entry buffers with at least 15 minutes left over. The published schedule can change. Real-time ticket inventory, queues, route accessibility, parking capacity and live weather cancellations are not confirmed.

Canada-side attraction hours must be verified with Niagara Parks. Unverified paid stops are not included in a promised itinerary.

05 / FIELD QUESTIONS

Before you set off

How long do I need at Niagara Falls?

Two hours is best used for scenic viewpoints on one side. Four hours may fit a paid attraction plus viewpoints, subject to published hours and transfer buffers. Six hours brings more flexibility. Queue and ticket availability can still change the plan.

Is Maid of the Mist open all year?

No. It operates seasonally. The planner checks the published dates it knows and excludes the boat outside the published season; verify the operator’s current schedule before traveling.

Can I visit both sides in four hours?

A border crossing introduces document requirements and unpredictable delay. For a reliable short visit, this tool plans a single-country route. Check Niagara border conditions separately.
