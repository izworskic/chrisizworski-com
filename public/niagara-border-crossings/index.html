Trust & provenance

What the engine trusts

Primary processing waits come from the border agency responsible for the direction. Bridge operators control crossing rules and operational context. 511 and weather sources stay in their own lanes.

Authority hierarchy: bridge owner/operator for bridge eligibility and facility rules; CBP for U.S.-bound processing; CBSA for Canada-bound processing; official 511 systems for approach-road events; NWS / Environment Canada for weather alerts. Material conflicts are exposed rather than silently averaged.

Why this is not just another border-wait table

A posted customs wait is only one part of the decision. The engine first removes crossings you cannot use, then rejects closed or expired observations, keeps U.S.-bound and Canada-bound data separate, and only then compares current waits.

Diversion cost is deliberately conservative

The fixed bridge-switch buffers are not advertised as live route ETAs. They are safety rails. A five-minute wait at another bridge is not treated as “better” if changing course is likely to consume more time than the queue savings.

Whirlpool is handled differently

The Niagara Falls Bridge Commission explicitly identifies Whirlpool as NEXUS-only and states that real-time wait technology is not currently available there. The page may show operator context, but that value is not treated as equal-confidence measured wait evidence for a bridge-switch recommendation.
