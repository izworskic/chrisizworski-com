How the calendar works

The location engine starts with NOAA’s cautious 10% last-32°F-freeze date when available, then applies crop-specific frost-relative windows. The current NWS forecast remains a separate live check.

Why this is not just a USDA zone calculator

Hardiness zones describe perennial winter survival. Vegetable timing depends on freeze risk, crop cold tolerance, soil warmth and crop development. The crop rules are intentionally stored separately from climatology.

Reference context: NOAA/NCEI, NWS and Cooperative Extension planting guidance.
