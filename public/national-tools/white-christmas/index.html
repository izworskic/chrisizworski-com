How the estimate changes through the season

September and October

History does most of the work. CPC seasonal temperature and precipitation outlooks can move the prior only a few points because they describe a broad three-month pattern, not Christmas Day.

November to mid-December

The December monthly outlook becomes more useful. Once current snowpack is seasonally meaningful, a nearby measured pack can begin moving the estimate.

The final week

The NWS forecast becomes the strongest weather input. Existing snowpack, forecast snow, rain and temperatures around freezing matter far more than a long-range outlook.

Christmas Eve

The estimate is much more confident, but it still is not a guarantee. Station distance, localized snowfall, elevation and melting can still make the exact yard different from the nearest observation.
