What this beta actually measures

USA-NPN’s Mid Green-down Median is derived from MODIS satellite greenness from 2001–2017. It estimates the historical day when vegetation was halfway from peak greenness toward senescence. The MAD layer shows how variable that timing has been.

What it does not measure

This is not a live 2026 leaf-color reading, not an exact “percent peak,” and not a promise of peak color. Species, drought, storms, elevation and current weather can move the real season. Use USDA Forest Service fall color reports where current observations are available.
