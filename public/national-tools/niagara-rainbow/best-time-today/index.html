Best-time questions

Is morning always best?

No. The correct window changes seasonally and with cloud cover, wind and the viewpoint. The live model uses the actual sun position every 10 minutes.

Does it have to be raining?

No. Niagara's mist supplies the droplets. Direct sunlight reaching the mist at the right angle can produce a rainbow on a dry day.
