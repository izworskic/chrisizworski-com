Why the temperature difference matters

Blue Spring is a warm-water refuge. The useful winter signal is not simply the spring temperature by itself, but how it compares with the colder St. Johns River. When the river cools and the spring remains stable, the refuge advantage grows.

Source boundary: Blue Spring temperature comes from USGS station 02235500. River temperature comes from the USGS St. Johns River near DeLand station 02236000, used transparently as the operational river proxy.
