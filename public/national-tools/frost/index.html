What “10%” actually means

The spring 10% date is not “frost-free.” It means only 10% of historical years in the NOAA normals record had a 32°F freeze on that date or later. The 50% date is the historical median.

For an immediate gardening decision, the live NWS forecast outranks the calendar.

Why station distance matters

Freeze climatology comes from the nearest usable NOAA normals station. A distant station is useful regional context, but terrain, elevation, shoreline influence and cold-air drainage can make your yard different.
