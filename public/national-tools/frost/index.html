The date is a probability, not a promise

The 50% date is the historical median. The 10% date is more cautious: only 10% of historical years had a 32°F freeze on that date or later. A forecasted freeze overrides climatological comfort.

Hardiness zone is different

USDA Plant Hardiness Zones describe average annual extreme winter minimum temperature for perennial survival. They do not tell you the last spring frost date.
