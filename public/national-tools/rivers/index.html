What you get

Active stream gauges nearest your chosen place, current discharge, gauge height, water temperature where measured, data age and a six-hour flow trend when enough observations exist.

Important limitation

A gauge cannot tell you that a river is safe. Local hydraulics, obstructions, weather, skill, access and craft matter. USGS real-time values are provisional and subject to revision.
