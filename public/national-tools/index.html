Why are these cards in this order?

The desk orders live hazards and time-sensitive opportunities ahead of routine context. Official flood/freeze signals outrank seasonal or low-urgency conditions. This is a display priority, not a universal safety score.

One location, separate source truth

The desk does not blend observations, forecasts, climatology and derived estimates into a mystery score. Each card keeps its own source class, freshness and uncertainty, then links to the full explanation.

Michigan stays deeper

The existing Michigan & Great Lakes tools remain intact. When Michigan already has a richer canonical product, the national tool points there rather than cloning it.
