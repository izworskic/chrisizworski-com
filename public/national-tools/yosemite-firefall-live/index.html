HOW IT WORKS

Three things must line up

01 · Source water

Nearby Sierra snow-water measurements, snowpack change, temperature-driven melt and recent precipitation establish whether Horsetail has a plausible source. Merced River discharge is corroborating basin context only.

02 · Western sky

The engine samples the setting-sun corridor west-southwest of Yosemite rather than trusting a generic local cloud percentage. Near the event window, GOES-18 can replace forecast uncertainty with satellite evidence.

03 · Solar alignment

Sun position is calculated minute by minute. The score hard-stops outside the Firefall alignment envelope; a pretty weather forecast cannot overcome impossible geometry.
