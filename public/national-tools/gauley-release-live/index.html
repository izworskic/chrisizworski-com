OBSERVED VS MODELED

No fake precision.

The tailwater gauge helps detect when the river begins responding below the dam. USACE operations can independently confirm release-level outflow. Belva measures downstream discharge. Rapid and public-access arrival windows remain modeled, with uncertainty widened downstream.
