Historical timing is a window

Satellite greendown history can show when autumn transition usually occurs and how variable that timing has been. That is more defensible than presenting an unsupported nationwide “82% peak” value.

Ground observations are evidence, not a statewide score

Recent USA-NPN Colored leaves records describe individual monitored plants. They can reflect normal autumn change or stress color, so they remain a separate evidence layer rather than moving the historical timing model.

Cold weather is context, not a color meter

Freeze climatology and the current forecast can matter for a trip, but neither directly measures the surrounding landscape’s color. The tools keep those claims separate.

Michigan remains its own deeper destination

Michigan fall intent should continue to use the established Michigan Fall Color Map, which has deeper state-specific reporting.
