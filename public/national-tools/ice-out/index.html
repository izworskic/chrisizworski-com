What this model uses

Official U.S. GNIS and Canadian CGNDB lake names, HydroLAKES morphology where the match is unambiguous, historical ice-out observations and regional calibration, validated seasonal freezing/thaw physics, operational weather, and NASA satellite imagery as visual evidence. Uncertain matches remain broad rather than being guessed.
Not an ice-safety tool. Ice-out probability does not estimate ice thickness or whether ice can support people, vehicles or equipment.
