03 / Snow and ice reality check

Snow cover matters. But snow and ice are different.

Snow can limit access to waste grain; cold can freeze shallow wetlands. The official NOAA snow-depth analysis is available for checking the regional situation. It is not yet sampled into the numerical forecast-pressure signal. No local marsh-ice or open-water measurement is currently integrated.

04 / Read the movement

How to use the freeze line without being fooled by it

01

Watch northern pressure

A run of subfreezing hours points to possible habitat changes, especially in shallow sheltered waters. It cannot establish ice cover by itself.

02

Look for available habitat

Ducks and geese may move south, east or west to find feeding habitat and open water. Rivers, deeper lakes and managed wetland units respond differently.

03

Check actual evidence

Review the dates and species of reports before visiting. A report does not verify a flock remains there or that the area is open to visitors or hunters.
