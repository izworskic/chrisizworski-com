How Skyway wind closures work
FDOT guidanceBridges in a potential impact area are monitored for wind and flooding. Once wind speeds increase beyond 40 mph, FHP may deem closure necessary.
Who closes it?Florida Highway Patrol / law enforcement implements the traffic stop. This page does not substitute a weather threshold for that official decision.
What the weather does hereNWS wind and alerts can raise the page's caution context, especially for motorcycles, RVs, trailers and high-profile vehicles, but never create a legal vehicle ban or closure.

Current Skyway toll rules

Florida's Turnpike Enterprise lists the same current Sunshine Skyway I-275 rates at the North and South plazas. The North Plaza handles southbound traffic; the South Plaza handles northbound traffic. Collection is all-electronic.
