Official crossing service explained

Mackinac Bridge Driver Assistance

If you do not feel comfortable driving across the Mackinac Bridge, Bridge Authority staff can drive your vehicle. Here is the current cost, phone number, and correct request point for each direction.

Call Bridge Services: 906-643-7600 Check live bridge conditions

Quick answer

The official Driver Assistance Service is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week. It currently costs $10 plus the appropriate vehicle toll, and the driver requesting the service must be at least 18. A waiver and Bridge Authority checklist are required.

Direction matters

Where to request a driver

NORTHBOUND • TO THE U.P.

Use the south-side shoulder

Pull onto the wide shoulder just north of Exit 339 near the booth. Call Bridge Services at 906-643-7600 to request assistance.

SOUTHBOUND • TO THE L.P.

Use the administration building

On the north side in St. Ignace, request the service from inside the Mackinac Bridge Authority administration building.

Do not confuse driver assistance with a wind escort. Driver assistance is for someone who wants Bridge Authority staff to drive their vehicle. A high-wind escort is a restriction applied to high-profile vehicles. Driver assistance does not make a prohibited vehicle eligible during a partial closure.

Before you arrive

Cost, availability and requirements

Availability24/7

The Bridge Authority lists the service as available 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

Service charge$10 + toll

The normal toll for the vehicle is added to the $10 driver-assistance charge.

Minimum age18+

The official service page states that drivers must be at least 18 years old.

RequiredWaiver + checklist

Bridge staff complete the Authority's current waiver and checklist before crossing.

What the checklist may cover

The Bridge Authority's published service information says the customer must agree to several operational items. These can include allowing the employee to adjust mirrors and the driver's seat, interior dash-camera recording during the crossing, and keeping animals restrained. Review the current official waiver when you arrive because procedures can change.

A practical choice

When driver assistance makes sense

You do not need to wait for bad weather to use this service. It is designed for drivers who simply do not feel comfortable driving across. That may include people who dislike heights, feel uneasy on long suspension bridges, are worried about the open-grate lanes, or would rather not handle a wind-sensitive vehicle themselves.

Always check the current official bridge status before approaching. A full closure stops all traffic. During a partial closure, high-profile vehicles are prohibited even if the owner would prefer Bridge Authority staff to drive.

Common questions

Driver assistance questions

Will someone drive my RV or vehicle with a trailer?

The service is for vehicles, but the current official restriction still controls. During a partial closure, a high-profile vehicle or vehicle towing cannot cross. Call Bridge Services if you need the Authority to confirm your specific vehicle or current eligibility.

Do I need a reservation?

The official page instructs travelers to request service at the correct side of the bridge and does not publish an online reservation system. Call Bridge Services from the south-side request point if directed.

Is the toll included in the $10 fee?

No. The official price is $10 plus the appropriate toll for the vehicle. Use the Mackinac Bridge toll calculator to estimate the crossing total.

Can driver assistance operate when the bridge is closed?

No vehicle may cross during a full closure. Wait for the official report to show that the bridge has reopened.
