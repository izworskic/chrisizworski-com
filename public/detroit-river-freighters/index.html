What counts as live

A fresh AIS report close to Detroit

The Detroit engine only creates this window when a named commercial vessel has a recent AIS position near the Detroit Riverfront. The shared Great Lakes tracker accepts reports no older than 30 minutes, and this Detroit signal looks within roughly 12 miles of the riverfront reference point.

AIS is informational, not a passage schedule. A vessel can slow, turn, stop reporting or pass before you arrive.

Where this points

Use the Riverwalk as the viewing corridor

The Detroit Riverwalk is a public riverfront corridor with direct Detroit River views and nearly five miles of connected walking space. Exact visibility depends on the vessel's position and your segment of the riverfront.

Open the live ship map immediately before leaving. That map, not this page, is the final position check.

Why it may say no

No vessel nearby is a useful answer

  • No named commercial vessel is inside the Detroit window.
  • The newest AIS report is stale or incomplete.
  • A dangerous weather warning prevents the outdoor recommendation.

The page does not fill the space with a generic riverfront suggestion when the live ship signal is absent.
