What counts as live

A fresh moving AIS report close to Detroit

The focused Detroit engine now requires a named commercial vessel with active reported movement and an AIS report no older than 10 minutes. It still looks within roughly 12 miles of the riverfront reference point.

A nearby stopped or crawling vessel does not keep this page pinned as a current passage. The lake-wide tracker can still show that vessel for general awareness.

Where this points

Use the Riverwalk as the viewing corridor

The Detroit Riverwalk is a public riverfront corridor with direct Detroit River views and nearly five miles of connected walking space. Exact visibility depends on the vessel's position and your segment of the riverfront.

Open the live ship map immediately before leaving. That map, not this page, is the final position check.

Why it may say no

No active passage nearby is a useful answer

  • No named moving commercial vessel is inside the Detroit window.
  • The newest relevant AIS report is older than 10 minutes.
  • A dangerous weather warning prevents the outdoor recommendation.

The page does not fill the space with a generic riverfront suggestion when the live passage signal is absent.
