What goes into the day picker

These are historical planning patterns, not a promise about conditions or a forecast of checkpoint minutes. When the 2026 holiday forecasts are published, they should replace these 2025 comparisons.

  • Airport crowd patterns: TSA 2025 national checkpoint counts. Thanksgiving totals are actual 2025 screenings, transferred to the matching holiday-relative day in the planner. Christmas crowds are qualitative estimates; no day count is invented. National screenings cannot reveal the security queue at a particular airport.
  • Holiday travel size and road windows: AAA Thanksgiving 2025 and AAA Christmas/year-end 2025. AAA's road timing guidance draws on INRIX. 2026 departures extrapolate from those past calendars, not actual 2026 traffic data.
  • Flight delays: Bureau of Transportation Statistics airport facts and monthly airline on-time tables. The displayed figures are 2025 November or December monthly delay histories, never a probability that your exact travel day will be late. Specific airport data are displayed only where verified; elsewhere a clearly labeled national monthly figure is shown.
  • Driving fuel: U.S. Energy Information Administration weekly regular gasoline prices, October 12, 2026 snapshot. Fuel cost estimate = one-way distance ÷ your miles per gallon × that regional pump price. Does not include tolls, detours, parking or future price changes.

Arrival buffer: A suggested planning allowance, not a measured TSA queue. The tool gives more time on dates with heavier national screenings. Start from your airline and departure airport's current recommendation; add time for checked bags, parking, children and international travel.

Important: AAA counts expected U.S. holiday person-trips, while TSA counts individual checkpoint screenings (including business and international trips). Those totals cannot be divided into each other or treated as interchangeable. Weather, local congestion and cancellations can override every pattern shown here. If it is a long drive, do not depart so early that you are fatigued.
