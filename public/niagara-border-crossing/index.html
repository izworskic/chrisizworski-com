How the decision works

The engine first removes crossings the traveler cannot use. It then removes closed or unusable choices, checks freshness and material source conflicts, compares only the correct direction, and applies a conservative switching buffer to alternatives. A bridge with a smaller customs wait does not win unless it also clears the detour guardrail.

Authority hierarchy

  • Bridge operations and static restrictions: Peace Bridge Authority for Peace; Niagara Falls Bridge Commission for Rainbow, Whirlpool Rapids and Lewiston–Queenston.
  • Entering the United States: U.S. Customs and Border Protection.
  • Entering Canada: Canada Border Services Agency for Peace, Rainbow and Lewiston–Queenston. NFBC provides Whirlpool’s NEXUS-only operator context because Whirlpool is absent from CBSA’s published 30-crossing wait feed.
  • Approach roads: NITTEC, 511 New York and Ontario 511.
  • Weather alerts: National Weather Service and Environment and Climate Change Canada.
