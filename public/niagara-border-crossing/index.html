How the decision works

Conservative by design

The engine first removes crossings that are ineligible, restricted or closed for your traveler type. It then requires fresh, comparable wait evidence. Your normal bridge has no detour penalty; alternates carry a conservative route-switch guardrail. A switch is recommended only when the alternate still clears the required benefit after that guardrail.

The guardrail is not a live route-time estimate. Exact net-trip routing requires a separately validated, licensed travel-time feed. Official 511 incidents and cameras are surfaced as proof and last-mile context until that standard is met.

Whirlpool Rapids is deliberately lower-confidence: NFBC states real-time technology is not currently available there, so its operator value remains context-only and cannot justify a bridge switch.
