    How this recommendation is made

    The live recommendation starts with published crossing restrictions, then compares fresh official waits for the same direction and traveler type. It will not send you to another bridge for a tiny reported difference that is likely to disappear in the extra drive. Approach traffic is shown separately until a validated route-time feed can be included.

    Whirlpool Rapids remains a special case: it is NEXUS-only, and Niagara Falls Bridge Commission says its wait information does not have the same real-time technology as the other crossings.
