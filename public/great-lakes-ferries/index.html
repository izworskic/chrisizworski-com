Which information should you trust?

Official ferry operators control routes, vehicle space, ticket price and cancellations. Our calculators use public 2026 schedules and fare tables where verified and label incomplete estimates. The project does not claim real-time booking availability. Always check the original sailing calendar before driving to a terminal.
