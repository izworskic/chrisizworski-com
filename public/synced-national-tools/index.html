Mid-September

What is worth checking right now?

The Gauley's fall release season runs from September into October, so Gauley release schedule and live release status is the page to open before a paddling or spectating trip. Fall color is starting across the northern tier, and when fall color peaks keeps the historical window separate from this week's weather. Nights are lengthening and the weeks around the equinox tend to bring stronger geomagnetic activity, which makes the aurora forecast tonight worth a look on any clear night in the northern states. Monarchs are moving south now on the monarch migration map, and first frost is a live risk in northern gardens, so check first frost dates before leaving tender plants out. Most waterfalls run lower now than in spring, so the waterfall trip window leans on current gauge evidence rather than the calendar. Blue Spring's manatee season does not open until November, and that tool says so rather than guessing.
