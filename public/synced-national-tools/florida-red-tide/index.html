How to read this

Background / not present means the recent sample did not show elevated Karenia brevis at that sampling point. Very low, low, medium and high are FWC's categorical abundance bands. This tool never converts missing sampling into “clear.”

Primary source: Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission, Fish and Wildlife Research Institute Harmful Algal Bloom database. The public layer represents the most recent eight days and is exported daily. For health guidance and beach-specific reports, use state and local public-health sources.
