What this tool is trying to answer

Tioga Road is a trip-shaping route. When it is closed, east-west itineraries can change by hours. Historical opening dates are interesting, but they are not today’s answer, so this page checks current NPS conditions instead of predicting from the calendar.

  • Check current status on the morning of a long crossing.
  • Do not treat a historical opening date as a current road report.
  • Construction delays can matter even when the road is technically open.
