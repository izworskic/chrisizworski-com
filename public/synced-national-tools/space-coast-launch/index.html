How to read this launch decision

Launch Library 2 supplies Florida mission records and time precision. The National Park Service provides Playalinda closure evidence. NWS supplies localized spectator-weather guidance near Titusville and Port Canaveral. Kennedy Space Center remains the official source for ticketed and restricted viewing. NASA maintains a separate calendar for its missions.
