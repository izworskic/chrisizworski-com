Regional rhythm

The reasons to check change with the season.

This network is useful because its decisions peak for different reasons through the year rather than depending on one short tourism event.

SpringGreat Lakes levels typically enter the seasonal rise while shoreline storm exposure remains important; longer days expand Niagara viewing opportunities and Acadia begins its busier travel season.
SummerNiagara border demand and visitor traffic rise, shoreline use and boating are active across the lakes, long daylight creates broad rainbow windows and Acadia visitation makes tide timing especially valuable.
FallGreat Lakes wind, seiche and erosion exposure become more consequential as storms strengthen; Niagara daylight shifts lower and Atlantic systems can change Thunder Hole wave energy quickly.
WinterGreat Lakes level trends and shoreline context remain useful even as ice changes local exposure, while Niagara crossing and viewing tools keep weather and operational limitations explicit instead of pretending summer conditions persist.
