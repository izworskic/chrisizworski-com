What this tool is trying to answer

A Lake Mead elevation is only useful if it changes what you do next. Low water can close old concrete ramps, create primitive access, or shift boaters to another harbor. This tool pairs the current Bureau of Reclamation reading with NPS access guidance.

  • South Cove’s primitive launch can be used above 1,035 feet, subject to local conditions.
  • A raw lake elevation is not a ramp-status report; NPS current conditions remain the final access check.
  • Construction and local ramp conditions can change independently of the lake level.
