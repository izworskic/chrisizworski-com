Regional rhythm

Why the network can work year-round

The three tools peak at different times and for different reasons, which is useful for regional search depth instead of concentrating all traffic into a single event week.

SpringSpring Chinook passage builds while boating and dam-visit conditions become more active.
SummerLocks, vessels, lake recreation, tours and multiple salmon runs create the broadest visitor season.
FallFall Chinook, Coho and steelhead make salmon passage a major live regional signal.
WinterDam and lock operations continue, while fish-count schedules and visitor offerings shift to winter patterns.
