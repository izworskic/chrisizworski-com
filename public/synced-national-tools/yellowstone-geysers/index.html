What this tool does and does not do

Yellowstone National Park directs visitors to current geyser predictions and emphasizes that natural features do not run on a schedule. This page republishes current prediction records from GeyserTimes, preserves the prediction window and source, and filters expired records. It does not calculate a proprietary eruption model.
