How to read this

A count tool, not a probability machine.

This page deliberately avoids turning salmon into a fake percentage. It shows observed fish-ladder counts, the recent direction of those counts, and historical seasonal context.

  • The 7-day direction compares the average of the first three available reported days with the latest three. A change of at least 15% is labeled rising or falling.
  • Counts at different dams may be reported on different dates, and fish leave the mainstem for tributaries. The corridor view is therefore checkpoint evidence, not a location forecast.
  • Fish Passage Center notes that current-year zeros can sometimes mean data have not yet been reported. This tool treats missing values separately rather than assuming no fish passed.
  • The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers' 2026 Bonneville plan supplies the seasonal count windows and historical peak-date envelopes.
