What this tool is trying to answer

Going-to-the-Sun Road has a huge difference between some lower sections being open and actually being able to drive across the park. This tool is designed around that distinction and reports only what the current NPS source supports.

  • Look specifically for through travel, not merely an open entrance segment.
  • Road status can change quickly for weather, construction or seasonal closure.
  • Vehicle restrictions and reservation rules are separate from the road being physically open.
