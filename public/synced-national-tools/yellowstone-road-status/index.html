What this tool is trying to answer

Yellowstone is too large for a generic park-open answer. Seasonal gates, temporary closures and entrance-to-entrance routing determine whether a plan works. The live card surfaces the current NPS road page and refuses to turn ambiguous source text into a false all-clear.

  • Confirm the road system before choosing your entrance.
  • Do not assume a mapping app knows about a temporary park closure.
  • Winter and shoulder-season travel can use a very different road network than summer.
