Questions worth asking before booking

Do Lake Express schedules use local time?

Read each port’s local-time label. Muskegon is Eastern Time; Milwaukee is Central Time. The planner displays both.

Can I go back to my car while underway?

Lake Express says passengers cannot access the vehicle deck during the crossing. Bring what you need into the cabin.
