Route-specific decisions

All departure and arrival times are local: Milwaukee is Central; Muskegon is Eastern.

2026 rates effective August 11 exclude optional extras; posted $6 fuel and $9 port/security charges per paying person and standard vehicle, per crossing, are included in our estimate.

The published 2026 October schedule has Tuesday restrictions and Saturday-specific departures. Allow 45 minutes before departure; boarding closes 15 minutes prior.

What is live and what is not?

These tools calculate against operator-published 2026 reference data and your inputs. The date selection does not retrieve ticket inventory or prove a ferry will sail. All current departures, booking availability and cancellations must be checked through the official operator links above. The planner deliberately withholds a total when rates are incomplete.
