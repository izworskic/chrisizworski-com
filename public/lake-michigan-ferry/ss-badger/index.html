Questions worth asking before booking

Is the westbound trip only three hours?

No. The crossing is about four hours. Wisconsin clocks are one hour behind Michigan clocks.

Is Badger the same ferry as Lake Express?

No. Badger connects Ludington and Manitowoc. Lake Express connects Muskegon and Milwaukee. The ports and crossing experiences differ.
