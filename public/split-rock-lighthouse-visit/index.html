    Three different ways to visit the same landmark

    Historic lighthouse & buildings

    Managed by the Minnesota Historical Society. Interior access requires admission covering the historic site; a state park sticker is not a lighthouse ticket.

    Historic grounds only

    MNHS offered a separate $8 grounds pass during the 2026 regular season. It excludes the lighthouse and keeper's home interiors.

    State park shoreline

    Managed by Minnesota DNR. A state park vehicle permit applies to park parking. Scenic views from park trails do not imply indoor lighthouse admission.

    MNHS states that historic-site visitors park free during operating hours, but a state park vehicle permit is needed when its visitor center is closed. State park parking and special event access are not interchangeable.

    November 10, 2026: Edmund Fitzgerald memorial

    The annual ceremony honors the 29 mariners lost when Edmund Fitzgerald sank in 1975, and others lost on the Great Lakes. The 2026 historic site is scheduled open from 10 AM until 6 PM. The lighthouse closes temporarily at approximately 4:15 PM while names are read and the bell tolls; the beacon is lit afterward and the tower reopens.

    A memorial ticket is not a guaranteed parking spot or seat. The event has its own ticketing, and prices may change closer to November 10. This planner deliberately does not present regular admission as an event ticket. MNHS provides a livestream option.
