Built for delays. The tracker first finds the aircraft assigned to the flight on your ticket, then follows that exact airplane. The inbound trip bringing your plane to you may have a completely different flight number. If your flight is already airborne, it tracks the operating aircraft directly.

Assignments can change. Airlines can swap aircraft before departure. The assignment is rechecked while you watch. Live positions are actual reports only; the map never invents movement between reports.
