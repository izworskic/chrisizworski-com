OTTAWA WINTER FIELD DESK

Where can you skate on the Rideau Canal?

The canal is a different place in winter. When the ice is ready, you can skate between bridges, old stone walls and the heart of Ottawa. The National Capital Commission decides which sections open, and this desk shows what its latest report actually says.

      THE RIDEAU IN WINTER

      A remarkable winter street through Ottawa

      During a good winter, the Rideau Canal Skateway extends about 7.8 kilometres. Some days only a few sections are open. On others, the canal becomes a long ribbon of people heading for an afternoon skate, a warm drink and the next bridge.

      Unlike a weather forecast, an NCC opening is a managed decision. The crew measures ice, maintains the surface and closes sections when conditions change. Weather that looks cold enough is not permission to step onto the canal.

      Dress for changing wind, confirm rental and washroom availability, and use the official entrances. In 2025–26, Patterson Creek's skating area had a separate bridge-rehabilitation closure. Do not assume that temporary closure carries into another season.

      How this desk decides what to show

      Live claims come only from all 14 NCC sections and the NCC's published last-edit time. The report must be no older than 18 hours, within the skating season, and structurally complete. Missing, future-dated or unfamiliar observations become unverified, never an inferred opening.

      Sections marked Good, Very Good, Fair, Poor or Open may be displayed as NCC-reported skateable only while that complete official feed is recent. Snow Covered and Walking Only do not become skating openings. Source-defined condition categories are not personal ice-safety judgments.
