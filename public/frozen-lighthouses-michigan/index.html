Behind the numbers

Four physical gates, one cautious estimate

Cold, water and wind

Wind at least 15 mph, air below 32 F, water below about 40 F, and a direction that blows from Lake Michigan toward the pier. Missing any gate means no modeled spray buildup.

The freshwater Overland predictor

PR = Va × (0 − Ta) / (1 + 0.3 × Tw), wind in m/s and temperatures in C. The light, moderate, heavy and extreme bands were developed for vessels, not lighthouse ice thickness.

What we cannot measure

No verified winter-long wave-height feed. GLERL ice concentration is lakewide, not local pack ice. NOAA Holland station water may be distant, and GLSEA is a lakewide temperature proxy.

Onshore sectors use the direction from an inner USCG aid toward the outer aid where paired positions exist. South Haven and Manistee use the west-facing shoreline. These are approximate fetch screens, not a wave model. When widespread lake ice is observed, the index is suppressed conservatively. A missing ice reading stays unknown.

Frozen lighthouse questions

Will the St. Joseph lighthouse freeze this week?

The St. Joseph North Pierhead and North Pier Inner lighthouses can collect dramatic ice when strong winds blow Lake Michigan water onto them in freezing air. This page checks the next 72 hours, one hour at a time, for wind from the lake, wind of at least 15 mph, air below 32 F and water below about 40 F. When those gates are met, the Overland formula supplies a relative spray-icing index. A positive index means conditions could build ice, not that ice has formed. A missing or old water measurement makes the result uncertain. The webcam linked below is the visual check, not part of this model. Wind that drives the most spray also makes the piers hazardous. Never walk on icy or wave-swept piers. View the lights from shore.

Which Lake Michigan lighthouses get covered in ice?

St. Joseph is the best known winter spectacle, but exposed pier and breakwater lights elsewhere on Lake Michigan's east shore can also collect freezing spray. This watch compares the two St. Joseph North Pier lights, South Haven South Pierhead, Holland Harbor South Pierhead (Big Red), Grand Haven South Pierhead Entrance, Muskegon South Breakwater, Ludington North Breakwater, Manistee North Pierhead and Frankfort North Breakwater. These are Coast Guard Light List locations, not invented map pins. Each location has its own wind approach angle, and the page compares the same 72-hour window for all nine. A west wind can build spray along this shore, while an offshore east wind usually cannot drive lake waves onto the piers. No forecast is proof that a tower is already covered in ice. Confirm conditions visually and remain on land.

What weather makes a Michigan lighthouse freeze?

Freezing spray requires a particular combination, not simply a frigid night. The practical gate here is sustained wind of at least 15 mph, air below 32 F, water below about 40 F and wind blowing toward the shore across open water. The model then calculates Overland's vessel-icing predictor using wind in meters per second and air and water temperatures in Celsius, with the freshwater freezing point set to 0 C. The published categories are light, moderate, heavy and extreme. Those categories describe a model developed for vessels, not measured lighthouse ice thickness. The watch translates them into a relative hourly index only. It has no reliable year-round wave-height input. Lakewide ice concentration is also a coarse screen: local pack ice can shut down spray before the overall lake reading shows it. An unknown input is reported, never treated as zero.

How long will lighthouse ice last, and where should I watch it?

Ice that has formed may persist through a sustained freeze, but it can thin quickly during rain, thawing air, warm sun or renewed wave action. This watch looks for later forecast hours above freezing or reporting rain, then offers a cautious persistence estimate. It does not track the thickness of existing ice or predict the exact date when an icicle will fall. For St. Joseph, the official Visit Southwest Michigan guide recommends viewing the North Pier lights from Tiscornia Park and the South Pier beacon from Silver Beach County Park. Winter access and parking can change, so check the site's latest information before leaving. The guide also links to a beach camera for visual confirmation. The strongest wind for building ice is also the wind that makes a pier deadly. Never walk onto an icy or wave-swept pier. View from shore.
