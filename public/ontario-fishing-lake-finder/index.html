How to read this tool

Evidence first. Fishing hype last.

Ontario’s Aquatic Resource Area data is the backbone. It provides fish-species summaries and, where available, lake characteristics such as depth, surface area and thermal regime. The finder joins those records to Ontario’s Waterbody Location Identifier and recent recreational stocking data. A selected lake is then checked against Ontario’s fishing-access layer.

  • A high match score means the lake fits the filters and has supporting source data.
  • A missing depth, stocking or access record is shown as missing — it is not guessed.
  • Nearby access does not prove legal access to every shoreline or route; verify conditions and ownership on the ground.
  • FMZ is planning context. Always check current Ontario regulations and waterbody-specific exceptions before fishing.
