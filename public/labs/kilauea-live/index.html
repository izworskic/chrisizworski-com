Live evidence

Look at the summit before you move

Webcam visibility is evidence, not a guarantee: fog and rain can hide active lava, and a dark frame does not prove the eruption stopped.

Use this for

Visual confirmation of glow, fountaining, spatter and whether clouds are blocking the crater.

Do not infer

No visible lava can mean cloud, darkness, camera trouble or a pause. HVO remains the eruption authority.
