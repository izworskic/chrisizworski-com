Six official trail experiences, real return distances

Ontario Parks' parking-to-destination round trips are not the same as the length of an individual named trail. A route with no owner-published duration is not a certified two-hour or half-day outing.

Moderate

Sea Lion Trail

2.4 km return

Approximately 1 hour hiking. The rocky shoreline and distinctive sea arch make a shorter option from South Kabeyun.

Difficult

Top of the Giant

21.8 km return

Full South Kabeyun return, not the 3.3 km named summit trail. Ontario Parks reports 6–10 hours for many walkers. Cycling may cover 13.4 km of the return, then 8.4 km must be walked; park bike racks are documented at Kabeyun/Talus.

Difficult

Head of the Giant

16.2 km return

Full South Kabeyun return to the park's steepest summit trail. An official bike-and-hike option comprises 12.2 km cycling and 4.0 km walking; no designated bike rack documented at the walking transition.

Difficult

Nanabosho Lookout

17.0 km return

Full South Kabeyun return across the Sawyer Bay approach. An official bike-and-hike option comprises 11.8 km cycling and 5.2 km walking, without an officially documented rack at the foot-only transition.

Moderate

Middlebrun Bay Beach

4.6 km return

A forest hike from its own trailhead to the Lake Superior shoreline. No source-certified complete outing duration or bicycle approach.

Moderate

Pass Lake Overlook

1.0 km return

Shorter published return from its separate trailhead. No source-certified complete outing duration, parking pin or detailed route overlay.

What the map does not prove

Ontario's government trail geometry provides research outlines for four of these trails; it is not surveyed turn guidance. The mapped South Kabeyun trail start does not connect to a certified parking lot/driveway coordinate in this dataset. We do not publish a walking line across the unverified gap, trail-open promises or GPS driving directions.

Ontario Parks identifies a signed South Kabeyun parking lot along Highway 587; a valid park permit and the posted restrictions still apply. The bike cutoff at Kabeyun/Talus is documented, but no live closure, trail condition or safety service is running here.
