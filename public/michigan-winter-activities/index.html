Snowshoe through a quiet forest

Snowshoeing makes deep snow part of the journey rather than an obstacle. Pictured Rocks has a documented winter snowshoe experience.

The moveChoose a short outing and avoid groomed ski tracks.

Before you go: Pictured Rocks closes most park roads to driving in winter; confirm entry and road access.

See ice curtains and frozen waterfalls

Water seeping through sandstone can build tall ice curtains near Sand Point. Formation is seasonal, never guaranteed.

The moveCompare the Sand Point ice viewing areas, then verify today's official warnings.

Before you go: Use designated parking; do not walk onto Lake Superior ice.
