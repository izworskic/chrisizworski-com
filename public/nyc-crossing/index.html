What the number includes

Crossing toll + Manhattan congestion charge

The estimate applies the selected payment profile, vehicle class, time period and the published crossing-credit rules. Congestion pricing depends on the streets and ramps used after the crossing, so use the destination selector as a first route condition. Fuel, parking and unvalidated approach tolls are not included.

Live ETA status: Port Authority crossing and approach times toward New York are connected for GWB, Lincoln and Holland and refresh every minute. These are not door-to-door times; approaches differ. Other crossings have no connected time feed. Future departure selections change costs, not the live traffic readings.
