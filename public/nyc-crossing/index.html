How to read the live times

Real measurements, with the segment scope shown

Port Authority times for the GWB, Lincoln and Holland include crossing approaches and can be compared with the authority's historical travel time. NYC DOT Traffic Management Center links add live measurements for Queens–Midtown, Hugh L. Carey, Brooklyn, Manhattan, RFK and Verrazzano corridors. When enough valid history exists, those NYC DOT readings are compared with an 8-week same-weekday, same-hour average from NYC Open Data. For Queensboro and Williamsburg, a fixed Mapbox driving-traffic probe is pinned through the bridge and compared with Mapbox's typical travel time. The exact measured segment is shown beside every time.

No fake fastest-route claim: these official measurements cover different segment lengths. A 4-minute bridge segment and a 7-minute approach corridor are not automatically comparable door to door. Future departure selections change road-charge math; live traffic always describes current conditions.

What the price includes

Crossing toll + Manhattan congestion charge

The estimate applies the selected payment profile, vehicle class, time period and published crossing-credit rules. Congestion pricing depends on the streets and ramps used after the crossing, so use the destination selector as a first route condition. Fuel, parking and unvalidated approach tolls are not included.
