Route-specific decisions

Miller lands at Lime Kiln on the south end of the island; Jet Express docks downtown in Put-in-Bay.

Jet Express is passenger-only. Miller accepts vehicles but recommends leaving cars on the mainland for most day trips.

Jet Express has a date-specific calendar; do not assume its late-night schedule is available on your date.

What is live and what is not?

These tools calculate against operator-published 2026 reference data and your inputs. The date selection does not retrieve ticket inventory or prove a ferry will sail.
