Questions worth asking before booking

Does Miller arrive in downtown Put-in-Bay?

No. Its Lime Kiln dock is on the south end of South Bass Island; allow for the connection to the village.

Does a ticket reserve my sailing?

Use the chosen operator’s current rules. Do not treat a calculated fare as a departure reservation or a guarantee of vehicle space.
