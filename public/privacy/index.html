Privacy and measurement

Privacy policy

This policy describes how ChrisIzworski.com measures site use now and the disclosures and controls that must be in place before future programmatic advertising is activated.

Status as of August 3, 2026: no Google AdSense or other programmatic display-ad network is active on this site. No placeholder Google publisher ID or speculative advertising-cookie configuration is included.

Current site measurement

The site uses Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to understand aggregate traffic, page use, and site performance. The site's growth CTA helper records aggregate named click events through the same measurement interface and does not write cookies or local storage. Hosting and measurement providers may process standard request and device information, such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, approximate geography, and timing data, under their own terms and privacy practices.

Advertising that may be activated later

If Google AdSense is activated, third-party vendors including Google may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or other identifiers to serve, limit, personalize, measure, and report ads. Google's use of advertising cookies can enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on a visitor's use of this site and other sites.

Before activation, this policy will be checked against the live configuration, a real owner-approved publisher ID will be used, and required consent or privacy messages will be configured. Visitors can learn about and control Google's advertising personalization through Google Ads Settings. Additional industry opt-out choices may be available through AdChoices.

Regional consent and privacy choices

If ads are activated for visitors in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the site will use a Google-certified consent-management platform as required for Google's publisher products. Applicable US-state privacy messages and choices will also be configured before relevant personalized advertising is enabled.

External links and embedded services

Pages link to official agencies, data sources, maps, social platforms, independent sites, and other tools. A visitor who follows an external link or uses an embedded third-party service is subject to that provider's privacy practices. The site does not control those external policies.

Data sharing and commercial reporting

The site does not sell personally identifying visitor information. Any future sponsor report will use aggregate measures such as pageviews and CTA clicks and will not intentionally disclose personally identifying visitor data.

Changes and contact

This policy will be revised when the site's actual measurement, advertising, consent, or data practices change. Use the contact hub to ask a privacy question or report a mismatch between this policy and the live site.

Last updated August 3, 2026. This page describes the site's operating policy and is not legal advice.
