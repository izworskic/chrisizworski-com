Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026

This policy describes information handled by chrisizworski.com and subdomains that link to this policy (the “Site”), operated by Chris Izworski in Bay City, Michigan. Other linked websites may have their own privacy policies and service providers.

Information Collected Automatically

When you request a page or use a tool, hosting and data providers may process standard technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, requested URL, referring page, and request time. The Site also uses these measurement services:

  • Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights, a first-party, cookie-free analytics service that measures page views and load performance without tracking individual visitors across sites.
  • Google Analytics (GA4), which uses cookies and similar technologies to report how visitors use the Site in aggregate: pages visited, time on page, general geographic region, and device type. Avoid including personal or sensitive information in search fields or shared page URLs.

Advertising and Google AdSense

Eligible content pages include Google AdSense code. Google and other third-party vendors may place and read cookies, use web beacons, and process IP addresses or other identifiers to provide, measure, and personalize advertising. Advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this Site and other websites. Ad-related requests may occur even when no visible ad appears; ad availability can vary by page, visitor choices, and Google's serving decisions.

Visitors may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, visitors can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Google and other advertising or analytics providers process information under their own policies. The Site is responsible for its use of these services; provider policies explain their additional processing. See how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services.

Locations, Saved Places, and Tool Inputs

Some tools accept a city, ZIP code, address, or map location to find nearby conditions. A “Use my location” feature requests browser location permission before accessing your device location. Depending on the tool, a location is used in the browser or sent to the Site's servers and relevant geocoding, map, weather, or conditions services to return a result. The individual tool may describe how it rounds or uses coordinates.

Some tools offer saved places or settings using storage in your browser. Clear the Site's browser data to remove those local preferences. A shared tool URL may contain the place you selected, so review it before sharing and use a city or general area when an exact address is unnecessary. You can deny or revoke location permission in your browser and use a typed location where the tool supports it.

Information You Send Directly

If you contact Chris by email, your email address, message, and any details you choose to include are used to respond and address your request. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information through a tool search or general inquiry.

Affiliate Links

Select tool pages on the Site include Amazon Associates links. As an Amazon Associate, Chris Izworski earns from qualifying purchases. These links are individually disclosed on the pages where they appear and do not affect the price a visitor pays. No purchase, order, or payment information from Amazon is shared with or accessible to this Site.

Third-Party Data and Embeds

The Site's live tools pull public conditions data from government and third-party sources, including NOAA and the National Weather Service, USGS, the Michigan DNR, and Windy.com webcams. Loading a page that displays this data may cause a request to be sent to that provider. Those providers operate under their own privacy practices, which this Site does not control.

Cookies

The Site uses cookies and similar browser storage for analytics, advertising, and tool preferences as described above. Embedded maps, videos, and other third-party content may also receive your IP address and set or read their own cookies when loaded. Browser settings can block or delete cookies and local storage, although some saved preferences or embedded features may then stop working.

Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and no information is knowingly collected from children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information through the Site, contact information is below and the information will be removed.

Your Choices

  • Opt out of personalized Google advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Where an advertising consent message is displayed, use its choices to accept, reject, or manage optional uses. Advertising opt-out links do not replace those consent choices.
  • Block or delete cookies and local storage, or revoke location permission, through your browser's privacy settings.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the Site's tools, analytics, or advertising setup change. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.

Contact

Questions about this policy or a request to remove information can be sent to izworski@gmail.com or through the contact page.
