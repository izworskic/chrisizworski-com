GOOSEBERRY / MINNESOTA

Walk toward the Upper Falls

The short winter waterfall walk

The Gooseberry River tumbles across a succession of ancient lava ledges. In winter, the walk toward Upper Falls keeps the waterfall experience close to the visitor center, without committing you to the longer and less-maintained paths.

THE ROUTE

Short visitor-center approach, not the full Falls View Loop

WHY THIS ONE

Minnesota DNR identifies the approach to Upper Falls as its only maintained winter waterfall walking trail. Its longer summer waterfall circuits are not that route.

BEFORE YOU LEAVE

Confirm the current park notices and walking surface. The park guide is not a same-day report of plowing or ice.
