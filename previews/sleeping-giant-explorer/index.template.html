03 KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

A long walk begins before the trail does.

01

Where to begin

Ontario Parks identifies the signed South Kabeyun parking lot along Highway 587, about 36.5 km south along the highway. It is not the park gatehouse. The exact legal driveway/parking coordinates are not yet verified for this tool.

Official parking and permit advice ↗
02

Where the bike stops

Cycling is permitted on South Kabeyun only as far as Talus Lake Trail. Do not cycle beyond the cutoff. Ontario Parks describes a rack at that junction. Head and Nanabosho have different walking transitions and no documented racks.

Official bike-and-hike guide ↗
03

What we cannot tell you

This is not a live weather, trail status, daylight or parking service. A route that fits a planning budget might be impassable or unsuitable on the day. Don't use the map in place of signed park information.
