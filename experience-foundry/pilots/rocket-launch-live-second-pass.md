“For this specific launch, where could I watch, what does the current evidence support, what remains unknown, and what should I do next?”

The scheduler returns 404 for removed IDs and source freshness, not operator clearance. National Florida has no NWS spectator grid, but its existing specialist has mission-aware NPS, KSC event and local sky evidence. KSC's national event watcher requires mission ID, matching event date/URL and source health; a matched event is not ticket availability. California, Texas and Virginia can use time-matched NWS coastal grids. Alaska and Hawaiʻi lack independently verified public spectator sites and NWS viewing points. Regional inventory: California has published public viewing candidates and limited Surf Beach hours, with flight closures still unverified. Florida has conditional park places and separate admission. Starbase has city notices and possible closures overriding beach policy. Wallops has NASA candidate places but no automatic Visitor Center launch-day opening. Kodiak and Barking Sands are restricted ranges with no documented public mission-specific spectator destination. Launch-pad coordinates do not certify public entry.

## Two credible, materially different designs

**A. Mission-linked evidence journey (chosen).** First screen identifies the selected rocket, schedule precision and pad, then one next evidence question under the existing Pad → Shore → Sky identity. Choosing another mission updates the evidence stage, the map and each linked guide. When timing is imprecise, check the target; when a restriction is observed, see the dated official rule; when documented public candidates exist, compare actual sites; when compatible NWS evidence is available, advance to local sky evidence. An exact KSC event listing leads to venue/ticket comparison, never a promise of admission. Mission identity is restored from destination-loaded data, not just a URL. Compact native selector and a single visible next action work on phones. More useful than a launch calendar because it explains what must be established before a trip. Cost: some comparative viewpoint detail lives one step deeper in specialist guides.

**B. Viewpoint-first spatial decision atlas (viable alternative).** First screen shows a mission-tagged shortlist of documented spectator places with comparable legality, hours, pad bearing and NWS sky. Selecting a place changes the spatial comparison and access rationale; the map, cards and mission remain one coherent state. Mobile layout would use a native place list with optional map, no hover-only actions. This exceeds an ordinary calendar by answering where a person might actually stand. It would be effective for Florida and California, but fails as a unified national primary interaction today: two ranges have no safe public spectator place and other regions have incomplete venue and admission sources. A generic map would obscure these meaningful differences. This concept is not a straw-man false GO dashboard; it is deferred for region-specific development.

**Tradeoff:** A reuses the real six distinct regional engines, avoids another provider/data model, and answers the national visitor's immediate next question. It can advance with evidence, so it must not repeat a permanent access loop. B places spatial choices earlier but depends on richer verified place data than is available across all six ranges. Neither mandates Foundry's existing preview recipe or page skin. Preserve the rocket Flight Desk's identity and native interactions.

## Accepted evidence-state progression

Schedule source missing, stale or mismatched: explicitly unknown; confirm original mission. Hold/scrub: previous target invalidated until operator updates. Texas confirmed city closure: restriction takes priority; never supply an alternate restricted approach. Day/month/approximate-hour target: resolve timing first. Alaska/Hawaiʻi: legal public viewing question remains unresolved. Documented sourced public place candidate, no compatible forecast: advance to compare places with explicit mission-opening uncertainty. Same candidate plus a valid forecast tied to precise target: advance to NWS sky comparisons, not trajectory visibility or launch GO. KSC exact-match official event: advance to venue and ticket choices, not confirmed entry. General open hours or calendar target cannot satisfy mission admission. Conflicts, stale forecasts and missing readings cannot promote a favorable verdict.

## Connected-state and measurement contract

National Foundry action builds a region path with selected UUID plus bounded source-stage tags. Regional results confirm their own loaded ID: Florida uses its loaded launch event and roster; Starbase, Wallops, Kodiak and Barking Sands use their existing loaded API result; Vandenberg's static guide verifies against the existing national cached API. A returned mission mismatch or removed ID displays a conspicuous unavailable notice. Region apps may show another mission as a NEW selection, never as a restored original. Return links include exact selected mission ID. A→B→A must reset weather, access and async KSC evidence.

New primary attempted handoff uses rocket_foundry_handoff_attempt, in-page evidence progression rocket_foundry_stage_opened; a successfully loaded destination uses rocket_foundry_mission_restored and an unverified/removed selection rocket_foundry_mission_unavailable. Existing legacy rocket_viewing_next_step remains separate. All event properties are bounded region, stage, destination and coarse outcome/status. No launch ID, name, coordinates, URLs, cookie or personal location. Attempted navigation does not constitute restoration, and restoration does not constitute a trip decision.
