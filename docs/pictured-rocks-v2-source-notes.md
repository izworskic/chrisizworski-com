# Pictured Rocks v2 source notes — 2026-09-25

Official National Park Service facts encoded as hard planning constraints:

- Chapel Loop is approximately 10.5 miles, has limited parking that fills early, and pets are prohibited on Chapel-area trails/beaches.
- Munising Falls is closed until further notice due to storm damage.
- Many parts of the park have little or no cell service; NPS recommends downloading the official map and saving park information offline before entering.
- Recreational kayaks and canoes should not be used on Lake Superior; visitors without appropriate sea-kayak experience should use an authorized guided tour.
- Pet access is limited and materially changes valid route options.
