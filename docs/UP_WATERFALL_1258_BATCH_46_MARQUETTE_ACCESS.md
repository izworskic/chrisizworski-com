## Reconciliation exception: two different Carp River features

**GIS #163** at 46.521504,-87.572418 is the little **Upper Carp River Falls** outside Ishpeming (field site 46.52167,-87.57631). Old route from SEMCO Energy property; 2019 first-person comment says the trail was posted no trespassing. No current owner permission, legal parking or public trail verified.

**GIS #169** at 46.503807,-87.446550 is the Marquette gorge **Carp River Falls**; its Michigan GIS river string says MORGAN CREEK, but Travel Marquette places the Carp River waterfall at 46.50367,-87.44668, only about 18m from the GIS point. Its separate **Morgan Creek Falls** source point is 46.50737,-87.44516. Travel Marquette says Mt. Marquette Road is closed to cars, requires a walk or bike from NTN South Trails, then a roughly half-mile rough continuation; an independent visitor report warns final descent into the gorge is precarious. Do not map a safe final path or conflate the stream identity. This is the most promising future qualified visitor guide in the batch.

## Other decisive source results

### Alder Falls, GIS #153

State GIS point 46.780948,-87.708440 and USGS-derived hydro feature are about 114m apart. An independent field record maps the lower main cascade near 46.78188,-87.70739 and identifies three smaller upstream drops. A regional tourism listing describes a 30ft cascade, but the larger total is not one vertical 30ft plunge.

**Access:** Older field guides use the rough Alder Creek Truck Trail off CR550 and describe a No Motorized Vehicles sign at the steep final path. No current authorized forest-road vehicle status, public signed parking lot, property corridor, or bridge status established. Private home driveways occur near the truck trail.

**Next evidence:** Confirm present road authority, land title and walking easement, legally signed stopping point and the complete public trail at the lower falls, keeping upper cascades distinct.

### Reany Falls, GIS #162

GIS #162 on Reany Creek is near USGS-derived 46.573626,-87.478228 (~73m apart), and independent 2022 field trip pin 46.57355,-87.47692 and GNIS references agree this is the Forestville Road waterfall, not a similarly named feature along Reany Street in downtown Marquette. The drops appear from the road bridge.

**Access:** Michigan tourism and UPtravel recommend parking by an old power station then walking back to the bridge; the current site-specific utility/land manager's legal visitor parking, any gate rules, bridge pedestrian facilities and safe road shoulder have not been established. Do not substitute Dead River Falls private route rights.

**Next evidence:** Confirm Forestville Road bridge pedestrian safety and present lawful visitor parking at the specific powerhouse lot; preserve bridge view without creek scramble or implying Dead River upstream access.

**Sources:** [Michigan named waterfall feature](https://gisp.mcgi.state.mi.us/arcgis/rest/services/BaseMap/General_Geology/MapServer/7) · [Michigan tourism Reany Falls](https://www.michigan.org/property/reany-falls) · [UPtravel Reany Falls](https://www.uptravel.com/listing/reany-falls/3406/) · [Reany Creek GNIS map location](https://geo.mytopo.com/feature/michigan/marquette/falls/2336608/reany-falls/) · [Field account Forestville bridge](https://camphikeexplore.com/2022/05/09/a-marquette-waterfall-tour/) · [Independent bridge-visible drops](https://www.gowaterfalling.com/waterfalls/minorfallsP-S.shtml)

### Ely Falls, GIS #170

Michigan GIS #170 and independently mapped Ely Creek feature agree within ~32m. Field account records two rocky drops, tallest roughly 3ft, near mine-affected tributary and cliffs. 2025 SEC filing documents the large active Tilden mining operation in the area but does not prove title at the waterfall.

**Access:** Historic directions suggest pulling off County Road 476 and wandering through an overgrown swamp route; field visitor says no consistent trail. The right to park and cross this terrain, private mining surface boundaries and any restrictions have not been established.

**Next evidence:** Identify exact public or privately managed parcel at GIS point, current road shoulder legality, any posted mining buffers and signed continuous public pathway; otherwise retain minor-waterfall research.

**Sources:** [Michigan named waterfall feature](https://gisp.mcgi.state.mi.us/arcgis/rest/services/BaseMap/General_Geology/MapServer/7) · [Ely Falls field guide and no maintained trail](https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/ely-creek/ely-falls/) · [Tilden surface-rights corporate filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/764065/000076406526000025/clf-20251231.htm)

### Schweitzer Falls, GIS #173

GIS #173 places Schweitzer Falls at 46.407401,-87.596355, close to independently visited coordinates 46.40778,-87.5975 and 2022 field guide coordinates 46.40739,-87.59637. Waterfall described as two-tiered ~20ft, not an invented easy public park.

**Access:** 2012 field description uses an unnamed two-track beyond Schweitzer Creek bridge and a faint bushwhack; the author specifically could not find an obvious safe approach to upper tier. No present land ownership, public trail designation, signed road parking, permissions or safe pool-crossing established.

**Next evidence:** Verify public road/lot and parcel boundaries on both creek banks, establish signed safe visitor foot corridor and clearly restrict upper-tier scrambling before promotion.

**Sources:** [Michigan named waterfall feature](https://gisp.mcgi.state.mi.us/arcgis/rest/services/BaseMap/General_Geology/MapServer/7) · [Independent field waterfall and 2012 approach](https://gowaterfalling.com/waterfalls/schweitzer.shtml) · [2022 independent water-feature visit](https://camphikeexplore.com/2022/05/09/a-marquette-waterfall-tour/)

### Tiger Falls, GIS #175

GIS #175 (46.402068,-87.564648) is ~779m from the independent USGS-v2 crosswalk point (46.403279,-87.574656). A first-hand field survey describes two loose 6-inch-to-1ft ledges and a field position 46.40336,-87.57497; a broad generic directory instead calls it 12ft with far-away Marquette directions.

**Access:** Old unmarked route descriptions use a meadow, marginal tracks and cross near creekside cabins. Present private title, public parking and a continuous right-of-way have not been verified; a tiny rapid is not a reason to lead visitors near cabins.

**Next evidence:** Resolve GIS-vs-field position to the exact low drops and verify all parcels if keeping in natural-feature map; do not promote, index or navigate without new authoritative evidence.

**Sources:** [Michigan named waterfall feature](https://gisp.mcgi.state.mi.us/arcgis/rest/services/BaseMap/General_Geology/MapServer/7) · [USGS-v2 location discrepancy](https://github.com/izworskic/chrisizworski-com/blob/main/data/up-waterfall-usgs-v2-crosswalk.json) · [Tiger Falls field measurements and cabin concerns](https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/schweitzer-creek/tiger-falls/) · [Misleading generic regional list](https://michiganupperpeninsula.com/places-to-visit/waterfalls/)

### Frohling Falls, GIS #180

GIS #180 and USGS-derived hydro site agree within roughly 64m; independent field site 46.36069,-87.32913 places the true plunge/chutes on West Branch Chocolay. Tourism estimates 30ft while field report describes up to 50ft combined tiered falls: figures refer to differing drop definitions.

**Access:** Regional tourism explicitly says no official maintained trail. Independent 2018 field comments identify Sporley Lake Road north approach as posted No Trespassing; other old routes start from M-94 shoulder and follow unmarked logging tracks. No proof of legal shoulder parking, safe road traffic, parcel ownership or public trail permission. Tourist use does not override posting.

**Next evidence:** Document land manager and road jurisdiction at a potential lawful southern entrance, current signed parking and a continuous public land corridor to safe bank; reject all posted north tracks.

**Sources:** [Michigan named waterfall feature](https://gisp.mcgi.state.mi.us/arcgis/rest/services/BaseMap/General_Geology/MapServer/7) · [UPtravel Frohling access assessment](https://www.uptravel.com/listing/frohling-falls/1514/) · [First-hand posted-private approach reports](https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/west-branch-chocolay-river/frohling-falls/) · [Regional township watershed context](https://chocolay.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/2021-Chocolay-Township-Master-Plan-adopted-03.28.22.pdf)
