# Issue #1258 Batch 40: public access evidence review

Ogemaw Falls GIS #59 and GNIS closely agree, but independent field waterfall coordinates disagree by more than half a kilometre. Older signed paths and roadside culvert parking lack parcel title and road safety certification.

Silver Bell Falls GIS #195 agrees with 2016 field geography; historic Hiawatha FR2276 and FR2293 approaches involve rough roads, possible gate and wet crossing; current USFS motor vehicle authorization and parking are unverified.

Dead River Falls #160: City of Marquette's adopted Master Plan p22 documents a FERC-mandated 65-acre public MBLP recreation property in the broader river corridor, not a specifically authorized Forestville falls powerhouse lot and gorge path.

Alger Falls #213: Munising's adopted recreation plan p25 explicitly says NO SAFE PARKING OPTIONS; the city separately lists DNR Wagner Falls with an established lot and path. No shoulder guidance should be published.

All four remain research-only; no new sitemap or public visitor card. 71 curated, 65 GIS linked, 96 pending. No emails or owner outreach.
