## Source-qualified public guides

**Overlooked Falls (#97, Ontonagon County).** Michigan DNR-managed Porcupine Mountains Wilderness State Park. Designated arrival is the Little Carp River Trailhead off South Boundary Road, not the waterfall GIS pin. Western U.P. visitor research identifies the falls just downstream from the first bridge a few hundred feet along the trail. This makes a shorter stop than Greenstone Falls; use the same parking area and continue on the public trail only if desired.

**Trap Falls (#118, Ontonagon County).** DNR-managed Upper Carp River/Government Peak Trail. Start at the signed Government Peak Trailhead on M-107. A DNR visitor guide describes a 3.2-mile round-trip hike and a 2–5 hour planning envelope. Some regional descriptions list a longer route; no precise travel time is promised. This is a half-day hike, not a roadside waterfall. Michigan GIS #118 and USGS V2 are 14 m apart in the research crosswalk; this geography does not establish legal access.

Both guides distinguish waterfall point from parking, include land manager, public route, designated parking, trail effort, seasonal road closure, and source-specific citations. No artificial live water flow or open-now report is shown. Neither has fabricated parking coordinates or imagery.
