Candidate: same instructions plus the Product Engineering OS reference. Record the actual loaded instructions, selected and rejected skills, decisions, artifacts, test evidence and scope. Require >=90% correct routing, zero unauthorized external tool activations, zero violations of ownership, ads, release, privacy or cost constraints, and no loss of any baseline successes. Require at least one demonstrated incremental improvement.
