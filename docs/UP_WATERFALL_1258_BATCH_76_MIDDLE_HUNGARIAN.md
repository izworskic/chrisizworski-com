# Upper Peninsula Waterfalls · B76: Middle Hungarian Falls

Review date: **2026-11-07**. Scope: publish one newly documented natural fall with five source gates, connect it to existing Upper/Lower Hungarian Falls, and stop repetitive off-trail/unverified locations. **Online-only; no emails, drafts, agency outreach or visitor fact verification.**

## Why this waterfall qualifies

The Keweenaw Land Trust (KLT) official public-trail brochure **explicitly labels Middle Falls**, traces its public gorge path between Upper and Lower, distinguishes ten acres of KLT land, the surrounding Michigan DNR gorge, and neighboring private land, and provides precise *roadside parking* coordinates **47.173159, -88.444743** at the Golf Course Road nature area sign. The old woods-road leg is walking only; the KLT states much of Golf Course Road is not plowed in winter. The whole gorge system is roughly two miles; do not claim a measured length or timetable specifically to Middle Falls.

Two third-party middle-*waterfall-feature* map points agree near **47.17193, -88.44781** and **47.1721, -88.4477**, about 20.7m apart. These are approximate independent natural-feature positions, not official waterfall surveying, a trail polyline, or a safe car destination. Michigan state GIS waterfall subset has lower Hungarian **#130** and upper **#134** but **no dedicated middle identifier**. Do not attach Middle to either neighboring state source ID. Do not conflate outside maps of the lower feature with the actual third fall.

Visit Keweenaw’s detailed walking guide describes turning downstream at the upper gorge path split, then reaching Middle Falls after a few hundred yards. Its difficult rock scrambling/descent for lower-angle photos is **not** a safe/required public walk and is not endorsed in our published directions.
