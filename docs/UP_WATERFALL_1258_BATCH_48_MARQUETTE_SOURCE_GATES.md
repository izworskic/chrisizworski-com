# Issue #1258 — Batch 48: six Marquette waterfall access decisions

**Reviewed November 2, 2026.** Research only; no owner contact, present-day road inspection or survey-grade routes. GIS point coordinates are natural feature positions, not permission, parking or legal navigation endpoints.

## Source-driven visitor decisions

### Upper Chocolay Falls — Michigan GIS #181

**Classification:** `private-shortcut-unsigned-forest-approach`. **Publication:** research-only, noindex.

The Michigan GIS feature is near 46.343362,-87.250939. A firsthand falls catalog places a small domed drop at 46.34446,-87.25166; this is geographical corroboration, not a trail route or public lot.

Independent descriptions acknowledge that the easiest approach crosses private property. Older two-track directions and a clearing are not evidence of legal parking or a public route in 2026.

**Visitor decision:** Do not navigate here without documented landowner permission or a formally verified public corridor.

**Evidence needed next:** Confirm the exact parcel and road authorities, a lawful signed stopping point, and continuous public foot access without crossing private land.

Sources: [Michigan state named waterfall feature](https://gisp.mcgi.state.mi.us/arcgis/rest/services/BaseMap/General_Geology/MapServer/7) · [Firsthand Upper Chocolay falls account](https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/east-branch-chocolay-river/upper-chocolay-falls/) · [Independent description of private access](https://upper-michigan.com/guide/waterfalls/upper-chocolay-falls.html)

### White City Falls — Michigan GIS #183

**Classification:** `private-shorelands-paddler-hazard`. **Publication:** research-only, noindex.

State GIS #183 places White City Falls at 46.390063,-87.719065; a firsthand field record places the same named chute at 46.39015,-87.71900. This is a feature location, never a parking coordinate.

A firsthand waterfall account reports gated private property at the cabin. American Whitewater's route description and an August 2024 trip report describe posted private land on both riverbanks, and technical whitewater hazards at the falls. A paddling river route is not a certified public footpath.

**Visitor decision:** Not cleared for a land-based visit. Do not direct visitors through private gates or suggest running whitewater as an alternative.

**Evidence needed next:** Require current landowner permission or an authoritative documented public shore access point, lawful parking and a safe continuous walking route before suggesting any land-based visit.

Sources: [Michigan state waterfall layer](https://gisp.mcgi.state.mi.us/arcgis/rest/services/BaseMap/General_Geology/MapServer/7) · [Firsthand field record and private gated cabin](https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/middle-branch-escanaba-river/white-city-falls/) · [American Whitewater: private shorelands and hazard reports](https://www.americanwhitewater.org/content/River/view/river-detail/2683/main)

### Black River Falls (Ely Township) — Michigan GIS #184

**Classification:** `dnr-2017-closed-footbridge-alternate-unverified`. **Publication:** research-only, noindex.

State GIS #184 is on the Marquette County Black River at 46.392669,-87.783502. Michigan DNR identifies the Ely Township falls in T46N R28W Section 10. This is distinct from the Gogebic County scenic byway.

DNR announced March 16, 2017 that the deteriorated bridge was barricaded and would be removed, and that its trail segment was permanently closed. DNR said it planned to improve an alternate approach, but no present-day approved trail alignment, signed parking, bridge status or completed alternate-route documentation was located in this review. Do not imply the entire falls site is closed today.

**Visitor decision:** Old footbridge route is forbidden by the DNR notice; a current alternative public visitor approach remains unverified.

**Evidence needed next:** Obtain current DNR Gwinn unit map and inspection of the alternate foot trail, official parking, barriers and current site access. Never reuse the condemned-bridge route.

Sources: [Michigan DNR official 2017 closed footbridge notice](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/MIDNR/bulletins/18dd92b) · [State GIS geographic identity](https://gisp.mcgi.state.mi.us/arcgis/rest/services/BaseMap/General_Geology/MapServer/7) · [Independent Marquette Black River identity](https://upper-michigan.com/guide/waterfalls/black-river-falls-marquette.html)

### Dee Lundeen Falls — Michigan GIS #185

**Classification:** `private-access-dispute-feature-coordinate-conflict`. **Publication:** research-only, noindex.

State GIS #185 places the feature at 46.565112,-88.013696, while a GNIS-derived name record (feature ID 1617009) reports 46.5693786,-88.0354114, about 1.7 kilometres away. Do not silently merge these into an exact visitor destination.

American Whitewater cautions that access at the falls may be disputed because of private property. Another waterfall guide reports private land and cabins. Neither older paddling suggestions nor proximity to a wilderness boundary certifies permission, land ownership, parking or safe public walking.

**Visitor decision:** No verified public land-based access; do not provide directions to private camps or cabins.

**Evidence needed next:** Reconcile the state GIS and GNIS positions, obtain authoritative parcel/right-of-way confirmation and a current manager-approved parking-to-viewpoint corridor. Do not send visitors to the nearer of two disputed feature coordinates.

Sources: [Michigan state GIS falls point](https://gisp.mcgi.state.mi.us/arcgis/rest/services/BaseMap/General_Geology/MapServer/7) · [GNIS-derived Dee Lundeen feature (ID 1617009)](https://geo.mytopo.com/feature/michigan/marquette/falls/1617009/dee-lundeen-falls/) · [American Whitewater private-access dispute](https://www.americanwhitewater.org/content/River/view/river-detail/10797/main) · [Independent private-land warning](https://upper-michigan.com/guide/waterfalls/dee-lundeen-falls.html)

### Trout Falls — Michigan GIS #186

**Classification:** `creek-evidenced-waterfall-visitor-evidence-missing`. **Publication:** research-only, noindex.

Michigan GIS #186 places a named Trout Falls feature at 46.382784,-88.009645. Michigan DNR's fishing waters list and USGS station 04062320 independently confirm Trout Falls Creek, not an officially managed waterfall visitor attraction.

No authoritative signed waterfall parking area or visitor pathway was located. The USGS water station is a creek monitoring-location record, not a waterfall's surveyed viewing area or a current public-access permit.

**Visitor decision:** Remain geographic research only; a creek or gauge listing cannot establish visitor access.

**Evidence needed next:** Locate credible first-hand feature documentation and its correct fall position, then verify land title, legal road parking and a continuous public trail before publication.

Sources: [Michigan GIS waterfall feature](https://gisp.mcgi.state.mi.us/arcgis/rest/services/BaseMap/General_Geology/MapServer/7) · [Michigan DNR Better Fishing Waters lists Trout Falls Creek](https://www.michigan.gov/dnr/things-to-do/fishing/where/better-fishing-waters) · [USGS Trout Falls Creek monitoring location (not a waterfall trail)](https://waterdata.usgs.gov/monitoring-location/USGS-04062320/)

### Caps Creek Falls — Michigan GIS #187

**Classification:** `unverified-visitor-description-coordinate-discrepancy`. **Publication:** research-only, noindex.

State GIS #187 maps Caps Creek Falls at 46.331182,-87.974281. A general waterfall-directory listing instead places it at 46.3214,-87.9403, several kilometres away; neither directory proves a distinct visited drop or a public footpath.

A local-interest directory explicitly reports no firsthand account or confirmed directions. Another general directory lists an apparent short hike and County Road FX address without accessible official supporting trail authority; those details are not fit for navigation.

**Visitor decision:** Do not create a route, parking pin or promised waterfall height from unsupported directory entries.

**Evidence needed next:** Obtain dependable imagery or first-hand fall evidence, reconcile the discrepant locations, verify ownership and any legal public road/foot access and certified parking before suggesting a trip.

Sources: [Michigan state named GIS feature](https://gisp.mcgi.state.mi.us/arcgis/rest/services/BaseMap/General_Geology/MapServer/7) · [Unverified falls directory record](https://upper-michigan.com/guide/waterfalls/caps-creek-falls.html) · [Conflicting unsourced location and short-trail claims](https://michiganupperpeninsula.com/places-to-visit/waterfalls/)

## Special safety/identity corrections

- Michigan DNR's March 16, 2017 closure of the Ely Township Black River Falls footbridge is **historical**. The bridge trail was announced permanently closed; DNR's intended alternate approach was not certified as open in this November 2026 desk review. Do not claim the entire falls site is presently closed or the alternative trail is currently open.
- Marquette County Black River Falls (GIS #184) is **not** the Ottawa Forest Black River Scenic Byway waterfall group in Gogebic County. Name-based joins must not mix access records.
- White City Falls riverbanks have reported 2024 posted private land; American Whitewater's technical Class III+/IV+ material is for qualified paddlers, **not** an alternate public sightseeing path.
- Dee Lundeen GNIS-derived feature ID 1617009 and GIS #185 map points disagree by roughly 1.7 km. Neither one is a verified visitor route. McCormick Wilderness references are not evidence the disputed fall or surrounding access corridor is public.
- Caps Creek directory trail-length, road-address, height and waterfall point do not pass the independent source gate. Trout Falls Creek is a documented waterway, not a certified official waterfall hiking destination.
