# Issue #1258 — Batch 52: Baraga and Keweenaw generic Upper/Lower waterfall names

Source review November 2, 2026. GIS waterfall geography is not a legal driving or hiking destination. Six candidates reviewed, all remain nonindex geographic research.

## Upper Falls (Sturgeon River): GIS #51, Sturgeon River

**Classification**: `sturgeon-upper-not-canyon-or-gorge-sturgeon`. No signed public parking and complete route certified.

Michigan state GIS #51 locates Upper Falls at 46.621677,-88.489310 on the Sturgeon River; firsthand Upper Falls coordinates 46.621070,-88.482070 differ by 557 metres. This is a slate slide below the Canyon Falls gorge, about 1.17 km from the curated Canyon Falls point and 15.76 km from the unrelated, separately curated Sturgeon Falls in Sturgeon River Gorge Wilderness. No generic 'Upper Falls' point should inherit directions to either other waterfall.

The Northern Michigan University Baraga attractions guide and the specialist Canyon Falls article identify a maintained visitor path from the US-41 roadside park to Canyon Falls. The specialist explicitly describes an UNMAINTAINED continuation along the cliff/canyon edge toward Upper Falls. A legitimate Canyon Falls lot does not establish a signed, legally verified walking corridor, safe viewpoint or official trail extension to this exact GIS #51 drop.

**Evidence age:** No November 2026 on-site cliff, road, trail, winter parking or stream condition check was available. Historic field directions do not confirm present-day public route clearance.

**Visitor decision:** Separate mapped waterfall, not a second officially cleared Canyon Falls destination. No GIS-to-parking marker or canyon-rim route.

**Needed to promote:** Reconcile Upper Falls GIS/field position, prove the continuous public land and allowed canyon-rim trail beyond the maintained Canyon Falls segment, and verify safe viewpoint/current restrictions before visitor publication.

## Big Falls (East Branch Huron): GIS #120, East Branch Huron River

**Classification**: `big-east-huron-rough-road-ford-private-banks`. No signed public parking and complete route certified.

Michigan state GIS #120 at 46.834803,-88.073480 is about 253 metres from a firsthand Big Falls location 46.837080,-88.073340 on the East Branch Huron River. The already curated Big Eric's Falls is about 3.40 km away on another section of the Huron River system; East Branch Falls is yet another separate downstream feature. These are not aliases or interchangeable visitor approaches.

Firsthand records describe either a dangerous seasonal West Branch Huron River vehicle ford plus deep wet forest roads, or a several-mile rough creekbed and bank hike from historical logging routes. A second source specifically warns that private property lies around the East Branch falls. A secondary directory's ATV/ford advice is not a lawful authorization to drive a water crossing, park or traverse private land. No current official road designation, owner-approved foot corridor or safe public parking for this specific fall has been verified.

**Evidence age:** The firsthand route observations are from 2008/2009 and do not establish current ford depth, legal ORV access, private posting or trail status.

**Visitor decision:** Backcountry geographic record only. Never route ordinary visitors to a forest ford, private bank or unverified GIS point.

**Needed to promote:** Verify feature and land ownership, current public-road/ORV permissions, lawful parking that avoids river fords, and a complete safe allowed foot corridor to this fall.

Its 2019 comment discusses INTERIM ownership of LOWER Gratiot lands, not a visitor-use permit or permanent title assurance for Upper. Do not transfer road or parking information from the separate Conglomerate Falls preserve or lower Gratiot point.

**Visitor decision:** Historically named Upper Gratiot feature; neither a currently visible waterfall nor public hiking route is verified.

**Needed to promote:** Confirm current Upper Gratiot hydrology and precise named drop, public property boundaries, an authorized parking area and the complete legal walk to the fall before promotion.

## Lower Gratiot River Falls: GIS #137, Gratiot River

**Classification**: `lower-gratiot-geographic-match-2019-interim-owner-not-current-clearance`. No signed public parking and complete route certified.

Michigan GIS #137 Lower Falls is 47.335138,-88.444003; firsthand Gratiot River Falls at 47.335340,-88.444360 is just 35 metres away, with a GNIS-derived Lower Falls record about 146 metres from GIS. It is a distinct lower drop around 1.03 km from GIS Upper Gratiot #136. In a June 23, 2019 KNA-attributed comment, the group identified these pictured ledges as Lower Gratiot and said it was INTERIM owner, seeking funds to protect public access, otherwise possibly selling the parcel by the end of 2020. There is no verified 2026 resolution of that historical ownership statement.

Current KNA organizational information lists Gratiot River North and Conglomerate Falls natural areas open to the public; that does not prove the Lower Falls parcel is presently owned by KNA, nor that the muddy access two-track off Bumbletown Road or shoulder stopping is authorized. Historical informal directions and 2019 INTERIM ownership cannot be converted into a present-day visitor parking/route claim.

**Evidence age:** No current legal parcel, road gate, seasonal passage or signed public foot trail verification for the exact Lower Falls site was found. Historic photos do not provide live conditions.

**Visitor decision:** Geographic lower-falls identity supported; modern ownership, parking and lawful walk to the falls remain unverified.

**Needed to promote:** Resolve the 2019 interim KNA property outcome with authoritative ownership/public-use evidence; then certify signed legal parking, current road rights and a continuous public foot route to GIS #137.

## Lower Montreal Falls: GIS #139, Montreal River near Lake Superior

**Classification**: `lower-montreal-7m-field-match-variable-gates-2026-project-pending`. No signed public parking and complete route certified.

Michigan GIS #139 Lower Falls at 47.392581,-87.841233 matches firsthand Montreal Falls at 47.392570,-87.841140 within 7 metres. A GNIS-derived Lower Falls is approximately 198 metres away, and Upper Montreal #140 is 705 metres upstream in the GIS layer. The Keweenaw Montreal River is not the unrelated Montreal River by Interstate Falls on the Wisconsin border.

Visit Keweenaw describes rustic Fish Cove parking and an UNMAINTAINED shoreline trail. A field guide reports gates on the historical Smiths-Fisheries road since 2021, up to three in 2024, changing hike length substantially. Keweenaw Outdoor Recreation Coalition reported its proposed NEW public parking area and trail connection still unapproved in 2026, corroborated by local reporting dated March 27, 2026. This does not prove all old approaches closed; nor can proposed infrastructure be presented as currently constructed or available. Current gate state, designated existing parking rights, walking-route continuity and shoreline ownership are not independently verified.

**Evidence age:** The 2024 reported road gates and 2026 proposed trailhead permit are dated observations, not a live November 2026 road-closure or public-access verdict.

**Visitor decision:** Lower waterfall location is well supported; do not promise the new DNR parking area or an unchanging, authorized driving-and-walking route.

**Needed to promote:** Confirm KORC/DNR project permission status, actual existing legal Fish Cove parking and road gate status, and uninterrupted publicly allowed shoreline path to lower falls, then source trip length under current access constraints.

## Critical non-alias and public-access distinctions

- Sturgeon Upper #51 differs 557 metres from a firsthand location; don't confuse with the managed Canyon Falls area 1.17 km away or curated wilderness Sturgeon Falls 15.76 km away.
- East Branch Huron Big #120 field coordinate differs 253 metres; curated Big Eric's Falls 3.40 km away is not this backcountry waterfall. Old river fords, logging tracks and nearby private land are not visitor directions.
- Gratiot Upper #136 versus Lower #137: 1.03 km separation. Upper GIS about 370 metres from GNIS-derived point. A KNA-attributed June 2019 comment says the Upper became part of a beaver pond. Lower GIS ~35 metres from firsthand point; that same dated comment says lower site was INTERIM owned and possibly at risk of sale after 2020. Current status unresolved. Existing KNA nature areas are not automatically these bank parcels or legal two-track approaches.
- Montreal Lower #139 matches firsthand location within 7 metres, Upper #140 differs from firsthand site by 267 metres; GIS Upper/Lower are 705 metres apart. The 2026 KORC/DNR proposed NEW public parking and Montreal/Bare Bluff trail connections were still unapproved in a March 27 2026 report. An existing rustic Fish Cove lot and unmaintained tourism trail do not prove the new project completed, nor do the older variable road gate reports establish November 2026 travel times.
