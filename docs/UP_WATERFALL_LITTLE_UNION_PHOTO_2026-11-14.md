# Verified Little Union River gorge photograph — November 14, 2026

## Subject and rights evidence

The previously unphotographed `little-union-gorge-falls` guide describes several modest cascades along the Little Union River within the Union Mine interpretive loop at Porcupine Mountains Wilderness State Park. It is **not** a promised single high plunge.

The photograph at https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Little_Union_River_Mossy_Falls_Wide-View_PLC-WF-16.jpg is explicitly captioned by its author as a wider view of the Little Union River jetting across mossy rocks. It is part of the same photographer's named Little Union River gorge series, alongside PLC-WF-9, PLC-WF-10 and PLC-WF-13, all depicting little falls in the same river gorge. The file is categorized under Waterfalls of Porcupine Mountains Wilderness State Park.
