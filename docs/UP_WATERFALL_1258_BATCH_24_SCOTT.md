## Batch 24: Scott Falls MDOT-park-side visitor guide (2026-10-31)

**Promotion is tightly scoped to legal MDOT park-side viewing.** [Alger County's 2024 recreation plan](https://cms2.revize.com/revize/algercounty/document_center/Board%20Minutes/2024/Packets/24-05-13.pdf), p35, explicitly identifies *Scott Falls Roadside Park* as MDOT-managed along Lake Superior on M-28, with **parking and waterfall view across the highway**. This uniquely establishes a public visitor viewpoint that DOES NOT require crossing M-28, unlike older falls-side travel accounts. Neither page verifies the park is open as of October 31, 2026. [Munising Visitor Bureau](https://www.munising.org/activities/scott-falls/) independently confirms opposite H.J.
