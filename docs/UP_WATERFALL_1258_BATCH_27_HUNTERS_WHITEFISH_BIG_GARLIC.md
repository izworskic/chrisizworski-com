### Michigan GIS #190 — Hunters Brooks Falls (Delta; Hunters Brook)
Authoritative Michigan DNR fisheries report *Hunters Brook, 2018-248* expressly describes a waterfall at the mouth where Hunters Brook joins the Escanaba River, a fish migration barrier. This verifies the existence and setting without inventing a hiking route. [DNR report](https://www.michigan.gov/-/media/Project/Websites/dnr/Documents/Fisheries/Status/folder4/statusreport_hunters_brook.pdf?rev=74e2fa629c024423b55eee2a2064118c). [American Whitewater's river reach](https://www.americanwhitewater.org/content/River/view/river-detail/929/main) separately documents the confluence and mentions an upstream private residence. [World Waterfall Database](https://www.worldwaterfalldatabase.com/waterfall/Hunters-Brook-Falls-13167) calls the geographic feature “Hunters Brook Falls” (unofficial name) and identifies it as **Posted** on private land. That is a specialist report, not a contemporaneous official parcel check; we do not make a definitive current closure/landowner claim.

Important: The DNR report also discusses broader river fishing access and a CR426 walk-in lot project on other reaches. This **does not authorize access to the mouth waterfall**. There is no independently verified public parking, pedestrian crossing, signed trail, safe viewpoint, or current posting/condition record for the falls. Classification: natural waterfall physically confirmed, public visitor route not established. Do not add directions.

### Michigan GIS #191 — Whitefish Falls (Alger; near Trenary)
[Munising Visitor Bureau](https://www.munising.org/activities/whitefish-falls/) places Whitefish Falls about four miles NW of Trenary, west of US-41, and describes an **unimproved trail / <=15 minute walk**. An [independent photographer's account](https://www.miroadtrip.com/locations/whitefish-falls) describes visiting an informal trail and what the writer believed was State Forest land; neither identifies an officially signed parking lot or provides a DNR ownership/permission map. [USGS GNIS feature #1622606 as quoted in independent geographic record](https://waterfallsamerica.com/waterfalls/whitefish-falls-mi-1622606/) has coordinates 46.218839,-87.051534, approximately **172 meters** from Michigan source GIS #191 (46.217693,-87.050041). This corroborates the small waterfall's identity, not arrival permissions.

A different [waterfall aggregation](https://michiganupperpeninsula.com/places-to-visit/waterfalls/) calls a “Whitefish River Township Park” at **46.2122,-86.9867**, approximately **4.91 km EAST** of the state GIS natural waterfall. Its name, distance, alleged trail/park ownership and routing are **not** transferred to #191. Do not confuse the feature with the separate, officially managed Laughing Whitefish Falls State Park. A future full visitor guide requires official parcel/land manager mapping, accepted arrival location, legal parking, complete short footpath and dated conditions.

### Michigan GIS #215 — Big Garlic Falls label (Marquette; probable LOWER Big Garlic identity)
State source feature #215 (46.682069,-87.573100) is about **51 m** from the [separately mapped Lower Big Garlic Falls](https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/big-garlic-river/lower-big-garlic-falls/) at 46.68166,-87.57340, but about **3.87 km** from [main Big Garlic Falls](https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/big-garlic-river/big-garlic-falls/) at 46.66763,-87.61922. Record #215 as **probable Lower Big Garlic** and do not equate it to the main/upstream cascades or present one another's trail.

The independent lower-falls field report contains 2018 and 2019 purported owner/neighbor comments saying the bordering banks are private. The main-falls bridge has a separate 2021 visitor comment reporting no-trespassing postings. These sources are **not surveyed title records** or a current official closure notice; they are warnings against implying free pedestrian access. Arguments in visitor comments about river navigability do not authorize a land-based visitor trail or a safe river-wading approach. The [NCTA Little Garlic Falls Spur](https://northcountrytrail.org/files/chapters/mac/Segment%20Guide_NCTA%20Marquette%20Area%20Chapter-2023.pdf) is a verified public trail on the **Little Garlic River** and does not connect visitors legally to these Big Garlic falls. No bridge turnout or cross-property/river route is promoted.
