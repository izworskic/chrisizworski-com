## Visitor decision and design

A single favorable hour is not enough to compare a two-hour visit. Compare full consecutive daylight hours using the existing relative weather-comfort preference (air nearer 76F or 72F for paddling, lower wind and rain probability). Prefer the less favorable hour's score, then mean, then earliest start. Two hours is an explicit planning assumption, not a scientific optimum or safe-duration threshold. No probability of safety, favorable fish activity, swim suitability or new physical model. No thresholds declaring a visit safe. Report air range, highest hourly wind speed and highest hourly precipitation probability; the latter is not the probability of rain over the entire period. The chosen window may still have uncomfortable weather. The other three activity categories currently share the same heuristic.

Require a valid issued NWS forecast (existing 18h/1h tolerance), two full daylight hourly periods, finite air, wind, and precipitation probability, explicit nonconflicting hour identity, exact adjacency in UTC, no expired or already underway start at computation and end within 36h. Identical duplicates cannot add time; conflicting or invalid duplicates are withheld. Preserve source-issued timestamp separately from valid interval and packet generation. Retain optional endTime, rejecting mismatch. Missing precipitation is not imputed. Existing single-hour fields stay for compatible consumers; new additive decisionWindows field reuses the already fetched periods. Only the Saginaw pilot consumes it; no fallback to unsupported one-hour claim there.

Display independently of current air and water observations, with local Michigan times, bounds and explicit relative-weather-only scope. A window can be underway after load, but is never newly selected after its start. Existing 30-minute packet policy, issue expiry and local temporal deadlines withdraw it. Manual refresh only; no extra upstream request, browser polling, route, dependency, database, push notification, cron or paid service. Static content, existing hazard links, canonical, ads and other clients unchanged.
