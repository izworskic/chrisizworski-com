# Batch 38, Nov 1 2026: source-gated falls

## Dead River Falls

FERC 2002 final environmental assessment pp.151-152: UPPCO P-10855 McClure powerhouse on Forestville Road had a gravel lot for about 10 cars then open to the public. The following paragraph explicitly says the 0.5-mile upstream informal path to the waterfalls EXTENDS INTO PRIVATE PROPERTY. McClure's P-10855 and municipal MBLP Forestville P-2589 are different hydropower projects. Recent travel descriptions of a powerhouse trail do not establish a present legal easement or 2026 parking rights. UPPCO publishes McClure hydropower flow and water level data approximately every 10 minutes, but this is NOT measured water flow over each waterfall and NOT a safety/closure signal.

**Needed:** Current UPPCO/landowner public visitor access map, exact route easement across private banks, legal signed lot at McClure and current release/closure conditions. Do not use NTN Forestville trailhead, state boat launch or MBLP ownership as substitute.

## Silver Bell Falls

State GIS Silver Bell Falls #195 on Nelson Creek at 46.450945,-86.970673 matches a 2016 field report at 46.45117,-86.97035, about 35m away. Field account places it within Hiawatha National Forest, with badly deteriorated FR2276, potentially closed gate and an additional mile to a short approximately quarter-mile fall footpath. Historic field driving access is not current USFS MVUM motorized permission, visitor parking, an authorized walk or guaranteed open gate. Do not conflate with Upper/Lower Silver Falls on Baraga's Silver River.

**Needed:** Current USFS Hiawatha parcel and motor vehicle use map, lawful seasonal FR2276 gate and parking status, signed complete trail and stream crossing safety.

Sources: https://gisp.mcgi.state.mi.us/arcgis/rest/services/BaseMap/General_Geology/MapServer/7 | https://gowaterfalling.com/waterfalls/silverbell.shtml | https://www.fs.usda.gov/r09/hiawatha

## Chipmunk Falls — Grand Marais

Alger County Michigan GIS Chipmunk Falls #205 at 46.669583,-85.955765 matches USGS GNIS #623209 at 46.6697032,-85.9557194 within about 14m. UPtravel calls a different IRON COUNTY site Chipmunk Falls - Amasa near Net River Road and warns no direct trail. Another statewide catalog falsely suggests Wetmore/FR2262 at about 46.2917,-86.5983, more than 40km from Grand Marais natural point. Grand Marais Chamber public waterfall guide does not document this Chipmunk visitor approach. Nothing establishes exact current parcel owner, legal parking or foot route. Do not inherit another Chipmunk's roads.

**Needed:** Current Alger parcel owner/manager, lawful public arrival and marked continuous walking route to GIS #205; confirm Iron County and Wetmore names are separate.
