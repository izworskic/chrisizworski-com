## Source and selection rules

- [Boatnerd 2025–26 seasonal roster](https://www.boatnerd.com/vessel-passage/winter-layup-lists/2025-26/) adds fifteen ships. Its actual season URL was discovered from the older roster's selector, not a guessed retired route.
- [Boatnerd 2024–25 seasonal roster](https://www.boatnerd.com/vessel-passage/winter-layup-lists/2024-25/) adds twelve independently dated records for the same curated hull identities.
- Every new roster entry remains **reported historical / community_report**. An arrival date printed in a community roster is not independent port-authority verification. The existing ten newer and two older verified records retain their sources and confidence.
- The roster pages are undated compilations: `published: null` and `reviewedOn: 2026-10-24` distinguish research date from publication date. `statusAsOf` and the timeline retain each reported arrival date. Season cutoffs stay January 30, 2026 / January 31, 2025.
- Select the first seasonal entry. Algoma Buffalo's later lock-area move, Evans Spirit's later terminal changes, Oakglen's May shipyard visit, and Cort's summer repair stops do not become additional winter berths. Thunder Bay's older record explicitly says its berth was not given.
- Do not count long-term storage, scrapping destinations, ferries, tugs, barges, overseas construction or summer repairs to inflate freighter winter coverage. The public archive still calls itself partial.
- New geography covers Hamilton, Montreal, Thunder Bay, Toronto, Sarnia, Goderich, Ashtabula, Cleveland, Ecorse, Port Weller, Midland, Windsor and Port Colborne. Coordinates are approximate harbor references; Google Maps links search public waterfront locations rather than private yard entrances.
- Reset bounds extend east to include Montreal. All eighteen harbor markers and picker choices remain available; five comparison bars appear immediately, with thirteen inside a native disclosure to avoid pushing the phone fleet down.

Previous winter berths are never carried forward.
