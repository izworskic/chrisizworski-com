# Batch 39 — Baraga first-source geography and public-access triage (2026-11-01)

## Plumbago Falls

State GIS #52 at 46.621751,-88.503255 versus specialist real Plumbago Falls at 46.64970,-88.43816 differ by about 5830 metres. Historical 2008-2009 trips describe >2mi route from Preacher Park / Lake Plumbago on a gated, branching, overgrown two-track, with dead-end footpaths, swamps and possible stream wading. No official parking/trail or current land-title and motorized access records established. Unverified online catalogue claiming easy US-41 roadside Plumbago Falls at 46.5814,-88.3411 conflicts even more with both field and GIS positions. Avoid giving impossible vehicle route or invented short scenic stop.

**Missing current visitor evidence:** Determine exact source feature identity on proper Plumbago Creek hydrography and published 2026 USFS/owner land parcel, lawful Preacher Park access, gate/MVUM road travel, safe footpath and seasonal closures.

## Ogemaw Falls

GIS Ogemaw #59 at 46.648930,-88.512220 differs about 537 metres from specialist Ogemaw Creek falls at 46.645410,-88.507430, while a second 2006 visitor lists 46.6473,-88.5110 nearer the GIS point. The same creek may contain multiple smaller drops; waterfall identity and exact point not reconciled. Historical visitors describe a small brown sign opposite a Baraga Plains Road pond and informal pull-off near culvert, with undergrowth, eroded creek-bank paths and scattered fallen trees; no county road commission designated parking or current manager approved public trail. Independent 2026 photo exists but cannot serve as county roadside approval.

**Missing current visitor evidence:** Verify current road commission legal pull-off/parking and signage at Baraga Plains Road, exact public parcel and authorized dry creek-side viewpoint, reconcile both falls coordinates/features, inspect seasonal road conditions and creek safety.

## Daults Falls

State GIS Daults Falls #83 at 46.710114,-88.422161 is about 285m from specialist 46.711930,-88.424790. Specialist confirms he initially photographed an upstream roadside drop, later found real falls downstream near/behind an occupied house and private driveway, and records repeated private property concerns. Old advice to stop on Herman-Nestoria Road and follow the creek behind the house is not authorized public visitation. Neither legal road parking nor continuous public bank easement was established; avoid creating a drive-to-falls line or neighbor trespass instructions.

**Missing current visitor evidence:** Current Baraga parcel ownership for both point candidates, road commission lawful visitor parking, owner-authorized waterfall bank viewpoint and complete signed dry footpath avoiding nearby house and driveway.
