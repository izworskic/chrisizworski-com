# Yellow Dog Falls image identification and rights audit — November 16, 2026

## Why this photograph is the exact waterfall
1. [Commons source: Yellow dog falls.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Yellow_dog_falls.jpg) was uploaded by author **Fang Aili** as their own original digital-camera photo, with the description 'Picture of waterfalls on the Yellow Dog River in the Upper Peninsula of Michigan'. Camera metadata dates capture **July 25, 2005**. Original image size is **850×558**.
2. The image depicts a visually distinctive flat, rectangular granite outcrop across the middle of the upstream waterfall, dividing the water into a left waterfall and a smaller right waterfall, with downstream stepped cascades in front. This combination (including individual granite seams, the upstream boulder shape and the riverbed) matches the independent named Yellow Dog Falls photo in **Figure 24, page 28** of David Adler, 'Geology in Michigan — Waterfalls of the Marquette, Michigan Area,' January 2022, *Geologically Speaking*: https://cdn.ymaws.com/aipg.org/resource/resmgr/documents/events/conferences/2022_mi/marquette_area_waterfalls.pdf . This PDF image is used **only for subject identification**; it is **not** copied or published. Its caption names Yellow Dog Falls.
3. The same guide's coordinates **N46°43.720′ W87°42.403′**, approximately 46.7286667, -87.7067167, match the existing Waterfall Explorer curated Yellow Dog Falls coordinates **46.72865,-87.70674**, also corroborated by Travel Marquette waterfall map. See https://assets.simpleviewinc.com/simpleview/image/upload/v1/clients/marquettemi/2019_TravMarq_WaterfallMap_2415d39a-f458-41f1-8e86-cacfa2a7d796.pdf .
4. An independent published photographic guide https://www.miroadtrip.com/locations/yellow-dog-river-falls depicts the same split-rock cascades from several angles. The images there are **not** used.
5. Nearby 'West Branch Yellow Dog River Falls - panoramio.jpg' is specifically excluded because its camera location 46.722495,-87.962577 belongs to the West Branch far to the west. This is not an image of the curated waterfall.
