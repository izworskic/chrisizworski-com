Review: October 28, 2026. Source research only, not public guides.

## Cascade Falls, Trap Hills (Michigan GIS 67)

USDA Forest Service 2009 Trap Hills brochure identifies a Cascade Falls Trail trailhead where Forest Road 400 meets Forest Road 468, roughly seven miles northeast of Bergland. Main trail: 0.75 mile each way, moderately difficult with roots and rocks. Optional Bluff Trail: one mile each way, more rugged and includes rock scrambling. Forest Service describes the area as nonmotorized outside collector roads.

Source: https://www.fs.usda.gov/Internet/FSE_DOCUMENTS/fseprd1148492.pdf

## Bonanza / Greenwood Falls (Michigan GIS 124)

Village of Ontonagon lists Bonanza Falls (Greenwoods) off M-64 between Silver City and White Pine. Northern Michigan University's U.P. Heritage Trail Network describes it one mile south of Silver City, west of M-64. A local waterfall guide describes a short access drive, parking area and informal approach to the Big Iron River cascade. The alias and general location now have independent corroboration.

Sources:
