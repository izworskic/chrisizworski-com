### GIS #154 — Bushy Creek Falls

Michigan GIS #154 46.734481,-87.694124 is roughly 167m from a specialist creek-mouth Bushy Falls point 46.73593,-87.69355. This small Bushy Creek waterfall enters Yellow Dog River; state GIS river field records the receiving river. Yellow Dog Watershed Preserve advertises a legitimate community forest trail from CR510 bridge to first Hills/Yellow Dog Falls and separately a 12-acre Bushy Creek property DOWNSTREAM of the confluence; neither proves the falls themselves and a complete trail sit inside public land. Specialist field notes describe an unofficial fading south-bank path, possible river-wading and a private cabin on north bank beyond the fourth Yellow Dog fall. Do not convert that into public route guidance.

**Remaining access gate:** Parcel map for exact natural falls and BOTH banks, signed owner-approved continuous dry path from lawful parking, posted current access and water safety; YDWP property downstream of confluence and official Hills trail are insufficient.

Evidence: <https://gisp.mcgi.state.mi.us/arcgis/rest/services/BaseMap/General_Geology/MapServer/7> · <https://yellowdogwatershed.org/land-protection-program/yellow-dog-river-community-forest/> · <https://yellowdogwatershed.org/land-protection-program/ydwp-properties/> · <https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/bushy-creek/bushy-creek-falls/>

### GIS #149 — Upper Falls — Salmon Trout West Branch

DNR 1988 report documents Salmon Trout Upper Falls on WEST BRANCH in T51N R28W section 15 ~0.5 river mile upstream of East-West junction. GNIS #2098060 at 46.8152778,-87.8530556 is ~18m from state GIS #149. Most lower watershed is historically on Huron Mountain Club private land; current club policy says roads gated and patrolled, but precise modern parcel intersection for this waterfall has not been carried out. Upper public stream-sampling stations near Dodge City are not visitor permissions.

**Remaining access gate:** Current exact land parcel and owner, express tourist sightseeing permission, approved parking/road, fully legal footpath and current restrictions.

Evidence: <https://gisp.mcgi.state.mi.us/arcgis/rest/services/BaseMap/General_Geology/MapServer/7> · <https://www.dnr.state.mi.us/publications/pdfs/DNRFishLibrary/TechnicalReports/TR88-7.pdf> · <https://geo.mytopo.com/feature/michigan/marquette/falls/2098060/upper-falls/> · <https://huronmountainclub.org/guest-info/> · <https://www.micorps.net/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/VSMP-YellowDog-QAPP.pdf>

### GIS #150 — Twin Falls — East Branch Salmon Trout

State source GIS Twin #150 46.806661,-87.848926 is ~40m from independent field Twin 46.80701,-87.84904, on the EAST Branch, not WEST Upper or mainstem Middle. Specialist September 2026 updated article distinguishes Twin from separate Hogback farther downstream across Huron Mountain Club line and warns old private-camp routes. Suggested tiny Commercial Forest corner access plus nine-mile bushwhack has no independently verified complete parcel/easement or legal tourist parking. Michigan DNR explicitly limits the public right on CF land to foot access for HUNTING/FISHING/TRAPPING; ordinary waterfall sightseeing and photography require owner permission, including beyond the small CF entry. Monitoring site STEB1 elsewhere on East Branch uses private JM Longyear gate.

**Remaining access gate:** Current DNR CF parcel overlay and authorized SIGHTSEEING permission for every segment, signed public lot, continuous walking corridor and safety/seasonal access before visitor guide. Do not promote a 9mi CF hunting right as general tourism.

Evidence: <https://gisp.mcgi.state.mi.us/arcgis/rest/services/BaseMap/General_Geology/MapServer/7> · <https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/east-branch-salmon-trout-river/twin-falls/> · <https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/east-branch-salmon-trout-river/dodge-city-falls/> · <https://www.michigan.gov/en/dnr/managing-resources/forestry/private/cf> · <https://huronmountainclub.org/guest-info/> · <https://www.micorps.net/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/VSMP-YellowDog-QAPP.pdf>

### GIS #151 — Middle Falls — Salmon Trout Mainstem

DNR fisheries report 88-7 places Middle Falls on MAINSTEM Salmon Trout River in section 14 about one river mile downstream of East-West junction, separate from East Twin and West Upper. GNIS #2098058 46.8111111,-87.8258333 ~177m from GIS #151 46.811603,-87.828052. DNR describes majority of lower basin inside private Huron Mountain Club, club policy gates/patrols roads, and 2022 MiCorps document identifies a DIFFERENT nearby Lower Dam monitoring station as HMC-private. Neither report is an exact current title intersect, and neither provides tourist parking or a permitted footpath. No confusion with Marquette Dead River Middle Falls.

**Remaining access gate:** Confirm current site parcel and manager, express visitor access, signed public trail and road parking plus present conditions; scientific monitoring access alone does not qualify.
