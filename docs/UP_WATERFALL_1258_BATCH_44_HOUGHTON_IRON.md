### Important source contradictions

**Manakiki:** The USGS GNIS-derived waterfall coordinate at [AnyplaceAmerica](https://www.anyplaceamerica.com/directory/mi/gogebic-county-26053/falls/manakiki-falls-631372/) agrees with Michigan state GIS within ~12m. The [Western U.P. regional waterfall map](https://explorewesternup.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/WaterfallMapBro_2022_Drft6-opt.pdf) lists Manakiki at `46.643023,-90.113405` (~273m from state GIS) and Maple Creek Falls at `46.646222,-90.113408` (~356m from its Manakiki point), even though its text describes Manakiki as *150 feet upstream* of Maple Creek Falls. The tourist map cannot be used as a geometric trail or parking line without resolving the disagreement. [Western U.P. Tourism](https://explorewesternup.com/things-to-do/waterfalls/) describes a gated-road/bridge footpath. Direct USFS PADUS, DNR state land/easement point queries and NFS trail search do not clear actual ownership, parking or route.

**Onion:** The [specialist source](https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/onion-creek/onion-falls/) field-maps a ~2ft slide at `46.56974,-88.98429` (~194m from state GIS), below a sandstone gorge. Official Forest Service published trails within 1km include **NFST-1137, NFST-1129, NFST-835**, but the exact GIS point had no USFS surface-parcel intersection; **none** of these trails was verified to lead to the falls. Old informal meadow parking on logging forest roads is not a signed legally established visitor arrival.

**Vista:** [Waterfalls of the Keweenaw documented three fruitless searches](https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/journal/third-failure-at-vista-falls/) and [Upper Michigan's directory](https://upper-michigan.com/guide/waterfalls/vista-falls.html) says the old named fall probably does not exist. The state GIS name remains a research label, with **no verified natural falls**. USFS parcel #120 and NFS segments **NFST-1664 and -1708** intersect/approach the mapped geographic area, but neither establishes a waterfall. Candidate is **`historic-map-feature-waterfall-unconfirmed`**, not a destination or live waterfall.

**West Branch Sturgeon:** [Visit Keweenaw's public 2022 visitor account](https://www.visitkeweenaw.com/blog/post/a-guide-to-exploring-hogger-falls-west-branch-sturgeon-falls/) documents a challenging Newberry Road/Forest Road 1755 hike but admits the final approach lacks a trail; snow-plowing and private driveways complicate parking. [Independent field account](https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/west-branch-sturgeon-river/west-branch-sturgeon-falls/) maps the natural drops near `46.72165,-88.799`. The tourism natural falls point `46.7217,-88.799` is ~526m west of original state GIS #85 `46.722406,-88.79218`; a GNIS-derived point is another ~283m east of the GIS point. These points may reference different drops in a longer river sequence, so do not create an auto-navigated visitor trail. Official USDA FS-managed surface parcel #120 intersects **both** state GIS and tourism natural falls coordinates, establishing Ottawa NF land at those points, NOT a signed legal parking-to-falls walk. USDA FS published trails within 1km at the state point: **none**.

**Ripley:** The [independent field report](https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/ripley-creek/ripley-falls/) describes a seasonal creek on the side of the ski area and historic advice to park near a now-private apartment building, avoiding yards. [Michigan Technological University](https://www.mtu.edu/mont-ripley/) operates Mont Ripley for skiing/snowboarding. This does not grant a public waterfall hiking easement, winter gate state or apartment parking rights. No federal/NFS/state parcel hit at exact point means no independent title for the precise creek section.

**Margeson:** Local [Iron County waterfall account](https://upper-michigan.com/guide/waterfalls/margeson-falls.html) reports a ~1mi historic approach beginning across private property, no sanctioned public entrance. In contrast, [Michigan DNR LOTS official point query](https://services3.arcgis.com/Jdnp1TjADvSDxMAX/ArcGIS/rest/services/DNRLOTSParcelsOPENDATA/FeatureServer/0/query?geometry=-88.246589%2C46.220319&geometryType=esriGeometryPoint&inSR=4326&spatialRel=esriSpatialRelIntersects&outFields=*&returnGeometry=false&f=pjson) intersects DNR Forest Resources Division state parcel `ObjectID 108172`, 40 acres, at the natural falls. Land ownership **at a destination is not legal permission to cross intervening private land**. A potential new approach would require verified state/road land continuity and legal signed visitor parking.
