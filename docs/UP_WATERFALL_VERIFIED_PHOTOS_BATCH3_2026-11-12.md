### Why these are the right falls

- **Scott Falls:** Photographer NMMIMAJ's own photograph, Commons Scott Falls 1, dated 7 June 2011, specifically describes Scott Falls at Michigan M-28 near Au Train and depicts the roadside sandstone alcove waterfall. 
- **Bridalveil Falls:** NPS Pictured Rocks NPGallery accession 1262f1c4-19a7-4682-8ae0-f4b0d928ae26 describes Bridalveil Falls descending a sheer cliff and sloping sandstone to Lake Superior, taken 20 October 2019; waterfront Bridalveil image visually inspected. Original archive: https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/1262f1c4-19a7-4682-8ae0-f4b0d928ae26
- **Elliot Falls:** NPS NPGallery accession 16afa580-8da9-42d2-babb-1cc99da0186b identifies the low Elliot Falls ledge at the east end of Miners Beach entering windy Lake Superior, October 6 2018; image not Miners Falls inland. Original archive: https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/16afa580-8da9-42d2-babb-1cc99da0186b
- **Manido Falls:** Christopher J. Mowery's self-published Commons photograph explicitly shows Manido Falls on the Presque Isle River, Porcupine Mountains, 11 October 2008, visually distinct from upper Nawadaha and lower Manabezho. 
- **O Kun de Kun Falls:** Fondycardinals' geotagged 26 April 2021 photo explicitly identifies the main O Kun de Kun Falls near Ontonagon County Baltimore River, camera 46.651425 -89.152358. This is the larger downstream fall, not smaller upstream Konteka Falls. 
- **Gorge Falls:** Mr.Z-man original Commons file named Gorge falls, dated July 2006, specifically depicts Gorge Falls on Gogebic County Michigan Black River, clearly a forceful plunge between narrow dark gorge walls. 
- **Eagle River Falls:** Photographer Chris857's self-uploaded high-flow Eagle River Falls image, with camera coordinates 47.412500 -88.296700 in Eagle River, Keweenaw County near its mouth at Lake Superior. The Commons page explicitly says capture date unknown, so upload date must not be presented as photo date. 
- **Houghton-Douglass Falls:** Geotagged original photographer Phoenix-Five 20 July 2022 image precisely identifies Douglass Houghton Falls in Houghton County at 47.206833 -88.426986. Camera view of multi-tier rock cliff corresponds to the named Hammell Creek falls; historic image not access approval. 
- **Bonanza Falls:** U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service Midwest Flickr original image 16902244964 explicitly titled Bonanza Falls near Silver City, Michigan, photographed by agency photographer Bobbie Halchishak on 17 October 2011; Commons FlickreviewR verified Public Domain Mark on 25 March 2025. Original archive: https://www.flickr.com/photos/usfwsmidwest/16902244964
- **Gabbro Falls:** Original Panoramio photographer Ian Shackleford explicitly identifies Gabbro Falls, 20 April 2007; Commons Panoramio license bot confirmed CC BY 3.0 in January 2015. Camera 46.502081 -89.992104 in Black River Basin near Gabbro Falls, not adjacent falls. Original archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20161009224840/http://www.panoramio.com/photo/1858188
- **Yondota Falls:** Ian Shackleford original 21 May 2004 Panoramio image explicitly identifies Yondota Falls on Presque Isle River, Gogebic County Ottawa National Forest; CC BY 3.0 preserved in Commons upload bot review of April 2015. Original archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20150704023016/http://www.panoramio.com/photo/3179479
- **Lepisto Falls:** Ian Shackleford original Panoramio image 2194944 12 May 2007 explicitly describes Lepisto Falls on the Presque Isle River in Michigan DNR land, Gogebic County. Commons Panoramio upload bot reviewed CC BY 3.0, visually confirms broad low river cascade. Original archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20161010085741/http://www.panoramio.com/photo/2194944
- **Jumbo Falls:** Ian Shackleford's original 19 May 2004 Panoramio photo 1877787 expressly identifies Jumbo Falls on the Jumbo River in Ottawa National Forest, Houghton County. This is the named river waterfall, not nearby misleading map marker. Original archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20161013015851/http://www.panoramio.com/photo/1877787

## Release safeguards
- Image URL, original creator and license are visible in the same `figcaption` as each image. A separate registry records original source, legacy archival information, identity evidence and capture date.
- No image is mislabeled as real-time flow or as proof of public visitor access. **Gorge Falls** retains restricted approach language; **Houghton-Douglass Falls** is still a developing scenic site; **Scott Falls** retains M-28 crossing safety context; **Jumbo Falls** remains a natural-feature location, not a vehicle parking point.
- All 13 photographs use Wikimedia Commons 960px thumbnails without intentional crop or retouching. External hosting remains a delivery dependency; an independently reliable local asset pipeline is still a worthwhile future improvement.
- Only the 13 edited guides have a new `2026-11-12` JSON-LD `dateModified` and corresponding `sitemap-up-waterfalls.xml` lastmod entries. We did not use a mass sitemap timestamp update.
- No fake photos on legal-access research holds. The NPS *Chapel Falls* image in the source set is documented as largely screened by vegetation; another historical candidate is only 515px across. Both are held rather than used as a poor hero image.
- **Coverage is not complete**: `data/up-waterfall-photo-review-queue.json` lists each of the remaining 53 unphotographed entries from the authoritative 80-ID catalog. Some entries are closed/research-only or covered by a separate flagship tool; do not infer these need an indexed guide, safe parking directions, or a promotional photo.

## Sources and licensing precision
- Michigan DNR public and commercial photo gallery: https://michigan-dnr.canto.com/v/DNRPublicPortfolio. This is not a blanket license for any photo embedded on Michigan.gov, and permission must be checked per gallery asset.
- NPS original photographs: https://www.nps.gov/piro/learn/photosmultimedia/waterfalls-gallery.htm. NPS official-duty photos are public domain; partner photos and other externally contributed images require independent rights review.
- Flickr Commons-reviewed and Panoramio preserved files: the exact file and uploader metadata are recorded above. CC BY and CC BY-SA allow commercial reuse when original credit, license and change terms are honored; each photo is individually checked.
- The user-facing waterfall guide is not an authoritative public access map. Historic waterfall photography does not lift seasonal, private-property or trail-closure restrictions.
