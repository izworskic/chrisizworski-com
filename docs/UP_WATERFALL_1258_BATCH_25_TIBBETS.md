## Batch 25 — Tibbets Falls public State land/NCT guide (2026-10-31)

**Official land/trail evidence:** [Peter Wolfe Chapter's April 2025 North Country Trail segments 33–46](https://northcountrytrail.org/files/chapters/pwc/PWC_33-46_Apr2025.pdf) identifies State of Michigan ownership for Sturgeon River segments 44,45,46, an explicitly **named Tibbets Falls parking area** on the west side of Baraga Plains Road, NCT blue blazes, signposts, and 2025 riverbank trail maintenance. Segment 44 describes a long series of low slate drops visible directly from the NCT while the trail ascends the bluff. NCTA warns slate rocks are slippery, and sandy westbound two-tracks are **unmaintained and unsuitable to recommend for driving**. [NCTA March 2024 Segments 47–58](https://northcountrytrail.org/files/chapters/pwc/PWC_47-58_Mar2024.pdf) separately corroborates the named lot. NCTA's GPS list is from 2014 and no precise current lot GPS or new walking track is fabricated. [Independent field waterfall coordinate](https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/sturgeon-river/tibbets-falls/) within ~55m of [state GIS #44](https://gisp.mcgi.state.mi.us/arcgis/rest/services/BaseMap/General_Geology/MapServer/7) corroborates physical identity. This is a safe, maintained **trail-based view**, not a river crossing, scrubby 2-track or direct GIS waypoint walk.
