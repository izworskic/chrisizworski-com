## Two physically different Tioga visitor experiences
**Public roadside-park cascades:** [MDOT April 2026 Tioga Creek Roadside Park notice](https://www.michigan.gov/mdot/news-outreach/pressreleases/2026/04/21/most-mdot-roadside-parks-reopening-april-28) identifies a genuine seasonal managed park on US-41/M-28, delayed opening that spring, and cautions parks often close in late October. [Outdoor Michigan park/owner reference](https://outdoormichigan.org/feature/11732) puts roadside facility at 46.57515,-88.33961; [Michigan DEQ/EPA Tioga River state park sample](https://www.waterqualitydata.us/provider/STORET/21MICH_WQX/21MICH_WQX-070070/) places Tioga River at US41 park 46.57527,-88.34066. [Independent June 2015 falls survey](https://gowaterfalling.com/waterfalls/minorfallsT-Z.shtml) places small Tioga *Creek roadside park falls* around 46.5734,-88.3398 (near 195m from park), notes lower cascade visible at/near park and short marked upstream path. [Later photographed report](https://www.miroadtrip.com/locations/tioga-park-falls-park) finds park crossing but overgrown/fallen-tree path. Visitor guide is deliberately PARK-SIDE view; uncertain current trail = turn back. Park seasonal gate not confirmed late Oct.

**Distinct, unsafe natural GIS source Tioga Falls #46:** Michigan GIS 46.593805,-88.348735 and [specialist GIS-matching field record](https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/tioga-river/tioga-falls/) describe a stronger cascade with a signed private camp/gate. It's approximately **2.19km** from Tioga Roadside Park, so the MDOT legal parking is NOT lawful arrival for GIS #46, not a trail/easement and no walking shortcut to private feature. Preserve source #46 as research-only with detailed warning and link to separate public alternative.
