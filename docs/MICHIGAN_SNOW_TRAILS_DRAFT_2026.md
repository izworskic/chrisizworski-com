# Michigan Snow Trails: working draft

Intent: help a visitor choose a real place for snowshoeing, Nordic skiing, or winter fat biking, and then connect them to the relevant specialist tools and official trail managers. It is an inspiration and discovery guide, not a live trail-status service.

The draft intentionally remains noindex and unlinked. The unmerged seven-guide prototype (PR #1221) supplied the initial structure; the draft now uses seven individual destinations or named winter trail systems. Research consulted October 28, 2026:

- Munising snowshoe route: https://www.nps.gov/piro/planyourvisit/winteractivities.htm. Munising Falls closure: https://www.nps.gov/places/munising-falls.htm.
- Vasa Winter Sports Singletrack: https://www.michigan.gov/recsearch/trails/vasa-pathway.
- Porcupine Mountains winter sports routes: https://www.michigan.gov/skitheporkies/activities/xc-ski-snowshoe-bike.
- Corsair skiing: https://www.fs.usda.gov/Internet/FSE_DOCUMENTS/stelprd3807291.pdf and https://www.fs.usda.gov/emc/nepa/revisions/includes/docs/infrastructure/recsitestrails/corsair-ea.pdf.
- Sleeping Bear Heritage Trail: https://www.nps.gov/slbe/planyourvisit/sbht.htm and https://www.nps.gov/places/000/sleeping-bear-heritage-trail-dune-climb-trailhead.htm.
- Silver Lake fat-tire rules: https://www.michigan.gov/dnr/things-to-do/winter/bike/silver-lake-fat-tire.
- Blueberry Ridge: https://www.michigan.gov/recsearch/trails/blueberry-ridge-pathway.
