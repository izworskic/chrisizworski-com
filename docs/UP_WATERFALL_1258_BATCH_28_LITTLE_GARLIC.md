# Issue 1258 batch 28 — Little Garlic Falls: verified NCTA public visitor spur
Reviewed 2026-10-31 from public online sources only. No emails/drafts/outreach.

## Public-visitor source gate
- Location: Michigan state GIS #157 at 46.667721,-87.578452 on Little Garlic River, **NOT** the separately disputed Big Garlic GIS #215. https://gisp.mcgi.state.mi.us/arcgis/rest/services/BaseMap/General_Geology/MapServer/7
- Land manager: State of Michigan Elliott Donnelley Wilderness; NCTA Marquette Area Chapter documented in its [official 2023 Segment 19 guide](https://northcountrytrail.org/files/chapters/mac/Segment%20Guide_NCTA%20Marquette%20Area%20Chapter-2023.pdf).
- Public route: named official Little Garlic Falls Spur from Harrison/Little Garlic Road, one mile each way with 118ft ascent, rocky path and two tributary bridges. NCTA advises Avenza Map #41. https://northcountrytrail.org/files/chapters/mac/Segment%20Guide_NCTA%20Marquette%20Area%20Chapter-2023.pdf
- Public parking: named western trailhead parking about two miles after CR550 turn onto Harrison/Little Garlic Rd, corroborated by [Michigan tourism](https://www.michigan.org/property/little-garlic-falls). Chapter 2023 Google driving map NOT verified; [current chapter maps July 2026](https://northcountrytrail.org/trail/michigan/mac/) supersede. Road, parking, plowing/bridges status not live.
- Alternate: [Travel Marquette](https://www.travelmarquette.com/outdoor-adventure/waterfalls/) lists roughly 2.2-mile short, 7.6-mile long options; no crossing of main river or private Big Garlic banks recommended. [Trail alerts](https://northcountrytrail.org/the-trail/trail-alerts/) before departure.
