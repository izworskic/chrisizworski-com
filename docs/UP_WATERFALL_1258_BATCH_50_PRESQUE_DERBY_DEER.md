# Issue #1258 — Batch 50: six Ontonagon geographic and access investigations

Reviewed November 2, 2026. All six remain research-only; a source point alone is never a public driving destination or an approved signed trail.

## Minnewawa Falls (GIS #54)
The Michigan GIS point (46.597361,-89.848944) lies about 55 metres from a GNIS-derived feature at 46.597169,-89.849610. Federal Wild and Scenic River documentation names Minnewawa Falls as a designated river-reach boundary. World Waterfall Database instead describes two low rapids chutes with roughly five feet of total drop, not an established large single-plunge waterfall.

Federal river designation does not establish a signed waterfall trail. No land-manager-authorized parking area, current road opening, permitted bank approach or safe continuous visitor route was found. A generic tourism listing proposes distant South Boundary Road coordinates that do not match the GIS feature.

Public decision: Research-only named rapids. Do not use its waterfall point for directions.
Gate: Verify land tenure, named river feature and current Ottawa National Forest or other manager permission; locate signed public parking and a complete authorized walking route before ever promoting.

## Nimikon Falls (GIS #57)
Michigan GIS #57 at 46.605242,-89.853424 is about 42 metres from the GNIS-derived 46.604947,-89.853777 record. World Waterfall Database classifies Nimikon as unconfirmed segmented rapids, with an estimated 15-foot drop accumulated around an island rather than a proven 15-foot vertical plunge.

Regional lists identify the name but no signed, maintained hiking route or manager-approved lot. Distant generic Firesteel Road coordinates contradict the GIS and GNIS point. Backcountry land rights, trail status, approach and safe viewpoint remain unverified.

Public decision: Retain as river-geography research, not an established public waterfall hike.
Gate: Confirm watercourse identity and land manager; require signed parking and a continuous legally authorized riverbank walk before promotion.

## Rapid River Falls (Ontonagon) (GIS #60)
The Michigan GIS #60 feature at 46.627099,-89.668576 is about 11 metres from GNIS-derived Rapid River Falls #635651 (46.627152,-89.668691). Despite that near match, the GIS river attribute says DEER CREEK, whereas the Lake Gogebic Chamber puts the falls on RAPID RIVER. Name/location agreement does not settle the waterway conflict. This is not the Rapid River Falls County Park in Delta County.

No authoritative parking, public land approach or designated hiking trail is identified. GNIS locations describe natural features, not legal vehicle stops. A directory's twelve-foot height estimate is not an independently measured fall.

Public decision: Geographic name supported; stream identity and legal visit unresolved. No driving route.
Gate: Resolve DEER CREEK versus RAPID RIVER attribute with authoritative hydrography and land manager, then certify current road status, public parking and a safe continuous footpath; protect the separate Delta County guide.

## Hidden Falls (Derby Creek) (GIS #61)
Michigan GIS #61 locates Hidden Falls on DERBY CREEK at 46.631427,-89.579969. A firsthand Hidden Falls account on WEIDMAN OUTLET CREEK lies about 1.58 kilometres away at 46.64478,-89.57298. A generic CASCADE CREEK Hidden Falls listing is about 33 kilometres away. Derby Falls GIS #62 is about 407 metres from this Hidden GIS point. ## Derby Falls (GIS #62)

World Waterfall Database calls its existence unknown and describes possible seasonal small drops in a different drainage. Hidden Falls GIS #61 is about 407 metres away. A mapped name is not an independent proof of a distinct visible waterfall.

No authoritative photos tied to the precise feature, land title, safe public parking, signed walking route or independent verified physical drop. Nearby Weidman Creek Hidden Falls hiking directions cannot be transferred to Derby Creek.

Public decision: Unknown physical waterfall and public access status. Research only; no navigation.

## Deer Creek Falls (GIS #72)
Michigan GIS #72 at 46.652344,-89.697161 matches GNIS-derived Deer Creek Falls #1622343 (46.652425,-89.697208) within roughly 10 metres. Lake Gogebic Chamber lists the falls northwest of Bergland. American Whitewater recognizes a named drop on White Deer Creek but infers a roughly 30-foot drop from contour intervals, not a measured survey. A generic waterfall-directory pin is around 50 km to the east and must not drive navigation.

The American Whitewater listing describes speculative paddling put-ins and has no trip reports; the drainage above the falls is only about two square miles. These do not prove a signed, lawful walk, legal parking, actual 30-foot plunge or reliable current flow.

Public decision: Named headwater drop, but visitor approach and reliable flow unverified; no driving route.
Gate: Confirm Deer versus White Deer stream labels, exact land manager, public right-of-way, approved road stopping and continuous safe trail; independently check fall height and seasonality before promoting.
