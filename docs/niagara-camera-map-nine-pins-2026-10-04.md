# Niagara nine-pin camera map

The Niagara border-crossing map uses CARTO Voyager raster tiles with OpenStreetMap attribution. The authoritative camera set remains the nine NITTEC international-bridge cameras: five Peace Bridge, two Rainbow Bridge, and two Lewiston–Queenston Bridge cameras.

At regional zoom, nearby cameras are visually spread a small number of pixels around their bridge cluster centroid so every camera is immediately visible and tappable. This spread is only a collision-avoidance presentation. When the user zooms into the detail level, the existing camera-map owner renders each camera at its exact NITTEC latitude/longitude.

Whirlpool Rapids remains camera-free because no dedicated official road camera is identified in the NITTEC international-bridge set.
