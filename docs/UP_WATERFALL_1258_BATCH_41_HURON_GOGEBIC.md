### West and East Branch Huron, Baraga
- **West Branch #121**: field specialist describes multiple upstream cascades, fords, private camp on riverbank. County brochure's *unnamed bridge falls* uses a different route and must not be assumed to equal the natural source #121. No verified parking, legal riverbank corridor or safe dry crossing. Source: https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/west-branch-huron-river/west-branch-falls/ and https://www.visitbaragacounty.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Baraga-County-Waterfalls-Map.pdf.
- **East Branch #123**: source and field agree ~5m. This small named drop is not the county-advertised Big Erick's Falls. Field source describes a one-lane bridge and blocked logging track, nearby private property, no accepted trail or turnout. Sources https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/east-branch-huron-river/east-branch-falls/ and https://www.visitbaragacounty.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Baraga-County-Waterfalls-Map.pdf.

### Ottawa National Forest country, Gogebic
- **Ajibikoka #20**: Michigan GIS #20 at 46.329847,-89.237160 lies roughly 41m from the 2015 field point 46.329862,-89.237695. GIS river attribute calls this BRUSH LAKE; field accounts identify the outlet as Sucker Creek. This is the same natural cascade, but waterway names should be displayed carefully. The regional U.P. tourism source names Ajibikoka and acknowledges serious navigation, while an independent field report documents gated FR5120, road-to-swamp transition and off-trail approach. Without current MVUM for the approach, parcel proof and official/safe parking, no visitor guide. Sources https://www.uptravel.com/outdoors-recreation/national-forests/ottawa-national-forest/; https://www.gowaterfalling.com/waterfalls/ajibikoka.shtml; https://www.fs.usda.gov/visit/maps.
- **Judson #24**: Michigan GIS #24 at 46.389566,-89.561062 is about 64m from the independent Judson Falls field point 46.38927,-89.56034. The local Lake Gogebic recreation map labels Judson Falls in the correct vicinity; that map is location context, not trail or parking evidence. Lake Gogebic area tourism map only identifies natural geography. Independent 2023 field evidence follows logging-road forks/gate and overgrown footpath. No authorized vehicle parking or complete legal route. Sources https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/gogebic-slate-river/judson-falls/; https://www.lakegogebicarea.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/LakeGogebicTimes2019.pdf.
- **Nelson Canyon #25**: Michigan GIS #25 at 46.391819,-89.579675 agrees within roughly 3m with independent Nelson Canyon Falls field point 46.39180,-89.57971; USGS GNIS #1620996 is also nearby. The named waterfall itself is strongly corroborated. Regional tourism https://travelironwood.com/western-upper-peninsula-waterfalls/ promotes a historic C Camp Road two-track walk, but specialist 2026 comment reports substantial overgrowth and a rough descent. Derivative site https://waterfallhikes.com/waterfalls/nelson-canyon-falls-near-marenisco-in-mi calls NFST-7418-D a developed USFS trail; **this is not accepted** without official USFS trail geometry connecting that trail to the falls, a signed trailhead and legitimate parking. Treat as unresolved conflicting trail descriptions.

## Parcel and regulation checks
Each of the five natural GIS positions was queried live against Michigan DNR LOTS state-owned land layer 0 and conservation easements layer 9. All ten returned empty feature arrays. This is **not** a proof of private land, and does not rule out USFS ownership or easements not included in DNR's layers. Baraga and Gogebic parcel/ROW record verification remains required (Western U.P. Planning & Development GIS viewer references: https://www.wuppdr.org/gis).

The Forest Service MVUM authority applies to motorized Forest Service roads, not automatically to all historic logging roads. Official source: https://www.fs.usda.gov/visit/maps and national MVUM lines: https://apps.fs.usda.gov/fsgisx05/rest/services/wo_nfs_gtac/IVMQuery/MapServer/0. No current officially verified legal FR5120 vehicle parking, complete C Camp Road forest trail, Judson logging-road gate, or safe Baraga river-fording route has been established.
