# Michigan winter hiking & snowshoe decision engine — implementation checkpoint

**October 22, 2026. Phase 1 research staging only; not published.** Tracking issue: #1109.

## What is actually connected

- Michigan DNR [Recreation Search Points OPENDATA](https://services3.arcgis.com/Jdnp1TjADvSDxMAX/ArcGIS/rest/services/DNRRecreationSearchPointsOPENDATA/FeatureServer/0). A **65-record** point snapshot is in `data/winter-hike/michigan-dnr-candidates.json`. Stable DNR IDs, names, hiking/snowshoe tags and **destination reference coordinates** are preserved. It is a deliberately sampled set drawn from pagination offsets 0, 70, 140, 210 and 280, not an exhaustive or statistically representative statewide inventory. Some candidates are generic or mixed-use trails needing manual adjudication. Sampling and source dates are part of the file.
- Michigan DNR [Trails OPENDATA](https://gisagodnr.state.mi.us/arcgis/rest/services/DNR/DNRTrailsOPENDATA/MapServer): layers 0 temporary closures, 1 reroutes, 2 hiking and 6 snowshoe. Count queries were 193 closure line segments, 9,469 hiking segments and 1,726 snowshoe segments on 2026-10-22. Segment counts **are not** trail counts, open-trail counts or safe-trail counts.
- `lib/winter-hike-decision.cjs` is a **pure offline interpreter**. It does not fetch live NOAA/NWS, resolve a road/parking location, process GIS line intersections, or generate routing. It must not be wired to a public go/no-go output without evidence adapters and manager access checks.
- `tests/winter-hike-decision.test.js` includes offline permission, provenance, age, closure and severe-weather fixtures. It is included automatically by the repository's existing `node --test tests/*.test.js` suite.

## Hard trust boundaries

1. DNR point `Latitude/Longitude` and PageURL are **unverified candidates**, not validated winter parking or confirmed responding official URLs. No Google Maps driving link to them yet.
2. `ActivitiesTags` are permissions candidates; do not assume snowshoe access on groomed XC tracks or motorized trail segments. Cross-check official local manager policy for every destination.
3. NOAA NOHRSC snow is modeled grid depth, not measured packed trail surface. The live NOAA raster is not time enabled. Reuse the first-party snow adapter, verify meters-to-inches and dated layer validity, never construct a fake historical NOAA series.
4. NWS hourly weather is forecast weather, not an observed trail report. A forecast must cover the actual weekend date; warnings need location and effective-time verification.
5. A numeric score in the pure module is an **uncalibrated internal heuristic**. Never present it to users as a quantified safety or trip quality probability.
6. Routine positive decisions require a recent manager-confirmed, **winter-specific** trail/road/parking status. Unknown, undated and contradictory statuses remain `verify`. Known trail/parking/road closures are always excluded. An uncertain geometry overlap is a check, not an asserted closure.
7. Do not resurrect the intentionally retired `/michigan-snowshoe-conditions/` path or duplicate the existing `/michigan-snow-depth/` and `xcski.chrisizworski.com` search intents.

## October 22 — live adapter research gate

`lib/winter-hike-live.cjs` fetches a bounded destination point from NOAA NOHRSC ArcGIS Snow Depth (Image layer 3/Footprint layer 2), NWS `/points` -> `forecastHourly`, and NWS `/alerts/active?point=...` independently with 8-second timeouts and a best-effort 15-minute module cache. Only curated Michigan coordinates are accepted. Hourly NWS results must span at least five forecast hours from 10 AM–4 PM on the **destination-local requested date**, with a recent update timestamp. The sampled NOAA pixel is **meters** before inch conversion, 0 is permitted only for a valid dated pixel, and the ArcGIS service time without a zone is treated at **date-only** accuracy using a conservative noon-UTC freshness proxy. Missing/stale/invalid fields are unknown, not zero.

Active NWS alerts are filtered to a conservative Detroit-local outing-date envelope, with fall/spring DST differences safely overincluded by up to an hour rather than missing a near-midnight active alert. **No-alerts-now is not a forecast of no future warning.** An unavailable alert feed remains a separate safety check. The current NOHRSC raster does **not** provide hourly/14-day history or a forecast of new snowfall; the adapter explicitly marks new snowfall `unavailable`.

`lib/winter-hike-trip.cjs` joins source evidence to static manager findings. It is deliberately research-only and can **never** upgrade a DNR reference point or current land-manager description to verified winter parking/road/access; even a good environmental signal returns `verify` until those independent prerequisites are met. `data/winter-hike/michigan-winter-parking-audit.json` logs four manager-source examples. Hartwick Pines has an official visitor-center street address and winter opening schedule, but this does **not** verify winter parking at the selected trail. The Tahquamenon Falls historic winter maps depict plowed road/walking access and actual snowshoe loops; the historical map is not current-season proof of plowing, or a routable lot coordinate.

Tests: `node --test tests/winter-hike-decision.test.js tests/winter-hike-live.test.js` (expected 28 prior + 23 live/parking tests). The live-feed unit suite uses deterministic mocked upstream responses and does **not** claim an end-to-end paid/production deployment, field trail check, full NOAA history, or verified maintained winter parking. Do not publish or merge without the remaining gate.

**Fail-closed hardening:** duplicated hourly periods no longer count as complete forecast coverage. NWS active alert features with missing/invalid start or expiration dates retain their warning name and return `status: unavailable`, rather than being silently interpreted as `no warnings`.

### What is implemented

- `public/winter-hike-snowshoe-lab/`: styled static route-browser page, responsive JS/CSS, accessible selects for snowshoe / boot hiking, region, date and text, source-driven route cards, an explicit per-route evidence request and a 7-hour NWS temperature line chart. Browser geolocation is **opt-in**, sorted by great-circle distance (explicitly NOT driving time), adds a local blue dot, and never pans outside Michigan for distant visitors. - `api/winter-hike-lab.js`: a read-only, noindex Vercel endpoint returning the curated 38-route catalog (cached 20 min) or a **selected** outing day evidence record (cached 5 min). Routes, activity and trip-date inputs are validated; no arbitrary user coordinates or upstream source URLs accepted. Only a selected route fetches NOAA NOHRSC modeled snow, NWS hourly and active alerts, and DNR GIS line layers when a valid DNR UUID is available. Existing cache and timeout rules apply. A targeted 30s function runtime allows bounded parallel source reads; Redis and paid APIs are absent.
- `lib/winter-hike-map.cjs`: maps only identity-matched official source lines, with upper bounds 45 line features / 5,000 vertices. **18 of 38** curated routes have a direct matching DNR recreation ID that *may* resolve source line segments, but that is NOT 18 fully validated paths. The **other 20** named route identities have official park/PDF documentation without a vetted digital route geometry match; these are shown as reference-area pins plus manager map links, **never made-up polylines**. Red segments mean official mapped closures; purple source segments are unverified walking lines. A purple segment must not be mistaken for an entire signed hiking loop.
- **Every route remains access-unverified**, and directions/navigation are always disabled. There are zero season-confirmed plowed parking lot coordinates, zero end-to-end verified winter route itineraries, and therefore zero real 'go' verdicts. An official park address or GIS location cannot override this. The UI labels bare-snow hiking and snowshoeing as things to *investigate*, not an unconditional recommendation.
- `scripts/benchmark-winter-hike-preview.mjs --check` is an **on-demand** no-network safety benchmark for all 38 routes plus closure, warning, missing NOAA and forged manager scenarios. It assigns a high loss to any false positive go/direction; actual publication remains blocked until the real-world field checks pass. It is not wired into every merge/build.

## October 22, 2026 — explanatory HTML / JavaScript charts and NWS forecast trends

Implementation lives in `public/winter-hike-snowshoe-lab/visuals.js` (zero external chart-library dependencies), `public/winter-hike-snowshoe-lab/style.css`, updated `app.js`, and `lib/winter-hike-forecast-trend.cjs`, all in draft PR #1110. The new JavaScript is for browser interaction, not Java. Maps retain the existing CARTO / MapLibre arrangement.

**1. Seven-day future temperature trend:** Parses up to seven local calendar dates from the same server-side NWS hourly forecast response already requested to check the selected weekend. The vertical bars show actual source-backed 10 AM–4 PM local forecast *temperature ranges*, not overnight highs/lows, measured past data or snow-cover change. A 32 F marker provides freeze/thaw context. Seven hourly points = complete selected daytime window; five or six = partial; fewer than five valid distinct hours = unavailable with null temperatures. Clicking a date changes the real outing date, refreshes the route's forecast and highlights the selected date. The interpretive text compares only complete daytime windows and clearly states it does not predict snowmelt or the NOAA snow-depth trend. No extra forecast API requests are added: one NOAA raster + one NWS points + one NWS hourly + one active-alert request per uncached selected site.

**2. Hourly air temperature and wind:** A native accessible SVG temperature curve with a dashed 32 F reference line; seven separate interactive wind columns from NWS hourly period wind speed, plus a selection panel describing forecast conditions, wind and estimated wind chill for the selected local hour. Plotting requires valid values for both air temperature and wind at the same hour, and distinct local-hour coverage. Invalid/missing data never become numerical zero. All source descriptions are escaped before they enter the HTML or SVG.

**3. NOAA depth scale:** 0-12+ inch reference gauge with source model valid date and clear heuristic bands: under 2 inches is potential boot-hiking context, 2-6 inches requires local surface checks, and at least 6 inches can indicate snowshoe potential. These are NOT safety or trail-surface thresholds. Marker and 2/6-inch ticks are placed at accurate relative positions. Missing or stale NOAA values produce an explicit unavailable panel, not a fabricated zero or a historical trend chart.

**4. Weather hazards and access:** Four labeled weather risk tiles reflect selected daytime freeze/thaw, rain/freezing rain indications, estimated feels-like minimum and the present NWS active-alert feed. Zero current alerts is labelled *at query time*, never a guarantee that no weekend warnings will be issued. The manager access flow preserves three pending checks: winter trail/route open, maintained approach road, and plowed parking lot with correct entrance. No good-looking weather chart can turn a research reference point into driving directions.

**UI/interaction constraints:** Use semantic `<button>` controls, event delegation bound to the persistent detail panel, `aria-pressed`, textual chart summaries, `role=img` labels, keyboard focus outlines, and mobile-scrolling seven-day cards. The preview remains `noindex,nofollow` and protected by Vercel authentication. No additional advertising slots or tracking were introduced, and no synthetic snow trend, precipitation total, ski grooming report or route safety score is shown.
