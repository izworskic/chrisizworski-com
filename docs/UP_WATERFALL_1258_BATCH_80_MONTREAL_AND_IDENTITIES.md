# U.P. Waterfalls B80 — Montreal and three false-positive investigations

**November 7, 2026 · online-only, no emails or visitors asked to verify.**

## What was published

**One new public destination:** [Keweenaw Montreal Falls](https://chrisizworski.com/upper-peninsula-waterfalls/montreal-falls-keweenaw/) with a single canonical, source-linked guide. This is physically distinct from the Wisconsin/Michigan border Interstate Falls, even though both rivers are called Montreal. The Keweenaw site has a published legal Fish Cove/Mandan visitor approach from Visit Keweenaw, rough forest road and unsigned westbound shoreline walk. Smith Fisheries Road is specifically prohibited as private access. No surveyed lot GPS, upper-tier trail or path to the waterfall-coordinate dot is provided. DNR's November 6, 2026 land transition is region-wide and does not authorize ad-hoc visitor parking or trail geometry on every parcel. KORC's proposed new trail and lot remain proposals in the most recent public evidence found.

## Three source-identity resolutions

1. **Miners Beach Falls**: NPS identifies Elliot Falls at the far eastern end of Miners Beach, with legitimate parking and beach access. Treat independent directory name as *search alias with geographic concordance*, not proven independently mapped new natural waterfall. Do not confuse with the inland Miners Falls (separate trail).
2. **Piers Gorge Falls**: DNR documents multiple numbered Piers/rapids and separate MI/WI hiking trailheads. Existing Misicot Falls = Third Pier guide covers the Michigan-side visitor outing; Sand Portage has a separate Wisconsin-side guide. No duplicate Piers Gorge waterfall page or generic single point.
3. **Peterson Falls**: Iron County Wisconsin officially says the upstream feature is “closed to the public” relative to nearby Interstate Falls. Keep research identity and flag in Interstate's guide; no visitor listing, parking, path or search-indexable public guide for Peterson.
