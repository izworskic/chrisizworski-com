# Rolling forecast lifecycle audit

Follow-on to the near-term issue policy experiment, stacked on #1948. The visitor experience is a sequence of refreshed predictions, not one frozen estimate. Test the same owning Soo model at the original first issue and fixed 15-minute ticks for two hours (or UTC day end). Use only latest observation within five minutes, and preserve direction relative to first issue. States: fresh eligible forecast, stale/missing, invalid/conflicting, outside region, observed in chamber per existing model, reported low speed <=0.5 kn, model abstention, direction changed or update outside the 1–60 minute lead range. Never carry stale ETA through unavailable state. No independent verification of chamber occupation or cause of slowdown. Record absolute midpoint revisions, withdrawals, resumptions and every episode/slot. Selection does not use the future detector. Separately score matched pre-crossing updates on same observed bracket, average per vessel-day first to avoid treating repeated updates as independent events. Full lifecycle includes post-crossing slots; this is intentional and not a forecast success denominator. Preserve original initial opportunity counts, source receipts and frozen correction. No new calibration, production change, data service or commercial-use assertion. One previously studied development date September 10 initially, chosen to diagnose the observed near-term miss; not fresh evaluation. Execute alongside the exact existing model and compare baseline counts. No claim of accuracy improvement from one date.
