# Waterfall photography batch 5 — Rainbow, Hungarian Upper/Lower, and Lower Montreal
Research and release review: November 14, 2026.

## Rule
An image must show the actual named waterfall/tier, have a documented creator, public license authorizing commercial display, and a direct source citation. Historical photos do not imply public access, parking legality, open paths, present water conditions or permission to enter private property.

## Accepted photographs

### Rainbow Falls, Ottawa National Forest Black River, Gogebic County

- Creator: HuntersLab616, self-shot, original EXIF 2020-06-15. CC BY-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

- Leave existing Ottawa NF construction warning unchanged. Photograph does not demonstrate current west-bank platform opening.

### Upper Hungarian Falls, Dover Creek / Keweenaw
- Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hungarian_Falls,_Upper_Falls.png
- Creator: Fondycardinals, own original 750 x 1334, CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Original explicitly identifies 'The upper falls in the Hungarian Falls', differentiated from same photographer's Commons photographs of Middle and Lower tiers.
- Commons page date 2017-10-25 is not proven capture date: show date unknown. Display image uncropped; existing PNG may have letterboxed pixels.
- Keep land trust / DNR / private parcel boundary and seasonally unplowed parking caveats unchanged.

### Lower Hungarian Falls, Dover Creek / Keweenaw
- Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lower_falls.jpg
- Creator: Fondycardinals, self-published lower tier, CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Exact file description states 'The lower waterfalls of the Hungarian Falls'. The original image is ONLY 544 x 749. Accept a single explicit image-source resolution exception instead of inventing higher-resolution pixels or substituting the upper waterfall.
- Commons date 2017-10-27 need not equal camera capture date; do not claim capture year or modify gorge safety text.
- Keep cliff, off-trail and creek crossing warnings intact.

### Lower Montreal Falls at night, Keweenaw
- Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Montreal_Falls_at_Night.jpg
- Creator: RomanKahler, original 1205 x 1805, dated 2017-08-14, CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Creator's caption explicitly names **Lower Montreal Falls at Night**, and Commons lists image in Waterfalls in Michigan. Named tier matches the existing guide's lower Montreal visitor objective.
- Night photography is not proof the Fish Cove two-track road, rocky coast or primitive path is safely passable after dark. The caption explicitly warns against interpreting night photography as a trip recommendation.
- Add the photo to the existing lower-falls visit guide without changing illegal Smith Fisheries Road / unsigned footpath warnings.

## Held candidates / audit discipline
- Yellow Dog River: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Yellow_dog_falls.jpg names 'waterfalls on the Yellow Dog River', **not** a precisely identified curated Yellow Dog Falls coordinate. Do not reuse until named-drop identity is established independently.
- A potentially attractive Superior Falls category has video files only, not a suitable still photo with exact subject/source.
- Other destinations stay unphotographed; image title/URL is never inferred to grant trail access.
