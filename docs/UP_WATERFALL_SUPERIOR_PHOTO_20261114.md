# Superior Falls: exact lower-tier photograph
Review: November 14, 2026

The existing Superior Falls guide covers the Montreal River hydroelectric gorge along the Wisconsin–Michigan border, reached from a signed Michigan-side access route. This is not the upstream Saxon Falls site and not Montreal Falls at the tip of the Keweenaw.
