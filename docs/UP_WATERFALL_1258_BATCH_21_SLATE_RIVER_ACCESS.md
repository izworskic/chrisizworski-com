### Additional authoritative land-rights check
The [TNC hunting and reserve policy](https://www.nature.org/en-us/about-us/where-we-work/united-states/michigan/stories-in-michigan/deer-hunting-in-michigan/) says only **25%** of Slate River Forest is currently in Michigan's Commercial Forest Program and around **one-third** has private hunting leases; leaseholders retain exclusive hunting privileges within leases. This does NOT contradict general foot-hiking permission on the reserve, but CFP/hunting rights are not a substitute for a parcel-verified hiking easement or exact turnout permission.
