# ShoreFeel interpretive editorial pass | November 22, 2026

## Visitor experience
A person choosing a beach needs the modeled air comfort immediately, a clear sense of the actual place, and a practical way to interpret the wind before going. The voice combines a knowledgeable park interpreter with a meteorologist. It is welcoming without claiming to be employed by a park agency or promising current conditions.

## Editorial evidence and constraints
- Existing Michigan location catalog: `data/beaches.json` (50 named beaches, including the Saginaw Riverfront that is not a swim beach).
- Existing U.S. shoreline inventory: `data/shorefeel-us-coast.json` (16 places, official park and swimming-advisory links).
- Existing Ontario inventory: `data/shorefeel-canada-ontario.json` (30 places, location evidence, Ontario Parks and municipal public-health sources).
- National Weather Service: [lake-breeze explanation](https://www.weather.gov/apx/lake_breeze), [beach-hazard forecasts](https://www.weather.gov/greatlakes/beachhazards).
- ECCC city forecast source and the explicit distinction from a beach thermometer are preserved from the existing Canadian engine. NOAA offshore data is never described as a water measurement at a swimming area.

The new copy uses named dunes, marshes, river mouths, channels, headlands, lighthouse views, and shore orientations already documented in the source catalogs. It invites visitors to notice wind direction, moving waves, shelter, and how the lake differs from an inland location. These are observations to make **on arrival**, not claims about observed or forecast conditions at the time of publication. There are no fabricated access conditions, data sources, temperature reductions, swimming endorsements, or reported water temperatures.

## Published surfaces
- Three canonical hub and explorer pages: Michigan, U.S. non-Michigan, and Ontario.
- Fifty Michigan standalone beach reports: new source-controlled `data/shorefeel-field-notes-mi.json` keyed by canonical slug and consumed by `scripts/generate-beach-pages.mjs`.
- Sixteen U.S. and thirty Ontario detail pages: authored `story` values in their existing catalogs, emitted by the shared `scripts/generate-shorefeel-details.mjs`; the two atlas lists are regenerated by their existing scripts.
- Live selection state is still the official weather response, not copy-driven inference; regional chooser notes and fallback phrasing are more helpful.
- Sitemap `lastmod` and detail JSON-LD content-modification dates updated to November 22, 2026 where actual copy changed. The five search-treatment titles and H1s, Person entity, canonical URLs, ECCC attribution and Auto ads implementation stay in place.

## Editorial acceptance
Every place must have a distinct note with grounded scenery, conditional weather interpretation and something worthwhile to notice. Keep reading short enough to leave the temperature visible first. Source constraints and swimming safety remain separate from invitation. Preserve the strong no-data language, posted-flag instructions, known access warnings, official links, keyboard/map fallback and no-JavaScript park links.

## Release checks and boundaries
Run targeted ShoreFeel Node tests, `npm run verify:all`, source hashing, generated HTML audits and existing Playwright phone/desktop/no-data checks on the exact PR head. Inspect screenshots before merging. Filled Google Auto ads can only be assessed after a real browser receives live creatives; a screenshot of a fixture does not prove their placement. This is an editorial change only: no paid service, Redis, new Vercel function, forecast math, scoring or data-input change.
