### Algonquin: distinguish an NCT hike from a waterfall visitor route

The independently geolocated [Black River Algonquin field visit](https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/gogebic-black-river/algonquin-falls/) identifies a roughly one-foot, split rapid. [World Waterfall Database](https://www.worldwaterfalldatabase.com/waterfall/Algonquin-Falls-13088) disqualifies its stature. USDA FS latest public surface-ownership layer (source date **2026-10-30**) intersects the mapped natural point in Ottawa National Forest, and official FS published-trails data detects NCT #60014 within 1,000m. The historic report requires an unmarked steep riverbank descent off the NCT and suggests a tiny shoulder pull-off. **Neither agency trail proximity nor natural-point ownership demonstrates official signed parking or a sanctioned final waterfall viewpoint.** Do not copy roadside guidance or promote a sixth Black River Scenic Byway trail.

### Ogima / Ogimakwe / Abinodji: paddling reach is not a hiking guide

[American Whitewater's Copper Creek report](https://www.americanwhitewater.org/content/River/view/river-detail/2690/main) specifically describes the sequence of three bedrock slides, class III–IV in whitewater use, accessed historically via an **unmarked logging road off CR519**, and notes old gate/fence conditions in visitor comments. A 2007 source photo caption at **Ogimakwe** describes *commercial forest land in Gogebic County*. No verified 2026 parcel owner or gate rights accompany the paddling account. Direct USDA FS PADUS point checks for #65, #69, #70 returned **zero federal surface-ownership polygons**, and official NFS trails within 1km returned **none**; do not call all three USFS-owned and automatically open to hiking.

Independent geography: [Ogima](https://mapcarta.com/22354350) ~38m, [Ogimakwe](https://mapcarta.com/22354356) ~19m, and [Abinodji](https://www.anyplaceamerica.com/directory/mi/gogebic-county-26053/falls/abinodji-falls-1618983/) ~24m from GIS source positions. These are three separate named slides, with **no proven shared visitor trailhead**, not one automatically walkable 2.4-mile trip. Third-party U.P. guides may give visitor mileage and "moderate" classifications without signed legal-route evidence; do not repeat as verified.

### Nokomis: material ownership contradiction

[Wikimedia Commons' original dated 2007 Nokomis Falls photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nokomis_Falls_-_panoramio.jpg) describes **commercial forest land in Gogebic County** and has a geotag on the Presque Isle River. [GeoNames independent feature point](https://www.geonames.org/5003563/nokomis-falls.html) is ~80m from state GIS #74. Contemporary third-party guides conflict: some assert Porcupine Mountains Wilderness State Park ownership and advise walking from developed Lepisto Falls; others call it a U.S. Forest Service site with a moderate signed hike. The **exact-source GIS point returned no federal FS-managed parcel, no DNR state-owned parcel/easement, and no published NFS trail within 1km**, which supports withholding both specific owner claims, fees and visitor directions. It does not demonstrate private-title status or definitively exclude a state park parcel outside LOTS coverage. Obtain authoritative park boundary/land-title, approved trail and parking before changing publication status.
