# Soo independent-date AIS proxy validation

Predeclared follow-on to the 2025 three-day study in #1968. Visitor decision: whether a trip to watch a ship can be planned from an estimated arrival window. The initial nine matched vessel-days showed a large midpoint improvement with a much wider window. Other 2024 archive work used a different +14 minute challenger and different crossing labels; it is not combined with this study.

## Frozen design
- Source: NOAA Nationwide AIS 2025, https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/77594 ; https://coast.noaa.gov/accessais/ (CC0 public domain dedication), official daily archive https://noaaocm.blob.core.windows.net/ais/csv2/csv2025/index.html. NOAA points are reported positions, not official lock events or comprehensive Canadian traffic. No raw records retained in artifacts.
- Four calendar dates selected before accessing observations: October 22, November 5, November 19, December 3, 2025. Late-season chronological extension, not a representative seasonal sample. No date substitution.
- Same owning Soo candidate, 5 to 15 nautical mile approach, eight-hour horizon, strict chamber rectangle and <=180 second entry bracket. One first eligible issue per vessel-day. Primary issue follows original observation policy. Secondary diagnostic uses fixed quarter-hour UTC ticks, only observations at or before the tick and at most five minutes old. Both use identical labels within their own paired comparison. No cross-policy matched comparison unless explicitly matched later.
- Freeze +49.77 minute midpoint residual and 74.47 minute diagnostic radius from run 45392249652. Verify prior artifact values before collecting; no model or radius refitting, no tuning based on fresh dates.
- Report all issue opportunities at vessel-day level, no-issue count, censoring, unmatched and unbracketed outcomes, matched conditional MAE and signed error, full bracket containment, width, unresolved containment bounds, each date/direction/reported type, and distinct matched identities as a count only. Repeat vessels can appear on different dates. No probability, false-positive rate or field accuracy claim.
- Proposed research screen, not release authorization: all four dates, >=30 matched, >=5 per date, >=10 each direction, >=70% resolved, candidate MAE <=80% of baseline, >=80% matched full containment, mean width <=150 minutes and <=2x baseline, at least three dates no worse on MAE. These are engineering decision thresholds chosen before outcome access, not statistically calibrated limits. Any absent gate fails closed. Primary and secondary results separate.
- Existing collector discards conflicting vessel-time rows; this can obscure intervening evidence and is reported as a limitation. Bracketed AIS presence is a proxy, not independent field truth. A passing screen cannot enable production forecasts.

Absolute 150 minute width gate alone passes. Original conditional MAE improves 30.3%, but fixed-grid upbound error worsens 17.36 to 43.30, downbound improves 53.81 to 31.40. Direction-specific matched subsets are descriptive and are not a causal explanation or authorization to tune. Fixed-grid signed errors change from -6.47 to +43.30 upbound and -53.81 to -4.04 downbound. The global offset overcorrects upbound observations. Neither policy is a live operational replay with actual receipt timestamps, nor an independent official passage label.

Decision: reject global +49.77-minute offset for production. Preserve existing live model and stale-state safeguards. The 2024 +14-minute challenger is not pooled: distinct issue rule and reference target, 16 scored of 91 candidates, insufficient holdout evidence despite all six numeric gates passing. See `docs/research/great-lakes-bias-corrected-october-results.json`. This 2025 cohort is spent as a holdout. If further research is justified, investigate direction and decision-time lead as diagnostic strata using development data only, compare unchanged baseline and width-constrained hypotheses, then predeclare a fresh disjoint cohort before fitting or testing. Do not choose correction parameters from the opened four-date holdout and then call it independent. Prefer visitor-useful narrow windows and truthful abstention over high containment achieved with broad windows. No live numeric update, probability claim, production promotion, new collection, or merge authorized by this result.
