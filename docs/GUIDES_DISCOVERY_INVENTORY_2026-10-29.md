# Guide directory inventory — October 29, 2026

The `/guides/` directory is an editorial entry point, not an inventory of every destination card or every live tool. An audit compared its links with the nine live entries in `public/assets/field-guides-registry.json`, source-page titles and canonicals, the gardening hub, and the live Michigan dark-sky guide.

- **Michigan and seasonal:** all nine registered seasonal guides, Michigan Adventure Finder, the fall-color destination-guide collection, and Michigan After Dark. The four separate Northern Michigan wine seasonal editions are linked directly from their parent card. Michigan After Dark and the Midwest dark-sky page are owned by the national outdoor tools repository and served by explicit hub rewrites.
- **Great Lakes travel and outdoors:** Lake Superior Circle Tour, Lake Superior Provincial Park, Apostle Islands, U.P. waterfalls, Mackinac ferry and bridge pages, Au Sable and Manistee guides, trout and fish references, shipwrecks, lighthouses, ship-watching locations, Duluth, Niagara Falls, ferry routes, paddling, beaches and boat launches. The beach and boat-launch cards link directly to their Saginaw Bay and Lake Michigan regional guides. Individual waterfall guides are discoverable through the U.P. waterfall collection rather than being listed as dozens of indistinguishable cards.
- **Gardening:** the existing Zone 6a, frost, tomato and seed-saving guides plus seed starting, native plants, last-spring-freeze probabilities, heirloom versus hybrid seeds, and Michigan seed libraries. The gardening hub supplies the full interactive tool collection.
- **Other destinations:** Old Sow Whirlpool is grouped separately from the Great Lakes.
- **Not promoted:** `/porcupine-mountains/` is currently marked `noindex,follow` in its source. Add it when its own release and indexing decision are complete. Other research-only previews remain outside the directory.

`tests/guides-discovery-inventory.test.js` checks the audited standalone links, local backing pages and noindex status, the nine live seasonal registry entries, wine editions, the dark-sky rewrite and collection counts. When a new guide is released, add a descriptive entry or a clearly linked collection, update its count and this inventory, and extend the test for a distinct standalone guide. Do not promote an unfinished child simply because its parent is live.
