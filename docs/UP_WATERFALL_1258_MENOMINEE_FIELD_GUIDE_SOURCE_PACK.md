## Sand Portage Falls (Michigan GIS 3, Dickinson County)

**Editorial hook.** Here the Menominee compresses into the rock-walled Piers Gorge, and a short trail on the Wisconsin bank reveals the river’s force from above. This is a walk for travelers who want a powerful river landscape rather than an easy roadside cascade.

**Arrive.** Use the Wisconsin side of Menominee River State Recreation Area. Wisconsin DNR identifies a parking lot off **U.S. 141, just east of Knuston Road**, for the Sand Portage Falls Hiking Trail. This is a road-level description, not a precise driveway coordinate; do not route to Michigan GIS waterfall coordinates.

**Walk.** Official DNR route is **0.5 mile** from the parking area to a view of Sand Portage Falls and Piers Gorge. The trail has wooded and open sections, a mowed surface and moderate hills. The falls do **not** have a designated viewing area; an optional descent toward water involves rocky ground and should not be represented as the standard visitor route. Accessibility is not certified.

**Know before going.** This is part of a binational state recreation area; consult Wisconsin DNR for property and trail guidance. Wisconsin DNR says park access roads are **not plowed during winter** and may be hard to use during wet periods. That is not an assertion of today's gate or conditions.

## Pemene Falls (Michigan GIS 188, Menominee County)

**Editorial hook.** The Pemene overlook ends on the rim of a wooded gorge where the river narrows against rock. It rewards a short forest hike, but it is not a developed platform.

**Arrive.** Wisconsin DNR identifies the Pemene Falls hiking-trail parking area **at the end of Verheyen Lane off County Road Z**, Wisconsin. Do not confuse the separate **Pemene Landing boat launch off Horseshoe Road** with the waterfall trailhead. Do not use the raw Michigan GIS #188 falls coordinate as driving directions.

**Walk.** The overlook is **0.5 mile from the trailhead**, along former logging roads and natural-surface forest paths. The park also offers a **0.8-mile short loop** and **1.9-mile long loop**. Some sections have steep slopes and rocky ground; signs are limited at junctions. The overlook is primitive, at the gorge edge, without a developed viewing area. Do not advertise it as accessible.

**Know before going.** DNR describes a mandatory canoe portage at Pemene Falls on the Michigan side; this does not establish a hiker shortcut. Access roads on the Wisconsin property are unplowed in winter. The Michigan GIS point has an unresolved position disagreement with descriptions of the visitor overlook; keep the coordinate flagged, not silently corrected.

**Sources:** https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/parks/menominee/recreation ; https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/parks/menominee/info ; https://www.michigan.gov/recsearch/parks/menominee ; Michigan GIS object 188.

## Quiver Falls (Michigan GIS 189, Menominee County)

**Editorial hook.** At Quiver Falls the river cuts through a historic logging corridor. Remnants of dams and cribbing upstream tell part of the story of how timber once traveled down the Menominee.

**Arrive.** Wisconsin DNR directs visitors via **Pemene Dam Road** and describes a dedicated **parking area and footpath** to the falls overlook. Unlike the Pemene gorge overlook, Wisconsin DNR specifically describes the Quiver parking and viewing areas as accessible to people with disabilities. Do not invent parking coordinates, exact footpath distance or an entirely step-free route.

**Walk.** Follow the designated footpath from the parking area to the overlook. DNR publishes no mileage in the relevant visitor text, so leave the walk length unspecified until independently verified.

**Know before going.** Wisconsin DNR says the property's access roads are unplowed in winter and may be difficult in wet weather. No current parking/gate or water-flow claim is made.

**Sources:** https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/parks/menominee/recreation ; https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/parks/menominee/info ; https://www.michigan.gov/recsearch/parks/menominee ; Michigan GIS object 189.
