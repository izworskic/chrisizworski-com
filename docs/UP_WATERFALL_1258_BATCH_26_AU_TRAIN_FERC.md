## Batch 26 — Au Train Falls final FERC license determination and visitor land-rights research (2026-10-31)

[**Primary FERC Dec 29, 2025 order P-10856-141**](https://mi-psc.my.site.com/sfc/servlet.shepherd/version/download/068cs00001ZSbG5AAL), 193 FERC ¶61,242, is FINAL, terminating UP Hydro's license by implied surrender effective the close of that day. It does **not** itself order dam removal or a visitor site closure. Current EGLE oversight applies to dam safety. FERC describes high-hazard dam classification, inadequate spillway compliance, UP Hydro bankruptcy and change in title: 18 of 22 project parcels foreclosed May 2025, transferred by bank to **D. Charles Trust Investments LLC**, including the powerhouse and most of buffer. It does not grant permission or show present legal parking at upper/lower falls. [2026 legal commentary](https://www.troutmanenergyreport.com/2026/01/ferc-terminates-au-train-hydroelectric-project-license-by-implied-surrender/) independently parses order. Prior Nov 2025 [regional news](https://www.uppermichiganssource.com/2025/11/17/federal-government-calls-surrender-au-train-dam/) reported a proposal, now superseded.

[Munising Visitor Bureau historical directions](https://www.munising.org/activities/au-train-falls/) name upper and lower parking approaches on Power Dam Road and lower gate walk; none verified against post-foreclosure current permission, EGLE warnings, gates, or closure.
