## Predetermined experiment

Choose the first existing engine candidate per vessel-day with heuristic score >=0.52 and 5–15 modeled channel nautical miles remaining. At issue time use only the observed fix and existing candidate engine, with no archive destination because the collector did not retain it. All issues enter the denominator before checking outcomes. Search later fixes up to 8 hours for the first position inside a strict unpadded modeled chamber rectangle. The immediately previous outside fix must occur after or at issue, within 180 seconds and imply <=40 kn straight chord speed. Unbracketed first observation, no observation or day-end censoring remain unresolved, not measured false positives. No official event label or precise entry time is inferred. The midpoint of the two observed times is an evaluation proxy only, not a confirmed crossing.

Compare naive channel distance/speed, existing slowed-approach formula/window, and one small challenger: median signed residual from June 15 training proxy matches (>=5 samples and >=3 vessel identities). August 15 separately determines diagnostic radius (max bracket residual, >=5 matches and >=3 identities). October 15 is untouched holdout, no fitting/selection from it. No random row split or inflated per-fix sample size. Report counts of issued/matched/unresolved, conditional midpoint MAE, median absolute error, signed bias, full bracket containment and overlap, interval width, and stratify by direction. Repeated vessel identities across days and sparse, selected dates limit generalization. Diagnostic radius is not an empirical probability or calibrated confidence interval. Thresholds are fixed research policy, not measured navigation limits.

Keep existing JS over StatsForecast/MAPIE or runtime Python: a small median-offset baseline is sufficient for this first three-date research and avoids additional packages or unjustified ML. Offline Python may be appropriate for larger independent samples. No live model change, no navigation advice. The actual independent outcome gate is blocked: chamber geometry is approximate OSM-derived and no operator-confirmed arrival labels available. Even if the challenger beats both baselines, keep forecastEnabled false; more independent days, vessel diversity, operational labels, and prospective validation required.
