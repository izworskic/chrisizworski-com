## Visitor question, objective and baseline
**Which authority has published event-specific information for the actual mission I selected?** Before: two approved official article indexes (NASA Wallops and Vandenberg News) in the signed scheduled Python reconciler; a Vandenberg HTML source returns 403 in automated runs, so a genuine upcoming Space Force announcement could be unavailable. Florida's verified KSC launch-viewing event collector exists but visitors in the national hub do not see it alongside the selected mission.

After: an independent official federal Space Force *news RSS* route for California and a read-only KSC *exact selected UUID* bridge for Florida. No duplicate LL2 source calls, no additional databases or subscriber accounts. The primary KSC watcher remains the owner of tickets/venues and the existing national launch truth remains the schedule source.

## Source evidence and restrictions
1. **Federal U.S. Space Force RSS (government-owned):** https://www.spaceforce.mil/DesktopModules/ArticleCS/RSS.ashx?ContentType=1&Site=1060&max=20 . Public RSS metadata including original article link, title, synopsis and actual RFC-822 publication timestamp. Source is national, NOT guaranteed to cover Vandenberg. It must **explicitly say Vandenberg** and contain exactly one recognized unique mission designation and an *explicit forward-looking launch date* to match California. RSS publication is NOT an event date. Future launches announced by commercial companies but not in this RSS remain unknown.
2. **Original Vandenberg HTML news:** https://www.vandenberg.spaceforce.mil/News/ . Continue to try, but HTTP 403 is 'unavailable' and does not disable the independent RSS. Vandenberg's local weekly roundup and GovDelivery launch alerts are useful to visitors but are not RSS/HTML inputs here until an officially reusable machine feed can be established.
3. **Florida KSC original event pages:** existing watch from https://www.kennedyspacecenter.com/launches-and-events/events-calendar/ and individual https://www.kennedyspacecenter.com/event/.../ URLs, verified by lib/space-coast-ksc-watch.js. The national browser now calls its existing /api/space-coast-ksc-watch?launchId=<same UUID> only for Florida, after the national mission has loaded. This is a *read only* viewer integration. It never republishes event HTML, creates tickets or infers that a viewing location is open. Last page check, source validity, event date, mission/launch ID and source-pad checks remain enforced by the existing watcher; browser adds a **within one local calendar day** gate and hides stale/unavailable outcomes.

## Evidence lifecycle, contract and guarantees
- Python worker remains scheduled every nominal 30 minutes, reads max 20 RSS items and 440 KB response, blocks external redirects, XML entities/DOCTYPE, non-government HTML article URLs, missing or stale publication dates, generic military stories, completed launches and off-range narratives.
- Only 2 eligible federal RSS Vandenberg future articles can become candidates per scan, after cross-checking against the same fresh LL2 Postgres manifest. Does not equate RSS publication with a new rocket launch. No artificial scoring/confidence percentages.
- Existing signed GitHub Actions OIDC POST with strict 'sourceStatus' registry now includes 'spaceforce-news-rss'; match rule 'official-rss-exact-designation-and-local-date' is distinct from 'exact-designation-and-local-date' for directly retrieved NASA/Vandenberg articles. Node enforces approved federal news article origin/path, identifier, region, date, and 'not-evaluated' access/launch statuses. The UI explicitly labels **RSS summary** as the source instead of claiming the linked full article was scraped.
- KSC read is from existing public server API, never joins source timelines by a fuzzy mission name. Network failures and overdue source checks display the original official calendar and Florida specialist link without asserting ticket availability.
- Both sources are bounded independent input layers; RSS failure does not erase the original ten-source desk, and KSC failure cannot alter official mission matches or national launch schedule.
- The ten-source official **land-manager** ledger does not increase its source count (still ten); its extra *event reconciliation* source status grows from two to three independent official announcement routes.
