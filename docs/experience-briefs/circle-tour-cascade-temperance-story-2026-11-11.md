# Circle Tour | Cascade River and Temperance River, Minnesota North Shore

**Verified:** November 11, 2026. **Repository:** `izworskic/chrisizworski-com`. **Scope:** existing stops 24 and 25, source-backed narrative and four file-licensed images. Correct an existing false statement about Temperance River's visible mouth. No new public URLs, specialists, route stops or map subsystem.

## Distinct visitor decisions and source hierarchy

- **Cascade River #24:** [Minnesota DNR map and guide](https://files.dnr.state.mn.us/maps/state_parks/spk00133.pdf) lists Cascade River Lower Loop as **0.5 mile**, Lookout Mountain Loop **3.5 miles**, ascending **600 ft**. [DNR virtual tour](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/state-parks/virtual-tours/cascade-river/dialup.html) provides park-specific interpretation. [MnDOT May 27, 2026 notice](https://www.dot.state.mn.us/news/2026/05/27-d1-cascade.html) announces construction, possible parking impacts at **Cascade River Wayside**, not a permanent closure of the waterfall loop; [Superior Hiking Trail Association conditions](https://superiorhiking.org/trail-conditions/) reports separate east-side Cascade River Loop work and changed mileage on a different Superior Hiking Trail loop. Do not treat a dated notice or a historic map as a current trail-open promise. Existing arrival desk distinguishes park office from waterfall wayside.
- **Temperance River / Lutsen #25:** [Minnesota DNR map and guide](https://files.dnr.state.mn.us/maps/state_parks/spk00268.pdf) identifies the **0.25-mile one-way** gorge walk from northeast Highway 61 parking and **3-mile one-way** rugged Carlton Peak route; **no swimming**, rocky gorge edges and swift water. [DNR virtual tour](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/state-parks/virtual-tour/temperance-river/dialup.html) documents the **visible river mouth** and explains the lack of gravel “bar”, corroborated by [University of Minnesota river-name research](https://openrivers.lib.umn.edu/article/rivers-north-shore-ojibwe-dialects/). [Lutsen Mountains](https://www.lutsen.com/mountain-info/hours-of-operation) operates separately from DNR: do not imply the state park's entry is a ski resort entrance, or that lift hours are verified by the DNR.
- **Visible-mouth correction:** The original stop-25 teaser, description and activity note incorrectly said the river vanished in a rock crevice or pothole with no visible mouth. This confuses separate waterfall phenomena (e.g. the Brule River's Devil's Kettle) with the Temperance River. Replace all three with source-verified description and adjust rigid seasonal resort copy.
