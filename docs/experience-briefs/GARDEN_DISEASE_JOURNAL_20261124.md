AHDB Great Britain potato Hutton criterion: https://potatoes.ahdb.org.uk/development-and-implementation-of-a-new-national-warning-system-for-potato-late-blight-in-great-britain-hutton-criteria

Source-specific scouting guidance is paraphrased. The hourly RH >=90%, temperature 68–81°F and 59–68°F filters are descriptive weather cues, not infection-duration thresholds. Powdery mildew does not require wet leaves in the same manner as downy mildew; no inference from rainfall alone. Wet foliage at inspection does not establish wetness duration. No weather-based diagnosis, infection probability, local outbreak assertion, fungicide schedule or treatment threshold. The Hutton reference is restricted to potatoes, two consecutive complete future civil days each with forecast hourly-sampled minimum >=50°F and >=6 hourly RH samples >=90%; developed in Great Britain for potato warning, not Michigan-validated. Hourly samples do not prove continuous humidity or exact daily minima. Local pathogen presence and spore reports not integrated; no negative assurance if criterion absent. No season extrapolation for dormant crops; living foliage must be explicitly confirmed. No unobserved plant disease or crop stage inferred. Leaf check counts are sample counts, not field prevalence; backup includes observations.
