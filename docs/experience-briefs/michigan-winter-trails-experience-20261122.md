## Visitor job and baseline

Choose a documented trail by winter activity, region and effort; see actual nearby forecast evidence; confirm trail rules, winter road and parking status with the authority; then resume comparing choices.

A fresh 390 by 844 production screenshot showed NO named option on the first phone screen. The desktop 1280 screenshot showed the interactive two-column comparison, plus an AdSense Auto ads unit generated by the current policy. The existing compared trail cards expose field notes and official source but not the already deployed route-matched weather inspector. No obvious way returns keyboard focus to the same choice from that inspector.

## Three investigated opportunities

1. First choice and weather follow-through, SELECTED. Compact the first screen, add weather to each source-backed compared option, and return to that selected comparison. Reuse 15 dossier IDs, existing shortlist, Leaflet map and NOAA/NWS weather lens. Small UX work, no new production API or data.
2. Real loop geometry and verified winter arrival. Source GIS is not uniformly matched to whole, public winter trails or plowed entrances. This demands a separate source-reconciliation project; regional map pins are not drive targets.
3. Current grooming and plowed-lot evidence. Operators lack one reliable, dated, unified feed for all 15 systems. Weather cannot be used as an open/safe status.

## Two materially different feasible concepts

**A. Compact evidence-linked comparison, selected.** First phone screen shows a clear heading, short source-honest introduction and activity/region/effort selection leading immediately to a named trail comparison. Each generated option offers field notes, official rules and a route-specific area weather check. The shared weather panel opens using that stable source ID, with an explicit way back and focus restoration. Weather unavailable remains unknown. Desktop keeps the forest-colored editorial framing; official source-backed dossiers remain below.

**B. Map-first selected-place drawer, deferred.** A small regional map, accessible list and activity controls occupy the first screen; a selected destination drawer links to the weather panel and source. This might accelerate geographic choice but requires more responsive focus and tile-failure work, while map coordinates remain unverified winter entrances. No mapped winter parking or turn-by-turn direction may be inferred.

**Reason for choosing A:** The visitor is choosing an activity and documented route, not navigating an authoritative winter GPS network. A fixes two observed breaks in the existing working flow and can be proved without creating unsupported geography.

## Distinctiveness veto and comparators

- Michigan Snow Depth is primarily a spatial snow magnitude/condition model. This tool compares documented, named routes and activities, then evaluates modeled snow independently.
- Michigan Cross-Country Skiing is an operator/grooming discipline-specific tool. This field guide covers snowshoes, skis, winter hiking and fat bikes with route-specific etiquette and official sources, without promoting NWS forecasts to grooming statuses.

The difference is in both decision and action: a two-route source-backed editorial comparison with route-matched reversible weather evidence rather than a snow ranking, and a multi-activity selection rather than a live ski-center condition table. Original credited real-place photography remains in the interpretive guide.
