These are river measurements, not verified waterfall discharge; no inferred connection to individual falls. No station proximity scoring. Request only 14 calendar days on explicit click, three requests max per click; abort after eight seconds. Schema v1: AVAILABLE (date, daily mean, qualifier, approval, optional seven-day comparison), STALE (date only), UNAVAILABLE (reason). Completed days use Michigan Eastern calendar; three-day freshness limit; no extrapolation, missing means unknown, conflict fails closed, zero is valid, incomplete current days excluded, flagged observations suppress comparisons. USGS revisions and provisional values can change. Comparison is not a seasonal percentile, safety rating or prediction.

## Second investment: station-specific seasonal context

Upstream DOI-USGS dataretrieval-python implementation https://github.com/DOI-USGS/dataretrieval-python/blob/main/dataretrieval/waterdata/stats.py documents the nested GeoJSON data/values and percentile arrays, with examples at https://doi-usgs.github.io/dataretrieval-python/examples/USGS_WaterData_DailyStatistics_Examples.html . Reuse official computed statistics instead of downloading decades of raw records or deploying an ML model. Exact observation month-day and parent series ID are required, including leap day; no mixing stations, monthly aggregates, series or units. Require at least 20 daily samples (an editorial minimum, not an agency guarantee), unique record, finite nonnegative monotonic percentiles and a nondegenerate 10th to 90th range. Typical is inclusive p25 to p75; unusual is strictly below p10 or above p90; no invented exact percentile. USGS period of record is not necessarily 1991-2020. Show historical median, middle-half range and sample count. Ice/other qualifier, unknown approval, missing source, mismatches and stale readings suppress comparison. Provisional remains visibly provisional. No inference of upstream/downstream waterfall discharge, dam operation, ice safety or trail access.

USGS station metadata explicitly reports drainage areas 790 sq mi (04045500), 200 (04031000), 358 (04032150); source links are the official monitoring-location pages. Display equivalent daily outlet volume depth as cfs * 86400 * 12 / (areaSqMi * 5280^2) inches, not effective rainfall, snowmelt, a rate of ice formation or actual waterfall flow. Suppress flagged readings. Connorville's published daily record begins 2023-06-21; Tula 04032000 ended 2023-06-19 at a different site with 261 sq mi drainage. Do not splice them; a 20-sample threshold may make Connorville seasonal context unavailable for years.

KERY/KIWD are airport points, not watershed sampling stations or guaranteed upstream locations. A separate visitor-triggered request retrieves at most 24 hours per station, two parallel requests, 8-second abort. NWS reported 24h precipitation is a rolling observation, not summed; requires source units and fresh timestamp. Null/unsupported/QC-failed values remain unknown. Temperature range is observed sample extrema only, with at least six unique readings, spanning back at least 20 hours, latest within three hours, maximum four-hour gaps. A crossing of 0C is a possible freeze/thaw cue only where snow or ice exists. No interpolation or melt/flow prediction. No snowfall conversion.
