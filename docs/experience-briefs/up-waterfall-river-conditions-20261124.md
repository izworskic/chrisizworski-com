# Waterfall intelligence: first investment (2026-11-24)

## Visitor job and design
The existing map, arrival desk, four regional plans and 80 cards solve location and access. The remaining question is whether river conditions have changed. Keep the existing map-first layout. Compared with a prominent waterfall-specific condition dashboard (not supported by gauge-to-falls evidence), a compact, opt-in river reading panel is honest and less intrusive. Unlike the Circle Tour, it does not change the itinerary. No destination eligibility, routes, photos, guide URLs, ad settings or schema are altered.

## Source register and schema

Stations 04045500 Tahquamenon near Paradise, 04031000 Black near Bessemer, 04032150 Presque Isle near Connorville. These are river measurements, not verified waterfall discharge; no inferred connection to individual falls. No station proximity scoring. Request only 14 calendar days on explicit click, three requests max per click; abort after eight seconds. Schema v1: AVAILABLE (date, daily mean, qualifier, approval, optional seven-day comparison), STALE (date only), UNAVAILABLE (reason). Completed days use Michigan Eastern calendar; three-day freshness limit; no extrapolation, missing means unknown, conflict fails closed, zero is valid, incomplete current days excluded, flagged observations suppress comparisons. USGS revisions and provisional values can change. Comparison is not a seasonal percentile, safety rating or prediction.

## Second investment: station-specific seasonal context
USGS modern Statistics API documentation https://api.waterdata.usgs.gov/statistics/v0/docs and https://waterdata.usgs.gov/statistics-documentation/ describe period-of-record day-of-year percentiles. Upstream DOI-USGS dataretrieval-python implementation https://github.com/DOI-USGS/dataretrieval-python/blob/main/dataretrieval/waterdata/stats.py documents the nested GeoJSON data/values and percentile arrays, with examples at https://doi-usgs.github.io/dataretrieval-python/examples/USGS_WaterData_DailyStatistics_Examples.html . Reuse official computed statistics instead of downloading decades of raw records or deploying an ML model. Exact observation month-day and parent series ID are required, including leap day; no mixing stations, monthly aggregates, series or units. Require at least 20 daily samples (an editorial minimum, not an agency guarantee), unique record, finite nonnegative monotonic percentiles and a nondegenerate 10th to 90th range. Typical is inclusive p25 to p75; unusual is strictly below p10 or above p90; no invented exact percentile. USGS period of record is not necessarily 1991-2020. Show historical median, middle-half range and sample count. Ice/other qualifier, unknown approval, missing source, mismatches and stale readings suppress comparison. Provisional remains visibly provisional. No inference of upstream/downstream waterfall discharge, dam operation, ice safety or trail access.
