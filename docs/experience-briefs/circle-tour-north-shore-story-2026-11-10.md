## Visitor decisions and controlling source boundaries

1. **Split Rock (27):** The Minnesota DNR's Split Rock Lighthouse State Park contains Pebble Beach, trails and shoreline. The lighthouse is a **separate Minnesota Historical Society historic site**, with different admission and operating hours. Park permits and historic-site tickets are not interchangeable. See [DNR park top stops](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/state-parks/split-rock-lighthouse/things-to-do.html), [DNR state park](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/state-parks/park.html?id=spk00266) and [MNHS lighthouse](https://www.mnhs.org/splitrock).
2. **Tettegouche (26):** Minnesota DNR's [North Shore waterfall itinerary](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/state-parks/itineraries/north-shore-waterfall-itinerary.html) distinguishes the **1.2-mile Shovel Point trail**, with about **300 stair steps**, from the **3-mile High Falls round trip**. [DNR's November 24, 2025 reopening announcement](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/news/2025/11/24/new-high-falls-pedestrian-bridge-opens-public-tettegouche-state-park) is historical infrastructure status, **not a live trail-open verdict**.
3. **Grand Marais (23):** [Visit Cook County's destination guide](https://www.visitcookcounty.com/places-to-visit/grand-marais/) describes the waterfront community, Artist's Point, local crafts and dining. A stroll on the harbor is not a trip up the Gunflint Trail. Shoreline rocks and breakwaters require wind/wave/ice caution; do not assert they are safe today.

The photo layer conveys the setting *after* the visitor's existing next decision, never live weather, access, admission or driving time.
