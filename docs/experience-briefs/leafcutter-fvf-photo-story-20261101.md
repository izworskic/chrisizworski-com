# Freighter View Farms: four real photo field notes for the leafcutter Experience OS

**Date:** 2026-11-01 · **Canonical:** https://chrisizworski.com/leafcutter-bees/ · **Owner:** izworskic/chrisizworski-com · **Scope:** editorial/authentic photo update to the released Experience OS 1.2.1, not an alternate tool or species model.

## Visitor decision and hierarchy

The primary answer (habitat first, then observe forage/nest/managed brood stage) is unchanged and remains before this feature. The addition answers: **"What does this actually look like in a real Michigan garden?"** A reader sees an authentic 2024-to-2026 personal field note with photo-backed explanations of nesting hardware, arrival packaging and exterior nesting trays, then returns to the existing care planner. It **never** promotes a photo of a shipping box to a universal handling protocol.

## Selected concept and distinctiveness

- **A. Product-style photo gallery**: four equally weighted tiles with a caption. Rejected: looks like a generic gallery and foregrounds attractiveness over explanation.
- **B. A photo essay, "From my garden on Saginaw Bay"**: one evocative sunset image anchors an asymmetric responsive field-note layout, followed by three targeted close-ups with stage labels, plain-language descriptions, visible photo credits and source posts. Selected. It differs from Michigan Gardening's planner modules and the leafcutter season-state illustration because the photographs are *observed historical field notes*, not a real-time state visualization or an advertised product collection.
- **Quality, not a template**: Retain the original identification hero, seasonal nesting interaction, scientific qualifiers, SEO canonical, creator identity, official NWS/USA-NPN evidence lanes and the ad setup. No new dependencies, endpoints, tracking, maps or animation.

## Quality and safety

- Photos are local, lazy loaded, async decoded, described with useful alt text, and framed by explicit photography credits and both original blog links.
- Tested responsive reading at 360, 390, 768 and 1440px; no clipped text or horizontal overflow; real images are expected to load in the offline/third-party-blocked test harness. Capture screenshot artifacts for inspection at each width.
- Keep warning labels about managed purchased cocoons vs wild cavity-nesting bees and about not opening unknown brood; no yield claims, biological GDD thresholds, or observed bee activity implied.
- Under no-JS mode, the feature remains plain HTML and all original care/season content remains accessible.
- Roll out only after `npm run verify:all`, actual browser CI, source/public audit, Vercel preview and production follow-up. Record what is verified, not visitor outcome metrics.
