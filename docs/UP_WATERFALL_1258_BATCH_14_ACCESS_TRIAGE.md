## Findings and next verification

### Douglas Houghton Falls — Michigan GIS #135 (HOUGHTON)

**Finding:** Michigan DNR confirms Phase 1 parking, an accessible path and Hammell Creek pedestrian bridge completed in 2025, but the path beyond the bridge remains unimproved, with no observation deck, guardrails or final dedication. Phase 2 is planned for 2027. Visit Keweenaw says the site is open for visitation; neither source authorizes interpreting the undeveloped cliff path as an accessible or finished overlook.

**Visitor safety:** Do not direct drivers to the lower private-property approach or imply the final waterfall overlook is complete. Before promoting a guide, reconcile DNR's formal-opening statement with Visit Keweenaw's visit guidance and identify any posted construction or closure restrictions.

**Evidence limitation:** State DNR and visitor bureau describe different milestones: visitation versus official dedication; do not collapse these into an unqualified open/closed claim.

**Next release gate:** Obtain current DNR site-specific visitor operating status, allowable accessible-path terminus, and bridge-to-overlook safety rules; do not use a lower-gorge private entry.

**Sources:** [Michigan DNR scenic-site status](https://www.michigan.gov/recsearch/parks/houghtondouglassfalls) · [Michigan DNR current project phases](https://www.michigan.gov/dnr/managing-resources/prd/key-project-updates) · [Visit Keweenaw visitor guidance](https://www.visitkeweenaw.com/outdoors/waterfalls/houghton-douglass-falls/).

### Dead River Falls — Michigan GIS #160 (MARQUETTE)

**Finding:** Travel Marquette's waterfall visitor map describes a 1.7-mile Forestville Road waterfall outing from a parking area right of the powerhouse, up a gated gravel service road and down to the river. Marquette Township's recreation plan names Dead River Falls in Forestville. NTN's separate Forestville trailhead is not proof that its parking is the same as the powerhouse waterfall approach.

**Visitor safety:** Use the tourism-described Forestville approach only after checking posted property, service-gate, parking and trail restrictions. Do not route to the GIS falls point or substitute an NTN Forestville/NCT lot without verified connecting public footpath.

**Evidence limitation:** Printed tourism map describes the visitor route, not current utility power-station permissions or a surveyed legal parking lot. Variable hike distances from independent guides are not parking-to-falls measurements.

**Next release gate:** Verify current owner/operator of powerhouse-side road and public lot, any gate or construction rule, and continuity of the visitor footpath; distinguish NTN Forestville Trailhead from the falls parking.

**Sources:** [Travel Marquette waterfall map, Dead River #3](https://assets.simpleviewinc.com/simpleview/image/upload/v1/clients/marquettemi/2022_TravMarq_Publication_ReviewV7_dragged__6473b902-4bb2-4721-9223-6dc8198a807b.pdf) · [Marquette Township 2022–26 recreation plan](https://www.marquettetownship.org/sites/g/files/vyhlif6666/f/news/marquette_township_recreation_plan_2022-2026_dnr_approved.pdf) · [Noquemanon North Trails access list](https://www.noquetrails.org/north-trails).

### Sturgeon Falls — Michigan GIS #45 (HOUGHTON)

**Finding:** Federal Recreation.gov and Wild and Scenic Rivers materials place Sturgeon Falls in Ottawa National Forest's Sturgeon River Gorge Wilderness. The North Country Trail Association's 2025 chapter guide documents a wilderness falls trail descending by steep switchbacks and a distinct signed approach from the NCT spur. No snow plowing is provided on connecting roads.

**Visitor safety:** Use official wilderness trailhead and published trail maps only; do not turn the falls GIS pin into a parking GPS. A wilderness switchback into the gorge is a strenuous footpath, not an accessible overlook or guaranteed open motor route.

**Evidence limitation:** NCTA's 2025 trail details establish historical approach and jurisdiction, not current road clearance, snow conditions, exact seasonal bridge/footpath conditions or a measured parking-to-waterfall distance.

**Next release gate:** Reconcile FR2270/FR2200 named public trailheads with current Ottawa National Forest motor vehicle use map, visitor parking and any 2026 trail/road notices before an indexable guide.

**Sources:** [NCTA Peter Wolfe chapter 2025 segment guide](https://northcountrytrail.org/files/chapters/pwc/PWC_33-46_Apr2025.pdf) · [Recreation.gov official federal wilderness overview](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/poi/238242) · [Federal Wild and Scenic Rivers overview](https://www.fws.gov/rivers/river/sturgeon-ottawa).

### Tioga Falls — Michigan GIS #46 (BARAGA)

**Finding:** A specialist waterfall field record places Tioga Falls at a Tioga River cascade near a private camp and explicitly describes a signed private-property gate blocking the easier south approach. Suggested river crossings and off-trail alternatives are not verified lawful visitor paths.

**Visitor safety:** Do not tell visitors to pass the gate, cross the river to bypass private land or take the GIS coordinates as a car destination. Keep research-only until an authorized public trailhead, parking and continuous permitted approach exist.

**Evidence limitation:** Third-party historical route description is not a legal parcel survey or 2026 gate status; absence of a public route is not proof that every possible access is legally closed.

**Next release gate:** Check land boundaries/permissions from owner or authoritative land manager and an independently designated safe public parking and trail route; no informal ford guidance.

**Sources:** [Independent field account and private-gate warning](https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/tioga-river/tioga-falls/) · [Michigan GIS named waterfall](https://gisp.mcgi.state.mi.us/arcgis/rest/services/BaseMap/General_Geology/MapServer/7).

### Upper Falls (Falls River) — Michigan GIS #91 (BARAGA)

**Finding:** L'Anse municipality describes a public Falls River nature trail and upper, middle and lower waterfall views, but a separate recent guide claims an Upper Falls label is on private land. GIS #91 upper coordinate is not enough to assign an exact trail view or resolve this same-name collision.

**Visitor safety:** Use the Village/Baraga Falls River Trail route only for documented public viewpoints; do not enter upstream private yards, cross tracks, walk beneath US-41 or assume GIS #91 can be visited legally.

**Evidence limitation:** Municipal trail summary does not georeference which of several 'Upper' falls points is public. Private-property statement is secondary and may describe a different upstream drop.

**Next release gate:** Match each named falls feature to the municipal trail's legally accessible section and parcel; resolve #91 versus other upstream 'Upper Falls' before public page.

**Sources:** [Village of L'Anse 2025 information manual](https://cms2.revize.com/revize/lansemi/VILLAGE%20INFORMATION%20MANUAL%202025.pdf) · [Baraga County Trails public Falls River Trail](https://www.baragacountytrails.org/trails/falls-river-trail) · [Conflicting private Upper Falls guide](https://upper-michigan.com/guide/waterfalls/upper-falls-lanse.html).

### Middle Falls (Falls River) — Michigan GIS #95 (BARAGA)

**Finding:** L'Anse village materials describe riverbank trail viewing of named middle and lower drops. Baraga County Trails publishes a 1.4-mile out-and-back Falls River Trail with downstream Main Street lot and upstream US-41 bridge-side trailhead, but the exact GIS #95 viewpoint and railroad boundaries are not independently aligned.

**Visitor safety:** Use only signed public Falls River Trail, avoiding railway tracks, hydro-plant property and risky riverbank scrambles. Do not present the natural falls GIS pin as parking.

**Evidence limitation:** The quoted public trail total is the entire trail, not measured from either specific waterfall. Informal guides suggesting crossings under or over bridges are not authorization.

**Next release gate:** Verify GIS #95 identity versus posted Middle Falls viewpoint and municipal lot/trail access rules, then assess public guide promotion alongside Lower Falls as distinct stops or one shared itinerary.

**Sources:** [Village of L'Anse 2025 visitor information](https://cms2.revize.com/revize/lansemi/VILLAGE%20INFORMATION%20MANUAL%202025.pdf) · [Baraga County Trails signed route and two lots](https://www.baragacountytrails.org/trails/falls-river-trail) · [Independent middle-drop identity account](https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/falls-river/middle-falls/).

### Lower Falls (Falls River) — Michigan GIS #96 (BARAGA)

**Finding:** Village of L'Anse and Baraga County Trails describe public waterfront-side river walking and lower Falls River cascades. The specialist geographic source distinguishes the lower natural drop from historic cement dam features; they must not be conflated into a dam-access route.

**Visitor safety:** Start from signed municipal waterfront/river parking if open and follow designated river paths only; do not cross a gated industrial bridge or go onto the old dam to see a GIS point.

**Evidence limitation:** Public trail can reach river scenery, but sightline to GIS #96 and legal bank/path boundaries require source confirmation. Municipal trail length is not lower-falls hike length.

**Next release gate:** Confirm separate Lower Falls (#96) versus adjacent artificial drop and authorized viewing position before promoting an indexable guide.

**Sources:** [Village of L'Anse municipal falls overview](https://cms2.revize.com/revize/lansemi/VILLAGE%20INFORMATION%20MANUAL%202025.pdf) · [Baraga County Trails route](https://www.baragacountytrails.org/trails/falls-river-trail) · [Lower Falls independent description](https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/falls-river/lower-falls/).

### Power House Falls (Falls River) — Michigan GIS #88 (BARAGA)

**Finding:** Village of L'Anse's 2025 visitor manual independently describes a small park at Powerhouse Falls and an upstream half-mile trail by the old powerhouse, separate from downtown lower/middle Falls River walk. It gives no current gate, structural-safety certification or exact lot GPS.

**Visitor safety:** Use only the park entrance and established path; do not enter old powerhouse buildings or cross utility tracks/structures, and do not assume the state GIS #88 marker is a driveable lot.

**Evidence limitation:** Historic power station and trail descriptions do not establish structural safety or current 2026 operation of the parking lot. Other generic Power Dam/Falls River records are different nearby features.

**Next release gate:** Verify current small-park operator, official entrance and parking permission plus correspondence of GIS #88 to the waterfall visible from the park before visitor-guide promotion.

**Sources:** [Village of L'Anse official visitor manual](https://cms2.revize.com/revize/lansemi/VILLAGE%20INFORMATION%20MANUAL%202025.pdf) · [Independent location description of Powerhouse Falls](https://www.waterfallsofthekeweenaw.com/falls-river/powerhouse-falls/) · [State GIS #88 natural feature](https://gisp.mcgi.state.mi.us/arcgis/rest/services/BaseMap/General_Geology/MapServer/7).
