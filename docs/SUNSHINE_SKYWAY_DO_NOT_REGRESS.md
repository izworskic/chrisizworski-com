# Sunshine Skyway — do not regress

1. Do not derive bridge closure/open state from raw wind.
2. Do not turn the FDOT 40 mph context into an automatic closure rule.
3. Do not label stale or missing FL511 data as current.
4. Do not calculate toll amounts in browser code.
5. Do not reintroduce current cash-payment language.
6. Do not invent vehicle prohibitions without an official rule.
7. Do not replace the embedded camera with link-only camera cards.
8. Do not iframe an HLS `videoUrl` as the only playback path; use video + native HLS/Hls.js.
9. Do not scroll the user away from the camera they selected.
10. Do not let camera, weather, map or toll failure erase the primary operational answer.
