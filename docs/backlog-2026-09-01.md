Ordered by measured opportunity, not by age. Source: Search Console export
`chrisizworski.com-Performance-on-Search-2026-09-01`, last 28 days (2026-08-02 to 2026-08-29),
web search. Every experiment window was read and closed on 2026-09-01; see

## Where the estate actually stands

869 clicks and 46,545 impressions in 28 days, average position 9.45 in the United States.
Roughly triple the ~300 clicks/month recorded a month ago.

Two facts shape everything below.

**One page carries the business.** `/soo-locks/` returns 347 clicks at 3.80% CTR from position
6.96 — 40% of all clicks on the estate. It is the only surface performing at its position.

**The rest lose most of their earned demand at the snippet.** Ranking is no longer the binding
constraint on the highest-volume pages; several sit at position 7 to 10 and convert under 1%.
Closing that gap to a normal curve for those positions is worth roughly **425 clicks per 28 days**,
about a 50% lift on total site clicks, with no new ranking required.

## The queue

Each item lists clicks left on the table at the page's current position, using a conservative
position-to-CTR curve. That number is an estimate of headroom, not a promise.

### 1. `/northern-lights-michigan/` — ~179 clicks
10,328 impressions, 110 clicks, **1.07% CTR at position 8.9**. The largest single opportunity on
the estate, and the experiment that just closed as a miss (target was 2.5%).

Position improved while CTR fell, and the export shows the reason: a wall of zero-click aurora
questions — `northern lights michigan` (81 impressions), `northern lights michigan tonight` (39),
`are there northern lights in michigan tonight` (36), `can you see the northern lights in michigan
tonight` (31), all at zero clicks. The page ranks for the question and does not win the click.

Work: rewrite title and description against that question set rather than the tool's own framing.
Note `tests/seasonal-search-protection.test.js` pins this page's title, description, canonical, h1
and JSON-LD hash — the pins stay, and any change must repin deliberately with the reason written
into the test. That is the intended flow, not an obstacle.

### 2. Mackinac cluster — ~91 clicks

**0.17%**, both around position 8 to 10. Combined they earn 3,846 impressions and 12 clicks.

The toll page is the more tractable of the two: the query set is explicit and answerable

Constraint: this page carries hard pins — exact title, `id="mackinac-conditions-answer"`, the h2

attributes, no FAQPage, and the phrase "Estimated best crossing window" is forbidden. Read
`tests/` before touching it. The suicide-related query cluster on this topic remains deliberately
untargeted.

## What changed in how this gets measured

`benchmarks/growth-experiments.json` now carries a power floor: do not freeze a page for a CTR
experiment unless its 28-day baseline clears both 4,000 impressions and 80 clicks. Measured across
all 21 experiments on 2026-09-01, only `/northern-lights-michigan/` and `/soo-locks/` clear it.
Everything else needed between 2,229 and 77,988 impressions per arm to detect a 50% relative lift
against baselines of 30 to 5,479 impressions — which is to say those windows were never going to
answer anything, at any length.

For pages under the floor: measure average position and impressions, which carry far more data and
move first, or ship and watch rank.
